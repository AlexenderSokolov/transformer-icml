# RATE-ROW-MAX：原始 add-one 的 K^(5/2)sqrt(1+ln K) actual TV 上界与全合法精度转移

本独立后继义务承接当前一般源研究，不修改旧 G/U、SRC、RATE、K3、K-LOW、MINIMAX、REFUTE+、MM5 的接纳或原依赖。inputs/row-max-rate-seed-20261028.md 和 scope_review 预检仅为候选输入，不是已接纳证明。该义务不成为旧 root 或已完成012/013的逆向前提；完成本义务亦不宣告v10完成。

## 准确实验及待证结论

对全部整数 K>=2、M>=1，令 N=M+2、字母表 A={0,...,K-1}。theta 为任意允许零边的齐次二阶转移表，mu 为任意初始对律；每条实验内部 theta 固定，sup 可以随 K,M 改选源。观察完整 X_1^(M+2)，令

    Y_i=(X_(i+1),X_(i+2)), 0<=i<=M,
    C_s,c=sum_(i=0)^(M-1) 1{Y_i=s,X_(i+3)=c},
    n_s=sum_c C_s,c, J=n_(Y_M),
    p_s,c^+=(C_s,c+1)/(n_s+K),
    R_K^+(M)=sup_(theta,mu) E TV(p^+_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M)),
    L_K=1+ln K,
    B(K,M)=K^(5/2)sqrt(L_K)/sqrt(M+1).

待证完整有限参数上界为

    R_K^+(M)<=min{1,48 B(K,M)}.                   (ROW-MAX+)

目标是同一完整轨迹上的 actual E[TV]；不是平方损失、fixed/all/conditional风险、独立测试查询、删尾或后缀平均。算法不获辅助行栈、隐藏源参数、混合参数或未来符号。

对满足以下全路径概率向量接口的共同有限族 F（特别是 SRC Fact fef8372728290b45 已实现的同一个原 Pi），令

    h>=h_0(M)=max{4,ceil(log2 M)+1}, Q=2^h,
    TV(F_(K,N,h)(x_1^N;s),p_s^+(x_1^N))
        <=78KM/[Q(n_s(x_1^N)+K)]+(K-1)/Q.        (I)

写 c=K^4/(M+1)、A_h=78KM/Q、g_h=(K-1)/Q，并定义

    Psi(0)=0,
    Psi(x)=x(1+ln(1/x))       if 0<x<1,
    Psi(x)=1                 if x>=1.

必须保留并证明以下准确精度转移，全部合法 h 均成立：

    sup_(theta,mu) E TV(F_h(Y_M),p^+_(Y_M))
        <=min{1,g_h+Psi(c A_h)},
    sup_(theta,mu) E TV(F_h(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))
        <=min{1,48 B(K,M)+g_h+Psi(c A_h)},         (ROW-MAX-I)
    sup_(theta,mu) E TV(F_h(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))
        <=min{1,64 B(K,M)}.                      (ROW-MAX-h)

F_h(Y_M) 简记完整已观察历史及实际末对输入下的输出。ROW-MAX-h 必须直接覆盖最低合法 h_0；不能把高精度结论自动转移到 h_0。对同一个原 Pi，以原公开序列

    h_N=ceil(log2(128(K+1)(M+3)^3)), e_N=(M+3)^(-2),

另证明

    sup_(theta,mu) E TV(Pi_(h_N)(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))
        <=min{1,48 B(K,M)+e_N}.                  (ROW-MAX-N)

## 候选机制与必须核查的数学环节

在有限辅助空间耦合每行至少2M个独立 theta_s 样本并复现原轨迹全部 departure，定义 S_s,j=sum_(l<=j)(e_(xi_s,l)-theta_s)。iid 只用于确定行前缀；不可在条件于随机 J 或 Y_M 后称样本 iid。任意初始化、J=0、小M、暂态、可约、周期及退化 theta 均须覆盖。

对确定整数 v>=1、v<=M，令 F_s,v=max_(0<=j<=2v)||S_s,j||_2，B_v=max_s F_s,v。候选先由有限 Doob L2 得 E F_s,v<=sqrt(8v)，再证明修改一坐标使 F_s,v 至多改变 sqrt(2)，从而

    P(F_s,v-E F_s,v>t)<=exp(-t²/(2v)), t>=0,
    ||B_v||_4<=6 sqrt(v(1+ln K)).

有界差分及尾积分须给可独立核查推导，不能把逐坐标范围误付为K因子。K²行的union只付sqrt(log K)；不以相邻高矩参数优化替代这一步。

在实际事件 E_v={v<=J<2v} 上使用逐路径TV/L2比较，再无条件Hölder保留行、计数与误差之间的全部相关性。使用已接纳 G/U Fact a12539a93d9f1ee3 的整数访问控制

    P(J<v)<=min{1,cv}, v>=1,

及明确的dyadic几何求和，覆盖所有非零J；数值常数须足以推出48。若引用 RATE Fact 42a4f0a54a7ca79c 的有限耦合或dyadic引理，须列入实际前驱；若重述自足证明，可不增加该依赖。

平滑和接口误差须用截断积分，不得先松成 K H(c) 再丢回 K³。对每个实数 A>=0，需要证明

    E min{1,A/(J+K)}<=Psi(cA)<=2sqrt(cA).         (TRUNC)

处理 A=0 及 t in (0,1) 的事件。若 x=A/t-K>0，则整数 J 给 {J<x}={J<ceil x}，且 ceil x<=A/t；若 x<=0 事件为空。严格说明据此应用整数UC及尾积分，不能在非整数阈值上直接套用整数UC。特别检查J=0、cA>=1和小M。将A=K用于平滑，将A=A_h用于接口误差，保持min截断。合法精度有Q>=2M；ROW-MAX-h的64常数和ROW-MAX-N的e_N应分别证明。

## 3. One fixed row: prefix maximum, mean, and bounded differences

### 3.1 A finite maximal inequality and the mean bound

The centered independent increments make S_(s,j) a vector martingale.
Conditional Jensen for the Euclidean norm shows that
Z_j=||S_(s,j)||_2 is a bounded nonnegative submartingale.

Here is the finite L2 maximal inequality being used. For any bounded
nonnegative submartingale Z_0,...,Z_T, write Z*=max_j Z_j. For lambda>0,
partition {Z*>lambda} by the first crossing time j. Iterating the
submartingale inequality gives E[Z_T|F_j]>=Z_j. Multiplication by
the F_j-measurable first-crossing event and summation give

    lambda P(Z*>lambda)<=E[Z_T 1{Z*>lambda}].

Integrating twice the two sides with respect to lambda, using
E(Z*)²=integral_0^infinity 2lambda P(Z*>lambda)d lambda, yields

    E(Z*)² <=2 E[Z_T Z*]
            <=2 ||Z_T||_(L2) ||Z*||_(L2).

If ||Z*||_(L2)=0 the desired bound is immediate; otherwise division
gives ||Z*||_(L2)<=2||Z_T||_(L2). All variables are bounded and the
finite first-crossing partition justifies every step.

For U=e_xi-theta_s,

    E U=0, E||U||_2²=1-||theta_s||_2²<=1.

In the expansion of E||sum_(l=1)^T U_l||_2², cross terms vanish by

    E F_(s,v)² <=4 E||S_(s,2v)||_2²<=8v,
    E F_(s,v)<=sqrt(8v).                            (6)

### 3.2 The conditional exponential bound, with its proof

A finite random variable W taking values in an interval of length d
and having mean zero satisfies, for lambda>=0,

    E exp(lambda W)<=exp(lambda²d²/8).              (7)

To prove it, set psi(lambda)=ln E exp(lambda W). Then psi(0)=psi'(0)=0,
and psi''(lambda) is the variance under the exponentially tilted law.
Every such law remains supported on the same interval [a,b]. For its
midpoint m0=(a+b)/2, variance is at most E(W-m0)²<=d²/4.
Twice integrating psi''<=d²/4 from 0 proves (7). A one-point law
(d=0) is included. The same proof applies to each finite conditional
law separately, so (7) is also valid conditionally.

View F_(s,v) as a function of the independent symbols xi_(s,1),...,xi_(s,T).
Replacing just coordinate l from a to b leaves prefixes j<l unchanged,
and adds e_b-e_a to every prefix j>=l. Its norm is at most sqrt(2);
the reverse triangle inequality and then the maximum over j show

    |F_(s,v)(x)-F_(s,v)(x')|<=sqrt(2)                (8)

whenever x,x' differ in only one symbol. This concerns a whole
K-dimensional vector. There is no summation of K coordinate ranges.

Reveal these T symbols in order and set

After fixing the first l-1 symbols, the conditional expectation as a
function of xi_l has range length at most sqrt(2): compare two values
of xi_l while averaging over the same product distribution of the
remaining symbols, and apply (8). Thus D_l has conditional mean zero
and conditional range length at most sqrt(2). Equation (7) gives

    E[exp(lambda D_l)|xi_1,...,xi_(l-1)]<=exp(lambda²/4).

Iterated conditioning over T=2v differences proves

    E exp(lambda(F_(s,v)-E F_(s,v)))<=exp(lambda²v/2).

For t=0 the right side is 1, so the same statement holds. This proves

Here sqrt(8)<3 and 8^(1/4)<2. The penultimate step is ordinary

The L4 triangle inequality follows, for example, by writing
E|U+V|^4<=E[(|U|+|V|)|U+V|^3] and applying Hölder separately to
the two terms, followed by division by ||U+V||_4^3 when nonzero.
All uses have finite moments. Thus (12) is a deterministic-prefix
moment bound and has not conditioned on the terminal row or on J.

## 5. Integer UC implies the exact real-A truncated integral

For every real A>=0 we claim

    E min(1,A/(J+K))<=Psi(c0 A)<=2sqrt(c0 A).         (13)

If A=0 all quantities in (13) are zero. Suppose A>0. For each 0<t<1,

    {min(1,A/(J+K))>t}={J<A/t-K}.

Write x=A/t-K. If x<=0 this event is empty because J>=0.
If x>0, J is an integer and

    {J<x}={J<ceil(x)},  ceil(x)>=1,
    ceil(x)<=x+1=A/t-K+1<=A/t.

The equality remains true when x itself is an integer. We use UC only
at the positive integer v=ceil(x), not at x or at A/t. Consequently,
in either case,

    P(min(1,A/(J+K))>t)<=min(1,c0 A/t).             (14)

Integrating t from 0 to 1 proves the first inequality in (13):
for z=c0 A in (0,1) the integral is

    integral_0^z 1 dt + integral_z^1 (z/t)dt
        =z(1+ln(1/z));

for z>=1 it is 1. The A=0 case agrees with Psi(0).
Furthermore min(1,y)<=sqrt(y) for every y>=0. Thus the same integral
is at most integral_0^1 sqrt(z/t)dt=2sqrt(z), which proves the second
inequality in (13), also for z=0.

This proves TRUNC for all real A and all finite K,M. In particular,
J=0, z>=1, and M=1 are not excluded. We will use A=K for smoothing
and A=A_h for implementation error. We never replace the result by
K times an untruncated inverse-moment estimate.

## 6. Dyadic decomposition and the actual add-one risk

### 6.1 The numerical dyadic estimate

For every a>0,

    sum_(l=0)^infinity 2^(-l/2) min(1,a 2^l)^(3/4)
        <=10sqrt(a).                              (15)

To see this, divide the summand by sqrt(a) and write z_l=a2^l.
It becomes z_l^(1/4) when z_l<1 and z_l^(-1/2) when z_l>=1.
If a>=1, only the second part occurs and its sum is at most
1/(1-2^(-1/2))<7/2<10.

If 0<a<1, let l0 be the first index with z_l0>=1, so 1<=z_l0<2.
For l<l0 the terms form an increasing geometric progression; the
largest, at l0-1, is less than 1. Their sum is therefore bounded by
1/(1-2^(-1/4)). For l>=l0 the terms are decreasing geometrically and
their first term is at most 1, so their sum is bounded by
1/(1-2^(-1/2)). The first constant is <13/2 because
2^(-1/4)<11/13 (equivalently 13^4<2*11^4), and the second is <7/2
because 2^(-1/2)<5/7 (equivalently 49<50).
Their sum is <10. Restoring sqrt(a) proves (15).
This is a numerical sum; no infinitely long sample stacks are required.

### 6.2 Selected row, arbitrary random visit count, and Hölder

On J>=1 let phat_(Y_M)=C_(Y_M)/J, purely as an analysis vector.
For dyadic integers v=2^l<=M, let E_v={v<=J<2v}.
These events partition {J>=1}, because 0<=J<=M. On E_v, (5),
Cauchy–Schwarz in R^K, and J>=v give the pathwise inequality

    TV(phat_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))
       =||S_(Y_M,J)||_1/(2J)
       <=sqrt(K)||S_(Y_M,J)||_2/(2J)
       <=sqrt(K)B_v/(2v).                         (16)

The maximum in B_v covers every possible selected row and every
prefix J<2v on this event. It is defined using the first 2v<=2M
samples of each row, so it is available on the finite auxiliary space.

Apply Hölder to B_v and 1_(E_v) on the unconditioned space:

    E[B_v 1_(E_v)]<=||B_v||_4 P(E_v)^(3/4).

Neither independence of B_v and E_v nor any conditional iid assertion
is used. Since E_v is contained in {J<2v} and 2v is a positive integer,
UC and (12) show that

    E[TV(phat_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))1_(E_v)]
      <=3sqrt(K L_K) v^(-1/2) min(1,2c0 v)^(3/4).  (17)

Sum only the actual blocks v<=M, then upper bound this nonnegative
finite sum by (15) with a=2c0. It follows that

    E[TV(phat_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))1{J>=1}]
      <=30sqrt(2) sqrt(K c0 L_K)
      <=43 B,                                    (18)

where (30sqrt(2))²=1800<1849=43². The extension of the numerical sum
past M makes no assertion about a random variable B_v for v>M.

### 6.3 J=0 and add-one smoothing

For a row with j>=1 departures, put u_K=(1/K,...,1/K). Then

    p_s^+= j/(j+K) phat_s + K/(j+K) u_K,
    TV(p_s^+,phat_s)=K/(j+K) TV(u_K,phat_s)
                    <=(K-1)/(j+K)
                    <=K/(j+K).                  (19)

The bound TV(u_K,q)<=1-1/K for probability vectors q follows by
expressing q as a convex combination of the point masses, all of which
are at TV distance 1-1/K from u_K. On J=0, the original add-one output
is u_K and its loss is at most 1. Therefore

    E TV(p^+_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))
       <=P(J=0)
          +E[TV(phat_(Y_M),theta_(Y_M))1{J>=1}]
          +E[K/(J+K)].                           (20)

Extending the last term to J=0 only enlarges the upper bound; phat
itself is never defined or used at zero counts.

UC at v=1 and (13) at A=K yield

    P(J=0)<=min(1,c0)<=sqrt(c0)<=B,
    E[K/(J+K)]=E min(1,K/(J+K))
              <=Psi(c0 K)<=2sqrt(c0 K)<=2B.        (21)

We used L_K>=1 and K>=2. Combining (18), (20), and (21) gives a bound
at most 46B, hence at most 48B. Independently, TV between probability
vectors is at most 1. Taking both bounds and then the source supremum
proves ROW-MAX+, equation (1), with its stated constant 48.

All inequalities above hold without a large-M reduction. In particular
c0>=1, tiny M, finite or zero visits, absorbing/periodic/reducible chains,
and arbitrary initial pairs are included. No source-dependent parameter
occurs in B.
