|Dense coefficient slots, including zeros/biases; parameter bits|O(K^3p^4); O(K^3p^5)|
|Active arithmetic/projection records; identity holds|O(Kp^2+K^2); O(K^2p^3)|
|All n tokens' dense pointwise/QKV bit work|O(nK^3p^6)|
|Uncached attention bit work, including finite evaluation|O(n^2(K+p)p^2)|
|Masks and positions|O(n^2+n log(n+1)) bits|
|Observed one-hot input|O(NK) bits|
|Rolling neural/QKV/working arrays, excluding separately stored data|O(nKp^2) bits|
|Finite evaluator code|O(p^4) bits|
|Finite rational log generation; its scratch|O(p^3 log^2(p+1)) work; O(p log(p+1)) bits|

For example, the principal displayed work bounds become
O(S_tau K^3 Lambda_tau^6) and
O(S_tau^2 (K+Lambda_tau) Lambda_tau^2); dense parameter bits become
O(K^3 Lambda_tau^5), depth O(K Lambda_tau^2), width O(K Lambda_tau).
These are sufficient construction bounds for this single configuration,
not optimality claims.

The rest of SRC's accounting remains part of the configuration. Query,
endpoint and mode acquisition, residual routing, metadata, masks, projections,
holds, copies, clears, and final output formatting are charged. There are six
heads total, at most four in one layer, maximum QK dimension 2K, and score
cap 2. The full finite evaluator and its control/address words are paid.
Releasing a buffer removes its future use; zeroing, when used, is charged.

Let R_rec be the actual sum of gate, term and hold records produced by the
SRC layout generator. Its generation/sorting has upper bounds
O(R_rec p^2) bit work and O(R_rec p) working bits. Dense files additionally
pay every coefficient-bit write, including zeros and biases. Position/mask
files, layout, microcode and their generation and writes are additional
named offline charges. Log generation is not the whole offline cost.
These statements retain SRC's actual generation procedure and conventions.

Total peak is

    max_over_phases (sum of ALL simultaneously live buffers in that phase).

These buffers include parameters/program, observed input, masks/metadata,
QKV and residual arrays, master/positive/output banks, controllers/addresses,
temporary evaluation workspace, generated files/data held in memory, and
offline log/layout/sorting scratch whenever live. In particular, the safe
forward dense phase sum includes

    O(K^3p^5)+O(n^2+n log(n+1))+O(NK)+O(nKp^2),

with the code/query/metadata charges described above. Offline scratch is
added to retained generated data in its own phase, or to forward storage
if deliberately retained. The rolling O(nKp^2) entry is not total peak.

Cached attention, addressed sparse programs and PURE expansion require
their own complete SRC configurations. We do not take separate minima
of table columns, import a cache without its generation and selectors,
or claim identical risk at different finite precisions. A physical fault
injector, new snapshot/correction device or history reread has its own
acquisition, wiring, storage and work costs. BUDGET specifies an allowed
perturbation experiment and a risk certificate, not a free implemented
injector. The master and V lifetime cuts themselves are already present
in the original paid SRC schedule.

## 6. The exact binary observation at the two cuts

For the rest of the proof K=2. We prove INFO-M and INFO-V in their separate
experiments. We use the actual SRC prefix, with arbitrary readers having
the complete information permitted in the statement.

For p in (0,1), let every theta row be q_p=(1-p,p) and let
mu=q_p tensor q_p. The homogeneous second-order path law is exactly

    P_p(X_1=x_1,...,X_(M+2)=x_(M+2))
        = product_(t=1)^(M+2) p^(x_t)(1-p)^(1-x_t).     (6.1)

This is a legal subclass of the mother experiment. In particular every
actual target, whatever Y_M is, equals q_p. Define

    E={X_(M+1)=X_(M+2)=0}.

Then P_p(E)=(1-p)^2; conditional on E, the first M symbols are still
independent Bernoulli(p). This is an immediate factorization of (6.1),
not a general-source conditional-row assumption.

The full exact side-information tuple permitted to the reader is constant
on E. Here are all parts of this assertion, using the interface in Section 2:

* Actual-mode A and B and retained endpoints l2_q,l1_q are all e_0.
  The mode is fixed. Any retained unused external-query bits are fixed
  public inputs, chosen as e_0,e_0 in this actual-mode experiment.
* The synthetic x_q,F_q,z_q are zero. Position/type/declared positional
  flags, masks, K,M,N,h, and control constants are public and fixed.
* Endpoint correction bits, if computed/retained at a readout boundary,
  are deterministic functions of these exact bits: j=1 and
  (J_0,J_1)=(1,0) here. They contain no additional random statistic.
* h0,f,rho are statistical means and are in the 19 replaceable masters.
  They are not a retained accurate flag exception. Earlier input symbols,
  including the initial pair, survive only through the declared statistical
  banks, not as separate accurate bits.
* Released evaluator or historical storage is not a permitted observation.
  Canonical word representation has no additional reserved payload carrying
  clean statistical information. At the V cut no clean sum, quotient or
  upstream statistical master is exposed, even if an upstream allocation
  is still charged in a phase's memory.

Denote this one full side-information tuple by b0. We have not omitted
any permitted exact bits by conditioning on E. We will turn E into a
positive-mass event for the unconditional actual loss, not claim a lower
bound for a different conditional risk.

## 7. Elementary finite lemmas for arbitrary readers

### 7.1 Conditional product moments and mean shifts

Let f on {0,1}^M change by at most d when one coordinate is changed. For
independent Bernoulli(p) coordinates, expose coordinates one at a time
and form the finite Doob martingale of f. With a past fixed, the two
possible next conditional expectations differ by at most d: pair equal
future coordinates and use the coordinate oscillation bound. Its next
martingale difference has conditional variance at most d^2/4, since
a random variable in an interval of length d has variance at most d^2/4
(use the interval midpoint as a comparison center). Distinct martingale
differences are orthogonal by iterated conditional expectation. Therefore

    Var_p(f) <= M d^2/4.                               (7.1)

To compare means at p and p0, couple independent coordinate pairs so
that each Bernoulli(p),Bernoulli(p0) pair disagrees with probability
|p-p0|. Such a coupling is obtained by the usual common interval
partition, or directly by its four finite cell probabilities.
Telescoping over the coordinates gives

    |E_p f-E_p0 f| <= M d |p-p0|.                      (7.2)

Chebyshev's bound P(|f-Ef|>t)<=Var(f)/t^2 follows by taking expectations
in (f-Ef)^2>=t^2 1{|f-Ef|>t}. The lemmas apply below to the conditional
law in (6.1). They do not make the computed finite masters independent.

### 7.2 Shared full observation atom, with arbitrary random readout

Suppose the two sources have constant targets q_- and q_+, and under
one common allowed kernel their FULL visible-input distributions both
assign probability at least beta to a single input z0. An arbitrary
common random reader at z0 has one output distribution nu independent
of the source: its seed is independent of the data and kernel and the
kernel cannot read that seed. The triangle inequality gives

    integral [TV(v,q_-)+TV(v,q_+)] dnu(v) >= TV(q_-,q_+).

Contributions from all other inputs are nonnegative. Hence its two
unconditional risks obey

    risk_-+risk_+ >= beta TV(q_-,q_+).                  (7.3)

For q_±=(1/2∓alpha,1/2±alpha) the target distance is 2alpha, so at least
one risk is at least beta alpha. This proves the needed two-source
information bound directly for arbitrary probability-vector output laws.
It uses no ordered-coordinate test and no tie convention; readers with
ties or any extra independent randomness are already included.
Different amounts of mass above beta at z0 cannot invalidate (7.3).

### 7.3 The identity-kernel lower bound and the small-budget range

For either experiment, the identity replacement is allowed at EVERY
nonnegative budget. Compose any permitted cut reader under this kernel
with the fixed finite clean SRC prefix on a full observed history.
The composition is a common randomized full-history probability predictor.
It uses only the history and public parameters, and its seed is independent
of the source. It has exactly the same actual loss as the cut reader
under the identity kernel. The a125 iid lower bound therefore implies

    inf_reader sup_source,kernel risk
        >= 1/(16sqrt(M+2)).                            (7.4)

This is a restriction/subclass argument for each reader, not the claim
that one source makes every reader fail. A predictor that could see
additional history is given more information, so its lower bound applies
to this subclass. The fixed prefix's computational cost is irrelevant
to this information statement. The witnesses in (7.4) can be binary iid
sources by the admitted Fact.

Set T0=2^20 and, separately for the two experiments, put

    x=min{1,delta}                  for Master,
    x=min{1,D/Q}                   for V.              (7.5)

If x=0 the desired bound is zero. If x>0 and Mx^2<T0, then
(M+2)x^2<T0+2<2^22. Consequently (7.4) is greater than
2^(-15)x, and in particular at least 2^(-16)x. This proves both
claims in the entire small-budget range, with no grid, large-M, or
concentration assumption. All that remains is Mx^2>=T0.

## 8. Master information lower bound, including all 19 coordinates

Assume x=min(1,delta)>0 and Mx^2>=T0. Set

    alpha=x/1024,      p_±=1/2±alpha.                   (8.1)

Use the two fixed iid sources (6.1). Since alpha<=1/1024<1/4, both
give P(E)>=(1/4)^2=1/16.

We now relate every ACTUAL master to a public center. On E the ideal
head weights at key t=3,...,N are respectively

    P1: 2^(1{X_(t-2)=0}+1{X_(t-1)=0}),
    P2: 2^(1{X_(t-2)=0}) 3^(1{X_(t-1)=0}),
    T:  2^(1{X_(t-2)=0}),
    0:  1.

Their value vectors, in the same order, are

    (x_t,l2_t), (x_t,l2_t), (l1_t,F_t),
    (l2_t,l1_t,F_t,z_t),    F_t=z_t l1_t, z_t=1{t=3}.

These are the actual SRC heads' ideal comparison ratios; finite evaluation
has not been substituted away. Each of the 19 ideal coordinates H_j has form

    H_j = sum_(t=3)^N w_t b_(t,j) / sum_(t=3)^N w_t,

where b_(t,j) is a bit, 1<=w_t<=6, and each term depends only on
(X_(t-2),X_(t-1),X_t) and fixed metadata. In particular its denominator
is at least M and H_j lies in [0,1].

Changing one of the M free symbols can change at most three key terms.
Each numerator summand is in [0,6], and each denominator summand in [1,6].
Thus both numerator and denominator change by at most 18. For
A/D,A'/D' in [0,1] with D,D'>=M,

    |A/D-A'/D'|
       <= (|A-A'|+|D-D'|)/D <=36/M,                   (8.2)

by writing the difference using A'/D'<=1. Boundary terms and F_t are
included in these same three affected keys. This proves the same
oscillation bound for every coordinate, including h0,f,rho.

Let m_j be the conditional expectation of H_j at p=1/2 given E. This is
a public number depending only on M and the fixed head/query definitions.
It can, for example, be defined by the finite average over all 2^M
histories with endpoint 00. The integer weights make this a finite
rational number. Define the public canonical center

    z_j=floor(Q m_j)/Q,       0<=j<19.                  (8.3)

It belongs to G_h^19 and |z_j-m_j|<=1/Q. It does not depend on the sign
of the hidden source parameter, the realized history, or the reader seed.
Defining this finite center imposes no computational assumption on readers
or kernels and does not supply them a hidden source input.

From (7.1), (7.2) and (8.2), under either conditional source law,

    Var_p(H_j|E)<=324/M,
    |E_p(H_j|E)-m_j|<=36alpha.                         (8.4)

Let Hhat denote the ACTUAL finite master bank. SRC supplies, pathwise,

    |Hhat_j-H_j|<=2/Q.                                 (8.5)

On the event that all 19 deviations
|H_j-E_p(H_j|E)| are at most x/4, equations (8.3)–(8.5) imply

    |Hhat_j-z_j| <= x/4+36alpha+3/Q <=3x/4<=x.          (8.6)

Here 36/1024<1/4. Also Mx>=Mx^2>=T0>=6 and Q>=2M, so
3/Q<=3/(2M)<=x/4. The numerical head error and center rounding have
both been paid explicitly.

By Chebyshev and the finite union bound, conditional failure of this
event is at most

    19 * (324/M)/(x^2/16)
       =98496/(Mx^2) <=98496/2^20 <1/4.                (8.7)

No independence between head coordinates is asserted or needed.

Define ONE deterministic kernel for all histories and all sources:
if the actual endpoint is 00 and ||Hhat-z||_infinity<=x, replace all
19 masters by z; otherwise leave them unchanged. This kernel uses only
the actual bank, exact endpoint, public parameters and budget. On every
path its replacement is canonical and its coordinatewise distance is
at most x<=delta. It does not read a hidden parameter or the reader's
random seed. No V edit is made, and gamma=0.

Under each source, (8.6)–(8.7) show that conditional on E the kernel
collapses to z with probability at least 3/4. All exact permitted bits
are b0 on E. The FULL input to the new reader is therefore the same
atom (z,b0), under both sources with mass at least

    beta=(1/16)(3/4)=3/64.

By (7.3), every common random reader has risk at least beta alpha
under at least one of these sources and this same allowed kernel.
Since beta alpha=3x/65536 >=2^(-16)x, this proves INFO-M in the
large-budget range. Together with Section 7.3 it proves INFO-M for
every delta>=0, M>=1 and legal h. Capping x at one uses only a smaller
allowed budget; this also establishes the needed inclusion for delta>1.

## 9. V information lower bound, including quantization and boundaries

Now take the separate V experiment: no master fault, actual clean bank V,
and arbitrary reader seeing only the replaced V bank and the exact bits.
Let x=min(1,D/Q)>0 and Mx^2>=T0. Use the same choice (8.1) of p_±,
with x defined for this experiment. Put

    Z_a=(C_(00),a+1)/M,       a=0,1.

This is an ideal comparison bank coordinate, NOT a probability vector
and NOT an additional reader input. The actual bank satisfies by SRC

    |V_a/Q-Z_a|<=78/Q.                                 (9.1)

Changing one of X_1,...,X_M while fixing the last two zeros affects at
most three counted triples. Therefore Z_a has coordinate oscillation
at most 3/M. Conditional on E, (7.1)–(7.2) give

    Var_p(Z_a|E)<=9/(4M),
    |E_p(Z_a|E)-E_(1/2)(Z_a|E)|<=3alpha.               (9.2)

For completeness, the exact endpoint and departure corrections in the
center are as follows. Set p_0=1-p, p_1=p. If M>=2, the first M-2
departures i=0,...,M-3 have three free symbols, the next departure
i=M-2 has two free symbols followed by 0, and the last i=M-1 has one
free symbol followed by 00. Thus

    E_p(C_(00),a | E)
      =(M-2)(1-p)^2 p_a
        +1{a=0}[(1-p)^2+(1-p)].                        (9.3)

For M=2 the first sum is empty. For M=1 the sole departure has triple
(X_1,0,0), and this expectation is (1-p)1{a=0}. Consequently the public
conditional means eta_a=E_(1/2)(Z_a|E) are

    eta_0=1/8+3/(2M), eta_1=1/8+3/(4M)    if M>=2;
    eta_0=3/2,        eta_1=1             if M=1.        (9.4)

These are exact, so no endpoint O(1/M) term has been discarded.
Generally 1/M<=eta_a<=1+1/M<=2. Define the public integer center

    v0_a=clip_[1,2Q](floor(Q eta_a)).                   (9.5)

Since Q>=2M, we have Q eta_a>=2 and Q eta_a<=2Q; the un-clipped floor
already lies in {1,...,2Q}. In particular clipping is legal and
|v0_a/Q-eta_a|<=1/Q. This verifies positive-bank constraints even for
M=1, and for all legal coarse grids.

The event |Z_a-E_p(Z_a|E)|<=x/4 for both a has conditional failure
probability at most

    2*(9/(4M))/(x^2/16)=72/(Mx^2)<=72/2^20<1/4.        (9.6)

On this event, using (9.1)–(9.5),

    ||V/Q-v0/Q||_1
       <= x/2+6alpha+158/Q <=x.                        (9.7)

Indeed 6/1024<1/4, and 158/Q<=79/M<=x/4 because
Mx>=Mx^2>=T0>=316. The 158 term pays both coordinates'
78-unit actual bank errors and both center rounding errors.

The capped integer budget D0=min(D,Q)=Qx is an integer. Define one
common deterministic kernel on all histories: if the actual endpoint
is 00 and ||V-v0||_1<=D0, replace V by v0; otherwise leave it unchanged.
It always outputs a canonical positive integer bank and spends at most
D0<=D. It depends only on public parameters, endpoint and the current
bank, not the hidden sign or any reader seed. Its legality is pathwise,
not conditional or high-probability. No master fault is used.

By (9.6)–(9.7), under each source the full input (v0,b0) has probability
at least (1/16)(3/4)=3/64. Apply (7.3). For every common random reader
one of the two iid source risks with this one kernel is at least

    (3/64)(x/1024)=3*2^(-16)x >= c_* x.

This proves INFO-V in the large-budget range, and Section 7.3 completes
all integer D>=0, M>=1 and legal h. D=0 is included, and a budget D>Q
contains the Q-budget subclass explicitly used here. No formula for a
small relative perturbation has been extrapolated outside its validity.

## 10. Infimum/supremum order and all finite-domain checks

For each fixed M,h,budget and for EACH arbitrary allowed reader, the
small-budget argument supplies an iid source from the admitted
full-history lower bound with the identity kernel; the large-budget
argument supplies one of the two displayed fixed iid tables with the
single common kernel constructed for that experiment. Thus the source
is fixed within the entire experiment, never secretly changed from path
to path. The supremum may choose the worse of the two sources for that
reader. Taking the infimum over readers preserves the same constant:

    R_master^*(M,h,delta)>=2^(-16) min{1,delta},
    R_V^*(M,h,D)>=2^(-16) min{1,D/Q}.                   (10.1)

In fact this proof uses only binary iid source tables in both experiments,
including the fallback supplied by a125. This is a statement about a
subclass used to witness the full-class minimax bound, not one fixed
source forcing all algorithms to fail at all horizons.

The common kernels are legal on every history, including the complement
of E and every failed concentration event (they use identity there).
Their good-event probability for budget legality is exactly one.
Their concentration events only establish a mass lower bound, and are
not exceptions to the permitted perturbation budget. Reader randomness
is independent of these deterministic kernels and all histories. The
full observation atom includes every permitted exact bit; neither
evaluator scratch nor ideal comparison quantities have been added to
the inputs or silently removed from allowed inputs.

All low horizons, small budgets, delta=0, D=0 and coarse grids were
included by the dichotomy in Section 7.3. M=1 additionally has the exact
center calculation (9.4) and no missing boundary departure. In the
upper direction, zero rows, zero visits, transient, reducible and
periodic sources are covered by the admitted full-scope ROW_MAX/UC and
pathwise SRC interfaces. In the lower direction the iid subclass is a
legal family inside that same source class; no property of arbitrary
conditioned row samples is claimed.

The two displayed lower bounds concern different information experiments.
They are not added and need not use the same source or kernel for a
given pair of budgets. They apply to arbitrary replacement readers,
not merely to the original ordered suffix.

## 11. Consequences and limits of the result

For K=2 consider any sequence M tending to infinity with legal h_M and
common readers at the stated cuts. If the corresponding uniform
source-and-kernel actual risk tends to zero, (10.1) implies respectively

    delta_M ->0,       or       D_M/Q_M ->0.

For example, failure of either convergence gives a subsequence on which
the corresponding capped budget is bounded below by a positive number;
(10.1) then bounds risk away from zero on that subsequence.

For the original Pi, UP supplies the sufficient direction at fixed K
when M tends to infinity, delta_M->0, D_M/Q_M->0 and gamma_M->0, since
its four terms tend to zero. We have not proved gamma necessary, or
that arbitrary new readers attain Pi's particular modulus. No sharp
joint-K fault-budget claim follows from the K=2 lower bounds.

The budget and cost section changes risk certificates, not the original
program or its fee conventions. Strong direct algorithms allowed to
reread the complete history are outside these no-bypass cut experiments;
these information bounds do not prevent them from recounting. They must
pay their own full acquisition/execution contract. No universal
Transformer lower bound, internal-bit/depth/width/work optimality, same
exact budget advantage, or physical correction mechanism is asserted.
The remaining sqrt(log K) statistical gap and genuine same-neural-budget
comparisons remain open. Standard normalization, truncation, product
moments, coupling and two-source arguments are used as standard methods,
with no claim of new tools or priority.
