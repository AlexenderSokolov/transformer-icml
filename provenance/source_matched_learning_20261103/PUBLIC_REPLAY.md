## Scope limits

The iid result requires θ∈[1/4,3/4], integer M≥8 and k≤floor(M/1024); inclusion of k=O(sqrt M) is only asymptotic. It does not apply to arbitrary learned predictions on the acceptance region.

The retracted source-mismatched route is not a training or learning lower bound. The known-candidate output-identification calculation, a matched unknown-source training baseline, and an independently fresh-source prior experiment are three distinct tasks. Full-trajectory training inputs cost N(M+3) symbols, and the restricted (U,V)-only two-point lower bound does not apply to access to entire trajectories.

The fresh-source prior depends on k. Its clean-risk/attacked-risk relation holds for every N under independence and the specified count/endpoint observation restriction, conditional on ε; it is not a universal sample-impossibility theorem or a claim that optimization succeeds. The mathematical randomized attack kernel is not an implemented efficient sampler. No new learning algorithm, empirical performance or general novelty is asserted.

The unchanged push/manual CI verifies repository inventory integrity only, not these mathematical conclusions.
