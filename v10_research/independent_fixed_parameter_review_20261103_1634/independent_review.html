  • Graph, coordinates and fixed parameters. Each 64-dimensional block allocates four heads with 16-dimensional head coordinates; dividing q·k by √16=4 produces the displayed scores. First block has two active scalar-value copy heads, second block has zero attention contribution, third has four active anchor heads; all writes use distinct residual slots. Allocated zero heads and all residual/position access are disclosed.
  • First block and boundaries. q=(4(2i²−2hi),−4i) and k=(j,j²) give i(i−h)²−i(j−i+h)². If i>h, the target j=i−h is causal and the non-target mass is at most 2Σ_{d≥1}e^(−id²)≤2e^(−i)/(1−e^(−i))<1/4 for i≥4. The two-hinge T sends any copied bit within that margin exactly to 0 or 1. The first copied value is correct at i≥4 and the second is correct as a data bit at i≥5. The b and v hinges give b=1 exactly for i=1,2 and v=1 exactly at integer i≥5. Their construction uses 4 copy-snap, 2 BOS and 2 validity ReLU units.
  • Second block. For all early fractional t-values, t_h∈[0,1] and each mismatch d_h≥0; v=0 therefore forces g_h=0 at all invalid positions. At candidate i=k+2≥5, the snapped pair is (X_{k−2},X_{k−1}) and exactly one g_h is 1. At terminal i=N+2≥4, (t₁,x)=(X_{N−1},X_N), so the four q_h are one-hot for the actual terminal pair. Eight ReLUs suffice for the four g and four q outputs.
  • Third block, pseudocount and prediction. Fixed q_i=4(i−2) and k_j=g_{j,h}+b_j give score N at the two BOS keys and exactly matching candidates at terminal i=N+2, and 0 at the other N−J_h data keys. Both BOS values are 1/2, hence after division by e^N the head is [C_h+1+e^(−N)D_h]/[J_h+2+e^(−N)K_h], K_h=N−J_h and 0≤D_h≤K_h. If B_h=(C_h+1)/(J_h+2)∈[0,1], the ratio difference is e^(−N)|D_h−B_hK_h|/(J_h+2+e^(−N)K_h)≤(N/2)e^(−N). At the terminal, ReLU(o_h+q_h−1) equals o_h for the unique active q_h and zero for all others, since 0≤o_h≤1. The resulting affine pair (Q,1−Q) is a probability distribution. The k=N triple ends at observed X_N; no X_{N+1} is accessed.
  • N=2 and uniform risk. When N=2, M=0, there are no valid candidates and the add-one comparator equals 1/2; the leakage formula still yields the stated pathwise bound, without claiming the network output itself is exactly 1/2. The cited R011 file SHA aaafd3823ab11e20ffdeff2164115c6c91db5efea6e2dffd9c3c78da0b7fbbbf explicitly assumes M≥1.
