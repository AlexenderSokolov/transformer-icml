## Calculation and dependencies

For r=i−1 and d=j−r, |j²−r²|≤2L|d|. Thus sη(i,r)−sη(i,j)≥i[d²−(1/4)|d|]≥(3i/4)d² for each nonzero integer d, independent of the sign of η. At most two positions occur per positive distance. For i≥4, off-target mass is at most 2/(e³−1)<1/4. Convex values in [0,1] make the copied scalar less than 1/4 from a data-bit target, so the original hinge T outputs that exact bit. At i=4 this is X₁; at i≥5 it supplies valid predecessor bits. The lag-two head is unchanged and snaps its data-bit target at i≥5.

At all i≤4 the validity flag v=0 and hence every g_h=0 even if the early snap changes; the BOS flags, input x and position-only marker are identical. At i≥5 the two snapped bits, v, and all candidate g_h agree. At terminal L≥4, the query uses the identical snapped lag-one bit and current input bit, including N=2. Thus block-3 anchor keys g_h+b and values x, terminal anchor queries, all anchor outputs, terminal one-hot selector and p, and the uniform-marker head z agree. Early q_h and p slots, as well as raw a₁, may differ. The fourth block's terminal queries read p,z while its keys and values read only j,j²,ln j; its final logits and binary softmax therefore agree exactly. No equality of full residual states or attention weights is required. With N=2 there are no valid triples; the two network outputs agree even though the common finite-score output need not be 1/2.

The perturbed entry −4→−4(1+η) has absolute change 4|η|≤1/(2L); it is precisely the same lag-one −ij² coefficient used in the positive η=1/(2q) drift construction.
