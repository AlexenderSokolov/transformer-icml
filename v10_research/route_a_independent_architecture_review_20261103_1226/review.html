This is an independent mathematical and implementation-accounting review, not a claim of implementation, external publication, full floating-point verification, or novelty. Conclusion

The stated exact causal construction, ReLU spline errors, seven-block realization, decoder perturbation constants, and finite-accuracy sufficient budgets check out under the declared structured-mask and ideal arithmetic model. Three clarifications are needed: ensure the actually implemented output remains a probability after final error; fix the streaming comparator's terminal-token timing; and specify the baseline for the statement about extra heads. The O(N) memory statement is correct for retained constant-width token states, but not for retaining all FFN hidden activations simultaneously.

1. Causal indexing and simultaneous operations

For i ≥ 3, the singleton heads retrieve Xi−1 and Xi−2, and the residual can retain Xi. The block-1 FFN can form both Ki = e(Xi−2,Xi−1) and Qi = e(Xi−1,Xi). Their coordinates must remain distinct. Indicators are ReLU(b1 + b2 − 1) for binary bits or complements. Arbitrary harmless features at positions 1 and 2 do not matter since neither is a terminal candidate.

At terminal position N, candidate i ∈ {3,…,N} corresponds exactly to the counted transition indexed i−1: Ki = eSi−1 and Yi = Xi. The query QN is eSN. All values are causal, and XN+1 is absent. This also works at N = 3. In block 2 the heads operate in parallel on the features produced in block 1; block 2's following FFN sees all their outputs and the retained terminal query. The stated h-selection sum is exact for one-hot Q and ha ∈ [0,1].

Head A multiplies the match's softmax weight by 2, so q = (Call + Cs)/(m + Js). Head B yields r = Call/m and ha = Ja/m. Hence (1+h)q−r = Cs/m on every path. With Js = 0, this is zero and the add-one result is 1/2. The log 2 score assumes the normal score scaling is incorporated in the score parameters; masks are model-R access, not standard dense causality.

2. Spline and perturbation constants

Linear interpolation of x² with mesh 1/K has error at most 1/(4K²). The three square approximations in PK, multiplied by 2, therefore give the claimed 3/(2K²) bound. The sharper actual error is immaterial. For H(h) = (1+mh/2)−1, |H″| ≤ m²/2; the interpolation remainder gives m²/(16K²). Its interpolant remains in [0,1]. Each requires O(K) ReLU hinges, and the constant number of such maps remains O(K) in width and stored coefficients.

For A = Cs/m ∈ [0,h], first |A0−A| ≤ (1+h)eq + er + eh + 3/(2K²). Changing the upper projection endpoint from h to h′ costs at most another eh. The denominator replacement contributes one more eh, using 0 ≤ (mA+1)/(mh+2) ≤ 1. The denominator is at least 2 + m(h−eh)+. This gives the first line of (2), including 3eh.

With z = (m Aclip+1)/(m+2), both z and HK(h′) lie in [0,1]. The last product approximation contributes at most (m+2)/2 times [3/(2K²)+m²/(16K²)], exactly the second line of (2). Projection to [0,1] is nonexpansive against the true probability.

3. Seven-block realization

A valid sequential schedule is:

  1. Two singleton-copy heads; FFN produces Boolean history keys and query.
  2. Two parallel terminal attention heads; FFN selects h using the query.
  3. Parallel square splines for PK(h′,q′), and reciprocal spline HK(h′), preserving needed coordinates.
  4. Compute y = ReLU(A0).
  5. Compute Aclip = y − ReLU(y−h′) and affine z. Because h′ ≥ 0, this is clip(A0,0,h′).
  6. Compute the square splines and affine combination for PK(z,HK(h′)).
  7. Compute the fixed-endpoint clamp ReLU(praw)−ReLU(praw−1), then output (1−p,p).

Blocks 3–7 can have zero attention values. Each FFN may have a constant-width state and O(K) hidden width; standard affine residual/output connections preserve or cancel coordinates as needed. The variable-endpoint clamp has genuinely sequential lower and upper stages. The final fixed-endpoint clamp has two parallel ReLU hinges in one FFN. The supplied schedule does not require a layer normalization or an unpriced external division.

4. Combined bound and finite accuracy

On J00 ≥ v/2, h ≥ u/2; e ≤ u/4 implies h−e ≥ u/4 and m/[2+m(h−e)+] ≤ 4/u. Since h ≤ 1, the port-error bracket is at most 6e, yielding 24e/u; its 3/(2K²) contribution yields 6/(uK²). The remaining spline term and ζ agree with (6). The exceptional low-count event is already charged in the statistical proof. The two stipulated lower bounds on K² allocate ε/3 to each of the non-ζ spline terms; e ≤ u0ε/72 allocates at most ε/3 to port errors and implies e ≤ u/4 for ε ≤ 1.

If all logits change by at most a, each exact-softmax probability ratio lies in [e−2a,e2a]. Thus the induced TV distance is at most min{1,(e2a−1)/2}. For values clipped to [0,1], the total head-coordinate error is at most that TV bound plus value-coordinate error and the specified softmax-TV implementation error. The proposed score/value/softmax three-way budget suffices for an individual head output. These are assumptions on ports and numerical gates, not an end-to-end finite-bit proof. Bounded copy errors strictly below 1/4 permit a robust clipped piecewise-linear Boolean threshold.

Final-probability correction. The actual implemented final p must be in [0,1], including after any final rounding error ζ, for (1−p,p) to be a probability distribution and for the final risk bound to be capped at 1. Explicitly require a range-preserving implementation or a final projection. Projection of an approximation within ζ of the ideal clipped p cannot increase its error. Without such a guarantee an out-of-range scalar is not a probability vector; although an uncapped absolute-error bound with ζ can still be written, the TV statement as stated is not well-defined.

5. Resources and comparison

There are four instantiated heads in total, at most two in each attention block, and seven blocks overall. The required terminal candidate mask has m entries per head; all other specified masks are singleton, so an executor that actually exploits masks touches O(N) attention edges. The constant number of O(K)-width FFNs costs O(NK) arithmetic. Dense attention can instead cost O(N²+NK). A constant number of coordinates per token gives O(N) retained token-state storage. Retaining every hidden activation of every token would cost O(NK), while inference may process or discard FFN hidden activations with suitable scheduling. Position/mask descriptions, coefficient encodings, exact log 2, exp/softmax, and full coefficient/accumulator precision are distinct charges and are not supplied as a bit-complexity theorem.

Streaming comparator timing. Its final successor-count update requires XN, so all counts cannot be finished before the terminal pair has been seen. The streaming counter can read XN, update the count associated with SN−1, form SN, and answer immediately. This preserves the stated O(N) updates, O(log N) bits per counter, and paid final division.

Head-count wording. Specify the baseline behind “two extra heads.” This construction has four heads total. Relative to a direct-q model with the same two-copy-head first block and only head A in the second block, it adds one second-block head, not two; relative to the ordinary streaming comparator there are four heads rather than two extra heads.

For t=1/2, the unconditional successor mean is 2w, so direct q tends to (2w+u/2)/(1+u), and its conditional absolute error tends to (1−4u)/[6(1+u)]. Conditioning on SN=00 preserves convergence in probability because its probability u is fixed and positive; boundedness then gives convergence of expected absolute error. The corrected model tracks the same-prefix comparator as its paid computational budgets shrink. This is a comparison of the specified readouts, not an unrestricted Transformer lower bound.

6. Independent check of output-projection addendum

I reviewed v10_research/route_a_terminal_inference_20261103_1223/output_projection_addendum_v1.html. Its correction is valid: for ideal p ∈ [0,1] and |p̃−p| ≤ ζ, the one-dimensional metric projection satisfies |clip(p̃,0,1)−p| ≤ |p̃−p| ≤ ζ. The added ζ is paid once, not twice; the existing deterministic and conditional-risk constants need no change. The final saturation must itself be exact or otherwise guaranteed range-preserving, and must occur after the last error included in ζ. If any subsequent arithmetic can leave [0,1], it must be included in a renewed range guarantee and error accounting. The binary output is mathematically interpreted as (1−pnum,pnum); a literal implementation of both coordinates must preserve normalization or account for its own rounding, which is outside the present port-level certificate.

The memory clarification is also sound as a retained-token-state claim: storing N constant-width token vectors is O(N), while an executor may require O(K) additional FFN workspace and stores O(K) spline parameters. Retaining all N×O(K) FFN hidden activations requires O(NK). Masks and position descriptions and any dense-attention workspace remain separate accounting items.
