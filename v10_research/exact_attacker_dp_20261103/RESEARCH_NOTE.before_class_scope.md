# Exact offline attack on fixed positional count-bank predictors

2026-11-03. Candidate proof and exhaustive diagnostic; not independently reviewed. No novelty claim. The accepted ACG/FPA constructions supply the predictor interface, not an algorithm for this optimization. This result is a classical dynamic program for a fixed count-bank readout, not a polynomial attack on arbitrary Transformers or general networks.

## Setup and exact statement

Let M>=1, 0<=k<=M, L=floor(M/2), and h=(h_0,...,h_{M+1}) in {0,1}^{M+2}. Legal z differ from h at at most k positions i<M; positions M,M+1 are protected. For departure i=0,...,M-1 let the triple be (z_i,z_{i+1},z_{i+2}). Let H_abc be triple counts among i<L and R_abc the counts among i>=L. Supply any fixed deterministic utility U(H,R,z_0,z_1,z_M,z_{M+1}) for which each evaluation and exact comparison is supported. Then a dynamic program returns its exact maximum over legal z, and an attaining z, without enumerating words. In the unit-cost integer/utility model the scan uses O(M^9) transitions and O(M^8) rolling states; at most O(M^8) distinct final states are evaluated. Each state has O(log(M+1)) bits plus O(1) endpoint bits. Retaining all predecessor layers to reconstruct an attaining word uses O(M^9 log(M+1)) bits; alternatively rerun each preceding prefix during traceback, giving O(M^10) transition work and O(M^8 log(M+1)) working bits plus the O(M)-bit output. Add the cost of reading h, evaluation, exact comparison, encoding the readout and its result, and writing the witness. For the count-bank value only, use O(M^8 log(M+1)) rolling bits. Sparse hashing adds its implementation costs; a dense indexed array of the same asymptotic size gives the deterministic bound with standard bounded-word addressing. Bounds may be very large and are not a practical-efficiency claim.

The fixed accepted ACG gate depends only on selected row u=(h_M,h_{M+1}) and J_u,C_u,D_u: J is the total number of departures at u, C the total with successor 1-first(u), D the number of u departures with i<L. The protected terminal pair determines u before the scan. For any fixed U(J_u,C_u,D_u,u), the same DP keeps only three counters and the last two symbols, using O(M^4) transitions, O(M^3) rolling states and at most O(M^3) readout evaluations. Backpointers cost O(M^4 log M) bits; recomputing prefixes gives O(M^5) transitions with O(M^3 log M) working bits plus O(M) output. This covers the exact ACG gate and FPA readout, because FPA's fixed rounded normalized J,C,D bank is a deterministic function of these integer counts and public M, with protected exact endpoint. It also covers any arbitrary fixed function of this three-count bank; no smoothness is needed. For an arbitrary readout of all four rows' J,C,D, a simpler direct DP has O(M^11) transitions, O(M^10) rolling states and O(M^10) readout evaluations, since the four J sum to the processed departures and the four D sum to the processed first-half departures. The general triple-histogram DP above is stronger asymptotically, but exact readout costs remain separate.

## Recurrence and proof

Process positions 0 through M+1, permit either binary value at t<M with cost 1{z_t != h_t}, and permit only h_t when t>=M. Initialize by selecting z_0,z_1 subject to these same rules, including M=1. Their initial mismatch cost is the state value. Thereafter, on appending c=z_t for t>=2, departure i=t-2 becomes determined; increment its triple coordinate in H when i<L, otherwise R. Also update the last-two-bit window. Keep, for every prefix length and exact state, only the least cost of any prefix attaining it, discarding entries with cost>k. A state records the initial pair, the current last pair, and the pair at the split once it exists; these are O(1) possibilities. The state also contains the segment histograms. Initialize the latter to zero. If L=0, the split pair is the initial pair.

At the end the last pair is h_M,h_{M+1}; compute U on each state with cost<=k and choose a maximum. Every legal word yields a path and every path yields a legal word. If two prefixes share the same state, all future permitted symbols, added edit costs, counts, endpoint and utilities are identical under the same continuation; hence the larger current cost can never help and minimum-cost dominance is exact. The induction holds even for adjacent edits and for triples crossing the split or editable/protected boundary. An arbitrary readout means a function of the declared bank and recorded endpoint information, not a readout of the whole word. If extra fixed-size prefix/suffix strings are needed, store their bits as finite state; if their length scales with M the bound changes.

Why O(M^8) segment states? For a segment of s consecutive triples with initial pair v and terminal pair w, write (a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h)=(N000,N001,N010,N011,N100,N101,N110,N111) and delta_x=1{w=x}-1{v=x}. Pair-flow balance implies e=b+delta_00, f=delta_01-b+c+d, g=d-delta_11, and h=s-(a+b+c+d+e+f+g). Thus four entries a,b,c,d in {0,...,M}, together with segment length and its two endpoint pairs, uniquely determine all eight counts. The scan knows s for each segment. The two segment start/end pairs are the initial pair, split pair and current pair; at most 4^3 cases. Even during the first half, regard the second segment as empty at the current pair, or use the analogous one-segment state until the split. Therefore at most 4^3(M+1)^8 count states at any fixed prefix length. Each has at most two successors and one minimum-cost update. Counter packing, addition, inequality and array indexing take O(log(M+1)) or polynomial-logarithmic bitwork per transition, depending on the RAM model; the full scan bitwork is O(M^9 polylog(M+1)), excluding utility and witness reconstruction. Output includes at least M+2 bits if returning z.

For the selected row directly increment J when the departure pair equals u, C additionally when the successor is 1-first(u), and D additionally when i<L. The same dominance proof applies; three counters in [0,M] and four last-pair possibilities establish the displayed bounds. The proof does not require a source, Markov sampling, mixing, stationary initialization, or randomness.

## Attack objective and limitations

The DP accepts any fixed bank utility. In the legal population game of the training note, the history-conditioned squared-loss utility is U_h(z)=(f(z)-p(h))^2 where p(h)=E[Y|H=h]. If p(h) or a valid estimate is available from the population/training process, optimizing it is an h-only attacker; the DP does not itself determine p(h). In particular, the grouped-learning theorem already uses its group estimate p-hat: replacing that theorem's exhaustive inner maximization by this DP with exactly the same p-hat and exactly the same fixed predictor readout leaves its empirical objective unchanged and introduces no additional statistical penalty. A changed estimator, approximate readout or comparison, or inexact maximization requires a separate analysis of the resulting error. Giving it the realized Y, or the current hidden theta, instead gives a label-aware or source-aware oracle, which can be stronger and is outside the history-only threat. A different arbitrary public utility remains source-independent if it depends on h and allowed fixed information only. The computation is offline attack generation for each observed word, not prediction runtime and not a guarantee of learning the predictor. For training over N predictors and n2 histories, a direct use costs up to N*n2 DP runs and their utility evaluations; shared preprocessing or amortization is not claimed. Exact comparisons and costs of evaluating finite neural readouts or rational losses are additional; if the readout only has approximate evaluation or arbitrary real oracle values, exact argmax cannot be asserted without a separate comparison oracle.

## Diagnostic

Run `python verify.py --max-m 6`. For every binary h of length M+2, every k=0,...,M, and M in the tested range, the code compares the complete mapping from all reachable banks to minimum edit cost, versus independently enumerating all edit masks of weight at most k. It separately tests all four J,C,D rows and the selected row, verifies an exact nontrivial readout maximum, and includes M=1, k=0, k=M and protected endpoint boundary cases. This is finite diagnostic evidence, not a replacement for the induction and flow proof. A separate `python verify_histogram.py 6` diagnostic implements the compact four-counters-per-segment flow encoding and exhaustively compares complete maps (including the initial pair and both eight-coordinate histograms) to brute force through M=6. It additionally checks the flow reconstruction on every enumerated word. All 3,072 comparisons passed, as recorded in `VERIFICATION_HISTOGRAM.txt`.
