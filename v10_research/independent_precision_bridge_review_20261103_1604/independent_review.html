Correction

In the positions-and-copying table, suppose there are k≤N−1 other keys, all with score 0, and the desired key has score A. The exact wrong-mass formula is k/(e^A+k), which is at most (N−1)/(e^A+N−1)≤(N−1)e^(−A). The frozen text writes the latter first expression as an equality despite saying “at most N−1”; equality requires exactly N−1. Preserve the original and bind a separate correction rather than silently modifying it.

Checks

  • Sharp softmax stability. Let h range over [a,b]. The exponential likelihood ratio is in [e^a,e^b], and its normalizer cancels in the Hilbert distance, which equals b−a. Maximization over the mass of A={q_i≥p_i} gives tanh((b−a)/4); since osc(h)≤2||h||∞, the bound becomes tanh(δ/2). For ℓ=(−δ,0) and h=(δ,−δ), the two probabilities swap and attain tanh(δ/2), including δ=0. The sup-norm perturbation is after a common shift; common shifts have zero oscillation.
  • Source normalization. Cohen and Fausti, Theorem 5.1 and equations (37)–(39) define their TV norm as full ℓ1 and bound it by 2 tanh(H/4). The document correctly divides by two for conventional half-ℓ1 TV. The cited softmax-specific source also gives the sharp result; it is established prior work.
  • Error composition and endpoint risk. On the fixed valid support, values in [0,1] contract TV and each value error contributes at most ν. Conditioning any numerical weights on valid support and assuming their normalized law is τ-close to exact softmax permits the invalid total mass λ to add at most λ; this is an explicit assumption, not a consequence of the valid-logit bound. Adding the final output error ρ yields the pathwise sum and hence both conditional risk bounds by triangle and reverse triangle. The ideal −∞ mask cannot be included as an ordinary finite-logit perturbation. Rounding, copying, extraction, underflow and normalization must be independently bounded; the bound is conditional on those guarantees and the companion reverse-chain theorem.
  • Exact integer comparator. For β=q ln2, real-transition weights are 1, 2^(−q), 2^(−2q) and BOS has total weight 2. Multiplication by 2^(2q) yields the displayed numerator and denominator, including +1 and +2. Since class counts total M, den≤(M+2)2^(2q), num≤den and both use O(q+log(M+2)) bits. Eight fixed-alphabet counters plus last-two-bit context use O(log(M+2)) working bits in the scan. Straightforward counter update cost is O(M log(M+2)); assembling and p_out-bit long division with L=O(q+log(M+2)) takes O(L(p_out+1)) bit operations and O(L+p_out) workspace. Output truncation has error <2^(−p_out), with an integer bit or equivalent endpoint convention for the value 1. This comparator is not an attention implementation.
  • Representation of temperature. The comparator uses integer shifts and treats q ln2 as a symbolic mathematical parameter: it need not encode ln2. If s approximates ln2 to 2^(−p), replacing β by qs gives normalized score errors bounded by max{2q,1}2^(−p) before further arithmetic error; the BOS score contributes its own one-copy ln2 approximation. General dyadic β rounding, integer range, finite-score mask and numerical softmax remain separate obligations.
  • Scope. The parameter choice involving π_s is analysis-level and not a source-blind prescription. The rare-family lower bound concerns the fixed output map and its population bias, not generic network temperature, precision, training or computational complexity. No source review or bound in this note establishes an end-to-end floating-point Transformer guarantee.
