# Independent audit of D’Angelo mapping

Scope: independent reading of the original arXiv v1 HTML, including §§2–4 and Appendices D–E at the specific claims below, and the PMLR record. This is a source and algebra review, not formal verification; no numerical tests were run. No existing files were changed, no RNG used, and no external writes made. PMLR metadata was checked at https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/d-angelo26a.html; proceedings PDF identity with arXiv v1 was not checked.

The main audit is faithful in its central mapping: Proposition 3.1 uses aligned equality scores with k coefficients, exact copying and early-key suppression are limits, its BOS output is a uniform pseudo-count on weighted counts, and Corollary 4.1 makes symmetric add-alpha exact in a beta limit. Corollary 4.2 assumes no BOS and equal beta; gamma=e^beta−1 expansion is algebraic for all finite beta, but the stated mixture interpretation is convex for beta≥0 (with empty-count conventions). See https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS1, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS2, and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S4.SS1.

Precision fixes:

1. The order-i model pools all masks of size i with weights proportional to their cumulative counts K_M, not necessarily equal weights on their normalized predictors. See https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S4.SS2, equation (13).
2. In Appendix E the theorem leaves RPE unrestricted; the Corollary E.1 mask-only aggregation does not visibly include arbitrary candidate-dependent RPE. For such aggregation, constant/fixed valid-key RPE or explicit position weights are needed; distinguish the paper’s stated claim from the more secure equality-block observation. The displayed mask x_{t-r1}=x_{s-r2} for r1,r2 in [k] also differs by one on the query side from Proposition 3.1, and the k² indexed set omits the current-token block of the (k+1)-block representation. These are localized source-reading caveats, not a claim that Theorem E.1 is false. https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A5.SS3 and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A5.SS4.
3. Appendix D.3’s footnote excludes s=T and counts T−k−1 positions, whereas Definition 3.1 in the no-BOS setting includes s=T and has T−k candidates. Identify this candidate-window difference when citing the heuristic. https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A4.SS3 and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS1.
4. The paper’s normalizer and aggregate display I{e^(2)_{T1}>0}, after defining the BOS score to be arbitrary real kappa. With kappa≤0 that literal test omits an extant BOS, although the preceding piecewise softmax assigns it weight e^kappa. This is an apparent local notation/algebra discrepancy, not a refutation of the construction. https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS2.

Route A: The k=1 formula agrees after supplying composite tokens, e.g. z_t=(x_{t−1},x_t), with query z_T, candidate context z_{j−1}, and candidate successor value z_j projected to its final coordinate. Alignment and causal construction of these enlarged-alphabet tokens must be charged. q_s=(r+gamma*u_s*p_hat_s)/(1+gamma*u_s), hence p_hat_s=q_s+(q_s−r)/(gamma*u_s) when u_s>0 and gamma!=0. These are algebraic identities, not a demonstrated finite-precision joint recovery circuit or an impossibility result.

Route B: For a,c∈{−1,+1}, beta*a*c=2 beta I{a=c}−beta, so paper equality coefficients are 2 beta_1,2 beta_2. For a uniformly weighted four-state context domain, the row-normalized product kernel has Walsh eigenvalues 1,tanh(beta_1),tanh(beta_2),tanh(beta_1)tanh(beta_2). This need not describe finite-history empirical denominators, nonuniform occupancy, or information in other heads or residuals.
