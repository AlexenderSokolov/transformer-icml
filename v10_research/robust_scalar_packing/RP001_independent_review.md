## Independently checked reasoning

1. **Asymmetric saw identity.** The iterated tents yield A=tri(r), B=tri(r+1/4), including the shifted argument t/2+1/(8N) on its full valid domain. On the first half-period s=r. On the second, ReLU(1/2-B)=min(A,1-A); taking the minimum with 1-A gives s=r until r=1-eta and s=(2N+1)(1-r) thereafter. Shared endpoints and t=1 agree by continuity. The staircase is genuinely monotone; it is not an asserted digit extraction oracle.
2. **Bounded implementation.** Saturated doubling computes min(1,N u) using k fixed-size bounded stages; since the other minimum operand is <=1, saturation changes no output. Tents, min, clipping, and retained rails use only bounded affine/ReLU primitives. Sequentially generating the three tent chains retains only a fixed number of scalar rails. Coefficients are dyadic and bounded by 4; coefficient description length O(log Q) is charged. No variable multiplication, unbounded intermediate, or concealed increasing-width circuit is used.
3. **Path and inverse.** The horizontal rail is flat throughout each vertical transition, then remains at the shared endpoint for the next initial plateau. There are N horizontal and N vertical edges, no H_N. Every nonincident pair has distance at least 1/N, including same-side separated verticals and the final vertical endpoint. For closer image points the edges are equal or incident, and the connecting arc length is at most sqrt(2) times their Euclidean distance. Moving portions have speed >=N; two plateau allowances eta/N suffice, including arbitrary preimages at a vertex. The displayed inverse formulas are actual preimages at both endpoints.
4. **Adversarial recovery.** Clipping cannot increase coordinate distance from the unperturbed bounded point. Rounding adds <1/Q, so Euclidean displacement is <=sqrt(2)(c+1)/Q. Nearest-point projection doubles this at most, and 2^a>=8(c+1) makes it strictly smaller than 1/N. The local inverse estimate yields exactly the displayed conservative 4(c+1)/(QN)+1/N^2 bound. This is pointwise, hence covers history-dependent/random perturbations without a probabilistic independence assumption.
5. **Finite decoder.** Coordinate projection on each axis-aligned edge and squared rational distance comparison require no square root or real oracle. The inverse denominators contain N and 2N+2, hence have O(log Q) bits. Enumeration of 2N edges and the optional full O(N log Q)-bit table are explicitly paid. Ties require no hidden data and do not change the recovery argument.
6. **Finite evaluator (delta-reviewed).** The revised T_ext formula is a bounded periodic extension of the first tent and computes exactly the original B. With P=2h, the shifted input remains on the P lattice. Each of the k tent operations defining v doubles lattice integers, giving divisibility by 2^k; k+1 operations defining A and B give divisibility by 2^(k+1). Their minima and the saturated doublings preserve the latter divisibility. Halving A loses at most one factor of two, so s and v can both be divided by N exactly on the original P lattice. All biases fit because P>=k+2. Signed full-product affine sums and ReLUs are therefore exact, including the final v+v/N and t-s/N. This strengthens the earlier valid stage-varying evaluator and removes its possible literal-uniform-FA syntax mismatch. The previous review is retained as `RP001_independent_review_stagevarying.md`.
7. **Causal/statistical scope.** FG001's prefix pooling is genuinely causal at every history row and excludes the arbitrary initial pair. Only terminal next-symbol IID estimation is claimed. The triangle inequality with the sample-mean bound and <=Q^-2 input error gives the stated risk. Exactly two quantized coordinates survive; no mean or count is granted to the decoder. Side labels can be ignored. The lower comparison uses zero attack and the ordinary finite alphabet/statistical floors; it does not require the same prior for both floors.
8. **Approximation-family admission.** The direct scalar path Lipschitz bound gives pre-cut ideal/finite error <=(2N+2)/Q^2<=1/Q on the specified parameter range. This establishes the IR001-style E/Q contract. The revised uniform-P primitive evaluator also follows FA001's signed full-product affine-floor semantics exactly, while zero-logit equal Taylor weights cancel in the first mean. It does not require FA001's larger generic sufficient precision formula because a stronger direct exactness argument is supplied for this particular network.
9. **Resource and attribution limits.** The ledger pays depth, all tokens, mask/table/coefficient/program storage, work precision, decoder enumeration, integer arithmetic, and isolation. Network primitive depth is correctly distinguished from bit-machine depth/work. Geometric scalar packing is credited to existing work; no claim about all J>2, original FG001 robustness, internal faults, training, or full Markov minimax achievability is made.
