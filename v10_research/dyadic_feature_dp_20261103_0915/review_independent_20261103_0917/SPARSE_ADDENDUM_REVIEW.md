# Review of sparse budget addendum

Reviewed `SPARSE_BUDGET_ADDENDUM.md` SHA-256 `057f6a98b5081cfd08e8072442e588e3931d08ed4e9d66fa5fce68f99a2194a2`. Its sparse upper bound is correct. At t, the independent edit choices occur in min(t,M) positions, giving K_t=sum_{j=0}^{min(k,min(t,M))} binom(min(t,M),j); protected steps introduce no further choice. Each retained DP key is realized by at least one such budget-feasible prefix, so S_t≤min(S,K_t), and the total candidate transitions are at most 2 sum_{t=0}^{T-1} min(S,K_t). The t<M-branch bound 2 is loose but safe at protected steps. State map update and variable-length key comparison, costs, witness provenance and actual model/readout time are correctly charged separately. For each fixed k, K_t=O((M+1)^k), including k=0; constants can depend on k and this is not uniform polynomial time for growing k.

One qualification to the equality: the stated K_t equals the number of raw *Hamming-budget-compliant* prefixes when both editable bit choices are allowed. The main note additionally permits marking transitions illegal or restricting terminal state. Under such restrictions the number of legal prefixes can be strictly less than K_t (e.g., M=1, h_0=0, k=1, forbid x_0=1: K_1=2 but just one permitted prefix). Thus replace “number of distinct raw legal prefixes is exactly” by “number of raw prefixes satisfying the edit budget and protected-symbol rule, before any additional state restrictions, is exactly,” or use “at most” for restricted legal prefixes. The upper bound and conclusions are unaffected.

The source is a new additive note, not an alteration to the bound final proof or the original data. No new computation, sampling or random operation was needed for this review.
