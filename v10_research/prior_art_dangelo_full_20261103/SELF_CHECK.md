# Author self-check of D’Angelo source-to-claim mappings

2026-11-03. This is the same auditor checking its own work, not an independent review, formal proof verification, or completion of the full proof-review procedure. Source: arXiv v1 full text and PDF linked in AUDIT.md. The narrower PMLR PDF remains unavailable as logged there. No tests, simulation, RNG, or external writes were used.

1. **Proposition 3.1.** Reopened [§3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS1) and compared each index: paper uses M_s={r: x_(T-r+1)=x_(s-r)}, N_M counts exact masks, β∈R^k, BOS term exp κ/|V|. First copy heads and second attention head match. Pathwise scope and no-BOS empty-denominator qualification retained. First-layer δ→∞ and early suppression →∞ checked directly in [§3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS2); finite β refers to final overlap logits, not all scores. Direct linear probability output verified in §3.2.3; no softmax or LayerNorm inserted.

2. **A mapping.** For an enlarged alphabet, define z_i=(x_i,x_(i+1)) from observed symbols, T′=N−1; paper k=1 score at query z_T′ and key value z_j is β I{z_T′=z_(j−1)}. For legal j=2,…,T′, context z_(j−1)=(x_(j−1),x_j), and the successor symbol is the second coordinate of z_j=(x_j,x_(j+1)). Sum second-coordinate one-hot vectors through a linear output map. Hence q_s=(r+γu_sp̂_s)/(1+γu_s) with n=T′−1 and γ=e^β−1 exactly for this specified range. This **does not** provide a free pair-token encoder, preserve an arbitrary prescribed initial candidate range automatically, or yield q,r,u and stable inversion from one head. Inversion requires γu_s≠0. A two-parameter native k=2 aligned score cannot give identical unit weight to all three non-full match patterns while also giving a different full-match weight: empty mask has weight 1, singleton masks e^β1 and e^β2, so singleton=1 forces β1=β2=0. This confirms the restriction in AUDIT.md.

3. **B mapping.** βac=2βI{a=c}−β, so matching coefficients in paper are 2β1, 2β2; the common score shift cancels under softmax. Under uniform binary-pair population, each normalized one-coordinate kernel has entries exp(βac)/(2cosh β), eigenvalues 1 and tanh β, and their tensor product gives 1, tanh β1, tanh β2, tanh β1 tanh β2. A finite trajectory with random, nonuniform context counts does not generally share that exact normalized kernel. This is an elementary mapping, not a new theorem from the paper nor proof of full-network information loss.

4. **Corollary 4.1, Lemma 4.1, Corollary 4.2.** Rechecked α>0, uniform BOS and κ=kβ+log(α|V|), β→∞; exact count add-α is a limit. For equal β, no BOS, binomial proof gives γ=e^β−1 and cumulative counts; β≥0 suffices for nonnegative mixture weights, with undefined zero-count component terms omitted by convention. Any β∈R still has a legitimate positive original mask softmax; signed cumulative coefficients for β<0 are a regrouping, not convex mixing. No unqualified finite β equality to add-α was imported.

5. **Lemma 4.2 / Appendix D.3.** Reopened [Appendix D.3, especially footnote 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A4.SS3). Additional source discrepancy: Definition 3.1 uses s∈[k′,T] including s=T, whereas footnote 2 in the β approximation expressly switches to s∈[k′,T−1], producing T−k−1 (without BOS). For the original k=1 no-BOS definition, K_empty counts T−k candidate keys, not T−k−1. Consequently the displayed tuning formula should not be transplanted as an exact expectation for the original closed-range estimator without explicitly reconciling query exclusion. This is separate from the authors' explicit independent, uniform-context and Taylor/concentration approximations; their exact impact on risk was not independently evaluated. Added this source observation to AUDIT.md.

6. **Theorem E.1 / Corollary E.1.** The former requires label-symmetric data, initialization fixed under parameter relabelings, and population gradient flow; it preserves symmetry rather than proving convergence. Corollary also requires preserved first-layer copies and specified successor aggregation. Its all-pairs masks over [k]×[k] strengthen, rather than weaken, the direct-prior-art warning; no claim about arbitrary SGD or all transformers follows.

7. **Source/target risk.** Rechecked that theoretical identity is per path, while §2 uses a task prior and last-token NLL, and empirical §5 evaluates KL under sampled Dirichlet tasks. Neither implies the user's uniform frequentist expected terminal-TV result, matching lower bound, or finite-bit resource theorem. This is a precise non-implication for this paper, not evidence that the direction is novel after all other literature.

8. **Sign discrepancy.** Rechecked arXiv v1 [PDF p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02800v1#page=7), where BOS score e_T1=κ is established for any κ∈R, but subsequent displays use I{e_T1>0}. For κ≤0 this indicator differs from BOS presence, despite the immediately preceding piecewise softmax counting exp κ. Treat as a localized paper-text discrepancy, not a demonstrated failure of the intended corrected algebra or of all results.
