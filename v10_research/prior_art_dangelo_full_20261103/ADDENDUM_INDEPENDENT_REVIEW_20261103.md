# Addendum: D’Angelo audit precision and provenance

Date: 2026-11-03. This is an additive amendment to `AUDIT.md`; that file is not changed by this addendum. The research coordinator relayed two findings from a separate independent reviewer; this document records those findings, separately checked against the cited primary text. Do not treat this addendum as an independently produced review itself.

## 1. Mixture weights: count weighted, not equal

In [Corollary 4.2 and equation (13)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S4.SS2), write K_i=Σ_{|M|=i} K_M. The order-i predictor is q_i(m)=Σ_{|M|=i} K_M(m)/K_i, which equals Σ_{|M|=i}(K_M/K_i) q_M(m) whenever the contributing K_M are positive. Thus the mask-specific distributions are mixed with weights proportional to their realized matching counts, not uniformly over masks. A zero-count component has zero weight and can be omitted. For the no-BOS, equal-β cumulative-count convex interpretation, require β≥0 (at β=0 only the zero-order weight survives); negative β remains legal in Proposition 3.1's positive exponential exact-mask formulation but yields signed coefficients in that cumulative expansion. The prior AUDIT.md phrase “averages over all size-i masks” must be read in this count-weighted sense, not as arithmetic equal weighting.

## 2. Appendix E: positional scores and indexing are conditions for the displayed mask-only form

[Section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S2.SS1) defines the attention score as content bilinear form plus a relative positional term, and [Theorem E.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A5.SS3) leaves RPE parameters unrestricted. [Corollary E.1 and its proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A5.SS4) derive equalities of token-comparison blocks and then display a normalized mask-only aggregation. Those content-block constraints alone do not eliminate a candidate-dependent valid-key positional bias. For a fully specified implication, require the RPE to be constant over included valid keys, or carry its factors exp(R_{T−s}) explicitly in the counts/weights. Masking and boundary conditions must also be specified. Thus the audit should not assert the exact mask-only normalized formula from label symmetry and copying alone under unrestricted RPE; it remains faithful to the paper to report the formula as *stated* by Corollary E.1 while identifying this additional condition in its derivation. The symmetry constraint on content blocks, up to a common attention shift, is unaffected by unequal positional biases.

The displayed Corollary E.1 mask uses x_{t−r1}=x_{s−r2}, r1,r2∈[k], while the main Proposition 3.1 aligned successor comparison uses x_{t−r+1}=x_{s−r}. Since the copied representation contains blocks indexed 0,…,k, do not silently identify those indices or describe the displayed [k]×[k] formula as automatically covering every one of the (k+1)² query/key block pairs. Mapping to aligned successor prediction needs an explicit shift and choice of valid candidate/value indices. These qualifications do not establish that the paper's generalized claim is false after suitable conventions; they identify unstated or unverified conditions for importing it.

## 3. D.3 candidate boundary

The independent review also flags the candidate interval difference already included in current `AUDIT.md`: [Definition 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS1) includes s=T; the [Appendix D.3 footnote](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#A4.SS3) excludes it when obtaining T−k−1 candidate positions without BOS. Reconcile the target estimator and range before transplanting the β heuristic. This is distinct from the approximation's independence and concentration assumptions.
