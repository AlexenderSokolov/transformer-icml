Exact softmax of logits involving the dyadic s is not symbolic exponentiation to powers of two. In the exact-real dyadic decoder the score perturbation is at most η/128 and resulting softmax-TV perturbation at most η/64; its valid-copy gates remain exact and its all-word output error is at most 9η/64. In the separately specified backend, exact integer row maxima and the discrete quadratic exponent pattern produce symbolic powers 2^{−k}; retaining k≤T and dropping larger k preserves the copy margins. The second head retains all three relative exponents 0,1,b+1 and has the sharper η/8 add-one error. This is a specialized approximate backend, not a generic exponential algorithm or ordinary floating-point guarantee.

Resource audit

The declared graph has two blocks, two head slots each, three active instances, d_model=32, d_head=16 with scale 1/4, and first FFN width ≤72; no LayerNorm, added BOS, extra vocabulary softmax, or custom attention mask is hidden. A straightforward dense executor computes 3N(N+1)/2 active scalar attention scores if evaluating every causal row, hence O(N²) scalar attention operations with constant dimensions; this is an operation count, not a hardware FLOP benchmark. Relative integer exponents require O(log(cN²)) bits, while raw logits can reach O(cN²+b) in magnitude. With aligned denominators and exact canonicalization, the stated conservative P=O(log N) bit budget and O(N²P²+(P+r)²) schoolbook bit-operation upper bound are consistent. The charged ln2 preprocessing is O(p⁵+T) bit operations and O(p²+T) bits; working/output O(NP+r) bits and the O(N log N)-bit position table, or paid exact position generation, remain separate. The output grid has fractional precision plus endpoint representation, not r total bits. Same-history classical counters and division already compute B exactly with O(N) count updates, so no computational advantage follows.

Prior art and v2 to v3

Direct comparison of the exact v2 and v3 text shows no change to the model, statements, bounds, indexing, constants, or resource ledger. V3 adds the Han–Jana–Wu terminal-conditioned moment/reversal overlap, nonreversibility and large-alphabet qualifications, a hash-bound journal-version addendum, and an explicitly unpassed prior-art gate. This repairs the v2 omission. Han–Jana–Wu Lemma 16 explicitly states conditional residual and occupancy moment bounds for finite reversible irreducible chains; the present pair lift is nonreversible since 00→01 has positive stationary flow while 01→00 is impossible. The journal Remark 2 must not be confused with arXiv v2 Remark 2. A fixed-k implication and complete nonreversible comparison remain open; differences alone do not prove novelty. The journal PDF was unavailable to this reviewer via the web fetch, so its precise version-specific Remark 2 was checked against the locally bound targeted comparison and correction, not reverified from the journal during this audit.

The primary-source statements checked also support the cautious comparison: D’Angelo et al. give finite-parameter matching and a BOS add-α limit; Rajaraman et al. state constant-dimensional k-gram constructions and O(log T)-bit approximation, with positive-count empirical estimates; Izzo et al. present a heuristic finite-score positive-match construction. Their assumptions and numerical models differ. R011 is the same pathwise add-one comparator under its own averaged-risk statement; conditioning on 00 does not follow from that average unchanged.
