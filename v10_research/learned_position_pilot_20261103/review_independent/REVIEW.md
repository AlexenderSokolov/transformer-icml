# Independent audit of learned-position smoke pilot

3 November 2026. Scope: code, protocol, retained outputs, and independently rerun outputs. This is an audit of the exploratory smoke, not confirmation of a theorem, a global Hamming-ball search, or a statistically powered test of feature learning. I did not modify sealed author sources or publish anything.

## Verdict

The implemented experiment matches its smoke protocol in the respects checked, and the numerical results in the report are supported by retained trajectories. The limited negative conclusion is reasonable: this single short run does not demonstrate useful learned positional protection. It does not establish that the readout is unable to use fixed-half information, that the architecture cannot learn a gate, or that positional protection is impossible. Readout, objective, and optimization calibration remain open.

## Independent checks

- Compared original and independently replayed evaluation paths and trajectory-metric NPZ arrays: identical keys and elementwise values.
- Reconstructed the output word for each trajectory in every one of 960 stress rows using retained proposal indices and checkpoint models. Recomputed TV against the true terminal row, sample means and pointwise SEs. Maximum discrepancy from saved per-trajectory TV and row summaries was exactly zero; no reconstructed cell violated the edit budget or protected endpoints. The 30 clean rows independently recomputed to zero discrepancy for TV means and finite KL means/SEs. Recomputed every finite-family CE objective and first-index argmax: selected indices matched exactly, with no finite maximum discrepancy. The audit program reuses pilot model forward/proposal/statistic functions, so it is not an independent reimplementation of those mathematical components.
- For each source and horizon, independently formed the four-state transition matrix and its square. Its minimum entry is 1/4 for the iid source, and exactly 1/M for each sticky source. Consequently both the stronger entrywise P²≥1/M bound and the tested P²(u,v)≥π(v)/M condition hold. This is a finite check for the nine declared source/horizon combinations, not a certificate for the training prior, which is unconditioned.
- The existing exhaustive small-word test independently forms the four triple-count rows and evaluates exact rational path masses. Given independent uniform row priors and an independent uniform start, the clean terminal-row posterior is (C+1)/(J+2). Both training and attacker use this clean-word B as target; neither passes it as a model input. For a frozen model and the stated prior, CE(B(w),q(z)) is the conditional expected next-label CE for any word-derived candidate z. This identity need not hold under the fixed stress sources and is not a claim that their true source probability equals B.
- The actual attack takes only a predictor, the clean observed word, k and a family. Actual predictors close over model parameters or word-derived baseline statistics, not hidden tables or future labels. Candidate edits affect indices 0 through M−1 and leave the terminal pair protected. Training differentiates the selected finite proposal as a fixed branch, before Adam's update; at a tie the first active branch is used. This is valid for the declared finite-max objective and is not a global edit-ball search. Analytical gradients are checked against central differences for all parameters in the retained tests, but this is numerical evidence at tested points, not a proof of all possible gradients.
- Detailed executable checks and their output are in this review directory. The replay and the check share the same implementation and environment, so agreement is reproducibility evidence rather than an independent implementation or external timestamp.

## Corrections and limits to preserve

1. **Fixed-half conclusion.** The fixed-half control supplies enough raw information to calculate the half-count D and total counts: its first-half head returns 2D/M at these even horizons, and its uniform head returns J/M. But the trainable tanh readout was neither directly supervised on the hard gate nor shown to approximate its threshold well. Failure to outperform uniform control after one initialization and 200 updates is not evidence of mathematical inability. The result should be stated as “the fixed-half control did not show a clear benefit over the count-only control in this smoke,” not “cannot use” or “cannot learn” the gate. Even that observation is conditional on the tested objectives and attacks.
2. **Random-control strength.** Frozen random heads were initialized with logit SD 0.01, so they are close to uniform: at M=128 their first-half masses are 0.50393 and 0.49960, and their greatest pointwise relative deviation from uniform is 2.72%. This is a valid control for this particular fixed small-random initialization, not a broad test of arbitrary frozen positional features.
3. **Attack comparability.** Every predictor, including each ablation and baseline, receives its own finite CE-maximizing attack on the same clean trajectories. Consequently comparisons are paired by clean word but need not use identical attacked words. Uniform-profile ablation changes both model inputs and the chosen attack, and may create feature distribution shift; its improvement is a descriptive intervention, not isolated causal proof that the learned positions themselves caused a change in robustness.
4. **A and infinite ties.** A can output 0 or 1, while B is strictly interior. Its CE is then infinite. When several candidates have infinite CE, the fixed first-index tie rule may select identity or a different infinite-loss candidate even if another candidate has larger true-source TV. This remains a valid source-independent finite-proposal CE attack, but A's TV numbers are not the maximum TV over that family and CE comparisons involving A require this qualification. More generally CE-selected attacks do not necessarily maximize true-source TV for any condition. Among 15,360 nonzero-budget A stress candidate sets, 4,009 had an infinite maximum, 3,440 had multiple infinite candidates, and 3,226 selected the identity as the first infinite candidate. Post-hoc, the selected candidate did not maximize true-source TV in 5,855 sets (maximum individual TV gap 0.9375). This post-hoc TV maximum is an oracle diagnostic only: using the source table to select an attack would violate the declared threat model.
5. **Statistical scope.** Single seed, 256 trajectories per source/start, selected panel maxima, only three sources and two starts, three horizons and restricted proposals do not justify a general-source/worst-case bound or a significance claim. Pointwise SEs are not uncertainty intervals for selected maxima. Each condition's selected maximum can be upward biased, and shared paths alone do not quantify between-model uncertainty.
6. **Protocol provenance and computational scope.** The local pre-run hash and logs support reproducibility and consistency with a predeclared exploratory protocol; they are not a trusted external timestamp or preregistration. This model uses a fixed local triple tokenizer and is not an end-to-end Transformer. Seeded float64 simulation is a numerical approximation and does not imply a finite-bit theorem.

## Code-level trace

- `pilot.py:15-20,35-37` computes clean-word B from terminal-pair counts and samples independent prior rows and start; `pilot.py:72-79` builds predictor features without B or the source table.
- `pilot.py:106-123` generates identity and legal proposals, computes B from the original word, and takes first-index CE argmax. `run.py:49-55` selects using the pre-update model and differentiates on clean plus selected words before the optimizer update.
- `run.py:85,91` obtains the fixed source terminal probability for scoring and calls `attack(predict,w,k,family)` separately for each model; the source-valued `target` is not an attack argument. Protected terminal bits ensure its terminal-row identity remains unchanged. The actual absolute error is evaluated only after attack selection.
- `run.py:69,89-103` rebuilds uniform-profile ablations, chooses their own attacks, reports source-conditioned error and selects sample panel maxima. Thus identical clean paths do not imply identical attacked paths.

## Practical disposition

Accept the run as a reproducible exploratory smoke, with the above limitations. Keep the stop decision: do not promote it to evidence of impossibility or an adaptive minimax claim. If continued, pre-specify a separate calibration experiment that tests whether the readout can represent and fit the known half statistic/gate under a clearly specified target, then separately study objective/optimization and source-independent attacks. Record failures and use multiple initializations and held-out conditions before interpreting learned positional features.
