Constant-width, finite-logit causal add-one baseline

2026-11-03. Candidate derived resource benchmark, not a standalone novelty claim. This report has not yet received independent full-report review. It preserves original N input tokens, uses no BOS token and no query-dependent attention mask, and pays for positional inputs, score scales and a specialized finite-bit softmax implementation. The real-valued standard decoder and that approximate backend are explicitly distinguished. No training, LayerNorm, generic floating-point implementation, vocabulary-logit output softmax, RNG or experimental run is claimed.

1. Model and comparator

Let N≥3, η∈(0,1], m=N−2 and x₁,…,x_N∈{0,1}. At i=N let the target pair be (x_{N−1},x_N). For each j=3,…,N the historical key is (x_{j−2},x_{j−1}) and its value is x_j. Write J for the number of keys equal to the target, C for their value sum, and B=(C+1)/(J+2). A standard causal mask exposes only j≤i. Use two residual blocks with H=2, d_model=32, head dimension 16 and scaling 1/4; one head in block 2 is zero. Tokens are embedded with x_i and public positional coordinates (1,i,i²), plus zeroed work slots. There is no hidden singleton mask. Public position coordinates may be stored in a paid O(N log N)-bit table or generated from a counter; their magnitudes reach N².

Set

c=⌈log₂(64N)⌉,  b=⌈log₂(4N/η)⌉,  T=b+1,
A=max{c(N+1)²,b+1},  p=⌈log₂(128A/η)⌉.

Store a dyadic s with |s−ln2|≤2⁻ᵖ; all listed learned coefficients are rational. Let the first head copy lag ℓ=1 and the second lag ℓ=2 using

Qᵢ=(8cs(i−ℓ), −4cs, 0,…,0),   Kⱼ=(j,j²,0,…,0),   Vⱼ=xⱼ.
Qᵢ·Kⱼ/4 = cs[2(i−ℓ)j−j²].                                           (1)

These Q/K vectors are linear projections of the 32-dimensional state. Equation (1) differs by the query-only term cs(i−ℓ)² from −cs[j−(i−ℓ)]², so softmax is unchanged by subtracting it. When i≥3 the desired j=i−ℓ exists and has distance zero. Under ideal symbolic s=ln2 its wrong-key mass is at most (N−1)2⁻ᶜ<1/64.

2. Exact Boolean gates without supplied validity flags

Let g(z)=ReLU(z)−ReLU(z−1), and for any two desired bit-or-complement signals let A₀ be their sum and z=2A₀−5/2. One tokenwise one-hidden-layer ReLU FFN computes

G(i,z)=g(z)−g(z−4(i−2))+g(1−z+4(i−1))−g(i−1).                    (2)

All copied bits and complements are in [0,1], hence −5/2≤z≤3/2. At i=1, g(z)+g(1−z)=1 and g(z+4)=1, so G=0. At i=2 the first two terms cancel and the last two cancel. At i≥3, g(z−4(i−2))=0 and the last two terms cancel, so G=g(z). If each approximate bit has error at most 1/16, then correct pairs give z≥5/4 and incorrect pairs z≤−1/4; thus G is the exact pair indicator. Compute four historical key indicators K_i and four query indicators Q_i using the two lag copies and x_i. Both are zero at i=1,2, and are exact one-hot vectors at i≥3. Each G is a sum of eight ReLU hinges of affine forms, so the eight indicators need at most 64 hinges. The same FFN also writes

zᵢ=ReLU(2−i),    Yᵢ=xᵢ−ReLU(xᵢ−(i−1))+(1/2)ReLU(2−i).

At integer positions z_i=1{i=1}, and Y₁=1/2, Y_i=x_i for i≥2. The original x₁ was available to first-layer lag copies and remains in a distinct residual coordinate. At most 72 FFN hinges suffice with reuse. Reserve disjoint residual slots: input coordinates, two copies, eight indicators, z_i, Y_i and output use fewer than 32. No sequential gate composition is hidden inside the first FFN.

3. Second attention head and all-word bound

The active block-2 head uses standard full causal attention, bilinear score

bs⟨Qᵢ,Kⱼ⟩+(b+1)s zⱼ,     value Yⱼ,                                  (3)

and writes its scalar attention result p̂ to a fresh output slot. One query coordinate is the constant 1, and the 16-dimensional Q/K projections incorporate the factor 4 required by scaling. Since K₁=K₂=0, position 1 is a synthetic pseudo-count, not a historical match. For the ideal s=ln2, position 1 has weight 2ᵇ⁺¹ and value 1/2, each full historical match has weight 2ᵇ, and all N−1−J other positions have weight 1. The normalized full-match plus pseudo-count component is exactly B. Therefore, for every path including J=0,

|p̂−B| ≤ (N−1−J)/[2ᵇ(J+2)+N−1−J] ≤ N2⁻ᵇ/2 ≤ η/8.             (4)

Output (1−p̂,p̂); attention convexity gives p̂∈[0,1]. This is a probability-valued readout, not an additional learned logit softmax.

4. Exact dyadic-logit decoder and finite-bit backend

For the mathematical standard decoder, compute the usual exact softmax of the rational logits in (1),(3). After the row-constant shift, every first-head coefficient multiplying s is −c[j−(i−ℓ)]², with absolute value at most c(N+1)²; the second head's coefficient is at most b+1. Replacing symbolic ln2 by s changes every centered score by at most A2⁻ᵖ≤η/128. If two logit vectors differ coordinatewise by at most a, their softmax vectors differ in TV by at most (e²ᵃ−1)/2≤2a when a≤1/2. Thus each true dyadic-logit copy differs from the ideal copy by at most η/64. Its error is at most 1/64+η/64≤1/32<1/16, so all tokenwise gates are exactly the intended bits. The mathematical dyadic-logit decoder therefore satisfies |p̂−B|≤η/8+η/64=9η/64 on every word.

For an explicit specialized numerical backend, do not claim exp(ks)=2ᵏ. For each causal copy row use the integer quadratic score to subtract its exact row maximum, so ideal symbolic-ln2 relative scores are −k ln2 for integer k≥0. Replace their exponential weights by 2⁻ᵏ for k≤T and zero for k>T, then normalize using exact rational arithmetic. The discarded ideal probability mass is at most N2⁻ᵀ≤η/8, because a maximum has relative weight 1. When i≥3 the correct lag has relative exponent 0 and is retained, and dropping nonmatching keys can only reduce its already ≤1/64 ideal wrong-key mass. Hence all numerical gates remain exact. The actual head's dyadic-logit softmax and this backend differ in TV by at most η/64+N2⁻ᵀ; this bound applies rowwise, including i=1,2.

For the second head the first-position pseudo-count is always a row maximum. Relative exponents are 0 for it, 1 for matching keys and b+1 for all nonmatches, and T=b+1 retains all of them. The backend thus reproduces the ideal second-head distribution exactly and satisfies (4), with no truncation error at this head. This is a certified finite-bit approximation for this discrete family of logits, not a general exp implementation or a blanket claim about framework floating-point inference. A final rounding to p_out fractional bits adds at most 2⁻ᵖ_out; clipping and exact complement preserve a probability vector.

5. Explicit cost ledger

Compute integer relative exponents k=c[(j−(i−ℓ))²−min_{h≤i}(h−(i−ℓ))²], compare k with T before forming any power, and retain only 2⁻ᵏ for k≤T. The stored integer k needs O(log(cN²)) bits, not cN² bits; the raw mathematical logits and position values nevertheless have magnitudes O(cN²) and O(N²). Align retained weights to denominator 2ᵀ, so numerator and denominator sums use at most T+⌈log₂N⌉+2 bits. The two copied rationals may be multiplied when evaluating hinge signs, and the second attention output has a denominator of at most T+⌈log₂N⌉+2 bits. A conservative explicit bit budget for every exact intermediate is

P=10[T+p+b+c+⌈log₂(N+2)⌉+100].

Input positions and exact dyadic model coefficients, integer score arithmetic, two-copy hinge comparisons, and signed rational additions and products all fit this bound under the stated aligned-denominator evaluation. For straightforward dense causal attention, no attention matrix materialization, and schoolbook integer operations, an upper bound is O(N²P²+(P+p_out)²) bit operations and O(NP+p_out) working/output bits, plus preprocessing. There are O(1) scalar model weights, each with O(p+log(b+c+1)) bits; d=32, L=2, H=2, three active head instances, and FFN width at most 72. Ordinary arithmetic operation count is O(N²); token state storage is O(N) rational words. Positional table storage is O(N log N) bits, or a deterministic counter and exact squaring can generate positions within the stated asymptotic time and O(log N) counter bits. Since b,c,T,p=O(log(N/η)), P=O(log(N/η)); constants are not optimized.

The stored dyadic s and on-demand powers of two are model/backend inputs. Deterministic preprocessing from ln2=2∑_{r≥0}[(2r+1)3^{2r+1}]⁻¹ truncated after p+2 terms, followed by dyadic rounding, costs at most O(p⁵+T) schoolbook bit operations and O(p²+T) bits by naive exact rational accumulation. The previous reviewed O(N)-dimensional one-hot construction uses dense O(N³) arithmetic and O(N²) state words; this variant changes both its positional encoding and numerical backend, so it is an explicit resource benchmark, not a lower-bound separation. A same-history streaming add-one counter needs O(N) updates, O(log N) bits per fixed-number counter and a paid final division, and computes B exactly.

6. Actual-terminal risk and prior-art scope

For the rare stationary family in Route A v3, with u=P(S_N=00)>0, v=mu≥60, θ₀₀=t, and δ∈(0,1), the earlier same-history comparator bound gives

E[|B−t| | S_N=00] ≤ min{1, δ+1220/v+√(ln[2(m+1)/(uδ)]/v)+1/(v/2+2)}.

By the pathwise inequality, add η/8 and the final rounding error for the specialized backend, or 9η/64 for the mathematical dyadic-logit decoder; cap at 1. Terminal dependence is retained, not replaced by an independent query. For v<60 use TV≤1. R011, K=2 and M=N−2, targets exactly the same B with unconditional loss averaged over terminal states; see the separate indexing clarification. The new calculation adds a resource-accounted approximation to that estimator, not a different counting estimator.
