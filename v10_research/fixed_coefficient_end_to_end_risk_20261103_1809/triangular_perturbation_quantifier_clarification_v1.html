Triangular perturbation quantifiers

3 November 2026. This is an immutable clarification of finite_n_explicit_bound_v1.html, SHA-256 fa808a23149bf3b7a4590f144cebdafe3a6ca1f1299c694d7bfe53e7b1ed0bd1.

The general condition qN[ln(qN+1)+ln(N+2)]/N→0 implies that the example's risk tends to 3/16; by itself it does not imply that its scalar-weight perturbation 2/qN tends to zero. For the latter conclusion additionally require qN→∞. The explicit example qN=⌊N/[ln(N+2)]²⌋ eventually satisfies both conditions. Each N then uses a different perturbed model, while the source and the unperturbed graph are fixed. This is a triangular sequence, not one fixed perturbed network.

For a fixed integer q, the perturbation remains fixed and nonzero, and the original fixed-model risk limit also follows. No claim is made for arbitrary η or for floating-point hardware.
