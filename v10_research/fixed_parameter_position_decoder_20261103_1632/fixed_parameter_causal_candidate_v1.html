Fixed-parameter causal decoder with paid unbounded positions

3 November 2026. Candidate exact-real construction for independent mathematical and architecture review. It is not a trained-model theorem, generic floating-point guarantee, bounded-logit result, standard vocabulary-softmax decoder claim or novelty claim. No random or numerical experiments were used.

Claim and scope. A single fixed finite parameter set in a three-block, fixed-width, full-causal scaled-dot-product attention/ReLU model maps every binary word X₁:N, N≥2, to a valid Bernoulli probability Q_N. With two paid special tokens and explicit unbounded positional inputs (1,i,i²), it approximates the ordinary same-history actual-terminal add-one estimator B_N pathwise: |Q_N−B_N|≤(N/2)e^(−N). Parameters do not depend on N or the source; positional magnitudes and effective match score grow with N. The output is a direct probability pair (Q_N,1−Q_N), with no additional vocabulary-logit softmax. Whether the same claim holds with a mandatory final linear-logit softmax or ordinary floating-point software is not established.

1. Input, architecture and paid access

Positions i=1,2 are two BOS tokens with scalar token value x_i=1/2. Positions i=k+2, k=1,…,N, contain x_i=X_k∈{0,1}. The fixed positional embedding supplies 1,i,i², and no coordinate containing i times a state or token indicator is supplied. The terminal position is L=N+2. The model dimension is 64. Each of three pre-specified blocks has four causal self-attention heads of head dimension 16, a residual connection, and one one-hidden-layer ReLU feed-forward network with residual connection. Attention scores are (q·k)/4 and the mask permits precisely j≤i. There is no LayerNorm, dropout or external relative-position mask. The first two heads of block 1 and all four heads of block 3 carry information; the rest have value/output maps zero. Thus the graph allocates four heads per block and uses six active head instances; evaluating a zero head may be skipped but it is not a free change of model.

Reserve disjoint residual coordinates for 1,i,i²,x; two copied values a₁,a₂; snapped values t₁,t₂; BOS flag b and valid-candidate flag v; four candidate-context indicators g_h; four query-context indicators q_h; four final-head outputs o_h; and one final scalar output. The unused coordinates remain zero. All Q/K/V/output/FFN maps below are fixed linear maps with fixed biases, shared across positions and all lengths. Only i and i² are supplied positional features.

2. Block 1: causal lag copying with fixed parameters

For each active head h∈{1,2}, set the first two Q and K coordinates and value as

q_i^(h)=(4(2i²−2hi), −4i, 0,…,0),
k_j^(h)=(j,j²,0,…,0),          value_j=x_j.
score_h(i,j)=2i(i−h)j−ij² = i(i−h)² − i(j−i+h)².

The first term is independent of j. If i>h, the target j=i−h exists within the causal prefix. Relative to it, all other score gaps equal −i d² for an integer d≠0. Thus the total wrong attention mass is at most

2Σ_(d≥1)e^(−i d²) ≤ 2e^(−i)/(1−e^(−i)) < 1/4,  i≥4.

All values lie in [0,1]. Hence a₁ differs from x_{i−1} by <1/4 at i≥4, and a₂ differs from x_{i−2} by <1/4 at i≥5. The stricter i≥5 condition ensures the lag-two target is a data bit. Outputs a_h are written into their disjoint slots by the head output matrices. Early positions have arbitrary convex combinations and are handled explicitly below.

The block-1 FFN uses the fixed hinge functions

T(a)=2ReLU(a−1/4)−2ReLU(a−3/4),
b(i)=ReLU(3−i)−ReLU(2−i),
v(i)=ReLU(i−4)−ReLU(i−5).

It writes t_h=T(a_h), b=b(i), v=v(i). On all inputs a_h∈[0,1] and therefore t_h∈[0,1]. For data i≥5, both snapped t_h are the exact predecessor bits x_{i−h}; for terminal i=L≥4, t₁ is the exact previous data bit. At integer positions b=1 exactly at the two BOS positions, and v=1 exactly at candidate positions i≥5. This requires eight hidden ReLU units and no position-token cross feature.

3. Block 2: valid-context and actual-query gates

All block-2 attention value/output maps are zero, so its full-causal attention has zero residual contribution. For anchor h=(h₁,h₂)∈{00,01,10,11}, define the affine mismatch

d_h(a,c) = (a if h₁=0 else 1−a) + (c if h₂=0 else 1−c).
g_h=ReLU(v−d_h(t₂,t₁)),       q_h=ReLU(1−d_h(t₁,x)).

A single-hidden-layer ReLU FFN writes these eight coordinates. Since t_h∈[0,1], d_h≥0. At invalid positions v=0 so every g_h=0, even if an early copy is fractional. At every data candidate i=k+2≥5, g_h is exactly 1 for h=(X_{k−2},X_{k−1}) and 0 otherwise. At the terminal i=N+2≥4, x=X_N and t₁=X_{N−1}, so q_h is a one-hot encoding of the actual terminal pair (X_{N−1},X_N). Query indicators at earlier positions need not be one-hot and are irrelevant to the final output.

4. Block 3: four fixed anchor heads and exact accounting

For each of the four anchors h, use Q, K and V coordinates

q_i^(h)=(4(i−2),0,…,0),  k_j^(h)=(g_{j,h}+b_j,0,…,0),  value_j=x_j.
score_h(i,j)=(i−2)(g_{j,h}+b_j).

The position-dependent product is performed by the normal Q·K bilinear score. The input does not contain a precomputed product i×state. For terminal i=L, i−2=N. The two BOS positions have g=0,b=1 and value 1/2; a valid matching candidate has g=1,b=0 and value X_k; all other data positions, including the first two invalid data tokens, have score 0. Candidate j=k+2 for k=3,…,N uses predecessor (X_{k−2},X_{k−1}) and successor X_k. In particular the terminal data position k=N is a legal causal key, while X_{N+1} is never used.

Let J_h,C_h be the valid same-history context and successor-one counts. After dividing the weights by e^N, the head output is exactly

o_h=[C_h+1+e^(−N)D_h]/[J_h+2+e^(−N)K_h],
K_h=N−J_h,     0≤D_h≤K_h≤N.

The +1 in the numerator is the sum of the two BOS values 1/2, and +2 in the denominator is their two unit relative weights. For every input word, including J_h=0,

|o_h−(C_h+1)/(J_h+2)| ≤ e^(−N)K_h/[J_h+2+e^(−N)K_h] ≤ (N/2)e^(−N).

The final FFN computes Q_N=Σ_h ReLU(o_h+q_h−1). Since q_h is one-hot and 0≤o_h≤1, this selects the head for S_N. A final affine probability readout returns (Q_N,1−Q_N). For N=2 no observed triple is valid and the target B_N=1/2; the same error bound holds, rather than asserting the model output is exactly 1/2. N<2 is outside the task definition.

5. Uniform risk, with inherited statistical theorem

Define B_N=(C_{S_N}+1)/(J_{S_N}+2), the usual same-history add-one predictor. The pathwise bound above holds simultaneously for every word and any N≥2, with one fixed set of parameters. Hence for any binary homogeneous second-order table and any initial pair law,

E[TV((Q_N,1−Q_N), θ_{S_N})] ≤ E[TV(B_N,θ_{S_N})]+(N/2)e^(−N).

The separately reviewed R011 companion gives, for its β=0 endpoint and K=2, a worst-case bound for B_N of min{1,2¹³√[32/(N−1)]}. Every finite pair transition matrix has a stationary law, and its β=0 minorization condition is vacuous, including off-support initial states. Consequently

sup_(all homogeneous binary order-two tables, all initial pair laws) risk(Q_N)
 ≤ min{1, 2¹³√[32/(N−1)] + (N/2)e^(−N)} → 0.

The supremum is over sources for each N; it is not merely pointwise consistency for a fixed source. This final statistical corollary depends on the inherited R011 theorem and its review, not on an independent reproof here. 6. Resources and unresolved numerical/output questions

  • Position representation. Input position magnitudes are O(N²); a stored table of exact i and i² uses O(N log N) bits, or they must be generated by a paid counter and integer-square procedure. There is no precomputed i×token/state feature.
  • Score range. Block-1 raw score terms have magnitude O(N³), and block-3 matching gaps equal N. These are not bounded logits or fixed-temperature attention. Large raw values and cancellation are part of the model cost even though learned weights are fixed.
  • Precision. Exact i, i² and integer score intermediates can be represented using O(log N) bits, but that observation alone does not prove accurate softmax outputs. One must specify max shifting, exponent approximation, summation, division and rounding and show copy errors stay inside the fixed snap margins and final prediction error vanishes. Fixed-word IEEE arithmetic or bounded exponent ranges are not certified.
  • Work. A straightforward dense causal evaluation has Θ(N²) query-key pairs per active head and constant model width. Actual bit operation and storage costs depend on the numerical backend and positional representation. The second block’s zero outputs may be skipped by a compiler, but the model includes the block. Training attainability and optimization are not addressed.
  • Output interface. The direct affine readout produces a probability pair. If a standard deployment insists on a further linear-logit vocabulary softmax, the required conversion is not supplied by this construction; it must not be claimed implicitly.

7. Prior art and decision boundary

The compact-causal v3 synthesis already gives a two-block, width-32 all-word add-one approximation with N-dependent parameters and a specified special numerical backend; it is not contradicted by this three-block fixed-parameter exact-real candidate. D’Angelo et al. already describe positional copying, soft matching and BOS smoothing, and Rajaraman et al. give conditional k-gram constructions. A Formal Framework for Understanding Length Generalization in Transformers studies causal attention and positional encoding. These comparisons are limited, not a novelty clearance; in particular a fixed-parameter length-generalization construction may be anticipated by further primary literature. No lower bound or training claim follows.
