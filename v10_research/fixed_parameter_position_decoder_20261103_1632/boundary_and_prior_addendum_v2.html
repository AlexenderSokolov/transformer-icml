Statistical edge case. R011 is stated for M≥1, i.e. N≥3. For N=2, M=0, the displayed global bound is nevertheless valid trivially because binary TV≤1 and its right-hand side is capped at 1. The pathwise comparison with the empty-history add-one value 1/2 remains valid for N=2. Do not attribute the M=0 case directly to R011.

Prior-art scope. The directly relevant primary-source comparison table must be read with the candidate. In particular Ekbote et al., What One Cannot, Two Can, already study two-layer one-head-per-layer conditional k-gram representation with their specified ideal attention limits and length-indexed positional resources, and He et al., Two Stones Hit One Bird, Appendix A.1, already use quadratic positional scores for localization. Neither k-gram representation nor quadratic copying should be described as novel. The present candidate is a different conjunction of assumptions whose novelty has not been established.
