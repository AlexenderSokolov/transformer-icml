# Completed rare-weight ablation: results and preservation limitation

3 November 2026. This is an additive report on the original sealed, completed exploratory run in `ablation_v1`; it does not amend its sources, protocol, data or checkpoints. The run was approved by root after the protocol seal. The three initialization pairs share one training set, two evaluation sets and one minibatch stream. They are not three independent data replications.

## Findings

The predeclared composite screen passed in all three initializations. Over the eight J≤3 structural feature vectors whose `(J,C,D,U)` groups were both excluded from training and absent in the earlier calibration data, the boosted/corrected mean absolute-error ratios were 0.317, 0.459 and 0.448. The corresponding corrected mean errors were 0.10835, 0.07514 and 0.11251; boosted errors were 0.03436, 0.03446 and 0.05040. These eight vectors span seven groups and are evaluated uniformly once per feature, not according to a source probability. The group selection was formulated after prior analyses, so it was not historically blinded. No model saw these groups in training.

The separate independent, unconditioned prior sample had lower mean KL in the boosted arm by 0.000241, 0.000436 and 0.000571; all final collision maximum errors were below 0.005. But the boosted prior mean **absolute** error rose, from 0.00888, 0.00755, 0.00977 to 0.01529, 0.01096, 0.01223. The empirical nonrare-training mean absolute error rose from 0.00553, 0.00434, 0.00641 to 0.01402, 0.00939, 0.01066, while rare-training mean absolute error fell from 0.05383, 0.04351, 0.05206 to 0.01918, 0.01651, 0.01958. Thus no blanket claim that every prior metric improved is justified.

On the independently drawn 1,024-word sample conditional on rare and held-out group, mean absolute error fell from 0.05760, 0.04728, 0.05650 to 0.02900, 0.02971, 0.03196. Its worst errors remained 0.594, 0.541 and 0.547 after boosting. The full exact 11-feature J≤3 holdout still had boosted maximum errors 0.385, 0.330 and 0.360. These results are not a small uniform teacher-approximation guarantee.

The corrected arm weights the rare/nonrare conditional means by the empirical proportions 133/2048 and 1915/2048; the boosted arm assigns half of prior mass to each. Both retain half the objective weight on the four supplied collision words. Both arms use exactly the same sampled indices and initial weights, so this is a controlled finite weighting intervention, but changing weights also changes stochastic-gradient variance. The results do not separate loss-landscape and noise effects, prove the capacity of width 32, establish source risk or minimax guarantees, or predict other seeds or datasets. The sampled rare conditional and unconditioned prior evaluation are different distributions. Collision words are training examples, not independent evaluation.

## Preservation and exposure record

The sealed source ran to completion, although a preservation defect was noticed while it was running. An attempted stop was too late: the process had exited successfully before the stop check. Filesystem timestamps record the seal at 06:40:23 UTC, frozen data at approximately 06:41:07 UTC and final results at approximately 06:41:14 UTC. The root reviewed the sealed protocol and authorized the six fits before execution. The completed console and all final metrics, including held-out predictions, have been read; this dataset is exposed and must not be presented as a pristine unseen confirmation for any future fit.

Preserved: original sealed protocol/source/tests; the complete console log; accepted words and Q targets; all sampled minibatch index arrays; 12,000 per-update CSV rows with loss and gradient/weight norms; 24 full model-parameter checkpoints at steps 0, 200, 1000 and 2000; all checkpoint and final metrics; and final per-word predictions. The source records the seeds and declared sampling settings. The checkpoints contain model parameters but **not Adam moment states**, so do not support exact interrupted-run continuation. Per-update rows contain gradient norms, not full gradient tensors.

Not preserved: the prior generator discarded rejected/truncated candidate words, latent transition tables, starts and random uniforms. Specifically, the train rejection batch drew 8,192 candidate words and retained 2,048, and the held-out rejection stream drew 122,880 and retained 1,024; 128,000 candidate words were discarded in total. The independent natural evaluation retained 8,192 words. Their latent generator tables/starts/uniforms also were not stored. Seeds and code support attempted reconstruction, but no discarded or latent data have been recovered and none should be described as captured. This violates the pre-run report's promise to preserve all raw generated data. Training updates and metrics were buffered until completion, which would have lost them on interruption, although the actual completed run did write the full arrays and CSVs.

## Files and verification

`PRE_RUN_HASHES.json` and `seal.log` bind and record the pre-sampling protocol; `run_console.log` is the captured original console. `ablation_v1/frozen_data.npz`, `pre_fit_checks.json`, `updates.csv`, `checkpoint_metrics.csv`, `final_metrics.csv`, `final_predictions.npz`, `checkpoints/`, `results.json`, and `ARTIFACT_HASHES.json` retain the run. The pre-fit checks show zero training-group intersections with either rare or structural holdout; the unconditioned prior validation shares 14 distinct words with training, so it is not word-disjoint. An independent reviewer checked the split, weighting algebra and sealed inputs before examining results. The completed results and precise output bindings still require independent audit. No new seeds or fits have been run after exposure.
