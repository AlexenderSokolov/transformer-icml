# Pre-run report: rare-objective reweighting

The existing tail diagnosis establishes three distinct facts: the half features determine Q for all valid M=64 words; many held-out tails lack the same training `(J,C,D,U)` statistic; and several sampled training cases are badly underfit. Those facts do not establish that width 32 is incapable of the target, or that an optimizer or a coverage gap is the sole cause. Existing inspected failures are exploratory and will not be used as blind evaluation.

## Falsifiable intervention

A new fixed-data, fixed-budget paired experiment contrasts the corrected empirical-prior objective with a deliberately rare-weighted prior objective. The two arms receive identical initial parameters, fresh frozen data, collision words, the same rare/nonrare/collision sampled indices, 128 examples per update, Adam configuration, 2,000 steps and checkpoint schedule. Only fixed scalar weights in the minibatch gradient change. Three seeds are paired initializations on the same data and stream.

Let R be the empirical training stratum with `J≤6 or D=0`, and p its exact fraction in the 2,048 accepted training trajectories. Let N be its complement. With conditional means, the original empirical prior loss is `p L_R+(1-p)L_N`. Both arms sample 32 R, 32 N and 64 collision examples with replacement. The corrected objective is `0.5[p L_R+(1-p)L_N]+0.5 L_collision`, so the expected gradient exactly equals that training-set mean. The boosted objective is `0.5[0.5 L_R+0.5 L_N]+0.5 L_collision`. Relative to an equally weighted 64-example rare/nonrare proposal, the corrected importance factors are `2p` and `2(1-p)`; boost uses 1 and 1. Neither arm may be called an unbiased estimator of the unconditioned prior: training is conditioned on the train group. The weight change also changes gradient noise; it does not identify optimization dynamics independent of the objective.

A deterministic hash partitions the full `(J,C,D,U)` statistic into training and holdout groups, with the four supplied collision groups forced into training. Training draws use only training groups. A fresh rare conditional sample from holdout groups is never supplied to training, giving a stronger split than word-disjointness. An independent unconditioned prior evaluation sample estimates the ordinary-prior average but may share words or groups with training. Their different sampling laws must not be combined as if both estimated the same risk. The held-out test is conditional on rare and hash assignment, has repeated and nonuniform groups, and is not representative of every rare group.

The pre-specified composite screen requires each of the three boosted final checkpoints to cut mean absolute error across the eight structurally held-out vectors in seven previously unobserved statistic groups by at least 20% versus its paired corrected checkpoint, with natural-prior mean KL increase at most 0.002 and collision max error at most 0.05. The screen is a falsifiable operational target, not a statistical significance claim, uniform bound, or data-independent choice of thresholds. Report all metrics and both losses even when it fails. Record group counts, exact-word overlap, and all final predictions and checkpoint outputs. A held-out win would support this specific weighting intervention; it would not prove causal necessity of coverage, capacity sufficiency, or generalization to all words. A loss would not rule out other objective weights or algorithms.

## Why not a wider sweep or an all-state experiment first?

The existing finite interpolation proof only guarantees a potentially enormous real-valued one-hidden-layer network, not the actual width-32 readout. A verified width-32 exact construction or impossibility would answer a sharper representation question, but no such result is currently available. Finite-sample evaluation of states or examples cannot establish width-32 impossibility, and even exhaustive evaluation of one fixed checkpoint cannot identify why training missed the target. This paired reweighting directly tests a concrete manipulable failure mechanism without an arbitrary width or optimizer sweep. It cannot determine the counterfactual effects of capacity or optimizer independently.

A separately verified dynamic program enumerates all feasible full half-count feature vectors with J≤3, one witnessed word per vector. Its deterministic holdout subset supplies an exact finite structural test: every feasible held-out vector in that restricted slice is evaluated once, without source-frequency weighting. For the primary test, exclude every group represented in the earlier calibration train, validation, collision, or boundary data, yielding eight feature vectors in seven novel groups. The full 11-vector held-out slice, including the previously inspected worst group, is reported separately. Earlier data determine only group exclusion and are not used for new fitting. Selection was devised after earlier analyses, so this is prospective relative to new fits but not historically blinded. All individual errors and worst-case errors will be reported. The finite enumeration does not estimate ordinary-source risk or prove what width 32 can represent.

The original accepted calibration, its words/checkpoints, and post-hoc tail artifacts remain immutable. Source, protocol and tests will be sealed before fresh data are generated; no fitting may start until root reviews the sealed protocol. All raw generated data, index streams, updates, metrics, full checkpoints, and original output logs must be preserved.
