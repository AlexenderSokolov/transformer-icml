Exactly what the 1,008 records contain

File exhaustive_cases_v1.jsonl has SHA-256 c19d158dcc74d415dca0a08606fcf570fb539fd2a63bf8b0e18ecfa508da1f1a. Each record stores its schema ID, N, full input bit string, η, b, c, terminal context, J, C, maximum copy error over relevant copies, whether all gate results are exact, exact final head output, exact add-one output, exact absolute error, exact contamination bound, and pass/fail; rationals are numerator/denominator. It covers every word for N=3,…,8 and η=1/4,1/32. The test code and full 1,008 case records are retained.

These records do not contain full internal execution traces. In particular, they do not save per-position or per-edge Q/K/V vectors, raw or centered logits, attention weights, gate preactivations and individual hinge values, per-token gates, or per-head port outputs. Such values cannot be reconstructed as an execution record from what was actually logged, even though the deterministic code and inputs allow a separate new run. No replacement or rerun is represented by the original records. The companion verification_and_scope_addendum_v1.html means full case records, not complete internal traces. No further run is authorized until the expanded output schema receives independent pre-run review.

Exact R011 estimator mapping

Source: cloud_work/conditions-20261028/research/resume_20261030/R011_global_rates_and_constant_adaptation_v1.md, theorem §1, SHA-256 aaafd3823ab11e20ffdeff2164115c6c91db5efea6e2dffd9c3c78da0b7fbbbf. Set K=2 and M=N−2. R011 observes X₁,…,X_{M+2}=X_N, defines Y_j=(X_{j+1},X_{j+2}) for j=0,…,M, and counts j=0,…,M−1, with successor X_{j+3}. Relabel i=j+2: i runs 2,…,N−1, Y_j=(X_{i−1},X_i), successor X_{i+1}, and terminal Y_M=(X_{N−1},X_N). Its full-history add-one predictor is exactly B=(C+1)/(J+2) of the present report for every path, including J=0.

R011 bounds unconditional TV averaged over the random actual terminal pair, with a worst-case source supremum; it does not directly give the present conditional risk E[|B−θ₀₀| | Y_M=00]. The rare source is stationary with P(Y_M=00)=u>0, and nonnegativity gives the valid comparison conditional risk ≤ min{1,R⁺/u}. In its β=0 class, R011 supplies R⁺≤min{1,2¹³ min[1,√(32/(N−1))]}; consequently the conditional result obtained this way is at most that expression divided by u and capped at 1. The separately derived rare-row bound may be sharper, depends explicitly on the rare state, and is genuinely conditional, but analyzes the same add-one estimator. A pathwise compiled-network error can be added to either bound. No novel unconditional add-one-rate claim follows.
