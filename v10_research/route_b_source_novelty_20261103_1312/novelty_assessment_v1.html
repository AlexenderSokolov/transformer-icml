Route B source-bound novelty assessment

3 November 2026. This is a focused source comparison, not a formal validity check or an exhaustive literature review. It considers the terminal-port criterion, the precision and rare-family upper bounds, and the proposed whole-model direction. It does not alter the original candidate or its corrections.

Assessment: the isolated Euler-feasible terminal-port criterion is an elementary characterization for a deliberately restricted information interface, supported by standard path-flow and Euler-trail facts. The available evidence does not justify proposing it as a standalone paper, a general Transformer limitation, or a newly discovered precision law. The rare-family marginal estimator gives a loose upper bound with public u and substantial exact side ports; its gap to direct counting is not a lower bound. A truly full-network result with matched resources and nontrivial accuracy could change this assessment, but is not established by these observations.

1. What is actually shown

For β₁,β₂≠0, exact J, total successor count C, query and two scalar separately normalized matching-head outputs reveal the two successor margins. Valid binary paths consistent with them form an integer set indexed by C_00, with feasibility tested by Euler trails in a four-state de Bruijn multigraph. Its diameter gives the exact minimax same-history add-α-statistic reconstruction radius for that port. Full residuals, token positions and other head/value channels are deliberately excluded. The finite-bit statement only assumes bounded head-output error and exact side counts; the tie-boundary and deterministic-population-ratio corrections must accompany the original artifact. The specific finite-word collision and small fixed-N lower bound do not imply asymptotic or architecture-wide impossibility.

3. Test the claimed advantage against easy baselines

For the one-parameter rare family with public u, θ=(t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2), w=(1−u)/3 and t∈[1/4,3/4], a network that ignores the past and substitutes t₀=1/2 has stationary actual-terminal average TV exactly 2u|t−1/2|≤u/2. Therefore any purported average-risk gain of order u is not compelling by itself. Conditional risk at terminal 00 is a different target: this constant has worst error 1/4, whereas the full-history rare-query lower has order (1+Mu)^−1/2. Existing R011 already supplies an unconditional full-history add-one upper for broad order-two sources, and direct joint-count heads / streaming counters are mandatory same-information baselines.

The old marginal estimator's bound O(1/(u√M)) on conditional 00 risk is only sufficient, is looser than the natural joint-count bound, and may reflect proof loss rather than information loss. It requires public u, exact counts J and C, numerical score/exp control and an older-match head. Comparing its upper bound to a joint-count upper bound cannot prove an actual separation. The count feature needed for J is itself a paid joint historical feature; if a permitted value map can multiply that feature by the successor, a simple extra value channel bypasses the port restriction.

4. Confidence, unresolved questions, and what would change the conclusion

Confidence is high in the source-statement distinctions and in the limited novelty conclusion for the literal candidate; this is not confidence that no later or unrelated work contains an equivalent result. Izzo analysis incorporates the separate corrected local audit (report SHA-256 d3875d54499912bf92b81fd2eb2e4b8dcf47e2b004bbd825bc27cac43f8d575c, addendum SHA-256 809f2b5f0e6c2631122d61299a5133f5fa8f3ee3b4c8c42ed3d3a78983068626). The source-specific descriptions were checked against the cited primary statements or scoped local primary-source audits, not rederived line by line.

A materially stronger result would give an actual causal full-network estimator or lower bound with all heads, residuals, FFN, masks and output channels explicit; a common no-history, same-information stream and jointly featured head baseline; sharply controlled finite bits and operations; the same actual-terminal conditional and average risks; and a genuinely nonredundant N,L,d,H,b regime. A provable matched-budget separation or a sharp statistical/computational tradeoff that survives these comparators could support reconsideration, subject to a broader source audit and independent proof review. A source-family-specific full R-model upper is under investigation, but by itself it need not beat the trivial u/2 or existing O(M^−1/2) baselines and therefore is not offered as a paper claim here.
