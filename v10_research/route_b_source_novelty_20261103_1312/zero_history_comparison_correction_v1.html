Correction to zero-history comparison

This correction supplements family_aware_single_head_candidate_v1.html, SHA-256 24f6de7002c68f1cb9ca4d7e08e5fbdb9f56a436fff61151997e68acfd69c979. Its introductory wording that the construction “does not yet beat these baselines” is too broad if interpreted as a claim about worst-case risk relative to the fixed, no-history t₀=1/2 rule.

That rule has exact worst-case stationary-average risk u/2. Under the candidate's assumptions, its own displayed bound at β=(ln 2)/2 is

sup_t R_N ≤ 16(√(65M)+16)/M + 4ε_q.

For any fixed u>0, a certified ε_q<u/8 and sufficiently large M make this upper bound strictly less than u/2. Therefore, within the stated ideal structured-mask model and error assumptions, the candidate does establish a strict worst-case risk improvement over that particular zero-history rule. It uses extra attention, position/mask, arithmetic and precision resources, and the result is not a same-budget separation, a comparison with the optimal no-history rule under every loss criterion, or an improvement over the stronger same-history joint-count/streaming baselines. It also does not establish novelty. The more conservative comparison conclusions elsewhere in the candidate remain unchanged.

The independent reviewer identified the wording ambiguity. The original candidate remains unchanged. No numerical test, RNG or source overwrite was used for this correction.
