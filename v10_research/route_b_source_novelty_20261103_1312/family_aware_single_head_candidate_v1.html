Family-aware causal single-head candidate

3 November 2026. This is a mathematical candidate for a complete declared structured-mask reference model R forward map, not a standard dense-causal decoder implementation, a complete finite-bit theorem, a training theorem, an optimality claim, a validated new theorem or a paper proposal. Independent proof review is pending. No numerical test, enumeration, RNG or external write was used.

The construction knows the model family and public u but never receives t or the test kernel. It is compared with a zero-history predictor of worst stationary-average risk ≤u/2, an O(M^−1/2) all-family add-one reference, and a same-history direct joint count. This candidate does not yet beat these baselines or establish that its computational costs are necessary.

1. Source, terminal risk and public parameters

Let N≥3, M=N−2, 0<u<1/4, w=(1−u)/3, and t∈[1/4,3/4]. The stationary binary order-two chain has pair-state law π=(u,w,w,w) and successor-1 probabilities θ=(t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2) in order 00,01,10,11. It starts with S₂=(X₁,X₂)∼π and uses its transition law thereafter. The prediction input is X₁:N; query S_N=(X_{N−1},X_N) is from that same path. The actual loss is |p(X₁:N)−θ_(S_N)|, equal to binary TV. Public u, M and a finite nonzero β may set weights; t, θ and future bits are not inputs or permitted design parameters. The stationary reversed pair chain has P*²(s,11)≥1/8 for each s in this family, hence ρ=7/8 is a two-step contraction factor. This is a common-atom certificate, not a two-step π-minorization.

2. Entire causal graph in model R

Use one token row i for each observed X_i. The first attention block has two singleton-mask heads, copying X_{i−1} and X_{i−2} when those indices exist, retaining X_i and both copied values in separate channels. Give i=1,2 harmless self masks; these rows never enter the next aggregation. No future bit or terminal query is inserted into historical values. A tokenwise affine map forms sign a_N=2X_{N−1}−1 at the terminal row and sign c_i=2X_{i−2}−1 for each historical value row i=3,…,N; successor value v_i=X_i. Each query and key is one-dimensional and the second-block terminal mask is exactly {3,…,N}. The second block has one attention head with terminal score β a_N c_i and value X_i; all other positions may be self-masked. It outputs

q = [Σ_(i=3)^N exp(β a_N c_i) X_i] /
    [Σ_(i=3)^N exp(β a_N c_i)].

The i=N row is a valid historical triple (X_{N−2},X_{N−1},X_N), while its terminal query uses the distinct pair (X_{N−1},X_N). The two functions coexist in distinct coordinates. For terminal first bit 0 let g=β; for terminal first bit 1 let g=−β. With pair state S_t=(X_{t−1},X_t), t=i−1, q can be rewritten as

q= [Σ_(t=2)^(N−1) exp(g(1−2X_{t−1})) X_(t+1)] /
   [Σ_(t=2)^(N−1) exp(g(1−2X_{t−1}))].

The one scalar q, exact terminal pair, and public constants are the only readout inputs needed. Unlike the earlier port construction, there is no assumption that exact J, C, r or all four query rows are exported. Other network coordinates may exist but are not needed by this upper bound. Singleton and early-row masks are charged model-R geometry, not ordinary dense-causal masks.

3. Deterministic population reference and output

Define for g∈{β,−β}

d_g=e^g(u+w)+2w e^(−g),
c_g=[e^g(w/2)+e^(−g)(3w/2)]/d_g,
a_g=u(e^g−e^(−g))/d_g,       ρ_β=e^|β|/[2|sinh β|].

The deterministic ratio obtained by substituting stationary means into the numerator and denominator is c_g+a_g t. It is not asserted equal to E[q] for a random finite path, nor to E[q|S_N=s]. Since β≠0, a_g≠0 and |a_g|≥u/ρ_β. From exact q or an approximation q̃ with |q̃−q|≤ε_q uniformly on every path, set

t̃_g = clip_[1/4,3/4] ((q̃−c_g)/a_g),
p = t̃_β                if S_N=00,
    1−(u/w)t̃_(−β)      if S_N=10,
    1/2                 if S_N∈{01,11}.
Output Bernoulli(p) = (1−p,p).

Both branch candidates can be computed by fixed affine transformations and clamped. The terminal binary pair can be converted to four one-hot flags by constant-size ReLU AND gates; a bounded branch value v and flag b can be gated as ReLU(v+b−1). For state 10, 1−(u/w)t̃ belongs to [0,1] because u/w<1 and t̃≤3/4. Thus the ideal output is a simplex vector and the second coordinate is an exact complement. This argument assumes exact query bits and exact downstream affine/ReLU/clamp arithmetic after q̃. If the final output differs by at most ζ after a separately paid probability projection and normalization, the TV bounds below gain at most ζ; an unverified finite-bit implementation cannot simply assume it.

4. Conditional control of the random ratio

Fix s∈{00,10} and condition on S_N=s, so g is fixed. For t=2,…,N−1 set W_t=exp(g(1−2X_{t−1})), Y_t=W_t X_{t+1}, Z_t=W_t. Both are functions of one edge of the reversed pair chain and lie in [0,e^|β|]. Under the stationary chain,

μ_Y=e^g(ut+w/2)+e^(−g)(3w/2−ut),
μ_Z=d_g,       μ_Y/μ_Z=c_g+a_g t.

These are population expectations of the separate summands. A common-atom coupling for P*² yields, uniformly for every terminal s, conditional mean discrepancy ≤16e^|β| for each sum and conditional variance ≤65M e^(2|β|). Indeed, the single-edge mean discrepancy at reverse time j is ≤e^|β|ρ^⌊j/2⌋ and Σ_j≥0ρ^⌊j/2⌋=16. The absolute covariance of two bounded edge functionals at lag h is ≤2e^(2|β|)ρ^⌊(h−1)/2⌋; Σ_h≥1ρ^⌊(h−1)/2⌋=16. The variance is therefore at most M e^(2|β|)+4M·16e^(2|β|). By Cauchy/Jensen, each conditional expected absolute sum deviation from its stationary mean is ≤e^|β|(√(65M)+16).

The realized denominator D=ΣZ_t is always at least M e^−|β|, and 0≤μ_Y/μ_Z≤1. Thus pathwise, before conditioning,

|q−μ_Y/μ_Z| ≤ [|ΣY_t−Mμ_Y|+|ΣZ_t−Mμ_Z|]/D.

After conditioning on S_N=s, no independence is used. Projection to [1/4,3/4] is nonexpansive relative to t, so

E_t[|t̃_g−t| | S_N=s]
 ≤ min{1/2, (ρ_β/u)[2e^(2|β|)(√(65M)+16)/M+ε_q]}.       (1)

The factor 1/2 follows because both t and t̃_g lie in [1/4,3/4]. These constants are deliberately loose. Equation (1) is a conditional statement for s=00 and s=10 separately, not a claim that their conditionally observed q has the unconditional expectation c_g+a_g t.

5. Actual-terminal average-TV bound and baselines

For 00 the output error is |t̃_β−t|; for 10 it is (u/w)|t̃_−β−t|; for 01 and 11 it is zero in exact downstream arithmetic. Since P(S_N=00)=u and P(S_N=10)=w, (1) gives the uniform-in-t upper bound

R_N ≤ min{u, 2ρ_β[2e^(2|β|)(√(65M)+16)/M+ε_q]}.       (2)

At β=(ln 2)/2, ρ_β=2 and exp(2|β|)=2, so the second term becomes 16(√(65M)+16)/M+4ε_q. Score differences are ln 2 and 0 after a common shift, hence ideal attention gives matching weights 2 versus 1. This observation is not a floating-point softmax implementation proof.

The no-history family-aware baseline replaces t by 1/2 in all four entries. Its actual-terminal average TV is exactly 2u|t−1/2|≤u/2, with exact public u. If M, u, β and certified ε_q are known at design time, one can choose either this constant or the above attention construction by comparing their bounds, yielding the minimum of u/2 and the second term of (2), plus any separately charged implementation errors. This switching is public-parameter dependent, not a t-dependent oracle. A statistical prediction at terminal 00 has a different loss: the same constant rule has worst error 1/4, while (1) is a loose O(1/(u√M)) sufficient bound.

Same-history direct joint counting followed by add-one smoothing is another baseline and has an existing conditional rare-row upper with √[log(2(M+1)/(uδ))/(Mu)] plus explicit secondary terms. The existing R011 full-history result gives O(M^−1/2) unconditional rate for fixed binary alphabet over a much broader source class. Equation (2) is therefore not an asymptotic improvement over these baselines; it neither proves a lower bound for direct-softmax mechanisms nor a same-budget performance separation. Its substantive content, if correct, is an explicit family-aware actual-terminal model-R mechanism with a quantified dependent-prefix risk and a scalar-output precision sensitivity.

6. Complete stated resource boundary

  • Two paid first-block singleton-copy head instances and one second-block terminal head; two attention blocks, one-dimensional terminal score/key and scalar final value, with constant-size tokenwise Boolean/ReLU branch readout. Copies and features are retained in separate constant-width coordinates. If the backend has only standard attention-plus-FFN blocks, additional constant many FFN-only blocks may be needed for clamp and gating and must be counted.
  • Under a sparse structural-mask executor, O(N) active attention edges plus O(N) constant-width local work; per-token state storage O(N), with possible streaming alternatives requiring their own causal implementation. Dense masking can cost O(N²). Masks, positional addressing, coefficient storage, exponential and normalization operations, and the cost of encoding u and the constants are not free.
  • Weights contain inverse slope a_g⁻¹=O(1/u) for fixed β. ε_q is an assumed final head-coordinate error, not a derived bound on score, copying, value, exponential, normalization, accumulator or model-weight rounding. Exact terminal bits and readout are assumed. Separate weight precision, overflow and exact-complement/output-simplex errors remain unproved.
  • There is no LayerNorm, no trainability claim, no representation of unknown u, no resource lower bound, and no proof of equivalence to ordinary fixed-score full-causal softmax without masks. A public-family streaming estimator using the same history, and the t₀=1/2 no-history rule, are mandatory comparators.

7. Sources and provisional status

The finite family and reversed common-atom facts come from the v10 rare-family report and its independent source review. D’Angelo et al. already analyze finite-score equality/partial-match attention in a disentangled model; Rajaraman et al. and Izzo et al. are direct empirical-count/soft-matching neighbors. The distinct population-ratio argument and bound are newly derived here, but no literature-wide novelty, formal verification or independent acceptance is claimed. Existing v10 R011/BCJ/FOT results and the companion source-bound novelty assessment must accompany any interpretation. An independent mathematical review, source-subsumption audit and explicit numerical design are still required.
