# Bounded primary-source citation audit

Audit date: 3 November 2026. All descriptions below are scoped reads, not full-paper verification or priority certification. No source PDFs or extracted full texts are included in this deliverable. Existing prior-art reports and their read-scope JSON were read in full; their broader source coverage is not relabeled as this reviewer's own full external-paper reading.

## Newly added bibliography identities and actual passage reads

- **Maity and Mitra (2025)**: Sreejeet Maity and Aritra Mitra, *Adversarially-Robust TD Learning with Markovian Data: Finite-Time Rates and Fundamental Limits*, AISTATS 2025, PMLR 258, 2431–2439. [Primary proceedings record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/maity25a.html). Read the primary Section 4 model/Theorem 2 passage from the cached official paper. It concerns robust stationary-mean estimation with mixing/corruption inputs, not this actual-terminal same-map raw-window experiment. The manuscript's limited comparison is supported. No full TD-proof review is claimed.

- **Weiss, Goldberg, and Yahav (2021)**: Gail Weiss, Yoav Goldberg, and Eran Yahav, *Thinking Like Transformers*, ICML 2021, PMLR 139, 11080–11090. [Primary record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/weiss21a.html). Read primary Sections 2–3 passages on selected aggregation, indices, and indicator averages. These support acknowledging established selection and averaging constructions, not a finite machine-resource theorem for the present program.

- **Merrill and Sabharwal (2023)**: William Merrill and Ashish Sabharwal, *A Logic for Expressing Log-Precision Transformers*, NeurIPS 36 (2023). [Primary paper](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/a48e5877c7bf86a513950ab23b360498-Paper-Conference.pdf). Read Section 3 Proposition 1 and its immediate setup/proof passage. Its individual attention-probability precision issue is distinct from summing exactly and rounding the aggregate. The manuscript does not claim a stronger contradiction or architecture-independent precision consequence.
