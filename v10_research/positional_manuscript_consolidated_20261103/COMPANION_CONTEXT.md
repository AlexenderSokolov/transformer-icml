# Companion work and excluded domains

The article deliberately fixes the supplied certificate at 1/M for its main observation theorem. It does not reopen the following previously settled project questions.

## Joint blindness and a supplied certificate are different experiments

[AB001](https://github.com/AlexenderSokolov/transformer-icml/blob/670b7fc34ed864716124e9a56876d1baaaed6503/v10_research/binary_budget_adaptation/AB001_joint_blindness_obstruction.md) rules out one full-history predictor blind to both the edit budget and the certificate attaining the entire certificate-dependent benchmark simultaneously. Its clean alternatives and attacked null must not be confused with a lower comparison in which the same certificate is supplied to all experiments.

[AF001](https://github.com/AlexenderSokolov/transformer-icml/blob/670b7fc34ed864716124e9a56876d1baaaed6503/v10_research/binary_full_history_domain/AF001_full_history_domain_classification.md) instead supplies the actual certificate to the predictor. For an arbitrary public domain E of (M,beta), its criterion is boundedness of min(M² beta, 1/(M beta)) on positive beta, with value zero at beta=0. Equivalently, E lies in a fixed union beta<=B/M² or beta>=1/(BM). The selector may use the actual supplied beta. The accepted all-start extension includes unsupported initial states. Neither question is marked open here.

The recent certificate-free Theta(log M) comparison relative to max(M^-1/2,k/M) is an inherited corollary, not an additional manuscript theorem or an assertion about the unrestricted known-budget minimax ratio. It is omitted from the article to retain its focus.
