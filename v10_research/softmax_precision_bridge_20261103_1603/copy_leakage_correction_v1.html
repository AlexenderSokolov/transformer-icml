Copy-leakage wording correction

This correction applies to the immutable precision bridge with SHA-256 67d431c334c72c562b4f6427961c149a5ffe7f3bc0b2c970565358178d2fc64a. Its §3 says there are at most N−1 competing keys and then uses an equality with N−1. With exactly n competing keys, all of score zero, and a desired key of score A, the wrong mass is exactly

n/(eA+n).

If only n≤N−1 is known, that mass is at most (N−1)/(eA+N−1)≤(N−1)e−A. This corrects the equality wording and leaves the stated upper bound and all other conclusions unchanged. This statement assumes the competitors have equal score zero; it is not a general attention lower bound.
