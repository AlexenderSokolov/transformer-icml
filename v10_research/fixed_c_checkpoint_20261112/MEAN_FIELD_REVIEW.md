# Independent mathematical review: source-average theorem

12 November 2026. Internal independent review, not formal verification or external peer review.

The published theorem's A/B/C approximation and risk bounds pass review under their explicit stationary-source, token-local initialization, full-prefix access, common-domain and Lipschitz-envelope assumptions. Its corrected depth corollary explicitly requires a specified nonzero source parameter or worst-source risk: theta=0 alone admits the zero-risk constant predictor 1/2.

The covariance estimate is dimension-free and permits arbitrary deterministic position dependence. Query-tag dependence is paid by summing the two unconditional branch errors; no conditional independence is asserted. Averaged unnormalized sketches, the quotient inequality, evolving-bank stability, nonexpansive reference projection, and inverse-square-root prefix recursion yield the displayed constants.

For free endpoint disclosure, each artificial fixed-tag run uses the same unconditional source law. Restriction to its matching event costs four; equal endpoint probabilities across the two sources give the stated risk sum. The artificial-tag domain/projection condition is essential. For randomized computation preserve the conditional two-source R_0+R_h inequality before averaging seeds; do not interchange expectation and maximum.

The final head must be Lipschitz on the reference-state domain as well as the actual trajectories. Perturbation recurrences require a coupling that remains in their common domain, or an explicit tube/global-bound argument. A pathwise perturbation bound need not pay a conditional factor four, although that conservative factor is safe. For U=0 or Lout=0 treat impossibility of the specified small-risk target directly instead of dividing by zero in an inverted depth formula.

No source-initialization generalization, finite-bit information contraction, deep-network risk frontier, aggregate depth-independent Frobenius-budget theorem, or training conclusion follows. Independent finite diagnostics elsewhere informed component reviews, but this checkpoint does not claim to reproduce all of them.
