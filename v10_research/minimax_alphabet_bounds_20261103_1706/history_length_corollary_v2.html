History-length corollary, v2

This version corrects the ε=0 boundary in the preserved v1.

This immutable corollary assumes the bounds in the companion statistical note, SHA-256 87b8129d99c89f8909a77fa9d711df70dfbe8a64bd107ccda1dc7fb90e8cb788. It is about statistical samples, not network depth, heads, computational cost or numerical precision.

For any A≥2, N≥2 and target worst-source expected actual-terminal TV risk 0<ε<1/288, the full-support stationary uniformly-mixing witness family in the companion implies the necessary condition

N ≥ A³/(288² ε²).

This follows from R_N≥(1/288)min{1,√(A³/N)}; the restriction on ε forces the square-root branch. It applies a fortiori to the larger class with arbitrary initial pair law. The source producing the worst error may depend on N, A and the estimator. At ε=0, the positive lower bound rules out achieving zero worst-source risk for every finite N.

On the stationary subclass whose reverse-mixing-sum bound is at most B, the companion’s add-one upper gives the sufficient condition M=N−2≥(50+120 max{1,B})² A³/ε². When that class contains the witness family, for example if B≥7 with the companion’s chosen mixing bounds, these are matching orders in A³/ε² with constants depending on B. This sufficiency is not asserted for arbitrary initial laws or unbounded mixing.

For fixed A=2 the iid two-point argument alone gives the necessary N≥[(1−√(2/15))/(8ε)]². These are classical-style minimax consequences and do not establish literature priority.
