# One-state minority erasure: overlap and finite testing

This small publication batch contains four retained research files: the finite-overlap candidate and its analytical review, and the finite-testing addendum and its analytical review. It is not the complete v10 research archive.

The finite-testing consequence assumes a predeclared public, realizable finite candidate family with positive transitions, a common bounded relative transition-log ratio, a common minority direction and the same public one-state strict one-edit channel. It does not require an additional rho endpoint-mass assumption.

These results describe a scoped overlap bound and finite testing construction. They do not establish a uniformly estimable unknown-source minimax predictor for the full continuous source class, a learned attention mechanism, a successful training experiment or a general Transformer theorem. Analytical review is not formal proof verification or external peer review. No research program was run to produce this publication batch.

`SOURCE_MAP.json` records each retained source's byte count and SHA-256 and the corresponding public-file identity. Historical proof/review hashes identify their original versions. Missing historical experiments, scripts or per-case captures elsewhere in v10 are not recovered by this publication. Other v10 proof, code, raw-data, failure and version archives remain separate batches.
