Yang, Strobl, Chiang and Angluin, “Simulating Hard Attention Using Soft Attention”, Lemma 7, bound softmax localization error for the quadratic score γ(2tj−j²) by (3/2)e−γ, independent of the sequence length. Their Theorem 13(b) uses fixed temperature τ=1, future-masked attention and positional features including (−1)i and i² to simulate a previous-position Boolean operator without parameters depending on total length; §4.1 explicitly discusses n-grams. This is a close primary precedent for the quadratic-position and attention-then-snap mechanisms, not just a generic observation that position helps. Their Theorem 26 gives a separate general hard-attention simulation using a length-dependent score-gap/temperature schedule.

The cited passages do not themselves claim this particular same-weight pre-LN insertion result for an unknown stochastic second-order source, same-history actual-terminal TV risk, fixed normalizing ε and ordinary vocabulary softmax. This bounded comparison is a difference of stated assumptions and target, not a novelty or priority clearance. Read the primary sources and the targeted prior-art audit together.
