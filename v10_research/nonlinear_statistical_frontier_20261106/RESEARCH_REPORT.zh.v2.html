必须区分两种任务：预测真实下一符号，只关心统计风险；模拟某个网络的完整输出，连其中无益的扰动也要逼近。后者可能严格更难，不能未经证明就把模拟难度当成统计必要资源。

三 核心结果：真实 IID 历史上的二次奇偶硬函数

令 N 为 4 的倍数，T=N+1，P 为不小于 T 的最小二次幂。原始位为 xi∈{0,1}，合成位为 0，Si 为前缀计数。定义：

U=Σi=1T C(Si,2)，F=U mod 2=Σk 为奇数xkΣj<kxj mod 2。

这里包含合成终端的自项。所有 x 都是实际公平 IID 来源的样本，没有把稀有编码词冒充平均来源。沿既有 AA/BB 所有权划分，F 的双方交叉矩阵秩为 N/2−1；双方各自的局部二次项只改变通信矩阵的行列符号。初等字符正交性与矩形相关界给出线性通信下界，允许任意交互和随机协议。

完整有界实现

第一次标量均匀读取给出 Si/i；以公开 i/P 做付费近似乘法，得到 Si/P，再形成平方减线性项。第二次标量均匀读取后乘以公开二进制有理数 T/P，得到 U/P³ 的高精度近似。

3log₂P 次斜率有界的 tent 折叠，在整数网格上给出 U 的奇偶位。上游误差控制为 O(P−3)，最终连续平台把结果恢复为精确逻辑位；没有免费 modulo、不连续比较或巨大单层增益。所有步骤固定宽度、总深度 O(log N)，系数长度 O(log N)。物理 PreLN 不声称逐位精确，使用完整输出的统一近似保证。

四 低风险却难以源平均模拟

把上述硬位接到旧的常宽后缀估计器 qb，b≈log N。设 ρ 为与 b−1/2 同量级的公开二次幂，在第一个已付费、有固定下界的 logit 上加入 ρ(2F−1)。其余统计程序不变。编译的是一个完整逻辑程序，不直接拼接不同物理网络，避免忽略完整特征 LN 对所有寄存器的耦合。

κcρ/2 ≤ RN ≤ 200ρ，因而 RN=Θc((log N)−1/2)→0。

上界来自旧后缀风险加小幅扰动。下界只是这个故意加入扰动的预测器自己的风险：条件于末端后缀，剩余 IID 前缀中的奇偶位仍接近平衡，两个输出分支保持 Θc(ρ) 间隔。它不是所有预测器的风险下界。

对任何返回归一化二元预测的随机交互模拟器，如果

E历史，协议随机数 TV(Q̂,Qphys) ≤ κcρ/100，

则其最坏情形通信位数 C 必须满足

C+b ≥ (N/2−1)/2−2，故 C=Ω(N)。

归约明确支付额外至多 b 个后缀位，使接收方计算同一逻辑基线，而不是假设基线统计误差可以忽略。随后按两个扰动分支的中点判别 F。TV 必须放在协议随机数期望内；不能换成随机输出平均后再取 TV，也不是无条件输出分布间的误差。

主构造使用三次有用标量读取，宽度固定，深度 Oc(log N)。两次奇偶读取的输出作用随 ρ 缩小，未声称有固定非消失影响。可合并第一次原始均值与后缀代码读取，得到两次调用、值维度分别为 (2,1) 的版本，但不能称为“两次标量读取”。

还可把扰动放在指定的逆多项式精度尺度；此时旧基线对数精度与物理编译精度必须分开选择，避免串行对数计算偷偷变成多项式深度。只有 ρ≈b−1/2 的主选择证明实际风险与扰动同阶。

进一步，若一个完整输出替身可用低于上述阈值的通信，在误差 ε 下模拟，而其与该网络的公平 IID 平均残差为 Δ，那么必有 ε+Δ>κcρ/100。因此当 ε 足够小时，不能保证 Δ=o(ρ)。这直接说明：先前闭合模型的加权行误差证书不能仅凭预测一致性就获得所需的小残差；闭合转移定理本身没有被推翻。

确切含义：一致预测并不自动保证完整输出有廉价源平均模拟。删掉这个人为扰动，就恢复旧的廉价预测器。因此反例打破的是过强的通用模拟路线，不是证明统计预测本身需要线性通信，也没有改善旧估计率。

五 宽模型的真实规范化闭合

旧逻辑并行程序早已有宽度 O(N)、两次均匀读取、深度 O(log N) 的全历史计数估计器，风险 O(N−1/2)。本轮补齐其真实 PreLN／普通 softmax 接口，不重新主张该统计率。

固定宽度编译中的非零 √d 范数填充不能直接用于增长宽度，因为会违反完整状态范数预算。新证明只添加计入宽度的零坐标。对配对零均值状态 h=2−Mu，通过 M 个 h←h+LN1(h) 的付费恢复步骤，再做最终增益为 2 的 LN 和半投影，完整误差至多：

‖u−zref‖ + 7‖u‖³/(12d)。

上述 LN₁ 是便于分析的等价写法；物理块使用增益为 2 的 LN，并将消费它的支路减半，因此保持全模型增益为 2。误差不是 M/d，因为早期小振幅误差形成几何级数。取 d≥N，并使用整体范数受控的并行对数折线，实现实际 Oc(N−1/2) 风险。所有非终端概率坐标先截断到 [a,1]，不误用它们本来就是概率向量的假设；所有复制、清零先计入准备程序，再选小振幅。

于是 R²D→0，排除忽略增长宽度和值维度的普遍 R²D≥常数主张。但该构造购买了 O(N) 宽度、O(N²) 分布式状态和 O(N³log N) 保守稠密工作量，并不反驳显式固定宽度的定理，也没有证明最优宽度。

## 1. Claim and deliberately limited consequence

Fix c in (0,1/2) and the existing stationary interior binary second-order source class P_c, which includes the fair IID source. Use the existing raw binary onehot input, public normalized positions, synthetic zero-symbol terminal, inclusive full-prefix causal mask, paid local affine/ReLU stages, and positive-epsilon all-feature PreLN/ordinary-vocabulary-softmax contract. All parameters are source independent.

For N divisible by four and sufficiently large, choose a power-of-two b with 4<=b<=N/2. There is a constant-width, three-useful-scalar-head family with depth O_c(log N+b), fixed complete-state/full-matrix/full-bias/primitive-gain bounds, and terminal worst-source expected TV risk R_N such that

    kappa_c rho/2 <= R_N <= 200 rho,
    rho = 2^(-ceil((log2 b)/2)).                         (1)

The lower inequality holds already on fair IID input. Under the OLD AA/BB ownership pattern, every randomized interactive terminal simulator with worst-case communication C and

    E_(fair IID input, coins) TV(Qhat,Q_phys)
        <= kappa_c rho/100                              (2)

must satisfy

    C+b >= (N/2-1)/2 - 2.                              (3)

Here kappa_c>0 is explicit in Section 6. Taking b=Theta(log N) gives fixed width, D=O_c(log N), exactly three useful scalar heads, R_N=Theta_c((log N)^(-1/2))->0, per-coefficient description O_c(log N), untied network-parameter description O_c(log^2 N), yet C=Omega(N). In particular (3) holds if the error guarantee is alpha_c R_N with alpha_c=kappa_c/20000, by the upper bound in (1).

The result obstructs generic polylogarithmic communication simulation of the ENTIRE low-risk predictor at sufficiently small risk-proportional error. It is NOT a statistical risk lower, a superior estimator, or a claim that useful prediction intrinsically needs linear communication. The hard component is an artificial vanishing nuisance in the final logits. Omitting it recovers the already-known easily simulated suffix estimator. No assertion is made about simulators at a larger constant multiple of risk, or about statistical lower-bound methods allowed to discard nuisance components.

## 2. An integer quadratic statistic on the actual IID sample

Write N=2m, T=N+1, P=2^p=2^ceil(log2 T), and K=P^3. Let raw bits x_1,...,x_N be zero or one and x_T=0. Define

    S_i=sum_(j<=i) x_j,
    U=sum_(i=1)^T binom(S_i,2).

The synthetic self-row S_T=S_N is INCLUDED. The first scalar zero-score full-prefix read gives r_i=S_i/i. Public input a_i=i/P lies in [0,1]. Local multiplication recovers z_i=a_i r_i=S_i/P up to a controlled approximation. The second scalar read averages the local values

    f(z_i)=(z_i^2-z_i/P)/2=binom(S_i,2)/P^2.

Its ideal terminal value is o_T=U/(P^2 T). Multiplication by the public DYADIC T/P, which is at most one, gives U/K. No integer-valued U register and no growing amplification matrix are used. Since 0<=U<=T N(N-1)/2<K/2, this normalized input is in [0,1/2].

Counting how often a pair enters the prefix gives, over the field F_2,

    F=U mod 2
     =sum_(1<=j<k<=N) (T-k+1)x_j x_k mod 2
     =sum_(odd k) x_k sum_(j<k)x_j mod 2.               (4)

The final equality uses N even: T-k+1=N+2-k has parity k. All bits in (4) are ordinary IID sample bits, with no communication-specific encoding or exceptional event.

## 3. Bounded ReLU realization with exact logical parity

### 3.1 A paid approximate product

On [0,1] let

    g(t)=2t-4 ReLU(t-1/2),
    s_L(t)=t-sum_(j=1)^L 4^(-j) g^j(t).

This is the standard dyadic square interpolant: s_L(0)=0 and sup|s_L(t)-t^2|<=4^(-L)/4. It has constant width, O(L) bounded affine/ReLU stages, bounded complete intermediate states, and dyadic coefficients of O(L) bits. Running two copies serially or in fixed parallel banks does not change these orders.

Choose the least L for which nu=4^(-L)/4<=1/(128 K), so L=O(log N). For a,r in [0,1], use

    M_L(a,r)=clip(s_L((a+r)/2)-s_L(|a-r|/2),0,1).

The exact difference of squares is ar, so |M_L(a,r)-ar|<=2nu. The absolute value is an ordinary fixed ReLU circuit. Importantly M_L(a,0)=0 EXACTLY, because both square inputs then coincide. Let zhat_i=M_L(a_i,r_i).

Set the actual second-read value to

    vhat_i=clip((s_L(zhat_i)-zhat_i/P)/2,0,1).

On the exact grid z_i=S_i/P, f(z_i)>=0 and f(z_i)<=1/2. Therefore clipping cannot increase its error. The derivative bound |f'|<=3/2 on [0,1] gives

    |vhat_i-f(z_i)| <= (3/2)(2nu)+nu/2 <4nu.          (5)

The value is exactly zero when the first read is ablated to zero. The second read is an ordinary scalar uniform average into a fresh bank, so its terminal error is at most 4nu. Multiplication by T/P does not enlarge that error.

### 3.2 Paid bounded tent folding and the terminal gate

At every row the actual second-read result is in [0,1]. Multiply it by T/P, then gate with the exact synthetic marker e_i through ReLU(y_i+e_i-1). This preserves the terminal value and makes every nonterminal decoder input exactly zero. All subsequent parity states remain in [0,1]. The marker is computed from protected raw-onehot shadows, as in the existing ordered-suffix construction.

For every integer 0<=u<=K, the elementary tent identity is

    g^(log2 K)(u/K) = u mod 2.                         (6)

For completeness, g(u/K)=min(u,K-u)/(K/2). Thus the first fold replaces u by min(u,K-u) and K by K/2; because K is even, numerator parity is preserved. Repeat until denominator one. The value is then zero or one with the asserted parity. Equivalently, the piecewise-linear graph of the L-fold tent alternates zero and one on the 2^-L grid.

Each fold has Lipschitz constant two and maps [0,1] into itself. Its log2 K=3p folds amplify the input error by at most K, giving final error

    4K nu <=1/32.                                    (7)

Apply the fixed plateau H(t)=clip(2t-1/2,0,1). It equals zero for t<=1/4 and one for t>=3/4. Hence the final LOGICAL output is EXACTLY F on EVERY Boolean history. Every nonterminal row outputs zero. No discontinuous modulo primitive, exponential-width lookup, or hidden unbounded coefficient occurs: the modulo description is justified by the paid continuous tent-and-plateau circuit.

The complete two-read logical program has fixed width and O(log N) stages. With either read individually ablated it returns F=0. Some histories have F=1, for example x_2=x_3=1 and all other bits zero. Thus both reads are useful in this program. Exact logical parity does not assert exact physical parity after normalization; Section 8 compares the full physical output uniformly instead.

## 4. Distributional communication hardness, including the old AA/BB split

### 4.1 Odd/even form and rank

If Alice owns odd positions a_q=x_(2q-1) and Bob owns even positions b_r=x_(2r), then

    F(a,b)=h(a)+sum_(r<q) a_q b_r mod 2,
    h(a)=sum_(p<q) a_p a_q.

The m-by-m strict lower triangular cross matrix has rank m-1: rows 2,...,m and columns 1,...,m-1 contain an invertible triangular minor. This is already a fair-IID product input law, rather than a special encoded distribution.

### 4.2 Preserve the AA/BB ownership contract

No change of the old ownership split is needed. Put N=4n. Alice owns positions 4r-3 and 4r-2; Bob owns 4s-1 and 4s. Ordering each party's odd coordinates before its even coordinates, the cross matrix in (4) is

    M = [ J  L ]
        [ U  0 ],

where J is the all-ones n-by-n matrix, L_(r,s)=1{r>s}, and U_(r,s)=1{r<=s}. The block U is invertible and L has rank n-1. Reordering row blocks gives a block lower triangular matrix with diagonal blocks U and L, so

    rank(M)=2n-1=N/2-1.                               (8)

Alice-only and Bob-only quadratic terms contribute row and column signs to the communication matrix and do not change its spectral norm. This observation is why the proof below allows both local quadratic phases.

### 4.3 Elementary rectangle bound

More generally, let both parties hold d independent uniform bits and

    C>=r/2-2.                                        (10)

                              <= N^(-1/2)/128.         (6)

For two logits, ordinary softmax followed by TV is Lipschitz with respect to their infinity norm, with constant at most 1. Therefore Q_ref=softmax(ell) has

    TV(Q_ref,p_actual) <= TV(B,p_actual)+C'_c N^(-1/2). (7)

### 3.3 The complete reference must precede amplitude selection

Append copies of ell into a dedicated output bank and clear EVERY other reference register, including old tokens, positions, anchor, histograms, selector banks, hinge banks and scratch. Pair each logical coordinate with its negative. The complete final reference vector is

    z_ref=(ell_0,-ell_0,ell_1,-ell_1,0,...,0).

Clearing the anchor is the last operation; no later reference affine bias requires it. Until then it is exactly preserved. All copies/clears are paid bounded linear/ReLU overwrites. The complete prepared logical program, including both reads, logs and all clears, has length

    m_ref=O_c(log N)

and uniformly bounded complete per-row norm. Also consider its two single-read ablations, each replacing just that read's output by zero and leaving every other parameter and stage unchanged. As proved in Section 5.1, both ablated references have legal all-row count states and return the default uniform output. Choose a common fixed tube radius Z and fixed primitive max-row Lipschitz constant L>=2 for all THREE complete prepared programs before selecting the small amplitude. Adding paired inert zero coordinates makes the actual even model width d satisfy

    256(Z+1)^3 N<=d<=C_c N,

without changing any nonzero vector's norm. These coordinates are counted as width and in dense resources. No nonzero norm-padding coordinate proportional to sqrt(d) is used.

## 4. Small-amplitude PreLN compilation, with full-vector error

Use the paired physical representation h=eta z, where eta=2^(-M). The bounded initial symbol/position embedding supplies the scaled paired raw shadows and anchor. Pairing is maintained by all output branches, including attention; hence the complete vector has exactly zero coordinate mean throughout. On this invariant subspace, epsilon-one gain-one LayerNorm is

    LN_1(eta z)=eta s(z)z,
    s(z)=(1+eta^2||z||^2/d)^(-1/2).

For x>=0, 0<=1-(1+x)^(-1/2)<=x/2. Therefore on the fixed tube

    |1-s(z)| <= eta^2 Z^2/(2d).                        (8)

For a homogeneous residual branch implementing G=F-I, gain-one PreLN gives exactly

    z_new=z+s(z)(F(z)-z).

Its deviation from the desired overwrite F(z) is at most C eta^2/d, using uniform complete-state and branch bounds. This identity does not assume that perturbed counts, selectors or gates retain their exact logical lattice values. Their physical errors are propagated through the continuous affine/ReLU primitives.

For either useful uniform read, the reference update has the form

    z'_t=z_t+B_0 (1/t) sum_(i<=t) A_0 z_i.

The physical normalized update gives instead

    z'_t=z_t+B_0 (1/t) sum_(i<=t) A_0 s(z_i) z_i.

Their local max-row difference is at most

    ||B_0||||A_0|| eta^2 Z^3/(2d),                     (9)

independent of N and of growing value width. Zero Q and K ensure genuinely ordinary softmax weights equal 1/t. The complete source-row normalization factor is retained in (9), not replaced by a token-local shortcut.

A complete standard block has zero dummy attention for a local operation, or zero FFN for an attention operation. A fixed number of standard blocks implements each reference primitive. With e_l the maximum over rows of complete logical-vector error, there are common fixed constants C,L, valid for the original and both ablated programs, with

    e_l<=L e_(l-1)+C eta^2/d,
    e_(m_ref)<=C' eta^2 L^(m_ref)/d.                   (10)

The constants depend on c and the fixed primitive types, not on width or N. Take xi to be the largest dyadic value at most N^(-1/2)/256, and choose the smallest M>=1 for which the coarse sufficient bound C' 2^(-2M)L^(m_ref)<=xi and the common tube constraints hold. Use this SAME eta=2^(-M), width and restoration schedule for all three programs. Because m_ref=O_c(log N),

    M=O_c(log N).

Tube induction is valid because the error stays below a fixed margin about uniformly bounded reference states. Crucially (10) is the error AFTER every output copy and scratch clear. Write the actual final physical vector as

    h_0=2^(-M) u,  ||u-z_ref||<=xi,  ||u||<=Z+xi.     (11)

Approximate scratch clearing causes no gap: its complete-vector residual is included in xi and will be restored along with the output bank.

Convert every interior gain-one LayerNorm to gain two and halve its consuming FFN first matrix or attention value matrix. Q/K remain zero. This exact reparameterization leaves the trajectory unchanged, and all matrices stay uniformly bounded. All actual LayerNorms therefore use the same epsilon=1, gain=2 and shift=0.

## 5. Dimension-diluted amplitude restoration and conventional final output

This is the new replacement for the fixed-width nonzero norm-padding route. Starting from (11), apply M paid local residual steps

    h_(k+1)=h_k+h_k/sqrt(1+||h_k||^2/d), k=0,...,M-1. (12)

Each step is an actual gain-two PreLN FFN residual block whose branch consumes one half of the LayerNorm output. A two-layer signed-ReLU identity implements that linear branch with bounded operator norms. It acts on the complete paired vector, keeps the mean exactly zero, and uses no attention. Inert zeros remain zero.

Let y_k=2^(k-M)u be ideal doubling, and delta_k=||y_k-h_k||. From (12), ||h_k||<=||y_k||=2^(k-M)||u||. Applying (8) at the physical amplitude and comparing to ideal doubling gives

    delta_(k+1)<=2 delta_k+||h_k||^3/(2d), delta_0=0.

Hence the geometrically weighted accumulated error is

    delta_M
      <= sum_(k=0)^(M-1) 2^(M-1-k) 2^(3k-3M)||u||^3/(2d)
       = ||u||^3(1-4^(-M))/(12d)
       <= ||u||^3/(12d).                               (13)

There is NO M/d loss. Earlier local errors are smaller by the cube of their smaller amplitude; their later amplification leaves a convergent geometric sum. All physical states in (12) have norm at most ||u||, uniformly in M,N,d.

Let P_out project to the positive ell_0,ell_1 coordinates. Apply the mandatory final gain-two LayerNorm and the affine vocabulary head W=(1/2)P_out, bias zero. Actual logits are

    ell_phys=P_out h_M/sqrt(1+||h_M||^2/d).

Since ||P_out||=1, (11), (13), and the same scalar inequality imply

    ||ell_phys-ell||_2
      <= xi + ||u||^3/(12d)+||u||^3/(2d)
      <= xi + 7(Z+xi)^3/(12d).                         (14)

With the chosen d and xi, (14) is at most N^(-1/2)/128. An ordinary vocabulary softmax produces Q_N. Its TV error from Q_ref is at most the same quantity. Together with (6), each original/ablated physical output is within N^(-1/2)/64<1/32 in TV of its own ideal floored reference. Combining (2), (7), and (14) proves (1).

The final output is not projection onto a presumed normalized probability bank. It includes the actual final LayerNorm, bounded affine logit map, and ordinary vocabulary softmax. No singular division, unbounded one-shot 1/eta amplification, nonzero sqrt(d) padding, or custom readout is used.

### 5.1 Both useful reads survive actual PreLN compilation

## 6. Parameters, sparse versus dense resources, and execution limitations

For fixed c, d=O(N), D=O(log N), one wide useful head followed by one histogram head, and O(log N) restoration steps are all paid. The attention count is two useful invocations, not two total sequential layers.

The logical reference's rational parameters have O(log N)-bit binary descriptions; the log coefficients and eta are dyadics with O_c(log N)-bit descriptions. Pairing, signed identity, and gain-halving only add fixed coefficients. Per-coefficient description is therefore O_c(log N). Rational descriptions are exact-real parameters, not floating-point guarantees.

If an all-dyadic parameter version is required, it also exists at the same order. Round the core matrices and biases, preserving their zero support, to coordinate accuracy delta_N=N^(-A) for a sufficiently large fixed c-dependent A. A d-by-d matrix's operator perturbation is at most d delta_N, and its full bias perturbation at most sqrt(d)delta_N. The O(log N)-stage reference has only polynomial total sensitivity L^(O(log N)). Thus choosing A large enough makes its complete output-vector error O(N^(-1/2)), while changing primitive norms by o(1). Anchor entries and pair antisymmetry can be retained exactly. Include this perturbation error in xi, or allocate separate fixed fractions of the same error budget. This is a mathematical approximation argument with O_c(log N) coefficient bits, not an executed compiler or an arithmetic-rounding theorem.

Conservative dense accounting, including zero entries in untied matrices:

- O(N^2 log N) parameter coefficients and O_c(N^2 log^2 N) parameter-description bits; explicitly storing every LayerNorm gain/shift also costs O(dD)=O(N log N) coefficients, dominated by this bound;
- O(N^2) rolling all-row real activation slots, plus O(N^2) attention-score slots if matrices are materialized;
- O(N^3 log N) dense projection/FFN scalar work;
- O(N^3) work for the two useful full-width attention products, or O(N^3 log N) if every dummy attention slot is also densely evaluated;
- O(N log N) bits for public positions, and O(N^2) mask bits if a full causal mask is explicitly stored.

For a declared sparse representation the construction's diagonal operations, bounded-fanout gates, trees, anchor columns and signed identity branches use only O(N log N) nonzero coefficients, hence O_c(N log^2 N) stored coefficient bits. Local sparse work is O(N^2 log N). The naive wide attention products still cost O(N^3); exploiting their exactly uniform prefix structure permits prefix-sum evaluation in O(N^2) arithmetic instead. These are alternative execution conventions, not interchangeable counts. The theorem uses neither sparse savings nor free dummy attention to hide its dense cost.

Coefficient generation, LN square roots, and the exact-real attention/softmax arithmetic have not been numerically executed or given a finite-word stability certificate. There is no training, learned realization, tied-weight, mandatory PostLN, or width-optimality claim.

    R_N^2 D_N = O_c((log N)/N) -> 0.                  (15)

## 1. Contract and the head-count distinction

There are N raw-symbol rows and a synthetic row T=N+1 initialized from public metadata only. Each observed initial row depends on its own symbol and public metadata. Replacing one symbol changes that row's complete initial vector by at most Delta. No uncharged local pair feature, terminal-symbol side port, or other-row readout is supplied. Every useful attention mask is the full self-inclusive prefix. Parameters can depend on N and even on the source, but are fixed during a prediction. All row-local operations are uniformly Lipschitz, with their number charged to depth. This includes nonlinear FFNs and full-feature positive-epsilon LayerNorm.

For one standard multihead block let z=LN(h), let Q(z), K(z), V(z) be the FULL concatenations of all head query/key/value vectors, and let W_O act on the full concatenated attention output. Assume, uniformly over all relevant rows and blocks,

    ||Q(z)|| <= Q0, ||K(z)|| <= K0, ||V(z)|| <= V0,
    Lip(Q o LN) <= LQ, Lip(K o LN) <= LK,
    Lip(V o LN) <= LV, ||W_O|| <= O.

Every head logit row has range at most B; put C=exp(B). Logits are the standard scaled dot products plus public position biases. A head's scale tau_h=1/sqrt(d_h) is at most one. Public biases do not change when the history changes. Set SQ=K0 LQ and SK=Q0 LK. All the displayed constants are frozen independently of N, depth, width, and the number H of heads.

These assumptions follow from frozen operator and FULL bias norms for the CONCATENATED Q/K/V maps, frozen complete hidden norms, and a positive fixed LN epsilon with bounded gain/shift. They do NOT follow merely from separate bounds on each head. Bounding every displayed head matrix by M permits the concatenated operator norm to grow as sqrt(H). A growing-H claim must state which convention is frozen.

For LN(h)=diag(gamma) P h / sqrt(epsilon+||P h||^2/d)+beta, with P the orthogonal mean-centering projection, its global Euclidean Lipschitz constant is at most ||gamma||_infinity/sqrt(epsilon). This follows by differentiating the radial map after P: its tangential eigenvalue is at most 1/sqrt(epsilon), and its radial eigenvalue is smaller. It is dimension independent. For epsilon=1 and scalar gain two the bound is two.

## 2. Full-concatenation sensitivity, with no H factor

Compare two reachable histories, and write delta_i=||h_i-h'_i||. Let s_i=(s_hi)_h be the vector of all head scores to one source position i. By splitting the bilinear score difference and applying Cauchy-Schwarz across head blocks,

    ||s_i-s'_i||_2 <= SQ delta_t + SK delta_i.              (A1)

For example, the query-change term is bounded by

    [sum_h ||q_ht-q'_ht||^2 ||k_hi||^2]^(1/2)
      <= K0 ||Q_t-Q'_t|| <= SQ delta_t.

No factor sqrt(H) enters. Independent per-head softmaxes are retained; they are not replaced by one softmax over head/position pairs.

Interpolate only the two score arrays: s(u)=(1-u)s+u s'. Each head score range remains <=B, so alpha_hi(u)<=C/t. Hold the primed values fixed during this interpolation, and write o_h(u)=sum_i alpha_hi(u) v'_hi. Each head value and o_h(u) have norm <=V0, because each block of a bounded full value vector has norm <=V0. The softmax derivative is exactly

    d o_h(u)/du = sum_i alpha_hi(u) (v'_hi-o_h(u)) (s'_hi-s_hi).

For a fixed source i, the Euclidean norm of the concatenated summand is at most

    (C/t) || ((v'_hi-o_h(u))(s'_hi-s_hi))_h ||_2
      <= (2CV0/t) ||s'_i-s_i||_2.

Minkowski and (A1), followed by integration, bound the score-change part of the complete concatenated output by

    2CV0 SQ delta_t + (2CV0 SK/t) sum_(i<=t) delta_i.

The value-change part is bounded by

    ||sum_i diag_h(alpha_hi)(v_i-v'_i)||_2
      <= (CLV/t) sum_(i<=t) delta_i.

Thus the full multihead attention output obeys

    ||o_t-o'_t||_2 <= 2CV0 SQ delta_t
                       + C(LV+2V0 SK)/t sum_(i<=t) delta_i.  (A2)

This proof uses endpoint value vectors and interpolation of logits only; it does not assume that a hidden-state interpolation is a reachable network state. It also includes attention-weight variation, not only value variation.

Let F bound the Lipschitz constant of the subsequent row-local residual FFN, including its LN. For a standard two-affine/ReLU FFN one valid choice is

    F = 1 + ||W_2|| ||W_1|| Lip(LN).

Including attention residual, output projection and FFN yields

    delta_t^(ell+1) <= a delta_t^ell
                         + (b/t) sum_(i<=t) delta_i^ell,   (A3)
    a = F(1+2OCV0 SQ),
    b = F O C(LV+2V0 SK).

These a,b are independent of H under the aggregate assumptions. More general row-local nonlinear primitives can replace the FFN provided their uniform Lipschitz constant is charged, and a fixed number of primitives per stage can be absorbed into fixed constants. An N-dependent deep circuit cannot be hidden in F.

If instead only each head has bounds V, V_L, S_Q, S_K, the same proof separately per head gives a valid weaker alternative

    a_H = F(1+2 O sqrt(H) C V S_Q),
    b_H = F O sqrt(H) C(V_L+2V S_K).

The historical sum-norm recurrence with H instead of sqrt(H) is also valid. Frozen H makes either constant uniform. If H grows and only per-head budgets are frozen, retain the H-dependence; no H-uniform logarithmic-depth conclusion has been established by these inequalities. Full norms of projected reachable states alone do not supply the missing aggregate derivative bound.

## 3. Conventional vocabulary softmax is a valid readout

    ||D softmax(z) v||_1

      <= sqrt(Var_p(v)) <= ||v||_2.

    TV(softmax(z),softmax(z')) <= (1/2)||z-z'||_2.

Replace only X_(N-1)=X_(T-2). Causality forces equality before T-2, and the synthetic row starts equal. Applying (A3) to the remaining three rows, let c_T=a+b/(T-2). The old nilpotent expansion gives the output-TV upper bound

## 1. What pure-square output equivalence cannot improve

      <= xi + ||u||^3/(12d)+||u||^3/(2d)
      <= xi + 7(Z+xi)^3/(12d).                         (14)

Thus d>=N and xi=O(N^(-1/2)) give O_c(N^(-1/2)) final logit error. An ordinary vocabulary softmax produces Q_N. Its TV error from Q_ref is bounded by a constant times (14). Combining (2), (7), and (14) proves (1).

The final output is not projection onto a presumed normalized probability bank. It includes the actual final LayerNorm, bounded affine logit map, and ordinary vocabulary softmax. No singular division, unbounded one-shot 1/eta amplification, nonzero sqrt(d) padding, or custom readout is used.

## 6. Parameters, sparse versus dense resources, and execution limitations

For fixed c, d=O(N), D=O(log N), one wide useful head followed by one histogram head, and O(log N) restoration steps are all paid. The attention count is two useful invocations, not two total sequential layers.

The logical reference's rational parameters have O(log N)-bit binary descriptions; the log coefficients and eta are dyadics with O_c(log N)-bit descriptions. Pairing, signed identity, and gain-halving only add fixed coefficients. Per-coefficient description is therefore O_c(log N). Rational descriptions are exact-real parameters, not floating-point guarantees.

If an all-dyadic parameter version is required, it also exists at the same order. Round the core matrices and biases, preserving their zero support, to coordinate accuracy delta_N=N^(-A) for a sufficiently large fixed c-dependent A. A d-by-d matrix's operator perturbation is at most d delta_N, and its full bias perturbation at most sqrt(d)delta_N. The O(log N)-stage reference has only polynomial total sensitivity L^(O(log N)). Thus choosing A large enough makes its complete output-vector error O(N^(-1/2)), while changing primitive norms by o(1). Anchor entries and pair antisymmetry can be retained exactly. Include this perturbation error in xi, or allocate separate fixed fractions of the same error budget. This is a mathematical approximation argument with O_c(log N) coefficient bits, not an executed compiler or an arithmetic-rounding theorem.

Conservative dense accounting, including zero entries in untied matrices:

- O(N^2 log N) parameter coefficients and O_c(N^2 log^2 N) parameter-description bits;
- O(N^2) rolling all-row real activation slots, plus O(N^2) attention-score slots if matrices are materialized;
- O(N^3 log N) dense projection/FFN scalar work;
- O(N^3) work for the two useful full-width attention products, or O(N^3 log N) if every dummy attention slot is also densely evaluated;
- O(N log N) bits for public positions, and O(N^2) mask bits if a full causal mask is explicitly stored.

For a declared sparse representation the construction's diagonal operations, bounded-fanout gates, trees, anchor columns and signed identity branches use only O(N log N) nonzero coefficients, hence O_c(N log^2 N) stored coefficient bits. Local sparse work is O(N^2 log N). The naive wide attention products still cost O(N^3); exploiting their exactly uniform prefix structure permits prefix-sum evaluation in O(N^2) arithmetic instead. These are alternative execution conventions, not interchangeable counts. The theorem uses neither sparse savings nor free dummy attention to hide its dense cost.

Coefficient generation, LN square roots, and the exact-real attention/softmax arithmetic have not been numerically executed or given a finite-word stability certificate. There is no training, learned realization, tied-weight, mandatory PostLN, or width-optimality claim.

## 7. Consequence for a proposed statistical depth frontier

Let R_N denote the worst-source risk in (1). Then

    R_N^2 D_N = O_c((log N)/N) -> 0.                  (15)

Consequently a purported lower bound R^2 D>=constant>0, asserted uniformly over arbitrary width under this same frozen positive-epsilon PreLN/ordinary-softmax full-norm contract, is false. Width and dense work cannot be omitted from such an unrestricted claim. More generally, (15) prevents this particular depth-only lower frontier from being rescued by the final normalization/output interface alone.

It does NOT contradict or improve the previous fixed-width suffix construction's depth-risk tradeoff. It does not establish the optimal width-depth-risk frontier, improve the inherited full-history statistical rate, or give a general low-risk simulation theorem for arbitrary evolving nonlinear row banks. The family trades O(N) width and much larger state/parameter/work resources for logarithmic depth. Its core computation is the already-existing parallel logical estimator; real PreLN introduces nonlinear radial factors, but this note does not claim that nonlinearity is statistically necessary or advantageous.

The old fixed-error logarithmic-depth sensitivity lower can also be applied after freezing the new primitive constants, since epsilon-one LayerNorm and bounded full-vector maps have dimension-independent Lipschitz bounds. This observation is not a new sharp constant theorem and does not prove width optimality. The essential new comparison here is the inclusion of the wide upper in the actual normalization/output contract.
