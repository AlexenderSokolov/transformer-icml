真实二阶预测中的有序因果记忆与终端模拟

本轮检查此前闭合公共控制和源平均残差路线，能否处理真实使用二阶上下文的因果预测器。结论分成两部分：位置一致的符号计数替代计算存在常数风险障碍；但另一类公开线性因果行更新可以精确折叠成有序位置加权摘要，并容纳一个完整的两读后缀预测程序及其条件性规范化实现。

没有获得新的估计率或一般多次注意力主定理。两读构造重现此前单读后缀估计器的 O(b−1/2) 风险与 O(log N+b) 深度，目的是核验“真正更新的非终端记忆”与可证明终端模拟的交集。所有结果为解析推导和独立文本审阅，未训练、未执行模型或科学数值复现。

一 共同问题契约

输入为 N 个二元原始符号和一个合成零符号终端行。未知源是平稳、齐次二阶 Markov 过程 Pc，全部转移概率位于 [c,1−c]，已知 c∈(0,1/2)。目标是同一观测历史实际末端上下文的下一符号分布，损失是期望总变差后对源取上确界。参数可依赖 N、后缀长度 b 与 c，但不能依赖未知转移核。

不提供裁剪后的后缀、真实末端符号对、三元组特征、转移计数或独立训练样本。全部位置选择、解码、计数、概率处理和归一化都计入付费深度。注意力为全前缀含自身的因果 softmax。正面构造的两个有效头均为标量值、零查询与零键，因此注意力得分恒为零。

统计测试下界沿用已有二元单向 AA/BB 输入划分结果，要求 N≥3；具体两读构造取 N≥6、4≤b≤N−2 且 b 是二的幂。精确实数数学语义不等同于有限浮点可运行性。

二 计数型替代计算存在常数上下文风险

取固定 θ∈(0,1−2c]，初始化 (X1,X2) 为均匀符号对，令下一符号满足 Pθ(Xt=x|Xt−2=a,Xt−1=b)=(1+θax)/2。该源平稳、真正二阶且均匀混合。路径概率是相距二位乘积项的乘积，所以将整条历史反转不改变其分布。

真实下一符号概率依赖 XN−1，反转后则依赖 X2。若预测器在反转下不变，包括仅使用符号计数的闭合替代计算，那么三角不等式与源的反转对称性给出

Rθ(Q) ≥ (θ/2) Pθ(XN−1≠X2)。

N 为偶数时右侧为 θ/4；N 为奇数且 N≥5 时为 θ(1−θ(N−3)/2)/4。因此 N≥4 时统一不低于 θ(1−θ)/4。取 θ=(1−2c)/2 得固定常数 bc>0。即使预测器知道 θ，缺少实际上下文仍产生这个风险。

所以若实际预测器风险为 rN，其到任何反转不变替代计算的源平均输出距离至少为 bc−rN。真正一致的二阶预测器不可能与同质计数替代计算满足 Δ→0。此前 Γ²S0=o(N) 的条件定义了一个可证明受限的子类，而不是通向二阶一致性的构造。

更一般地，记 A=EθTV(Q(X),Q(reverse X))，则风险至少为上述对称下界减去 A/2。它给出一个必要的有序输出信号检验，不是充分的架构设计法。

三 公开线性因果行更新的精确折叠

另一条可验证条件不要求每行只依赖本地符号。设初始特征 fi(Xi) 有固定范数，随后经历公开、源无关、非负行随机的因果矩阵 P1,…,PH。它们之间没有数据相关的查询、键、门、归一化因子或非线性行变换。最终终端向量可以精确写成

zT=eT⊤PH⋯P1f=Σiwifi(Xi)，wi≥0，Σwi=1。

中间各行可以包含整个可用前缀的信息，但 Alice 直接发送自己拥有符号的加权和即可，Bob 加入自己的部分。所有进入最终解码器的数据通道必须包含在这些向量里；多个摘要需要累计维数，不能留一个免费上下文通道。

若固定摘要维数为 v、后续付费解码器的统一 Lipschitz 增益为 G，则量化该有界向量给最坏历史模拟

C ≤ min{N,Kv[1+log(1+G)+log(1/ε)]}。

这只是公开线性映射的结合律与有界向量量化，不是新的逼近算法。它与此前整行银行重建的 Ω(N) 障碍不矛盾，因为这里只重建最后的线性统计。任意非线性分布式变换或数据相关得分都不自动满足折叠条件。

# Reversal-invariant closure cannot approximate a consistent second-order predictor

6 November 2026 UTC. Analytical candidate v1; independent review pending. This sharpens the scope diagnosis of the homogeneous two-type surrogate, not a general Transformer impossibility.

## 1. Source and precise symmetry

Use signed binary symbols and a fixed theta in (0,1-2c], with c in (0,1/2). Initialize (X_1,X_2) uniformly and let

    P_theta(X_t=x | X_{t-2}=a,X_{t-1}=b)=(1+theta a x)/2.

This is stationary, genuinely second-order, inside P_c and uniformly mixing. Its path probability is

    P_theta(x_1:N)=1/4 product_{t=3}^N (1+theta x_{t-2}x_t)/2.

Reversing x preserves the product, hence the history law. The actual next-symbol probability of +1 is p(x)=(1+theta x_{N-1})/2. Reversal changes that target to (1+theta x_2)/2.

Let Q_N(x) be a normalized binary predictor whose +1 probability g(x) is invariant under reversal. It may know theta, have arbitrary depth/width/precision, and use any order-sensitive statistics that happen to preserve reversal. Source-independent random coins are allowed if the predictor's conditional output law is reversal invariant; deterministic invariance is sufficient.

## 2. Exact lower bound

For deterministic g, reversal invariance of both g and the history law gives equal expectations of |g(X)-p(X)| and |g(X)-p(reverse X)|. The scalar triangle inequality therefore yields

    E TV(Q_N,p_actual)
       >= (theta/2) P_theta(X_{N-1} != X_2).               (1)

The two parity subsequences are independent stationary symmetric first-order chains with step correlation theta. Thus for N>=4:

    lower = theta/4                               if N is even,
    lower = theta(1-theta^((N-3)/2))/4             if N is odd.

In particular every N>=4 satisfies risk>=theta(1-theta)/4. For odd N>=5 the exponent is at least one; even N is stronger. Choosing theta=(1-2c)/2 supplies a fixed positive constant b_c=theta(1-theta)/4. Randomized conditional-output-law invariance gives the same statement by applying reversal to the joint law with an independent random seed, or by averaging the scalar loss over that output kernel.

This is terminal CONTEXT loss, not uncertainty about theta: even oracle knowledge of this fixed source does not remove the obstruction. It uses no communication theorem or approximation precision assumption.

## 3. Consequence for source-average closure

Suppose an actual predictor A_N and a reversal-invariant surrogate Q_N satisfy sup_{P in P_c} E TV(A_N,Q_N)<=Delta_N. Then, on this fixed witness,

    R(A_N)>=b_c-Delta_N.

More sharply, if R(A_N)<=r_N, then its source-average discrepancy from EVERY such surrogate must obey

    sup_{P in P_c} E TV(A_N,Q_N)>=b_c-r_N.               (2)

Hence a uniformly consistent genuine second-order predictor cannot have Delta_N->0 to the homogeneous two-type surrogate, regardless of the favorable Gamma^2 S_0=o(N) condition previously studied. That condition defines an impossibility subclass, not a route to consistency.

The same reasoning gives a quantitative symmetry test for arbitrary A. Write asym_N=E_theta TV(A(X),A(reverse X)). Two triangle inequalities and reversal of the source give

    R_theta(A)>= (theta/2)P_theta(X_{N-1}!=X_2)-asym_N/2.

Thus a low-risk model must preserve a definite order-sensitive output signal on this source. This is a necessary test, not a sufficient architectural construction.

## 4. Relation to existing work and escape routes

The homogeneous two-type common-control surrogate depends only on symbol counts, so it is reversal invariant. The fixed-rank general theorem does NOT require this: position-dependent token-local bases can retain ordered suffix information. A supplied exact terminal pair also breaks the symmetry and must be paid for under the raw-input contract.

The local BOUNDED_SCORE_TERMINAL_CONTEXT.md (4 November) already uses this lag-two source to show a fixed-depth bounded-score terminal-access obstruction via penultimate-token sensitivity. This note reuses that known witness and target mechanism; its distinct scope is a depth-free exact symmetry bound and a quantitative residual obstruction for the new closure. It is not a new source, a new generic information method, or a priority claim.

Closest checked sources remain Hahn's bounded-depth sensitivity work and the Markov-representation papers recorded in CURRENT_FRONTIER_PRIMARY_COMPARISON.v2.md. Their ordered copying/counting mechanisms explain why real second-order constructions cannot be represented by a mere homogeneous histogram closure. This note makes no fresh primary-literature novelty certification.

# Public linear causal row updates admit exact terminal collapse

6 November 2026 UTC. Analytical candidate v2; v1 is preserved. Independent review requested after explicit input, norm and terminal-channel clarifications. A separate structural sufficient condition, not an instance of a common broadcast controller.

## 1. Condition

Raw inputs are N binary symbols and one public synthetic row, so the raw-bit fallback costs at most N. Let each initial row contain a token-local bounded vector f_i(X_i) in R^v; synthetic input may be public. Before the final useful terminal read, data-dependent row evolution is LINEAR with publicly known source-independent coefficients. For clarity take row-stochastic nonnegative causal matrices P_1,...,P_H acting on these vectors, each generated by a public data-independent attention pattern. There is no intervening data-dependent key, query, gate, normalization factor or nonlinear row map in this condition. Public bounded linear feature maps can be included and their resulting signed coefficients/norm budgets must then be charged separately.

For the row-stochastic case, the terminal answer is exactly

    z_T=e_T^T P_H ... P_1 f = sum_i w_i f_i(X_i),
    w_i>=0, sum_i w_i=1.

Every intermediate nonterminal bank may depend on its entire prefix. Alice need not reconstruct any such bank: she directly computes sum over her i of w_i f_i(X_i), using public w. Bob adds his own terms and the public self term. For a fixed norm budget V, if ||f_i||<=V, Alice's vector has radius at most V. Quantizing it to accuracy eta costs O(v log(1+V/eta)) bits independently of N and H, with raw-bit fallback N. All local protocol computation is unrestricted exact-real; computing w offline is not a free neural operation, but the neural network itself performs the original paid H mixing stages.

All data reaching the subsequent decoder must be included in the collapsed vector z; there is no unaccounted retained terminal channel. Public coordinates can be hardwired, and multiple retained data summaries must be concatenated with their dimension/norm costs. If the subsequent paid terminal decoder is G-Lipschitz from z to output TV on the projected domain, choose eta=epsilon/max(1,G). Thus

    C<=min{N, K v[1+log(1+G)+log(1/epsilon)]}.             (1)

K may depend on the fixed norm budget V; if V grows retain the explicit logarithmic dependence log(1+V max(1,G)/epsilon). Several terminal summaries add their dimensions. H itself is not the communication multiplier because the linear cascade is collapsed EXACTLY before quantization. In the nonnegative stochastic case its mixing operator has max-row gain one; all decoder amplification stays charged in G.

## 2. Two uniform prefix averages

For P_{ti}=1/t when i<=t, and zero otherwise, with T=N+1,

    (P^2 f)_T = (1/T)sum_{j=1}^T a_j f_j,
    a_j=sum_{i=j}^T 1/i.

This is a public position-weighted token-local sum, although the second read's actual values are prefix means and are not token-local. The terminal-specific simulation is exact (Delta=0). ## 4. What the new algebra actually adds

These are direct comparisons of the local formulas, not external-paper claims.

### 4.1 Public-linear collapse

Writing e_T^T P_H...P_1 f = sum_i w_i f_i is associativity/distributivity. Stochasticity bounds the summary radius without reconstructing every prefix. Quantizing after exact composition avoids paying H separate errors. However, decoder amplification still appears through log G, and G can grow exponentially in paid depth. Thus the absence of a literal H multiplier does not eliminate all depth dependence or prove a depth-free prediction theorem.

The condition applies to the two-read logical transport stage after its token-local preparation. It does not assert that all preceding rowwise computation is linear. A physical full-feature LN inserts data-dependent value factors and is outside the exact identity; P must use its separately proved approximation error. This restriction is central, not cosmetic.

### 4.2 Harmonic re-encoding

For the public prefix-average matrix, direct interchange of sums gives

    (P^2 f)_T = (1/T) sum_j [sum_(i=j)^T 1/i] f_j.

No new statistical information is created: O applies this known linear operation to the prior geometric code and compensates the known coefficients in its terminal digit recurrence. The synthetic self term is part of the harmonic coefficient. The bounded successive ratio and dyadic approximation margin are useful proof obligations, but the risk theorem, count accumulator and divider are inherited.

Algebraically the terminal signal is one public weighted token-local statistic. This does NOT by itself provide a cost-free one-head neural replacement: public weight generation, representation precision and decoder gains would still require a ledger. The separate earlier one-read suffix construction already establishes that two reads are unnecessary for the statistical task. Ablation distinguishes the two paths inside this particular program only.

Consequently, this route is best described as an elementary composition/example with complete accounting. It is not an independent estimation method, an attention-count separation, or evidence that general nonlinear evolving memories are compressible.

### 4.3 Positive-epsilon compilation

P's specific contribution to its own proof is checking that the inherited compiler covers both full-prefix averages, the exact terminal gate, all continuous decoder stages, the entire prepared vector and both ablations. The recurrence e_(l+1)<=L e_l+C eta^2 is ordinary stability bookkeeping. Paying restoration instead of inserting an unbounded 1/eta matrix is essential to the claimed resource class. These points can make a valid local theorem application without constituting a new compiler technique.

Its strongest defensible interpretation is a concrete normalized closure example using the cited compiler and new-program arguments. It is not a compiler from arbitrary attention networks, and this literature comparison does not independently discharge its proof dependencies or certify their reviews.

### 4.4 Reversal obstruction

The lag-two source and terminal-context mechanism already occur in `terminal_context_obstruction_20261104_0822/BOUNDED_SCORE_TERMINAL_CONTEXT.md`. S pairs a path with its reversal under an invariant source law and then applies the scalar triangle inequality. Its useful refinement is to state a depth-free exact-symmetry bound and a quantitative lower bound on surrogate discrepancy. It does not show that arbitrary fixed-rank positional summaries are reversal-invariant. The two-read weighted code is visibly positional, so there is no conflict with S.

Neither similarity of names nor one different assumption proves equivalence or separation. Any proposed reduction from a primary construction must account for:

1. Raw full-prefix input and the synthetic terminal, with no free suffix/pair port or hidden preprocessing.
2. Total useful head invocations, plus every paid local stage. Two attention layers and O(log N+b) primitive depth are different claims.

6. Ordinary affine vocabulary-softmax output, all decoding/restoration work, and the decoder's end-to-end sensitivity.
