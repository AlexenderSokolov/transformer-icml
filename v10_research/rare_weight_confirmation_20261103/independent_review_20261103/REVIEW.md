# Independent review: preservation-corrected follow-up

3 November 2026. This review covers the draft available during review. No seal, experiment-scale random sample, experiment fit, or edit to the draft or earlier results was performed. Fifteen existing synthetic static tests passed independently; the tests include one synthetic gradient update and small artificial random fixtures.

## Scientific design

Independent literal recount of the old calibration and completed-ablation `*_words` arrays confirms that the proposed low-J reserve contains 20 enumerated features in 18 distinct `(J,C,D,U)` groups absent from those previous arrays. Every reserved feature has J=2 or 3; all 20 belonged to the original train side and are moved to holdout by this amendment. Collision groups are excluded from the reserve. The hash/reserve predicate, new-training filter and pre-fit assertions enforce group disjointness for the primary rare-heldout sample and all reserved structural groups. The natural-prior evaluation remains an independent unconditioned sample, with group and word overlap permitted. The reserve and previously reported 11/8-vector sets are secondary; the primary endpoint is the 1,024-word sampled rare-heldout mean error. These sets have been exposed in various ways, and the planned study is explicitly non-blind.

The objective weights are correct: given empirical rare fraction p, independent minibatch means receive coefficients `(0.5p, 0.5(1-p), 0.5)` in the corrected arm and `(0.25, 0.25, 0.5)` in the boosted arm. The two arms reuse sampled indices and each paired initialization. The corrected objective is the training-group-conditional empirical prior mean, not an unbiased estimate of the unconditioned natural prior. Three initializations do not produce three independent data replications; changed gradient noise and finite-budget optimizer effects remain entangled with the objective intervention. The specified thresholds support an exploratory descriptive screen, not a significance test, source-risk theorem, uniform guarantee or width-32 capacity claim.

## Preservation and interruption

The generator draws transition probabilities, starts, and successive transition-uniform vectors in the original order. Before each batch consumes randomness, an immutable fsynced intent records the set, batch index, size, horizon, and starting PRNG state. A candidate file records every candidate in a committed batch, eligible and selected flags, latent probabilities, starts, uniforms and PRNG states; selected words are consumed only after batch publication. An intent without its candidate file marks an uncertain pending batch; the intent does not preserve random values generated only in RAM. Frozen selected data and minibatch streams are saved before fitting. The accepted launcher exclusively creates the console file, redirects both streams, and fsyncs its creation. The runner uses exclusive output-directory creation, non-replacing atomic publication, ancestor-directory fsync, and incremental fsynced CSV records. Each model checkpoint includes parameters, Adam moments, step and hyperparameters. Full update-journal records have framing, SHA-256, shapes, all 353 gradients and all 353 proposed post-update parameters; a complete record is write-ahead and does not alone establish the update was committed. The protocol and synthetic tests now acknowledge this window and the runner does not automatically resume interrupted work. The review-approval marker checks hashes of all listed draft and upstream inputs before sealing.

### Resolved review findings and remaining limits

The previously identified interruption and completion-validation gaps have been addressed in the final reviewed draft. A durable intent precedes batch randomness, and the protocol explicitly states that a generated-but-uncommitted batch can be lost. The intent permits the starting state to be identified but is not a preservation claim for RAM-only data; there is no automatic replay or repair. The standalone journal validator defaults to checking prefix integrity; its `--complete` mode requires precisely the six expected arm/seed sequences, 2,000 consecutive entries each, and 12,000 entries total. Tests cover an interrupted draw after intent, an empty or incomplete journal in completion mode, correct completion, and a missing step. Because the journal is write-ahead, even a complete journal is evidence of prepared records rather than proof that the last update was applied or evaluated; reconciliation with checkpoints, CSV and final results remains necessary.

The earlier ambiguous `full completion` validator label has been replaced with `all expected prepared records`, and the protocol explicitly requires reconciliation of the update CSV, final checkpoints and optimizer state, metrics, results and console before claiming experiment completion. No design blocker was found in this bounded review. Root review and permission remain mandatory, and any claim that all raw values survive an arbitrary mid-generation crash would exceed the stated contract.
The guarantees rely on the filesystem honoring fsync and hard-link semantics; they do not extend to arbitrary storage failures. A saved PRNG state or an update intent is not a raw-output record and should not be described as one. The runner does not itself prove cross-platform bitwise replay.
