Disposition: The population lower bound and quantitative proof are valid within the stated model. The original finite-M formula omitted the optional BOS input's deviation; the correction fixes both notes. Read all three together. My earlier independent review of the underlying note missed this optional-coordinate bookkeeping issue; the present review explicitly corrects it.

  • Rectangle and curvature. I=[1/8,1/6], J=[1/20,1/18] lies inside the family wedge; u≥1/8 and w=(1−u)/3≥5/18, hence π_min≥1/8. For f_v(u)=v/u, f″=2v/u³≥2(1/20)/(1/6)³=108/5.
  • One-dimensional complexity. The population bank is affine in (u,v). Along each fixed-v line, each width-W ReLU layer subdivides any existing affine segment at at most W preactivation zeros, hence at most (W+1)^d pieces after d layers. Optional clipping splits each into at most three, so R=3(W+1)^d is safe, including affine skip connections and fewer layers/units.
  • Integral bound. On an interval of length h, for offset h/4, the centered second difference of f is at least m h²/16 on the middle half. Its integral is at least m h³/32. Since second differences of an affine function vanish, triangle inequality bounds the same integral by four times the interval's L1 error, giving m h³/128. Summing over ≤R segments and Jensen's inequality yield m|I|³/(128R²). Uniform v averaging cancels |J|, uniform u density divides by |I|, and actual terminal 00 has probability u≥1/8. With |I|=1/24 and m=108/5, the prior risk lower is 3/(81920R²).
  • Finite-M correction and quantifiers. The eight bank-coordinate bound is g₈(M)=[8√(129M/4)+12]/(M+1). If the optional 1/(M+1) BOS input is passed to the readout, its limit is 0 and its ℓ1 error contributes an additional 1/(M+1), making the common valid g_BOS(M)=[8√(129M/4)+13]/(M+1). The correction states exactly this and preserves the original +12 for the eight-coordinate-only model. For every M and every readout with the fixed architecture and Lipschitz constant K, prior risk ≥3/(81920R²)−K g_BOS(M). Thus a length-indexed sequence with uniform K≤K₀ has a positive eventual prior risk, without exchanging infimum and limit. In particular, the prior establishes a population average lower for each such rule, and hence a worst-source lower by averaging; it does not identify one fixed source that defeats every length-indexed rule.
  • Scope. The quantitative result is for a scalar clipped finite ReLU readout of the specified histogram bank, optionally with a bounded vanishing BOS coordinate. The original individual-fixed-network softmax argument does not supply this uniform-width constant. Additional attention, richer features, unrestricted division, unbounded length/position information, or length-varying networks with no uniform Lipschitz bound remain outside the claim; no general Transformer impossibility follows.

The correction changes only the finite-M boundary accounting and not the limiting lower. No general novelty, source-specific finite-sample minimax, implementation or formal verification claim is made.
