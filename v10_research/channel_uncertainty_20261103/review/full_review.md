## Independent mathematical audit

1. **Quantifiers.** The rule is chosen before the joint channel/source/start/budget/kernel supremum. True W is fixed but hidden; its memoryless noise is fresh conditional on the complete pre-channel state. Public coins are independent, not extra path information. Fixed physical input length, no calibration or feedback, and exact supplemental counts/protected pair match the inherited model. Uniformity over W is genuinely stronger than the known-W problem.

2. **Boundary is exact.** Existence of an identical-row Q in both rowwise TV balls implies d≤TV(P0,Q)+TV(Q,P1)≤2α. Conversely Q=(P0+P1)/2 is exactly d/2 from each row. Under this one admissible channel any receiver output can be simulated with baseline randomization. Each robust rule is therefore bounded below by baseline minimax; ignoring all channel outputs gives the reverse inequality without minimax attainment. Hence equality with the baseline is valid for α≥d/2, including equality, all d=0 cases, α>1, R=0 and any finite M-dependent length. Baseline divergence proves impossibility, not merely failure of the proposed test.

3. **Nominal test is uniform and uses no true-channel likelihood.** For A={P1>P0}, sum of positive signed differences gives P1(A)−P0(A)=d. TV controls each event probability, so q0≤τ−γ and q1≥τ+γ with γ=d/2−α. Neither bound uses nominal support exclusivity. Repeated fixed gate produces conditionally iid Bernoulli(q_b) observations. Under b=0 the closed upper-tail event covers threshold ties; under b=1 the strict lower-tail event is contained in the closed tail. Both errors separately obey exp(−2Rγ²).

4. **Self-contained concentration check.** For Bernoulli Z, f(t)=log E exp(t(Z−EZ)) has f(0)=f'(0)=0. Under exponential tilt, f''(t)=p_t(1−p_t)≤1/4 for every real t, with degenerate Bernoulli cases immediate. Taylor's integral remainder gives f(t)≤t²/8 for either sign of t. Independence and Markov's inequality yield exp(−tRγ+Rt²/8); minimizing at t=4γ gives exp(−2Rγ²), and negative tails use −t. Thus no unverified external concentration lemma or average-to-maximum conversion is needed.

5. **Risk coupling and constants.** Exact and decoded receivers share receiver randomness and coincide on correct gate decoding. Unsquared TV loss is in [0,1], so instancewise risk increases by at most the uniformly bounded error probability; error independence from the source/path is unnecessary and not claimed. Since g_M≥M^−1/2, the normalized increase is ≤sqrt(M) exp(−2Rγ²). R≥ceil(ln M/(4γ²)) makes this ≤1. For M≥64 this is a positive integer, so the R≥1 testing definition is consistent. Feasible targets G_j+1, not arbitrary tiny constants, are correctly stated.

6. **Positive-radius necessity.** δ=α/2 lies in (0,1/4) when 0<α<d/2≤1/2. Mixing each nominal row with the uniform law on the entire stipulated finite alphabet changes TV by at most δ, gives every symbol mass ≥δ/s, and preserves a nonzero row difference by factor 1−δ. Thus a fixed full-support distinct-row W* lies strictly within the uncertainty set, even for disjoint or partial nominal supports and jointly nominal-null columns. Its likelihood constant satisfies 1<κ*≤s/δ. For arbitrary codewords, product domination is (κ*)^R; independent mixtures and conditioning on shared coins preserve it.

7. **Accepted lower interface is used in the valid direction.** Freezing W* converts each robust rule into an admissible known-channel rule. Known-channel minimax permits more designs, not fewer, so its universal lower holds for this subset. The BSC001 source uses only product likelihood domination, leaving target separation unscaled and multiplying clean actual-terminal loss alone. Nonnegative null loss is transported before any signed inequality. The BCC001 substitution is explicitly accepted. Its finite implication M+1≥2^160 and (κ*)^R≤(M+1)^(1/8) gives factor ≥2^−32 ln(M+1), hence eventual R>ln(M+1)/(8 ln κ*) for any uniformly bounded robust factor. No attack is selected using observed channel noise or predictor seeds. The arbitrary-code necessity is stronger than a repeated-gate-only analysis.

8. **Source classes remain separate.** First-order upper imports the repaired FOT002 suffix gate, allowing arbitrary initial pairs without assuming a homogeneous initial edge; G_F=1024+2^20. General binary order two requires the supplied all-state P²≥Π/M and uses the genuinely order-two gate from O2T001 with G_O2=2^17+2^23=8519680. Its receipt and full interface review cover arbitrary/unsupported starts. First-order stationary lower witnesses satisfy the certificate and embed into order two with unchanged actual-terminal next-symbol targets. Source inclusion transfers lowers only, never first-order uppers. The candidate respects this distinction.

9. **Zero radius and resource boundaries.** At α=0 the uncertainty set is exactly the nominal singleton, so the accepted four support classes return. At every fixed positive radius below d/2 the full-support witness forces logarithmic length even when d=1 and nominal supports are disjoint. Constants may deteriorate at either endpoint; no α_M claim is made. Rational nominal probabilities and rational α allow exact A and threshold comparisons plus a conservative computable length; arbitrary real parameters give statistical existence only. Supplemental raw sample scanning versus physical communication remains separated. No unknown-W whole-message upper or known-W ML code import is asserted. The separate identical-row whole-message impossibility follows from the admissible clean IID prior and is valid.
