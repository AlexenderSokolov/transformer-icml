## Reconstruction and verification

1. **Port and signed β.** With e=e^β, D=eJ^=+e⁻¹(M−J^=)>0 and q=[e C^=+e⁻¹(C−C^=)]/D, so C^==[Dq−e⁻¹C]/(e−e⁻¹). Query bit 1 requires subtraction from C to recover the coordinate-zero marginal; the reported A=C00+C01 and B=C00+C10 follow. β can be negative but must be finite and nonzero; at zero this algebra loses rank, although graph constraints can still identify particular cases. The endpoint S_N is supplied from the same path, with history t=2,…,N−1 corresponding to M=N−2 edges from S₂ to S_N; the final observed X_N is a historical successor but its own unobserved successor is not used.
2. **Feasible set and statistic.** Marginal equations force C=(x,A−x,B−x,C−A−B+x). Each integer in the stated interval meets the box constraints. Directed counts 00→00/01, 01→10/11, 10→00/01, 11→10/11 encode exactly the binary triples; an Euler trail traversing all M edges and ending at observed S_N reconstructs a binary word, including overlap consistency. Fix S₂ when disclosed and otherwise union over all four possible starts; degree balance alone is insufficient without connected support. The possible B_s=(C_s+α)/(J_s+2α) values have slope ±1/(J_s+2α). Thus singleton F iff unique B_s; the exact worst-case absolute-error radius for a single port is diam(F)/[2(J_s+2α)], attained at the midrange and needed on the two extreme paths, regardless of gaps in F. If J_s=0, C_s=0 fixes x and B_s=1/2; this is consistent with singleton F, not an exception to the theorem. The triangle bound with E[diam(F)/(2(J+2α))] is pathwise before conditional or unconditional expectation, not a lower bound for θ estimation.
3. **Precision.** |ΔC^=|≤D ε/[2|sinh β|]≤M exp(|β|)ε/[2|sinh β|]=Mρ(β)ε, ρ=1/[1−exp(−2|β|)]. Strict bound <1/2 gives nearest integer recovery; absolute fixed-point rounding error ≤2^(−b−1) implies sufficient strict 2⁻ᵇ Mρ<1. This is signed-β correct, handles ties strictly and requires exact J,C,β and exact formation of D. It is not a lower bound on precision for all feasible-path decoders.
4. **Stationary signal and the corrected distinction.** π=(u,w,w,w), w=(1−u)/3, θ=(t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2) gives E[A/M]=ut+w/2, E[B/M]=w; this computes population edge counts, not E[q], and can be accessed only by using q together with J,C,β. For terminal 00 the correction's separate ratio-of-stationary-expectations has numerator e^β(ut+w/2)+e⁻β(3w/2−ut), denominator e^β(u+w)+2we⁻β, and derivative u(e^β−e⁻β)/denominator. All are correct, including negative β. It is explicitly not E of the finite random ratio, either conditional or unconditional.
5. **Conditional bias and variance.** Given S_N=00, reverse chain starts at 00 and each A summand is the indicator of a reverse edge corresponding to a forward transition from older-bit-0 state to successor 1. Two-step common atom 11 of mass ≥1/8 gives state mixing bound r^floor(j/2), r=7/8; edge law inherits it, so the nonstationary conditional mean differs from M(ut+w/2) by ≤Σ_{j≥0}r^floor(j/2)=16. An earlier/later edge pair separated h≥1 has absolute covariance bounded by 2r^floor((h−1)/2), allowing overlapping edges when h=1. As Σ_{h≥1}r^floor((h−1)/2)=16, total conditional variance ≤M+2M·2·16=65M. Jensen/Cauchy gives E|A−M(ut+w/2)|≤√(65M)+16. At S_N=00, target is t, u>0, and clipping cannot increase absolute error; Mρ ε_q error in A gives the claimed plus ρ ε_q/u, capped at 1. No forward martingale is assumed valid after terminal conditioning.
6. **Sufficient constants and model scope.** M≥1040/(u²τ²) makes the √ term ≤τ/4; M≥64/(uτ) makes the bias ≤τ/4; ε_q≤uτ/[2ρ] makes the numerical term ≤τ/2. If q rounding is ≤2^(−b−1), 2⁻ᵇ≤uτ/ρ suffices. The resulting O(u⁻²τ⁻²) is only this proof's sufficient M; it is not necessary, minimax, a lower bound, or a trained-network rate. The same public u is assumed known for the readout, not unknown t. Original note's estimate only concerns terminal 00; it is not full-stationary risk for all terminal states.
7. **Comparator addendum and actual-terminal lower bound.** The two-point full-history lower bound uses the same u and stationary pair law for both t_±, with Δ=t_+−t_-=[16√(1+Mu)]⁻¹. Exact reverse conditional KL bound ≤(256/3)(1+Mu)Δ²=1/3 plus Pinsker and two-point triangle gives the claimed (1−1/√6)/[32√(1+Mu)] for the maximum of the two conditional risks, even for full-path rules given u. Consequently it applies to restricted-port rules, with a common rule independent of t; it does not establish a lower rate for one fixed θ.
