Sharper worst-case sensitivity comparison at u₀

2026-11-03. Additive corollary of the unchanged v2 lower and same-port endpoint upper. It concerns only the stated q/r/h cut and the two endpoints, not each individual u, the complete t interval, all transformers, a finite-ReLU implementation, or a novelty claim. Independent verification of this corollary is pending.

Fix finite β>0, γ=eβ−1, public u₀∈(0,1/4), η∈(0,1), λ(u)=γu/(1+γu), and Λ=1/λ(u₀). For any fixed finite coordinate budgets Lq,Lr,Lh≥0 satisfying

L_q λ(u₀) ≤ 1−η,     L_r ≥ Λ,     L_h ≥ Λγ/2,

consider the same class of common q/r/h-only decoders and path-independent or common-conditional-law seed assumptions as in v2, with exact ports and output. The worst-case conditional risk has

liminf_{m→∞} inf_{F_m} sup_{u∈[u₀,1/4), t∈{1/4,3/4}}
 E[|F_m(q,r,h)−t| | S_N=00] ≥ η/4.

Proof: for every admissible Fm, the supremum includes the single allowed source frequency u=u₀. Apply v2 (4) there, with λ(u₀), δ=1/m and fixed finite coordinate bounds. Its fluctuation terms vanish as m→∞, uniformly over all Fm obeying those bounds. Taking the infimum cannot remove this lower. This argument does not say every larger u has the same lower.

The high-sensitivity comparator from the previous addendum uses the same q,r,h and the same public u₀, with (Λ,Λ,Λγ/2) sensitivities; therefore it respects the very same Lr,Lh budgets but not the low Lq budget. It has conditional risk tending to zero uniformly over u∈[u₀,1/4) for the two endpoint t values. It uses exact division and [1/4,3/4] clipping, and may distort interior t.

For γu₀≪1, Λ=(1+γu₀)/(γu₀) is of order 1/(γu₀), so this makes explicit the q-coordinate sensitivity cost within this limited experiment. Neither this asymptotic comparison nor its finite-m precursor establishes an all-network N–L lower, matching FFN width/depth upper, score or finite-bit necessity, or general publication-level novelty. The original rare-family test, D’Angelo/Izzo/Rajaraman antecedents and unrestricted-history counter baseline remain mandatory qualifications.
