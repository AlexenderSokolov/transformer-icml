Correction: randomized-decoder seed must remain common after conditioning

2026-11-03. This additive correction applies to the unchanged sensitivity bottleneck v1 report and its sum-before-maximum clarification. Independent review found a missing randomized-decoder quantifier. Deterministic decoders are unaffected.

In §1 of v1, replace the assumption that the decoder seed merely has a common source-independent marginal distribution by either of these sufficient assumptions:

  1. The seed is independent of the entire source path under each of the two sources and has the same law for both sources; or
  2. The conditional seed law given SN=00 is explicitly identical under the two sources.

Common marginal law alone is insufficient: the seed may be correlated with the path in a source-dependent way, and conditioning on SN=00 can produce different seed laws. Under either stated repair, call their common conditional law Pω. Average the sum of the two population losses over Pω, then take the maximum of their expectations. The coordinatewise Lipschitz constants must hold for every relevant seed, and F, β and its public metadata must be the same for both sources.

The port perturbations can be path-dependent and mutually dependent, provided their declared pathwise or conditioned-expectation error bounds hold; they do not change the requirement on the decoder seed's common conditional law. The lower bound and constants in (4) remain unchanged under the repaired assumption. A raw-history/residual bypass, source-dependent seed information, or a changed conditional seed law is outside the theorem.

This correction is required to use the randomized version, not a novelty claim or full proof acceptance. The separate reviewer report will be linked when available.
