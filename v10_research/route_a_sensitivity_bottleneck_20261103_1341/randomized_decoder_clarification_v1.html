Clarification: average the two-loss sum, not the per-seed maximum

2026-11-03. This additive clarification applies to the unchanged v1 sensitivity report. It makes the randomized-decoder step in §3 explicit and does not alter the bound. The decoder seed has the same source-independent distribution under the two sources, and its coordinate-Lipschitz bounds hold for every seed.

For seed ω, the two population losses satisfy

|F_ω(p₋)−1/4|+|F_ω(p₊)−3/4| ≥ 1/2−Λ_q λ/2.

First average this sum over the common seed law. Then the maximum over the two expected losses is at least half their sum, giving (1−Λ_q λ)/4. Equivalently one may apply the triangle inequality to the two mean outputs, then Jensen. Do not average the per-seed maximum and interchange max and expectation: that interchange is generally invalid. Subtract the same uniform Lipschitz fluctuation/port/output term from each source's expected loss to obtain equation (4) of v1.

This is an elementary quantifier clarification, not a new proof acceptance or novelty claim. The two actual conditioned trajectory laws need no coupling.
