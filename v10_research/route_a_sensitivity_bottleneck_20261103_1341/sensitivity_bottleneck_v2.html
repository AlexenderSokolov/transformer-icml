ROUTE A: SCORE VERSUS READOUT SENSITIVITY AT A DECLARED q/r/h BOTTLENECK — v2

2026-11-03. Status: candidate mathematical lemma, not a novelty certification, full validation acceptance, trained-network guarantee, or architecture-wide lower bound. An independent LLM review checked the lower, resource algebra, population tightness and sharper conditional moments, subject to the caveats below. Exact-fraction tests are diagnostics only. This deliberately restricted three-port bottleneck is not the full residual-stream decoder in the shared D model.

1. Two sources, actual terminal event and information boundary

Fix m=N−2≥1, 0<u<1/4, w=(1−u)/3, v=mu≥60. For either t_−=1/4 or t_+=3/4, draw one stationary binary order-two path with π=(u,w,w,w) and successor-one probabilities (t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2) on 00,01,10,11. Both sources have the same π, P(S_N=00)=u and pooled mean μ=2w. This equality is essential. The sources differ in θ_00 and θ_10, but no raw history or extra row-specific features may cross the declared cut.
Use the same fixed score β≥0, γ=e^β−1, and the same terminal-00 decoder F for both parameter values. On the observed word, J counts departures from 00, C counts their one-successors, T counts all one-successors, h=J/m, r=T/m and q=(T+γC)/(m+γJ). Let the population points be
 p_t=(q̄_t,μ,u),  q̄_t=(μ+γut)/(1+γu),
 λ=γu/(1+γu),  q̄_+−q̄_−=λ/2.                           (1)
Here h is a random observed fraction, while u is the source's fixed stationary mass; they are not interchanged. q,r,h and public source-independent metadata are the only sample-dependent quantities at the cut. The actual history, other heads, raw/residual streams and later input rereads are excluded. The decoder may be given u and the pair of candidate t values: this only strengthens its power for the lower. Conversely, the unknown-u population comparator in §4 below needs neither u nor the true t.
F:[0,1]^3→[0,1] obeys separate coordinate Lipschitz bounds Λ_q,Λ_r,Λ_h, uniformly over its domain and, if randomized, for every relevant seed. The seed is independent of the entire source path under each source and has a common distribution, or more generally its conditional law given S_N=00 must be identical for the two sources. Equal seed marginals without either condition are insufficient. Use exactly the same F and β on both sources. Its inputs are clipped after port errors |q̃−q|≤e_q, |r̃−r|≤e_r, |h̃−h|≤e_h pathwise; the same proof works if the three bounds hold in the corresponding conditional expectations. Errors may depend on the full path and need not be independent of each other or the terminal query. The computed scalar may incur output error at most ζ, followed by exact projection to [0,1] and exact complementary probability coordinate. If final saturation has an extra error ξ, separately add ξ while preserving its range. Define
 R_t=E[|output−t| | S_N=00],
where output is a valid probability. This is conditional true-θ TV, not empirical-frequency fidelity, loss averaged over terminal states, or a risk for a decoder with a raw-word bypass.

2. Explicit conditional moment controls

Take any δ∈(0,1) and write
 ℓ=log[2(m+1)/(uδ)], x=γu,
 b=15/v+√[305/(4v)],
 A=16/m+√(61/m),
 G=[A+x√(3ℓ/(4v))+xb]/(1+x/2)+δ+1220/v.                (2)
All constants are explicit; G may exceed one. Under either source and condition S_N=00,
 E|q−q̄_t|≤G,    E|r−μ|≤A,    E|h−u|≤ub.                 (3)
Proof. Reverse the stationary pair chain from S_N=00. In this family both forward and reverse two-step kernels have common atom 11 of mass at least 1/8 (rare-family v3 report, §2 and §4); ρ=7/8. The m states S_N,S_{N−1},…,S_3 contain exactly the m successor bits X_N,…,X_3 whose average is r. Since ∑_{j≥0}ρ^{floor(j/2)}=16, the conditional mean of r differs from μ by at most 16/m. For i<j the conditional covariance of the two [0,1]-valued bits is at most 2ρ^{floor((j−i)/2)} in absolute value; both the lagged conditional law and lagged marginal are within that distance of π. The diagonal variance is ≤1/4 and ∑_{d≥1}ρ^{floor(d/2)}=15. Hence Var(r|S_N=00)≤(m/4+60m)/m²≤61/m, proving the A bound by Cauchy–Schwarz.
The m preceding row indicators yield |E[J|S_N=00]−v|≤15 and Var(J|S_N=00)≤61(v+15); therefore E|h−u|≤ub and, by Chebyshev, P(|J−v|>v/2|S_N=00)≤1220/v. This is a reverse-chain statement, not an iid-row assumption. Let Z=C−tJ. A predictable-indicator Bernoulli exponential supermartingale, union bound over integer J and both signs, then division by P(S_N=00)=u gives P(|Z|>√(Jℓ/2)|S_N=00)≤δ. It is bounded before conditioning; no conditional forward martingale is asserted.
On both good events J≤3v/2 and h≥u/2. The exact relation
 q−q̄_t=[r−μ+γZ/m+γ(t−q̄_t)(h−u)]/(1+γh)
with |t−q̄_t|≤1, followed by (i) the A bound, (ii) |Z|≤√(3vℓ/4), and (iii) E|h−u|≤ub, gives the first fraction in G. Outside the good event |q−q̄_t|≤1, so add δ+1220/v. This proves (3) without equating expectation of a ratio to a ratio of expectations. The bound is intentionally conservative, especially its 1220/v term.

3. Finite random-prefix sensitivity lower

Under §§1–2,
 max{R_{t_-},R_{t_+}} ≥
 [ (1−Λ_q λ)/4 −Λ_q(G+e_q)−Λ_r(A+e_r)−Λ_h(ub+e_h)−ζ ]_+.  (4)
Proof. For a fixed seed, evaluate the same F at p_- and p_+. By (1), only q differs and the difference is λ/2, hence |F(p_+)−F(p_-)|≤Λ_q λ/2. The triangle inequality gives
 |F(p_−)−1/4|+|F(p_+)−3/4|≥1/2−Λ_qλ/2.
Average this SUM over the common conditional seed law, then use max of the two expected losses ≥ half their sum; this gives (1−Λ_qλ)/4. Do not average a per-seed maximum and interchange max with expectation. For each t, coordinatewise Lipschitzness, (3) and the port bounds give
 |E[actual output |S_N=00,t]−E_seed F(p_t)|
 ≤Λ_q(G+e_q)+Λ_r(A+e_r)+Λ_h(ub+e_h)+ζ.
Equivalently the reverse triangle inequality applies directly to each expected absolute loss. The two conditional trajectory distributions need not be coupled and their samples are not assumed independent of their own terminal queries. Risk is nonnegative, so positive-part the result.
This is a true terminal-conditional prediction lower, but ONLY for the declared common isolated q/r/h decoder and its stated sensitivity limits. It is not a minimax lower over arbitrary transformers, hidden states, functions of the original word, other heads or alternative statistical procedures. At β=0, λ=0 and the population q port equals the pooled r; equation (4) remains valid. At u=0 the conditional query event has probability zero, and no result is claimed.

4. Exact population sharpness and the role of unknown u

If q,r,h are replaced by the corresponding population points p_t, then for any Λ_q≥0 the minimax risk over all decoders with q-coordinate sensitivity ≤Λ_q is exactly
 R_pop^*=[1−Λ_qλ]_+/4.                                     (5)
The lower is (4) without fluctuations. For an upper, if the fixed u is given as public metadata, the clipped affine map
 F_0(q)=clip_[1/4,3/4](1/2+Λ_q[q−((1−λ)μ+λ/2)])
achieves (5) and has zero r,h sensitivity. It is the SAME function at t_− and t_+, but may depend on the public u and β. It is not a single pre-specified u-blind decoder for every u.
An alternative exact-real population comparator does not take u as an input. Define λ(h)=γh/(1+γh) and
 F_1(q,r,h)=clip_[1/4,3/4](1/2+Λ_q[q−(1−λ(h))r−λ(h)/2]).
At h=u,r=μ and q=q̄_t, F_1 has error (5) for both endpoints. Its coordinate sensitivities on [0,1]^3 are at most (Λ_q,Λ_q,Λ_qγ/2): use |λ'(h)|≤γ and |r−1/2|≤1/2. Consequently this u-blind comparator proves sharpness under joint budgets only when Λ_r≥Λ_q and Λ_h≥Λ_qγ/2. It uses exact division and an ideal exact-real clip; no corresponding finite-bit implementation, ReLU-network width/depth upper bound, or matching architecture-resource theorem follows. The comparator is calibrated to the two public hypotheses and does not claim optimality for every t∈[1/4,3/4].

5. A norm/depth implication for a specifically declared decoder

Consider only a decoder whose sole variable input is the three-vector (q,r,h) through an identity or norm-at-most-one embedding. It consists of D post-cut residual FFN blocks
 x↦x+A_2 σ(A_1x+b_1)+b_2,
followed by a linear scalar map of operator 2-norm ≤W and projection to [0,1]. Assume W≥1, ||A_1||₂,||A_2||₂≤W in each block, and σ is coordinatewise 1-Lipschitz; biases and public constants do not depend on the unknown t. No LayerNorm, post-cut attention, skip of raw history, or other input-dependent side channel is included. Then each block is (1+W²)-Lipschitz in Euclidean norm, so every coordinate sensitivity is at most
 K=W(1+W²)^D.                                             (6)
The port itself can come from the earlier explicit two-block structured-mask q/r/h extractor, but its extraction error and access permission are separate. The decoder's operator norms, depth, width, biases, input embedding, output projection and all finite-precision costs must be charged; (6) alone is not a bit-complexity or FLOP bound.
Let Δ=G+A+ub+e_q+e_r+e_h. If max_t R_t≤ε<1/4 and
 KΔ+ζ≤(1−4ε)/8,                                            (7)
then (4) with Λ_q=Λ_r=Λ_h=K implies Kλ≥(1−4ε)/2. Since λ≤γu≤e^β u,
 β+D log(1+W²)+log W ≥ log[(1−4ε)/(2u)].                  (8)
This is a necessary inequality under (7), not a proof that such a decoder exists, a matching depth law, or an unrestricted Transformer lower. If this decoder is appended to the particular two-block extractor, total block count is L=D+2 before any separately counted final numerical saturation; (8) can then be stated in terms of that specific pipeline's L. It does not prohibit a different architecture from bypassing the cut or encoding the evidence before it.
Feasibility of the small-fluctuation side condition is part of the claim, not a hidden asymptotic. For exact ports/output and a fixed β, take u_m=m^(−1/4), δ=1/m and K_m=O(m^(1/4)); then v=m^(3/4) and, using (2), K_mΔ→0. Thus (7) eventually holds for every fixed ε<1/4. No RNG or numerical experiments are needed for this comparison of powers and logs. For other schedules the condition must be checked explicitly; in particular K can grow so fast that the lower is vacuous. A finite-precision implementation must additionally make the charged K(e_q+e_r+e_h)+ζ small enough. Merely positing b bits does not imply that guarantee.

6. A concrete counterexample to whole-network extrapolation

Allow the raw word to bypass the q/r/h cut and compute the same-history add-one count B=(C+1)/(J+2). For v→∞ with v≫log[2m(m+1)/u], the previous route-A terminal-conditioned statistical bound gives E[|B−t||S_N=00]→0 for both t values. As a function of an extraneous q coordinate, this decoder can have q-sensitivity zero because it ignores q entirely. It therefore contradicts any attempt to apply the positive λ=0 case of (4) to a model that still exposes raw evidence. This is an actual-history counter, not an oracle for θ; its row counts, arithmetic, precision and access still need resource accounting. Residuals, additional heads, or other ports can play the same logical role. Information loss through one head is not information loss in the whole network.

7. Prior art, scope and retained evidence

The population mixing factor λ and q/r/h algebra are elementary consequences of the soft-count interpolation in D’Angelo et al., Proposition 3.1 and Corollary 4.2, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02800v1#S3.SS1 and #S4.SS2. Their Appendix D.3 already gives a β≈log(1+m) schedule under a prior-dependent pseudo-count heuristic. Izzo et al. Appendix B.3, https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/4a765a1bbf8332154e58bf452fbf806c-Paper-Conference.pdf#page=38 , already bounds soft-to-empirical error in terms of length, score and match count. Rajaraman et al. https://arxiv.org/html/2407.17686v1#S4 already give two-layer attention-only representations of the corresponding conditional empirical frequency for k=2, while R011 already establishes unconditional actual-terminal add-one bounds. See route_a_novelty_gate_20261103_1307/novelty_comparison_v1.html and its independent citation audit for exact scopes.
The triangle inequality (5) is a standard bounded-sensitivity inverse problem, not in itself a novel principle. Its combination with this single rare family, actual-terminal conditional moment controls, explicit port noise and a declared FFN norm budget is a narrow candidate result. No full literature-wide non-subsumption or research-level novelty is established. It must not be sold as a new architecture, a lower bound on all heads, a necessary precision law, or a depth benefit; the explicit counting bypass and population comparator are mandatory baseline evidence.
