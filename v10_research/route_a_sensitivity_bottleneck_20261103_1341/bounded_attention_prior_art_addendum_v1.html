Prior-art addendum: bounded attention, dilution and Lipschitz readout

2026-11-03. A targeted primary-source check found additional direct antecedents to the broad principle behind the restricted-port sensitivity result. This is not an exhaustive survey or a proof that these papers subsume the rare-family conditional theorem. It makes a novelty claim for generic attenuation/sensitivity/depth especially unsafe.

Hahn, TACL 2020

Michael Hahn, Theoretical Limitations of Self-Attention in Neural Sequence Models, official PDF, §6 and Lemma 5: under the paper's boundedness/architecture assumptions, changing one earlier input symbol changes the last-position soft-attention representation by O(1/n), with constants depending on model parameters; the paper then uses a Lipschitz prediction map to turn representation closeness into prediction limitations for its formal-language tasks. This is already a direct precedent for “attenuated information plus bounded readout sensitivity” and its dependence on the network. Its task, perturbation, norms, architecture and asymptotics are different from comparing two stationary Markov source laws conditioned on a rare terminal state. We did not check that Hahn's theorem implies the present q/r/h bound, and its fixed-model asymptotics must not be silently turned into this report's explicit varying-W and varying-depth formula.

Liu et al., NeurIPS 2023

Bingbin Liu, Jordan Ash, Surbhi Goel, Akshay Krishnamurthy and Cyril Zhang, Exposing Attention Glitches with Flip-Flop Language Modeling, Appendix C.2, Proposition 3, shows a single soft-attention head with bounded token norms and bounded query/key operator product cannot maintain arbitrarily concentrated weight as sequence length exceeds an exponential-in-score-norm scale. The task is flip-flop retrieval and the proposition concerns maximal individual attention weight, not the total mass of a rare context group or conditional Markov prediction risk. It is still clear antecedent for bounded-score attention dilution and score/norm versus sequence length.

Interpretation and access

The finite-prefix theorem here additionally specifies a same-trajectory rare-state event, q/r/h cut, two source laws with identical stationary r and u, explicit conditional fluctuations, port sensitivities and a post-cut residual-FFN norm budget. Those are concrete distinctions, not proof of originality or significance. The triangle lower and norm product are elementary, and the abstract upper uses exact division and an endpoint-specific clip. Compare also D’Angelo, Izzo, Rajaraman and the internal R011 results listed in v2 before framing any claim.
