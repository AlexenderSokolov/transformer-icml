Same q/r/h port: a two-endpoint upper and sensitivity comparison

2026-11-03. Additive result for sensitivity bottleneck v2. Scope: two public hypotheses t∈{1/4,3/4}, same actual-terminal event, same q/r/h-only cut and a public lower bound on u. It is an abstract exact-real decoder using division, not an implemented finite-ReLU/depth/bit upper, a full-network separation or a novelty certification. The clipped output may distort interior t values; no upper for the full interval [1/4,3/4] is claimed.

Finite-prefix upper

Fix finite β>0, γ=eβ−1 and a public u₀∈(0,1/4). For u∈[u₀,1/4), let λ(h)=γh/(1+γh), and choose the source-independent public parameter Λ=1/λ(u₀)=(1+γu₀)/(γu₀). This is a charged parameter: it depends on β and the known bound u₀, but not on u, t or the observed word. Let

F₁(q,r,h)=clip_[1/4,3/4](1/2+Λ[q−(1−λ(h))r−λ(h)/2]).

At the population point (q̄t,μ,u), q̄t−(1−λ(u))μ−λ(u)/2=λ(u)(t−1/2). Because λ(u)≥λ(u₀), the explicitly specified [1/4,3/4] clip makes F₁ equal to t at both endpoints, even when Λλ(u)>1. Clipping to [0,1] would not have this property. The decoder has coordinate sensitivities bounded by (Λ,Λ,Λγ/2) on [0,1]³. Applying v2's terminal-conditioned moment bounds gives, for v=mu≥60, δ∈(0,1), and the declared port/output errors,

max_{t∈{1/4,3/4}} R_t ≤ min{1,
    Λ(G+e_q) + Λ(A+e_r) + (Λγ/2)(u b+e_h) + ζ}.

If final saturation is approximate, separately charge its ξ error and guarantee that output remains in the simplex. No source-dependent seed is used in this deterministic decoder. The quantities G,A,b are exactly as defined in v2, with the actual source u; the predictor itself receives h rather than u.

For fixed β,u₀ and exact ports/output, take δ=1/m. Uniformly in u∈[u₀,1/4), v≥mu₀, γu≤γ/4 and ℓ≤log[2m(m+1)/u₀], hence the right side is Oβ,u₀(√(log m/m)). This is a conditional true-row TV upper for the two endpoints, not a population-only assertion. An independent reviewer checked this finite upper; see independent_three_port_upper_review_v1.html.

Resulting restricted sensitivity comparison

Fix β,u₀ as above and fixed finite Lr,Lh, η∈(0,1), and Lq≥0 satisfying Lqλ(1/4)≤1−η, where λ(1/4)=γ/(4+γ) is the boundary value. For every sequence of common q/r/h-only decoders Fm with coordinate sensitivities at most (Lq,Lr,Lh), with decoder randomness independent of each full source path or having the same conditional seed law for the two endpoint sources, v2 equation (4) and the uniform vanishing of G,A,ub imply

liminf_{m→∞} inf_{F_m} sup_{u∈[u₀,1/4), t∈{1/4,3/4}}
 E[|F_m(q,r,h)−t| | S_N=00] ≥ η/4.

Exact ports/output are assumed in this asymptotic comparison. The one fixed F₁ above has larger allowed sensitivity parameters (Λ,Λ,Λγ/2) and its corresponding supremum risk tends to zero. Both use exactly the same three port values and public metadata; neither is allowed to read the raw word or a residual bypass. The low-sensitivity result still holds if Fm changes with m, because v2's bound is uniform over that constrained class. The upper's parameter Λ is charged and depends on the public lower bound u₀.

This is an elementary restricted-port sensitivity comparison. It is not a separation under equal sensitivity/weight budgets, not a theorem that greater depth is needed, not a complete Transformer lower bound, and not a matching finite-bit computational upper. The high-sensitivity F₁ is a mathematical comparison that uses exact division. External novelty remains unverified; D’Angelo's soft interpolation, Izzo's match-count score upper, Rajaraman's hard-count representation and existing R011 risk results must remain explicit antecedents. The raw-history add-one bypass in v2 shows why the cut cannot be silently enlarged.
