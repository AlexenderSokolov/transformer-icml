Holding r and h sensitivity budgets fixed

2026-11-03. Additive clarification of the unchanged same-port comparison and v2 theorem. This is a restricted q/r/h-port, two-endpoint, exact-real analysis, not a whole-network, equal-weight-budget, finite-bit or novelty claim.

Fix β>0, γ=eβ−1, u₀∈(0,1/4), η∈(0,1), and let Λ=1/λ(u₀). Choose finite nonnegative sensitivity budgets with

L_q λ(1/4) ≤ 1−η,
L_r ≥ Λ,     L_h ≥ Λγ/2.

The low-sensitivity class may use any (possibly m-dependent) common decoder with coordinate bounds (Lq,Lr,Lh) and seed independence from the full path under both t values, or identical conditional seed laws given SN=00 for the two endpoint sources at each u. v2 gives, for each u∈[u₀,1/4) and m with mu≥60,

max_{t∈{1/4,3/4}} R_{u,t}(F_m)
 ≥ η/4 − L_q G(u,m) − L_r A(m) − L_h u b(u,m).

With δ=1/m, the subtracted error vanishes uniformly in u∈[u₀,1/4), so taking infimum over such Fm, supremum over the half-open interval of u and the two t values, then liminf in m, gives a lower bound η/4. The upper comparator F₁ from the prior addendum uses the same q/r/h values, has sensitivities ≤(Λ,Λ,Λγ/2) and uniformly vanishing risk on the two t endpoints. It therefore fits the same selected r/h limits Lr,Lh; only the q-sensitivity allowance differs. Since u₀<1/4 and η>0, the high allowance Λ exceeds the stipulated low Lq.

This quantifies exactly what is compared. F₁ still uses real division and a [1/4,3/4] clip and may distort interior t; the result is not a same-q-budget separation, not a proof that adding layers attains Λ, and not a finite-precision implementation or unrestricted Transformer lower. Its two hypotheses, source-independent common decoder, terminal conditioning and absence of other history/residual ports are essential. An independent LLM review checked this corollary and its sup/seed quantifiers; its verbatim report is retained separately.
