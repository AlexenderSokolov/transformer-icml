### Exact full-word erasure

SC001 lines 55–70 and manuscript lines 162–209 correctly count nested subsets. Conditional on original count n and rounded count r=b⌊n/b⌋, each output arrangement has probability 1/binom(ℓ,r). Mixing over n in the same cell leaves the same distribution. Two independent protected IID bits remain part of the message, producing at most 4(⌊ℓ/b⌋+1) labels. The full output is generated from that finite message by a parameter-independent channel; composing a full-word estimator with this channel is a randomized finite-message decoder. This handles the entire arrangement and does not assume data were only a histogram. Empty strings and incomplete last cells are included.

For a uniform parameter on an interval of length h, fixing an independent decoding seed leaves at most D output points. The union of radius-t neighborhoods has mass at most 2Dt/h regardless of stochastic encoding. Integration yields h/(4D). It is not necessary, and would be wrong, to claim the prior remains uniform after observing terminal bits; only the suffix event is conditioned on before those bits are included as labels.
