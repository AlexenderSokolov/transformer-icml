Independent review: finite-history ideal-temperature bound

3 November 2026. Independent analytical review, not formal peer review or a novelty certification. No random operations, enumeration, numerical experiments, source changes or external writes were used.

Reviewed artifact: finite_n_temperature_tradeoff_v1.html, SHA-256 bf009276ea2a39deeaf74b7c29941a60852219fcf8ea895b06bb8f97603a9ef2.

Disposition: no mathematical blocker found within the stated scope. The result concerns the specified ideal BOS output map on one stationary source, deterministic temperature, and error conditional on the actual terminal pair 00. It is not a lower bound for general Transformer architectures, generic parameters, training, bit complexity, or source-unknown prediction.

Checks

  • Indexing and BOS. With BOS x₁ and xᵢ₊₁=Xᵢ, T=N+1 and k′=4, candidate values s=4,…,T correspond exactly to X₃,…,X_N. Their preceding pairs are (X₁,X₂),…,(X_{N−2},X_{N−1}); query is (X_{N−1},X_N). Thus M=N−2 real transitions are used, including the transition ending at the observed final bit, and X_{N+1} is not used. Scaling the binary BOS contribution with κ=2β+ln2 by e^(−2β) gives +1 in the one-bit numerator and +2 in the denominator. This applies to the ideal copy/suppression limit, not an entirely finite-logit construction.
  • Source and mixing. The displayed π is stationary for P, and the displayed P² is correct. Comparing its rows gives maximum total variation 1/2, hence δ(Pʰ)≤2^(−⌊h/2⌋). For bounded transition functions separated by h≥1, conditioning after the first transition gives |Cov|≤δ(P^(h−1)); no independence is assumed. The resulting variance bound is M/4+2M·4=33M/4.
  • True terminal conditioning. The stationary event E={Y_M=00} has probability 3/10. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the conditional mean absolute deviation by √(Var/P(E)), without treating E as independent of the sample. Thus each conditional deviation is at most √((55/2)M). This argument is valid uniformly over deterministic z∈[0,1], since all weights and transition functions remain in [0,1].
  • Bias and ratio. Stationary a=(9+6z+z²)/40, b=(3+6z+z²)/10≥3/10, and q=a/b yield 3/4−q=z(6+z)/[2(3+6z+z²)]. On [0,1], the asserted lower bound follows from 39−32z−7z²≥0 and the upper bound directly. The displayed ratio identity is exact and uses 0≤Q≤1. The deviation is bounded by ((20/3)√(55/2)+10)/√M≤45/√M, since √(55/2)≤21/4 and 1/M≤1/√M for M≥1.
  • Equivalence and constants. Triangle and reverse triangle inequalities give max{0,7z/20−45/√M}≤R_M≤min{1,z+45/√M}. If R_M≤C′/√M eventually, then z≤(20/7)(C′+45)/√M; conversely z≤C/√M implies R_M≤(C+45)/√M. For finite β≥0 this is equivalent to β≥(1/2)ln M−O(1). It is not a separate variance lower bound or an optimal finite-M tuning result, and excludes trajectory-dependent β.
  • Prior work. The cited D’Angelo et al. Proposition 3.1 and equation (8) give the mask-weighted mechanism; Corollary 4.1 gives the BOS hard-match add-α limit. Their Lemma 4.2 is an approximate prior-averaged pseudo-count calibration for the no-BOS interpolation; its displayed formula grows logarithmically in T at fixed positive prior parameters, alphabet and order. It is not the present fixed-source actual-terminal TV criterion. The report correctly avoids claiming interpolation or logarithmic scaling as new.

This review does not establish exhaustive prior-art coverage. It does not independently certify the full Transformer realization beyond the explicitly stated idealized formula.
