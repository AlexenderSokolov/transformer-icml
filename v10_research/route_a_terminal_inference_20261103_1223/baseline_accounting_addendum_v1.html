Baseline timing and head accounting correction

2026-11-03. Additive correction to the unchanged Route A v1 report. The separate output-projection addendum also applies. The candidate remains provisional.

Section 8's statement that the streaming comparator has finished its counts before seeing the terminal pair is wrong. The final observed symbol XN is needed both to finish the update for row SN−1 and to determine the terminal pair SN. The comparator updates that count on seeing XN, forms SN and immediately returns (CS_N+1)/(JS_N+2). It maintains all four row counts and all four successor-one counts; no future symbol is read. The O(N) updates and O(log N) bits per counter are unchanged.

Section 8's “two extra heads” must not be read as the incremental cost relative to the direct-q baseline. Both constructions have the two first-block copying heads. The direct-q construction then uses one matching head; the corrected construction adds one uniform head in the second block, plus the output-selection and ReLU arithmetic. The corrected architecture has four head instances in its two attention blocks. It is not a two-extra-head comparison relative to the direct-q architecture.

A distinct independent reviewer identified these corrections. They do not change the pathwise statistics, TV bounds or asymptotic counts. They do matter for fair same-information comparisons.
