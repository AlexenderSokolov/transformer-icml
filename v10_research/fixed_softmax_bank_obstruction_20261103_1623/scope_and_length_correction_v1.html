Parameter-region and length-input clarification

This immutable clarification applies to analytic_attention_obstruction_v1.html, SHA-256 95631f57531d8e9d0cc31f4bccaee9230367e60ca758529965089a183c83f30d. It preserves the proof and corrects two descriptions.

First, the set 1/10<u<1/5, u/4<v<3u/4 is an open sub-wedge, not a compact rectangle. It suffices for the argument and has π_min≥1/10. A genuinely compact rectangle with nonempty interior contained in it is [1/8,1/6]×[1/20,1/18]. The identity argument applies to any source subdomain with nonempty open interior.

Second, the model excludes an explicit unbounded N or position channel, but an allowed BOS coordinate 1/(M+1) is injective in M and therefore encodes length in an information-theoretic sense. Length is not claimed to be completely hidden. The proof instead relies on fixed finite continuous operations and finite parameters having a finite limit as that BOS coordinate tends to zero. A reciprocal/rounding oracle, unbounded length input, or parameters/scores scaling with N is outside its scope. No inference is made about models with those resources.
