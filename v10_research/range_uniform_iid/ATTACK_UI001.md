# Independent attack on κ-uniform IID floor

2026-11-02. Independent obstruction search; UI001 candidate draft was NOT read. BA001 model was read. No counterexample found; this is not a completed independent verification.

## Findings

1. **Positive denominator forbids steep mean maps.** For any function f of at most r independent Bernoulli(p) bits, p in [1/4,3/4], the score identity gives |(E f)'| <= 4r E|f|. For frozen-query numerator A and positive denominator T, |A|<=T and |A'|,|T'|<=4rT. Thus |(A/T)'|<=8r, independently of κ, positions, arbitrary signed bounded values, or arbitrary real tables. Incommensurate head slopes and fixed correlated dithers cannot make the deterministic comparison curve cover more than O_{D,r}(Q) unit-grid boxes. This alone does NOT control random heavy-weight contributions.

2. **Nonadaptive heavy-envelope conditioning has a genuine pitfall.** A giant maximum may belong to a local pattern that does not occur. Revealing all large-envelope sites can leave actual denominator much smaller than the original envelope sum. Several heads can have mutually exclusive activating patterns. Any proof replacing actual denominator by an unconditional envelope sum must address this.

3. **Adaptive heavy exposure may close exactly that gap.** First reveal finite boundary side information, fixing actual query. For each head, while unresolved terms remain, select one with largest conditional maximum weight and reveal its at most r unknown bits. A trial realizes this maximum with conditional probability at least 4^{-r}. Conditional maxima never increase as bits are revealed. Successful terms are distinct and become fixed. Therefore after k successes, the fixed denominator is at least k times the largest remaining envelope. Taking k=C Q², O_r(Q²) trials suffice with high probability, by conditional Bernoulli domination; repeat across fixed D heads. Stop trivially if no unresolved term remains. This argument does not involve log κ.

4. **Residual concentration calculation to formalize.** Given any adaptive transcript, unexposed bits remain independent Bernoulli(p). An unresolved term has envelope at most b_* <= fixed denominator/(C Q²). For numerator or denominator term Y_i, E[Y_i²] <= b_* E[w_i]. The overlap graph of trailing-r windows has bounded degree <=2r-2, so Var(sum Y_i) <=(2r-1)b_* E[T]. Since E[T]>=fixed denominator, the relative standard deviation is O_r(1/(sqrt(C)Q)). Chebyshev and exact ratio subtraction then put all heads in an O(1/Q) tube with high probability, including random denominator control. Previously fixed terms have zero variance. Conditioning future exposures only decreases envelopes and increases the fixed denominator, preserving earlier heads' stopping certificates.

5. **Why exposing O(Q²) bits should not kill the lower bound.** Cap trial budget deterministically and pad exposed distinct coins to N=O_{D,r}(Q²), whenever n permits; otherwise the full-history c/sqrt(n) bound already implies c_{D,r}/Q. Adaptive positions are determined by previous exposed outcomes, so the transcript likelihood is exactly p^a(1-p)^(N-a), with no extra parameter information from the positions. The revealed empirical average locates p within O(1/Q) with high probability. Conditional comparison maps remain 8r-Lipschitz, so on that parameter interval residual quantized messages occupy only O_{D,r}(1) labels per transcript. One can avoid a false pointwise posterior-density bound at prior endpoints: under the uniform prior, E[sup_p posterior_density(p | transcript)] is O(sqrt(N))=O(Q), by the binomial Shtarkov sum sum_{a=0}^N binom(N,a) sup_{p in I} p^a(1-p)^(N-a) = O(sqrt(N)). Hence R=O(1) candidate decoder outputs per transcript have prior probability O(R Q δ) of δ-accuracy. Combining high-probability good events yields δ~1/Q lower risk. Random shared seeds can be fixed throughout.

## Status and attack boundary

No rigorous asymptotic counterexample was found using positional dominance, signed values, irrational head ratios, correlated offsets, or finite query branches. A full theorem proof still needs explicit budgets/constants, a clean adaptive-transcript construction with early exhaustion, and a proved/referenced elementary Shtarkov bound. The structural argument above is promising and substantially stronger than merely failing to find a counterexample, but this report does not label an unseen candidate accepted.
