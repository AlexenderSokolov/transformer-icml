Correction: fixed dominating measure

3 November 2026. This correction applies to unbounded_alphabet_reduction_v1.html, SHA-256 5e8f3c963d0ba9386fad3e8b77cadb57b0b442293c4167b2b799e3dd40bcd78e, and does not overwrite that file.

Delete the phrase “a single fixed dominating reference measure” from the list of restrictions not covered. Every conditional density in that construction is dominated by the same Lebesgue measure. Because the standard Gaussian density φ is strictly positive everywhere, all are also dominated by the same standard Gaussian probability measure. Thus a fixed dominating reference measure alone does not avoid the obstruction.

What the argument does not establish is an obstruction under uniform upper and lower bounds on the conditional-density ratio to one fixed reference measure, or under the other structural restrictions specified in the original. The Doeblin minorizing probability measure μA in that proof changes with A; this is a different notion from domination by a fixed measure. The lower bound and all other qualifications of the original remain unchanged.
