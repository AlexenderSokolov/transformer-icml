A population-risk obstruction for one fixed histogram readout

3 November 2026. This is a restricted-model result, not a general Transformer impossibility theorem or novelty claim. No random or numerical experiment was used.

Claim. A single length- and source-independent, finite ReLU readout of the specified eight-dimensional normalized context/successor bank cannot have asymptotically vanishing actual-terminal average binary-TV risk simultaneously for all sources in a fixed stationary, uniformly mixing binary order-two family with π_min≥1/10. The same holds if a final binary softmax is applied to two fixed ReLU logits. The witness source depends on the chosen readout. The result does not cover another attention layer, a division oracle, unbounded length/position signals, horizon-dependent parameters, or other residual/value channels.

1. Precisely restricted model

For a length-N trajectory, M=N−2, define J_r and C_r as the number of observed triples whose predecessor pair is r and the number of those followed by 1, respectively. The terminal query is the actual pair S_N. The permitted summary consists only of the following eight normalized coordinates, the terminal pair encoded in a fixed finite representation, and optionally a BOS coordinate 1/(M+1):

U_r=(C_r+1)/(M+1),     V_r=(J_r+2)/(M+1),     r∈{00,01,10,11}.

These are the outputs of the separately described zero-logit uniform value bank, but this note grants them exactly and does not claim how a standard causal attention stack computes them. The readout is a single fixed finite feed-forward ReLU network whose weights do not depend on N or the unknown source. Either its scalar output is clipped to [0,1], or two ReLU-produced logits receive a final binary softmax. No further attention, division, dynamic-depth loop, unbounded positional or length channel, precomputed source-dependent statistic or input reread is allowed. ReLU output can include residual affine operations and skip connections. A numerical implementation whose output converges uniformly to this fixed real computation on the bounded bank as N grows has the same conclusion; no unproved floating-point guarantee is assumed.

This model is narrower than a two-layer standard causal Transformer with unrestricted values and residuals. Training may choose the one readout, but that same choice must serve all lengths and all unknown source parameters. Source-specific or horizon-dependent trained weights fall outside the claim.

2. A well-mixing open family

Choose u∈(1/10,1/5), t∈(1/4,3/4), v=ut and w=(1−u)/3. A stationary binary order-two chain has π=(u,w,w,w) and next-one probabilities

θ₀₀=t=v/u,    θ₀₁=1/2,    θ₁₀=1−v/w,    θ₁₁=1/2.

All legal probabilities are strictly between 0 and 1, and every pair-state mass is ≥1/10. The conditional next-bit law after the actual terminal 00 is Bernoulli(v/u). As shown by the reverse two-step common atom R²(a,11)≥1/8, the reverse uniform mixing sum is bounded by 16 over the entire family. Its forward pair chain is irreducible and aperiodic. These assumptions are stronger than merely requiring an unknown stationary order-two source.

The limiting eight data frequencies, ordered by predecessor row and successor bit, are

00: (u−v, v),     01: (w/2,w/2),
10: (v,w−v),      11: (w/2,w/2).

Thus the bank’s limit b(u,v)=(π_rθ_r,π_r)_r is affine in (u,v) over the nonempty open wedge

Ω = {(u,v): 1/10<u<1/5,  u/4<v<3u/4}.

The BOS/pseudocount terms vanish in this limit. The true 00 target v/u is not affine on any open subset of Ω, since its mixed derivative ∂²(v/u)/(∂u∂v)=−1/u² is nonzero.

3. Finite ReLU readout cannot agree on the whole family

Fix the terminal input to 00. A finite ReLU network is continuous piecewise affine with finitely many polyhedral pieces. Since b(u,v) is affine, its scalar output F(b(u,v),00), after optional clipping, is a finite piecewise-affine function of (u,v). The finitely many cells cover Ω, so at least one has a nonempty open interior. On that cell the network is affine, while v/u cannot be affine on any open set. Hence there exists (u,v)∈Ω for which

Δ = |F(b(u,v),00)−v/u| > 0.

If the final output is instead a two-logit softmax, equality with v/u on the wedge would require the difference of the two fixed ReLU logits to equal log[v/(u−v)]. The latter also has nonzero mixed derivative −1/(u−v)² on Ω and cannot be affine on any open cell. Thus some parameter again has Δ>0. The result applies to any fixed finite width/depth, but Δ may depend on that network; no universal numerical gap over all network sizes is asserted. The finite piecewise-affine property is classical; see Serra et al., affine linear regions of ReLU networks.

4. From the fixed readout gap to actual-terminal risk

Let B_M denote the observed normalized bank and b=b(u,v) its stationary limit for the witness source. The ergodic theorem gives B_M→b almost surely. Define G_M=F(B_M,00), a counterfactual evaluation at a fixed 00 query used only in the proof. Since the clipped or sigmoid output is bounded and continuous, E|G_M−F(b,00)|→0. Stationarity gives P(S_N=00)=u for every N, although S_N and B_M are dependent. Therefore

| E[1{S_N=00}|G_M−t|] − u|F(b,00)−t| |
    ≤ E|G_M−F(b,00)| → 0.

For the actual decoder, the 00 term above is part of its unconditional loss, and all other terminal terms are nonnegative. Hence

liminf_(M→∞) E|Q_M−θ_(S_N)| ≥ uΔ > 0,
lim_(M→∞) E[|Q_M−t| | S_N=00] = Δ > 0.

This is a population-risk obstruction, not merely a single unusual history. The same argument integrated against any source prior with strictly positive density on Ω yields a positive asymptotic average loss for each fixed readout. A prior or rule may not depend on the randomly realized source in a way that reveals t.

5. An explicit finite-M error term

The forward pair chain also has a common two-step atom at 11 of at least 1/8 throughout this family. Its Dobrushin coefficients therefore satisfy δ(P^h)≤(7/8)^⌊h/2⌋. Each of the eight underlying count indicators is a bounded function of one pair-chain edge. Stationary covariance summation gives Var(count)≤(129/4)M. For M≥1, summing Cauchy–Schwarz over the eight bank coordinates and paying the +1/+2 boundary terms yields

E ||B_M−b||₁ ≤ [8√(129M/4)+12]/(M+1).

Let L be any finite ℓ1-Lipschitz constant of the fixed clipped readout F(·,00) on the bounded bank domain; for a fixed two-logit ReLU network followed by sigmoid such a constant also exists. Then, without conditioning on an independent query,

E|Q_M−θ_(S_N)| ≥ uΔ − L[8√(129M/4)+12]/(M+1).

This becomes positive for large enough M for the witness source. Neither L nor Δ is uniform across all choices of network, so this is not a dimension-independent or finite-sample minimax lower bound.

6. Why this does not prove a full-network impossibility

An unrestricted continuous readout can evade the population obstruction on sources with π_min≥μ>0. For instance F(U_s,V_s)=clip[U_s/max(V_s,μ/2)] is continuous on the bounded bank and tends to θ_s for every such stationary ergodic source. It uses division, which the finite ReLU readout above does not provide exactly. A sufficiently large fixed ReLU network can approximate that continuous map to any chosen fixed error on a compact domain, so there is no source-averaged error floor uniform over all finite network sizes. The claim is only that no one fixed finite ReLU readout has zero limiting error simultaneously over the whole family.

An additional softmax/attention layer, position-dependent states, a length signal growing with N, an explicit ratio or rounding operation, data-dependent features, horizon-specific weights, or training with source-revealing information can change the conclusion. The existing length-indexed compact-causal construction uses different, N-dependent scores and a specialized numerical backend; it is not contradicted.
