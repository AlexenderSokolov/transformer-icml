Independent review: four fixed-temperature anchor heads

3 November 2026. Independent analytical review, not formal peer review or a verification of a standard Transformer backend. No random operations, sampling, tests, source edits or external writes were used.

Reviewed artifact: four_anchor_inversion_v1.html, SHA-256 f3cb56814c417e88568634b01cf3f4005714d95ee548fef715c6c6ef65478fc3.

Disposition: no mathematical blocker found within the specified ideal access model. The construction has four distinct anchor queries, two output coordinates per head, an explicit normalization reference and a nonlinear readout; it does not contradict a necessity theorem for a single fixed scalar soft-match output.

Checks

  • Keys and exact algebra. For every historical triple i=3,…,N, the score of context r at anchor h exponentiates to 2^matches(h,r). With one zero-score BOS of value (0,1), Z_h=1+Σ_rK_hr J_r, a_h=(KC)_h/Z_h, b_h=1/Z_h. Therefore a_h/b_h=KC and 1/b_h−1=KJ. BOS provides an independent measurement of the normalization; the +1 and +2 in the final add-one estimate are introduced later by readout. Candidate i=N uses the last observed successor X_N and no future bit.
  • Matrix and conditioning. K=B⊗B with B=[[2,1],[1,2]]. Since B⁻¹=(1/3)[[2,−1],[−1,2]], the displayed inverse is correct. B has eigenvalues 3 and 1; K is symmetric positive definite with singular values 9,3,3,1, condition number 9 and row-absolute-sum inverse norm 1. With a=e^β>1, eigenvalues of [[a,1],[1,a]] are a±1, giving condition number ((a+1)/(a−1))² for K; at β=0 the matrix is singular.
  • Pathwise result and empty history. Inverting the four recovered measurements gives all C_r and J_r for each individual input sequence. Selecting the actual final pair s and returning (C_s+1)/(J_s+2) is exactly same-history add-one, even for J_s=0. For M=0 the BOS alone gives (a_h,b_h)=(0,1), so C=J=0 and the final output is 1/2. The statement is pathwise and does not require a source or statistical independence.
  • Precision inequality. Put L=1+4M; then 1≤Z_h≤L and b_h≥1/L. If both output coordinates have absolute error ≤η≤1/(2L), then b̃_h≥b_h/2. The reciprocal difference is ≤2ηL². For the ratio, |ã/b̃−a/b|≤η/b̃+(a/b)η/b̃≤2ηL[1+(L−1)]=2ηL² because 0≤a/b=(KC)_h≤4M=L−1. Applying ||K⁻¹||∞=1 preserves the infinity-norm bound. The strict choice η<1/(8L²) makes every recovered count error <1/4, permitting unique nearest-integer recovery. This sufficient absolute-precision condition is O(log M) fractional bits when a format covering [0,1], including endpoints, is granted; it is not asserted necessary for all schemes.
  • Scope, accounting and references. Context keys, successor values, support and final terminal pair are explicitly granted or produced at additional cost. Exact logits, softmax, BOS indicators, arithmetic, copying, masks and output rounding are separate implementation obligations. The four anchors need not equal the terminal query; signed inversion plus reciprocal/ratio operations lie outside the prior scalar port. The β=0 bank instead assumes eight enriched value features and known support size and likewise pays routing and readout. The report limits inherited statistical guarantees to source classes where separately established. Its external comparison to Rajaraman et al., Theorems 4.4–4.5 is qualified; their constructions use different architectures/limits and Remark 4.2 states a precision qualification. The cited internal fingerprint is correctly identified as not an accessible proof link.

No claim of general fixed-depth finite-word Transformer realization, formal verification, lower bound for unrestricted networks, or novelty is established by this check.
