Independent review: analytic homogeneous-attention obstruction

3 November 2026. Scoped independent analytical review, not formal peer review, exhaustive novelty clearance or a general Transformer lower-bound certificate. No numerical tests, random operations, source edits or external writes were used.

Reviewed artifact: analytic_attention_obstruction_v1.html, SHA-256 95631f57531d8e9d0cc31f4bccaee9230367e60ca758529965089a183c83f30d.

Disposition: The analytic obstruction is valid under the declared fixed finite, position-homogeneous interface. One wording correction is required: the example described as a “compact rectangle” is actually an open sub-wedge, neither a rectangle nor compact. It can instead be called an open sub-wedge; the same analytic argument works from any nonempty open subset of the larger wedge.

  • Positive type frequencies and common domain. On Ω, p₀₀,₀=u−v and p₀₀,₁=v are positive; each 01 and 11 probability is w/2>0; p₁₀,₀=v>0 and p₁₀,₁=w−v>0 since w>1/4 and v<3u/4<3/16. They sum to 1. The entire ray (λu,λv), 0<λ≤1, remains in Ω. At its endpoint (0,0), the 00 and 10,0 masses vanish, but the other masses sum to 1.
  • Frozen branches and denominators. Freeze all finitely many ReLU and clipping branch choices. Their affine continuations, finite products and fixed-size softmax modules are real-analytic; fixed-size softmax denominators are sums of positive exponentials. At every point of Ω and at the origin, each historical-head denominator Σ_a p_a exp(ℓ_a) is strictly positive, even if the frozen branch is not the active branch at that point, because all branch scores are finite. By continuity it stays positive in a neighborhood of the origin. The intersection of the finitely many positive-denominator domains contains Ω and a neighborhood of the origin; the latter intersects Ω near the origin, making their union connected and lying in one connected analytic component. This explicitly supplies the connectivity step, not merely positivity at two disconnected points.
  • Identity principle. Finitely many closed activation-pattern regions cover the open wedge (or any open sub-wedge). At least one has nonempty interior. If a fixed model matched v/u everywhere on that open source set, on that interior the branch analytic function G=uF−v would vanish. The real-analytic identity theorem extends this zero identity over the connected positive-denominator component, including an origin neighborhood. But ∂G/∂v at (0,0) equals −1 because F is analytic and finite there. Contradiction. A final fixed-size binary softmax is still analytic within each branch. This establishes a source-dependent positive limiting gap for every fixed model, not a uniform constant over all weights or widths.
  • History, special tokens, risk. At fixed source and counterfactual query 00, empirical triple-type frequencies and normalized bank converge by ergodicity. A bounded number of special tokens has finite score and value on the convergent bounded inputs, so its mass is O(1/M) and vanishes relative to historical types. The attention denominator stays positive. Bounded convergence transfers the limiting prediction, and stationarity gives P(S_N=00)=u. The indicator inequality used in the note requires no independence between terminal and history. It yields conditional-00 limiting loss Δ and unconditional liminf at least uΔ; continuity supplies a positive-prior-loss neighborhood for a density positive on the source set.
  • No conflict with bypasses. Four-anchor recovery divides by the BOS coordinate, applies nonlinear inverse normalization and rounding, which are explicitly excluded. A continuous reciprocal readout on a source class bounded away from zero is not a finite network of the allowed analytic/ReLU form with denominators remaining positive through the origin. Position-varying representations, N-growing scores, hard masks and other residual inputs are excluded. The result is not an impossibility for general causal Transformers, parameters trained separately per source or length, or arbitrary numerical implementations.

The cited identity principle and histogram/attention precursors are established methods. This check does not decide novelty or verify an external archive.
