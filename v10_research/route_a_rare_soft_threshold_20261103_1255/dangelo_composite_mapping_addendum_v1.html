Composite-token mapping and mask nuance

2026-11-03. Addendum to the unchanged prior-art comparison. D’Angelo et al.'s order-2 construction compares the two coordinates separately: one-coordinate and full matches receive different weights, so its ordinary order-2 output is not literally the binary full-match-versus-nonmatch q in this candidate.

Instead, define composite tokens z_j=(X_{j−1},X_j) for j=2,…,N, alphabet size four, apply the paper's no-BOS k=1 mask construction to z_2,…,z_N, and project each z_i successor value to its second coordinate X_i. Query z_N=(X_{N−1},X_N) is compared with predecessor z_{i−1}=(X_{i−2},X_{i−1}) for candidates i=3,…,N. This gives the same ideal two-weight q. The composite-token binding, excluded boundaries, projection, copying and numerical errors must be explicitly paid; the correspondence is a statement about the ideal finite-match estimator, not free implementation in an ordinary dense decoder.

The paper's Corollary 4.1 uses BOS and an infinite-β limit for exact add-α. Appendix D.3 approximates β selection using prior-dependent log-loss and approximations; it does not by itself prove a q/r/h-only adversarial-port lower bound or the specific rare-row conditioned TV inequality. Neither difference establishes novelty.
