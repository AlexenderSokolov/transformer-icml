Exact antecedent comparison: Route A versus Rajaraman and D’Angelo

2026-11-03. This is an additive correction and source-specific comparison; it does not change or accept the earlier seven-block candidate. Primary sources read at the statements cited below, together with the Gate 0 audit. It does not certify novelty or exhaust all prior work.

Rajaraman et al. (NeurIPS 2024)

Definition 2.1 of arXiv v1 defines conditional empirical k-gram as the count of matching k-symbol contexts followed by a symbol, divided by the matching-context count. For k=2, sequence length N and output symbol 1, the predecessor index i=3,…,N corresponds exactly to context (X_{i−2},X_{i−1}) and successor X_i; its terminal query is (X_{N−1},X_N). Hence its target is C_s/J_s on the very same prefix for J_s>0. It is not an independently sampled query. The authors explicitly leave their displayed ratio undefined at J_s=0.

arXiv v1 Theorem 4.4, corresponding to conference Theorem 2, states a two-layer attention-only representation with k heads and d=(k+2)S+k+1. For k=S=2 these are two layers, k=2 heads in the specified construction and d=11. The arXiv v1 Theorem 4.5 / conference Theorem 3 also gives L=ceil(log₂(k+1)), one head per layer, relative positions, d≤2k(S+1)+S: for k=S=2, L=2 and d≤14. This is a strong direct antecedent to any claim that constant-depth attention represents the same-prefix order-two empirical frequency. It is not appropriate to present our seven-block structured-mask realization as a first, better or necessary architecture for this task.

The source proof uses positional embeddings and increasingly concentrated softmax for the target matching pattern; attention-only arXiv Remark 4.2 explicitly asserts O(log T)-bit implementation achieves O(1/T) additive error. This report has not independently verified that precision assertion in a detailed numerical model, but it must not be omitted. By contrast, arXiv Theorem 5.1 / conference Theorem 4 is the distinct three-layer, one-head-per-layer, layer-normalized result; its Remark 5.2 / conference Remark 2 says the proof has not bounded finite-bit approximation error and expresses a precision expectation. Do not interchange theorem numbers or those precision statements.

Our seven-block candidate uses additional exact structural singleton and prefix-exclusion masks, finite β=log 2 at its soft head, an extra uniform head, and paid ReLU arithmetic to approximate (C_s+1)/(J_s+2) even at J_s=0. These are differences in declared mechanism and boundary treatment, not by themselves evidence of lower resource cost, new expressivity or novelty. In particular the finite-K spline does not generally output the exact 1/2 fallback; its error is bounded and a counterexample is recorded in the Route A addendum. Direct counters are a stronger same-information practical baseline. No full fixed-bit or standard-dense-mask comparison has been proved.

D’Angelo et al. (ICML 2026)

Proposition 3.1 already supplies the relevant finite-match weighted count/mask identity on arbitrary valid paths; it covers the composite-symbol one-token equality mapping underlying q after explicitly paying or assuming pair binding. Corollary 4.1 obtains symmetric add-α with BOS in a β→∞ limit. Neither a finite-β mixture identity nor add-one smoothing alone is an original claim here. Appendix D.3 already studies approximate optimal β under a Dirichlet task prior and log-loss using approximation assumptions; do not treat β calibration in the abstract as new. The present candidate's conditional fixed-source unsquared TV, same dependent terminal query, and explicit finite-N error bound are a different stated experiment; this observation is not a novelty certificate.

For finite β the original proof's lag copying and early-key exclusion use limiting score choices, as described in the local D’Angelo independent audit. Our hard singleton masks are an additional structured access permission, not a finite-logit implementation of that original dense-causal copier. The two papers' output and normalization conventions also require alignment before any equal-resource comparison.

Research consequences

The candidate direct-q result should be framed as an elementary quantitative calibration of a restricted soft estimator on the specified rare, fast-mixing family, with explicit terminal-conditioning error. Any precision collision derived at a q/r/h-only cut is a same-history comparator-fidelity statement at that isolated cut. It is not a lower bound on the full transformer, arbitrary residual channels, finite-bit optimality, or true θ-risk without a separate statistical argument. Match Rajaraman, D’Angelo, prior accepted R011 and the same-history counter before any contribution or efficiency claim.
