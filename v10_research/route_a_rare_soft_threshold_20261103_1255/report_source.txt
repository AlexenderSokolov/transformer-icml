ROUTE A: FINITE-HISTORY SOFT-SCORE AND PORT-PRECISION THRESHOLDS
2026-11-03. Status: mathematical candidate, not novelty certification, full-network necessity, accepted theorem, finite-bit implementation, or complete proof-validation process. This artifact extends the provisional route_a_terminal_report_v3, whose structured-mask and exact-real/conditional-error scope remains in force. The rare-family v3 and its scope clarification supply the transition and two-step common-atom facts. Exact finite examples use no randomness. Independent mathematical review of the direct-head bound and separate independent review of the restricted-port collision are recorded separately; their verdicts are bounded, not external peer review.

1. Experiment and quantities
Take N≥3, m=N−2, and a stationary homogeneous binary second-order chain with π=(u,w,w,w), w=(1−u)/3, 0<u<1/4, t∈[1/4,3/4], θ=(t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2), ordered states 00,01,10,11. Only X_1:N is visible; the actual terminal state S_N=(X_{N−1},X_N) is drawn from that same path. Put v=mu and condition on S_N=00. Let J be the number of earlier departures from 00, C the number followed by 1, T the pooled number of 1 successors among all m departures, h=J/m and r=T/m. Then P(S_N=00)=u, θ_00=t and E r=μ=2w. The conditional-query risk of a Bernoulli prediction is E[|prediction−t| | S_N=00]. Target X_{N+1} never enters the input.

For a finite score β≥0 set γ=e^β−1≥0 and define the *uncorrected direct output* q_β=(T+γC)/(m+γJ). This is one soft head's output, not the full network. At β=0 it is the pooled predictor r; for β→∞ and J>0 it converges to C/J. Full residuals, uniform heads, hard counting, smoothing or correction circuits remain allowed alternatives in the problem as a whole; no lower bound below applies to them.

2. Explicit finite-N terminal-risk calibration
For v≥60 and δ∈(0,1), define
 ℓ=log[2(m+1)/(uδ)],
 D₀=√(31/v)+2√[3ℓ/(4v)]+2[15/v+√(305/(4v))]+δ+1220/v.       (1)
For every γ≥0 and every t∈[1/4,3/4],
 | E[|q_β−t| | S_N=00] − |μ−t|/(1+γu) | ≤ D₀.              (2)
No queried-row iid or independence from the terminal event is assumed. The bound may be larger than one; it is not a claim of a sharp rate. It is uniform over β, but the numerical threshold v≥60 and the dependence on u,N,δ are explicit.

Proof. Let Z=C−tJ and q̄=(μ+γut)/(1+γu), a deterministic source-level quantity, not a sample statistic. From the reverse chain in the rare-family v3, its two-step probability of 11 is ≥1/8 from every state. Writing ρ=7/8, ∑_{j≥1}ρ^{floor(j/2)}=15. Conditioning on S_N=00 gives |E[J]−v|≤15 and Var(J)≤61(v+15), as derived in route_a_terminal_report_v3. Thus
 P(|J−v|>v/2 | S_N=00) ≤ 1220/v,                          (3)
where v≥60 ensures |EJ−v|≤15≤v/4 and 61(v+15)/(v/4)²≤1220/v. Moreover E[|J−v| | S_N=00]≤15+√[61(v+15)]≤15+√[(305/4)v].

For any λ, the forward predictable-indicator Bernoulli differences give E exp(λZ−λ²J/8)≤1. On each fixed J=j≥1 choose the corresponding λ, union-bound over j and signs, and then divide by P(S_N=00)=u. With conditional probability ≥1−δ,
 |Z|≤√[Jℓ/2].                                              (4)
At J=0, Z=0. This conditioning does not preserve or assume a forward martingale; the bound was obtained before conditioning.

The *forward* pair chain also has a two-step common atom of mass ≥1/8. Since each X_i is a bounded function of the current pair, stationarity and the common-atom contraction imply |Cov(X_i,X_{i+j})|≤ρ^{floor(j/2)}. The m-term pooled successor average thus obeys Var(r)≤(m/4+2m·15)/m²≤31/m. Since P(S_N=00)=u, Cauchy–Schwarz yields E[|r−μ| | S_N=00]≤√[31/(mu)]=√(31/v); this requires no independence.

On the event |J−v|≤v/2 and (4), write h=J/m. Algebra gives
 q_β−q̄ = [r−μ+γZ/m+γ(t−q̄)(h−u)]/(1+γh).                (5)
There h≥u/2 and J≤3v/2. Take conditional expectations of absolute values, use |t−q̄|≤1, the pooled bound, and the conditional first absolute moment of J. The γZ/m term is at most γu√[3ℓ/(4v)]/(1+γu/2); the occupation deviation term is at most γu[15/v+√(305/(4v))]/(1+γu/2). Since γu/(1+γu/2)≤2 and 1/(1+γu/2)≤1, and |q_β−q̄|≤1 on the exceptional event, these terms plus δ+1220/v are at most D₀. Finally ||q_β−t|−|q̄−t||≤|q_β−q̄| and |q̄−t|=|μ−t|/(1+γu), proving (2).

3. Necessary/sufficient direct-head score thresholds
Choose t=1/2 and write c(u)=|2w−1/2|=(1−4u)/6. This coefficient tends to zero as u↑1/4; no lower bound uniform over the whole u interval is claimed. For any target ε>0, (2) implies that, if the direct-head conditional risk is at most ε, then necessarily
 γu ≥ [c(u)/(ε+D₀)−1]_+.                                   (6)
If ε>D₀, the following is sufficient:
 γu ≥ [c(u)/(ε−D₀)−1]_+.                                   (7)
Translate either condition to β by β≥log(1+ right-hand side/u). Equality cases and the positive-part convention cover β=0. The necessary condition is necessary only for the uncorrected q_β output at this fixed t, not for general attention networks, heads with extra features, or all estimators. The sufficient condition is a conservative upper bound, not the optimal score or a matched minimax theorem.

For a regime u≤1/8, ε small relative to c(u) and D₀≤ε/2, c(u)≥1/12 and (6)–(7) show the direct-head threshold has scale e^β−1≈1/(uε), with constants depending on the chosen ε-to-c(u) range. If ε is near or larger than c(u), or u↑1/4, that simplification is wrong. If δ=1/m, then D₀→0 whenever v→∞ and log(2m(m+1)/u)/v→0. In particular a fixed β with u→0 retains a positive direct-head bias along such a sequence, but if u is fixed and β→∞ this obstruction vanishes. No necessity for depth L follows.

4. Same-history add-one and a sharpened port perturbation formula
The comparator B=(C+1)/(J+2) is defined for all J, including B=1/2 at J=0. The earlier v3 report gives a structured-mask finite-logit realization approximating B, and a conditional TV-risk upper; its finite spline does not exactly preserve the empty-row fallback. On J>0,
 |q_β−B|≤1/(1+γh)+1/(J+2);                              (8)
the first term is the soft-head mismatch to C/J and the second is |C/J−B|. Thus on J≥v/2 and β large the direct output also approaches the same-history baseline, consistent with (2), and Rajaraman's existing conditional k-gram representation is the relevant hard-match comparator.

For γ>0 consider the isolated q/r/h arithmetic interface, q',r',h'∈[0,1] with |q'−q|≤e_q, |r'−r|≤e_r, |h'−h|≤e_h, and the real decoder B'=clip([m((1+γh')q'−r')/γ+1]/[mh'+2],0,1). The following pathwise inequality refines the v3 coarse bound:
 |B'−B|≤ [(1/γ+h)e_q+e_r/γ+(|q−B|+e_q)e_h] / [h−e_h+2/m], (9)
whenever h−e_h+2/m>0. If that denominator is not positive, use the always-positive exact denominator h'+2/m in the preceding derivation instead, or the v3 bound with (h−e_h)_+.

Proof. The unprojected fraction minus B has numerator m[(1/γ+h)(q'−q)−(r'−r)/γ+(q'−B)(h'−h)] over mh'+2. Take absolute values, use |q'−B|≤|q−B|+e_q, then h'≥h−e_h; projection cannot increase distance to B. This identity exposes the individual input sensitivities rather than charging one undifferentiated precision b.

For h∈[u/2,3u/2], e_h≤u/4 and γu>0, (8)–(9) imply
 |B'−B| ≤ [6+4/(γu)]e_q + [4/(γu)]e_r
       + (4e_h/u)[1/(1+γu/2)+2/(v+4)+e_q].             (10)
Hence large γu reduces the effect of q,r perturbations and may also reduce sensitivity to h because q is close to B; an O(e_h/u) worst-case bound can be loose. These are sufficient port-level inequalities with exact real arithmetic in the readout. They do not establish bit counts for computing exp, accumulation, intermediate states or arbitrary residual pathways. To combine with a finite ReLU spline, use the separate v3 approximation and final-saturation errors and charge all additional operations.

5. Deterministic q/r/h-only collision at positive and large J
The following is a restricted same-history comparator-fidelity lower, not a lower bound on true θ-risk. Fix n≥1, k≥0 and integer d∈[1,n]. Start both words with 00. Let length-six suffix blocks A=010100 and B=100100. Appending either to a word ending in 00 returns it to 00; each contributes six departures, two pooled successor ones and two departures from 00. A contributes one successor 1 from 00, B contributes two. Construct word W_A by n A blocks and W_B by replacing exactly d of them with B. Append the same suffix F=10(10)^k0, which returns to 00 and contributes 3+2k departures, J increment 1, C increment 1 and k+1 pooled ones.

Both words end in 00 and have
 m=6n+3+2k,  J=2n+1,  T=2n+k+1,
 C_A=n+1,     C_B=n+d+1.
Thus r=T/m and h=J/m agree exactly; q_B−q_A=γd/(m+γJ), while B_B−B_A=d/(J+2). An adversary allowed to perturb *only q* by at most
 e_q=γd/[2(m+γJ)]                                           (11)
can output the midpoint on both words, with exact r,h, exact terminal pair and public m,β. For any common decoder using only that specified port, including source-independent randomization, the maximum of its expected absolute errors relative to the respective B values is at least
 d/[2(J+2)].                                                (12)
Proof: identical observations have the same decoder law, and the triangle inequality gives E|Y−B_A|+E|Y−B_B|≥|B_B−B_A|. This applies to every decoder on the isolated port, not just an inverse formula. No output or raw-history bypass is allowed; if the full transformer exposes any such bypass, (12) cannot be asserted for it.

Taking d=n gives J=2n+1 and a fidelity floor tending to 1/4 while e_q=γn/[2(m+γJ)]. By taking k large one can make h=J/m small while taking n large, including J→∞. More generally varying d quantifies comparator-accuracy resolution in (11)–(12). These are positive-probability words for each allowed u,t, but the proof uses no probability mass lower bound and no two-source testing. Because both terminal targets equal t when the source is held fixed, this collision does NOT prove a lower bound on E[|prediction−t| | S_N=00] and is not a necessary precision condition for predicting the unknown kernel. That is a separate open question.

6. Architecture, prior art and limits
The q head is realized with finite scores 0 and β on the two context classes only after causal pair binding and boundary access have been specified. The v3 structured realization uses two paid singleton lag-copy heads and two second-block heads; its β=log 2 corrected decoder adds a uniform head and ReLU arithmetic to the direct-q model. Later exact clipping/complement handling is separately charged. Standard dense-causal copying at finite score, full bit/exp costs, L-N lower bounds, and performance superiority over a direct counter are not proved.

Rajaraman et al. NeurIPS 2024, conference Theorems 2/3 (arXiv v1 Theorems 4.4/4.5), already represent the same conditional k-gram C/J at k=S=2 in two attention-only layers, with either two heads in the first construction or one head/layer in the second; Definition 2.1 leaves J=0 undefined. Theorem 4 of the conference (arXiv 5.1) instead is three layers with layer normalization. Attention-only arXiv Remark 4.2 asserts O(log N) bits achieve O(1/N) error; its detailed precision model is not independently verified here. Do not claim the seven-block construction as a first architecture for the empirical conditional frequency.

D’Angelo et al. Proposition 3.1 and Corollary 4.2 already supply soft-match interpolation, and Appendix D.3 already approximates optimal β in a different Bayesian log-loss setting. For the exact binary full-state-match q here, apply their k=1 construction to composite z_j=(X_{j−1},X_j), project the successor to its final bit and pay for binding/copying/boundary logic; their usual k=2 per-coordinate scores do not give this same two-weight q automatically. Their Corollary 4.1 obtains add-α via BOS in an infinite-score limit. See prior_art_comparison_v1.html and dangelo_composite_mapping_addendum_v1.html. This report's fixed-source, terminal-conditioned finite-N TV bound and isolated adversarial-port collision have not been matched to all literature; no novelty claim follows from their wording.

R011 already gives unconditional actual-terminal add-one TV upper bounds, including the arbitrary order-two source case, and SM001 already uses stationary reverse conditioning under a stronger two-step π-minorization. The risk in (2) is conditional on the rare terminal row and studies a restricted direct q head; do not present a standard add-one statistical rate, reverse conditioning, β calibration or q/r/h inversion as a first result. Report the dependence c(u), compare same-history add-one and a streaming count implementation, and keep conditional-versus-overall risk separate. The 972 exact-rational collision cases and script/log are diagnostic checks, not stochastic proof, prior-art clearance or evidence of general network necessity. No sampling, RNG or external publication was used.
