The proposed corollary follows from (2). For the lower-score regime, v=m^(a/2), γu≤m^(−a/2), ℓ=O(log m), D₀→0, and c(u)→1/6, uniformly for deterministic 0≤β_m≤(1−a)log m; hence the conditional expected direct-q loss tends to 1/6.
