## Exactly accepted conclusions

For the binary protected-terminal-pair experiment, one predictor sees the full corrupted word, may know the supplied all-state certificate beta, and does not know the actual strict edit budget k.

- **Weak certificate:** beta<=C/M^2, fixed C<infinity. Ordinary add-one attains the known-k binary profile for every budget and every initial law, with a constant depending on C. The estimator itself is independent of beta and C.
- **Strict intermediate curve:** on every prescribed curve with M beta_M->0 and M^2 beta_M->infinity, no such full-history predictor attains the profile with one finite constant over all actual budgets, even if that constant and predictor may depend on the curve. Stationary witnesses suffice. The accepted BK002 finite constrained-risk inequality is applied to the same actual-beta estimator map using source-class nesting and k=floor(beta^-1/2), not by silently changing supplied metadata.
- **Strong certificate:** beta>=c/sqrt(M), fixed c>0. Ordinary add-one attains the profile for every budget and every initial law, with a constant depending on c. The algorithm is independent of c and beta.

This is a PARTIAL phase diagram only. In particular the full-history interval from beta of order1/M to below order M^-1/2 remains unresolved by KA001. A histogram-only obstruction must not be promoted to a full-history impossibility. For power laws beta=M^-gamma, KA001 proves positive gamma<=1/2 or gamma>=2 and negative1<gamma<2, leaving1/2<gamma<=1 open in full history.
