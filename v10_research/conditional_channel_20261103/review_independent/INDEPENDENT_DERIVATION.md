# Independent pre-candidate derivation

2026-11-03. Derived before author candidate was present. This is not yet source-bound review or root acceptance. Accepted UMC001/UWC001/CAF001/CMF001 candidates and receipts were inspected; their statistical and lower-bound dependencies remain imported, not freshly reproved.

Let F0 contain the entire source/corruption experiment and all independent protocol coins, so the no-feedback input word and counterfactual exact and baseline losses are F0-measurable. Let Fi add outputs through time i. Assume each conditional output law given F(i-1) has row-TV distance at most alpha from the nominal row indexed by the actual input. Restrict the adversary to one kernel on all prestates/histories satisfying this condition. Conditioning on actual complete coins is necessary, not merely conditioning on transmitted bits or the public seed. This condition allows adversarial dependence on prestate, previous outputs, and current input, and is stronger than a statement about marginal fidelity.

For bounded adapted indicators Zi with conditional means mi, Hoeffding's conditional log-mgf bound is E[exp(t(Zi-mi))|F(i-1)]<=exp(t^2/8). Iteration gives the sum bound exp(nt^2/8). A deterministic conditional drift gap gamma therefore gives both threshold errors <=exp(-2n gamma^2), conditional on F0 or any block-start sigma-field. Independent errors are unnecessary. More generally, conditional event bounds Pr(Ei|past)<=p imply Pr(intersection over any ordered subset S of Ei|initial)<=p^|S| by removing the last selected event with the tower property. Nonselected intervals cause no problem because earlier events are measurable at later selected block starts.

CAF disjoint supports: per-use tentative error <=alpha. The subset lemma proves the same majority union bound 2^R alpha^(R/2). CAF unequal overlapping supports: define designated input-1 slot's E event C and input-0 slot's E event W. Conditional on pair start, Pr(C)>=p-alpha and Pr(W)<=alpha, even when slot two reacts to slot one. Correct-only event C\W has probability >=p-2alpha; wrong-only W\C has probability <=alpha. At alpha<=(p/24)^2<=p/4, correct-only>=p/2. This does NOT require or imply identical ternary probabilities for the two semantic inputs. For pairs' nonerasure indicators, conditional drift >=p/2 gives abstention <=exp(-np^2/8). Conditional wrong-block probability <=alpha gives the same Chernoff bound by multiplying conditional inequalities 1+alpha(e^t-1)<=exp(alpha(e^t-1)), hence wrong nonabstained <=alpha^(pn/16). The original risk accounting holds because the counterfactual baseline loss is F0-measurable. No marginal event probability is multiplied by a correlated baseline risk.

UWC: every repetition block has conditional hard error <=exp(-8)<2^-8 at its start. For inner-code competitor distance s, nearest-Hamming defeat entails ceil(s/2) hard errors among disagreement positions. The subset bound yields 2^s(2^-8)^(s/2)=8^-s, hence inner conditional error <=2^-8k. Apply the subset lemma again at outer-symbol block starts. At least K+1 inner errors cost <=binom(4K,K+1)2^(-8k(K+1))<=2^-6B. Deterministic code design, field algebra, payload, validation and resource costs are unchanged.

All accepted memoryless lower witnesses lie in the stronger conditional class, uniformly over all histories and prestates. Therefore CMF lower necessities, including near-midpoint chi-square transport and no-free-data Fano, are imported unchanged, not extended via a new chain rule for adaptive channels. Their far/near upper splice also survives because both upper ingredients just proved survive. The midpoint iid identical-row witness still gives exact supplemental equality to baseline and whole-message risk >=1/8. No feedback or path-dependent free data is introduced.

Marginal-only insufficiency: define a precise weaker class by conditioning each coordinate on complete F0 but NOT earlier channel outputs. For fixed finite R, let eta=min(alpha,1)>0. With probability 1-eta generate the nominal product channel, and with probability eta replace the ENTIRE transcript by iid uniform symbols on the full output alphabet, independently of F0. Each coordinate conditional on F0 is (1-eta)P_hi+eta U, within TV eta<=alpha of its nominal row. For arbitrary randomized code, on replacement the decoder is baseline-only (supplemental) or independent of IID Bernoulli parameter p (whole-message). Its loss contribution is nonnegative and at least eta times the baseline minimax factor in the former experiment; the latter has worst clean risk >=eta/8 by averaging p uniformly over [1/4,3/4]. Thus any fixed positive marginal radius precludes bounded adaptation at arbitrary finite schedules, even for nominal noiseless channels and every code. A global flip is not a valid all-code impossibility proof because complement-aware redundancy can defeat it. Global uniform replacement is.

Independent exact finite checks enumerate optimal adaptive Bernoulli policies by dynamic programming, adaptive pair-policy vertices, whole-transcript replacement marginals, and coding rounding inequalities. They corroborate these steps but are not universal proofs.
