## WPC001: complete deterministic audit

For `l=r−2`, the frozen departures `0,...,l−1` end at pair `(X_l,X_(l+1))`, hence determine exactly the first r symbols. Similarly frozen departures `h,...,M−1`, with `h=M−r+2`, determine the last r symbols. There are `l + M−h = 2r−4` frozen edges. Retaining equal interior length fixes the absolute suffix attachment point. These arguments include r=2, empty frozen edge lists, and touching/overlapping window endpoints under `2r≤M+2`.

Deleting only the two self-loop types leaves a valid pair path and neither removes the availability nor reverses the order of visits to two distinct states. Thus ordered interior 11 then 00 visits are exactly the needed condition for inserting positive A and B blocks in that order while retaining the skeleton. If either loop count vanishes, its state need not be visited; the nonempty type still has an available vertex because it was deleted there. If both vanish identity is valid. There is no claim that failure of this condition rules out every conceivable alternative transformation.

Appending a loop at11 contributes one distinguished 1 symbol, not three; likewise at00 it contributes one distinguished 0. The initial pair contributes exactly two other ones. The other appended ones are `u+v+w+f1`, and zeros are `p+(u+1)+(v+1)+f0`. Thus the exact o,z,q formulas are correct. Endpoint flows give `N100=u+1`, `N110=v+1`, and `c=p+v+u+1+w`, in particular `c≥1` and `u≤c−1`. The target suffix index is `J=B+u+f0`, not a trailing-run count.

The ordered-block Hamming lemma follows from the two exact mismatch identities. If any distinguished one lies after the target cut, all distinguished zeros follow it, so zeros before the cut are at most z. Otherwise all ones after the cut are among the o other ones. Empty A is handled by the latter case; empty B by the same logic with its absence vacuous. No unstated ordering of other symbols is required. Substitution gives `z+q≤2c+2f0` and `2o+z−q≤3c+3+2f1`; `f0,f1≤2r−4` yields the announced `3c+4r−5`. For j≥2 and the legal j≤M range, both null and output end00, so the entire Hamming distance is charged to editable coordinates only.

**Editorial finding, not a mathematical failure:** the received preservation paragraph said exact preservation included “triples crossing from each frozen region into the interior.” This could be read as claiming unchanged positional labels of unfrozen boundary-crossing triples. That is not needed and is generally false. The true statement is frozen edge labels and their endpoint symbols are unchanged; the first/last r symbols are unchanged; unfrozen triples can move/change positional label while the complete histogram is preserved. Root agreed and the author was asked for a minimal clarification with genuine before/diff provenance. Received snapshots are retained here. No theorem, construction, or bound requires modification.

## MW002: source, conditioning, and finite constants

The displayed stationary vector satisfies `Pi P=Pi`. Multiplying the transition matrix gives exactly the sixteen displayed P² entries. On the asserted parameter range, each is at least t/2, including `(1−t)t` and `(1−a)a`; `t/2≥1/M` and `Pi(v)≤1` suffice for every all-state certificate entry. `a≥At≥2/M` gives `a−beta≥a/2`. The four displayed length-three paths have probability at least `lambda=a/4`, including at a=1/4. Thus their common atom provides a valid block minorization and coupling, not a false global square-root-beta mixing claim.

For edge indicators at separation h≥1, condition on the post-edge state; contraction applies over h−1 transitions to a [0,1]-valued next-edge probability. This gives covariance magnitude at most `p alpha_(h−1)`. The geometric sum is `3/lambda`, so `Var V≤7Mp/lambda`. State-count variance follows in the same manner. Dependence of adjacent edges and random coupling time is not ignored. Filling minorization blocks with conditional bridges preserves the original path laws. Prior to a meeting at boundary sigma there are at most sigma differing departures/edges; afterwards paths coincide. Therefore the claimed count comparison is pathwise and `E sigma≤12/a` is valid.

Symbol-reversed stationary reversal indeed obeys `R P* R=P`; conditioning original endpoints11/00 becomes a chain started00 conditional on terminal11. Since `floor(M/3)≥M/4`, `alpha_M≤exp(−aM/16)≤16/(aM)`, giving terminal conditioning probability at least1/2. The four count-failure bounds are obtained respectively by Markov on sigma, edge-count Chebyshev, mixed-state Chebyshev (deviation at least k/64), and Markov on J00. Exact substitution bounds their conditioned union by `398458887/34359738368 < 1/16`.

Reversal changes departure J00 by +1, preserves mixed-state count, and sends N001 to original N100=N001+1. These endpoint corrections are explicitly paid; integer J00≤M/8 implies J00≤floor(M/8). The lower u bound loses the one necessary unit and remains at least ck/4 because ck≥1024.

The two ordinary hitting vectors satisfy every first-step equation, with zero at their own targets. Their maxima have exactly the stated form on a,t≤1/4. The strong Markov property bounds the additional time to hit00 then11 from *any* time-l state by `6+3/(2t)+3/(2a)≤6/t`. Different targets make the order strict. Reversal sends `[l,h]` to itself since M−h=l and M−l=h. The interval has length at least M/2. ## Asymptotic and upper comparison audit

For any prescribed `2≤r_M=o(M)`, b=max(r,sqrt(M)) is o(M), and k=floor(sqrt(Mb)) satisfies k/M→0, k/b→infinity, r/k→0, and k/sqrt(M)→infinity. Thus every fixed finite condition holds eventually, including the very stringent M/k² condition. The interval stays nonempty and `L=log(M/(4Ac k))→infinity`. A single all-budget profile constant forces clean S=O(M^−1/2), hence S=o(t). The floor in u0 leaves rho≥1/64 eventually. Therefore risk/profile diverges by the logarithmic factor. The estimator is k-blind, whereas the adversary is legitimately allowed k and alternative clean sources can vary with the tested budget.

SC003's actual estimator depends only on triple counts and protected terminal pair. Its rate profile satisfies the quoted bound by `log(1+x)≤x`; theorem constants remain separate absolute factors, not an asserted unit-constant risk bound. CB005's gate uses the four row counts in departures0,...,n−1. The last needed pair is `(X_(n−1),X_n)`, so first n+1 symbols are exactly sufficient. The histogram and terminal pair provide the remaining inputs. This independently accepted all-start upper includes the stationary setting. Neither argument proves an exact linear threshold or every-linear-width success.
