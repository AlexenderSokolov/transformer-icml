## Universal pathwise calculation and indexing

For any word Z of length M+2, there are M+1 overlapping pair vertices Y_0,...,Y_M and exactly M triple edges Y_i -> Y_(i+1), i=0,...,M-1. For every pair u,

    out(u)-in(u)
      = sum_{i=0}^{M-1} [1{Y_i=u}-1{Y_(i+1)=u}]
      = 1{Y_0=u}-1{Y_M=u}.

This includes loops, coinciding endpoints, and even M=0. Hence out-in plus the observed final-pair one-hot equals the initial-pair one-hot on every compatible observation. The recovered pair is the CORRUPTED pair (Z_0,Z_1); no clean endpoint is recovered or required.

More directly, in((a,b)) = sum_c H_cab counts exactly the pairs at positions 1,...,M. These are the M ordinary transitions of Z_1,...,Z_(M+1), not all M+1 transitions of the original word. Thus the original FOT002 outgoing-degree-plus-terminal-minus-initial display reduces identically to incoming degrees. An edit of Z_0 has no effect on these suffix counts. Their sum is M and each is nonnegative. Exact counts are essential: the argument does not establish reconstruction from rounded or noisy counts.

The deterministic inverse is asserted on compatible observations (those generated by a word), which are the entire experiment's support. It can be extended arbitrarily elsewhere. A one-hot flow vector alone is not claimed to characterize compatibility: disconnected edge multisets can pass that test. No such characterization or efficient compatibility algorithm is needed for the statistical equivalence.

These deterministic maps preserve each source/kernel observation distribution, including randomized predictors. Thus the r=2 experiments are information-equivalent.
