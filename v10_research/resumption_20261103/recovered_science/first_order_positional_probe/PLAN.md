A useful next test is whether the same phenomenon already occurs in the natural first-order binary problem. This could simplify the mechanism and clarify which assumptions matter; it is more relevant now than another network implementation or a numerical plot of known bounds.

## Exact exploratory experiment

For a homogeneous binary first-order chain, use integer N>=64 departures, raw word X_0,...,X_N, actual terminal symbol X_N protected. A strict offline source-independent kernel has integer budget 0<=k<=N and edits at most k of X_0,...,X_(N-1) on every outcome. Observe the exact four transition counts and first/last r raw symbols (integer 1<=r<=N+1). Predictor is k-blind, risk is unconditional unsquared TV at the actual terminal symbol. Start with all transition tables and arbitrary initial laws, and stationary lower witnesses. No certificate assumption is imposed in this initial probe.

This changes both source order and natural histogram order. A result here is NOT automatically a theorem for first-order sources given the original eight triple counts, nor a substitution into the accepted manuscript. Protecting one final symbol rather than a pair is also explicit. Any comparison must retain these differences.

The research plan examines an all-start upper using two-state weighted hitting scales and positional gating, and independently examines a common-kernel lower with pair-count-preserving run relocation and occupation mixture. Look first for a valid finite lower or a counterexample to logarithmic separation. Verify exact edge/terminal shifts and legal edits. Do not assume a first-order lower follows by restricting the prior second-order witness family.

Stop this probe when a complete candidate proof, a concrete obstruction, or a sharply specified missing lemma is established. No manuscript replacement or result acceptance without fresh independent review.

The competitive factor in this probe is normalized by the explicit rate g_N(k)=max(N^-1/2,k/N), not directly by the exact known-budget minimax risk. One fixed map at each (N,r) serves every k. Any conversion to an exact-oracle-relative factor must pay the proved sandwich constants.
