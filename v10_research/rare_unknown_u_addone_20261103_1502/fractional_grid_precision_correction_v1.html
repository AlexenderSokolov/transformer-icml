Fixed-point format correction

This immutable correction applies to unknown_u_addone_matched_rate_v1.html, SHA-256 37a1b01e27f315097c5c910005605b877ba9b6d0de160940c7acc375b6af849b. In §6, the phrase “nearest b-bit fixed-point rounding has ε≤2^(−b−1)” is too broad because a total b-bit format need not have grid spacing 2^−b or represent 1.

For the displayed inequality, explicitly use a [0,1] dyadic grid {k·2^−b : k=0,…,2^b}, with b fractional bits and sufficient range to include both endpoints, and round to nearest after clipping to [0,1]. Then its absolute rounding error is at most 2^(−b−1). The exact representation may require an additional endpoint/integer bit or another separately specified encoding. Thus the candidate's sufficient scalar-head-output error inequality is valid under this defined format; it is not a statement about arbitrary b total bits, about the other network components, or a bit lower bound. The candidate itself remains unchanged.

The independent reviewer identified the format ambiguity. No tests, randomness, enumeration, old-file overwrite or external write was used for this correction.
