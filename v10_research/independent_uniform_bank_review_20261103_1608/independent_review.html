Independent review: uniform-head value bank

3 November 2026. Independent analytical check, not formal peer review or a standard-Transformer implementation certification. No tests, sampling, source changes or external writes were used.

Reviewed artifact: uniform_head_bank_v1.html, SHA-256 57671e10c5cac0a6f8bf19e0c678a766d59ca69cfee1b3d95dfb36b96cbe0cb2.

Disposition: No mathematical blocker found under the declared enriched value, residual-query and nonlinear-readout interface.

  • Pathwise formula. M valid candidates plus one BOS with identical scores receive weight 1/(M+1). Summing the selected context-successor coordinate gives C_r+1, and summing its occupancy coordinate gives J_r+2; their common factor cancels in the ratio. At M=0 the head output is the BOS itself, (1,2) per row, and the ratio is 1/2. No unobserved next bit is used. The value range is [0,2], not a simplex.
  • Projection and ratio error. Let true coordinates obey 0≤u≤v and v≥2/(M+1). Clipping the approximate denominator to this lower bound cannot increase its distance from v. Clipping the approximate numerator to [0,v′] changes its error by no more than the maximum of the original numerator and projected denominator errors: if clipped above v′, use u≤v; if clipped below 0, use u≥0. Thus both final errors are ≤η and 0≤u′≤v′. The identity u′/v′−u/v=[u′−u+(u′/v′)(v−v′)]/v yields error ≤2η/v≤(M+1)η. Extra numerical error in division and output rounding is not included.
  • Bits and scope. η=O(M^(−3/2)) is sufficient for O(M^(−1/2)) pathwise output perturbation at large M; with a fixed-point interface that represents the required range, O(log M) fractional bits suffice. This is a sufficient boundary specification, not an end-to-end guarantee or lower bound. Constructing predecessor/successor joint features, exact support, BOS, selector and division is charged separately; the terminal pair comes from the observed history. The construction lies outside the earlier one-scalar fixed-output map and so does not refute its restricted lower bound.
  • Prior art. The authors explicitly treat histogram/counting constructions and their own companion as antecedents, without asserting that those sources prove this exact precision model or that this presentation is novel.
