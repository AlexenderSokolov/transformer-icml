Finite softmax construction for the rare terminal rate

3 November 2026. Separate candidate, companion to reverse_conditional_matched_rate_v1.html, SHA-256 05827cc6dce1f5b0ef7d1cc82ca2a13265a76f41823a9f4f41f706ce042e03c3. This note gives a constant-width, finite-score structured-mask realization for a slightly smoothed reverse estimator; it does not overwrite the prior construction. It is not yet independently reviewed, not a novelty assertion, not a standard dense-causal decoder, and not a complete finite-bit or training theorem.

The family and exact u are public; t is unknown. The rate is for actual-terminal conditional 00 TV on one stationary trajectory. The lower bound allows the whole history, but the construction uses special model-R masks. The entire bit-level numerical implementation and access/compute cost comparison remain open.

1. Same source and target

Let N≥2, M=N−2, 0<u<1/4, w=(1−u)/3, t∈[1/4,3/4], stationary pair law π=(u,w,w,w), and successor-1 probabilities θ=(t,1/2,1−ut/w,1/2). Observe X₁:N and condition on the actual terminal S_N=00. Define H_abc as the historical triple counts, K=H_001 and J=H_001+H_101. For M=0 set output 1/2. For M≥1, consider

t̄=clip_[1/4,3/4] ((w/u)·K/(J+1)).

Unlike the earlier unsmoothed estimator, t̄ is 1/4 when J=0. It is always in [1/4,3/4], so its conditional loss never exceeds 1/2.

2. Exact finite-score causal implementation

In the first model-R attention block, use two paid singleton-mask heads to copy X_(i−2),X_(i−1) into separate coordinates at each i≥3, retaining X_i. Early rows are self-masked. Tokenwise ReLU gates form

f_K(i)=ReLU(X_i−X_(i−2)−X_(i−1)) = 1{001},
f_J(i)=ReLU(X_i−X_(i−1)) = 1{ending in 01}.

Therefore f_K≤f_J and both are binary. At terminal N, the second-block single head attends exactly to rows i=3,…,N, using scalar query 1, scalar key log(M+1)·f_J(i), and scalar value f_K(i). All its unmasked scores are finite: either 0 or log(M+1). Its ideal output is

q = exp(log(M+1)) K / [M−J+exp(log(M+1))J]
  = (M+1)K/[M(J+1)].

Hence a constant-width affine/clamp readout returns clip_[1/4,3/4]((w/u)·M q/(M+1))=t̄. The terminal pair (X_(N−1),X_N) remains in separate coordinates; f_J,f_K at i=N are based only on the observed triple (X_(N−2),X_(N−1),X_N). The query is not inserted into earlier values. Gate the result with a Boolean indicator of S_N=00, output 1/2 otherwise, and form the exact binary probability vector (1−p,p). For M=0 bypass the empty aggregation and output 1/2. The conditional guarantee below does not assert a particular overall risk for the arbitrary off-00 fallback.

3. Matched conditional statistical rate

Condition on S_N=00. In the stationary reversed pair chain started from 00, R(01,00)=h=ut/w, J counts departures from 01 and K counts 01→00. The companion proof establishes uniformly for M≥6

P(J<M/192 | S_N=00) ≤ exp(−M/768),
D=K−hJ is a reverse-time martingale sum with E[D² | S_N=00]≤hM.

On J≥M/192, projection is nonexpansive relative to t and

|t̄−t| ≤ (w/u)·(|D|+h)/(J+1).

Cauchy and the given parameter range give the conditional expectation of the D term at most 96/√(Mu), and the h term at most 192t/M≤144/M. The complement costs at most 1/2. Thus for M≥6,

E_t[|t̄−t| | S_N=00] ≤ min{1/2, 96/√(Mu)+144/M+(1/2)e^(−M/768)}.  (1)

For x=Mu≥1, u<1/4 gives 144/M=144u/x≤36/√x; the exponential term is at most 6/√x. Hence the displayed upper is at most 138/√x≤196/√(1+x). For x<1 clipping suffices; for M<6 the constant bound suffices, including M=0. Therefore for every allowed u,t,N,

E_t[|t̄−t| | S_N=00] ≤ min{1/2,196/√(1+Mu)}.                       (2)

The already established conditional full-history two-point lower is c/√(1+Mu), c=(1−1/√6)/32, for one common rule with public u and unknown t. Thus these upper and lower bounds match in minimax order in this declared model-R class, which contains the constructed rule and is a subset of full-path predictors. The lower is statistical even for unrestricted access to the full history, not a new attention/head/precision lower bound. The constants are very loose.

4. Numerical and resource ledger

Two singleton-copy heads plus one scored aggregation head are used across two attention blocks; key, query and value for the second head are scalar. Boolean triple features, the affine readout, clip, terminal-state gate and complement can be formed with constant-size ReLU circuitry and a constant number of pointwise stages; if the implementation restricts each block to one FFN stage, charge any extra blocks needed. Under a sparse structural-mask executor the active attention edges and constant-width local work are O(N); a dense executor can cost O(N²). Positions, masks and score/weight description, storage, dynamic range and arithmetic operations are not free. The score gap grows as log(M+1), and the readout scale contains w/u.

If, in addition to exact copied bits, exact logits and exact downstream affine/clamp/gating arithmetic, the scalar head output is q̃ with |q̃−q|≤ε_q on each path, nonexpansiveness of clipping adds at most (w/u)ε_q to (1)–(2). For b-bit nearest fixed-point rounding of that scalar, ε_q≤2^(−b−1); choosing

2^(−b−1) ≤ (u/w)/√(1+Mu)

makes this particular error at most 1/√(1+Mu). This is a sufficient output-coordinate requirement, not a proven lower bound or full b-bit implementation. Errors in encoding u, score log(M+1), exponentiation/normalization, values, copies, accumulators, weights, gates, post-output projection and exact complement are separate proof obligations. The estimator must not use unknown t in its parameters. One-pass exact joint counters K,J and an exact or approximate ratio are a same-history computational baseline, and the existing direct-count conditional bounds and R011 average-risk bound must be compared on the same masks and accuracy.

5. Scope and source comparison

Like the companion, this uses classical reversed-transition estimation and conditional martingale concentration; it should not be sold as a new statistical principle. The score-driven pseudo-count is close to the soft-matching and BOS/add-α literature, including D’Angelo et al.; an equality of mechanisms or novelty/non-novelty across all literature has not been established. The companion's lower source, independent rarity review, and source-bound prior-art assessment must accompany any interpretation. In particular, this does not prove a matched-budget advantage over direct joint features, a training algorithm, a standard-D implementation, or a global precision frontier. Independent proof and source review are needed before relying on the candidate. No RNG, enumeration, numerical tests, old-source overwrite or external write was used.
