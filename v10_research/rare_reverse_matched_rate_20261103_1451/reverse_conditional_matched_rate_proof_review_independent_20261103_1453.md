## Statistical proof

- Stationarity gives the reversed chain specified in the candidate, started at 00 after conditioning on the actual event S_N=00. Its probability is u>0. Reverse departures from 01 are the forward triples 001 or 101, and reverse 01-to-00 successes are exactly forward 001 triples. Thus J and K are computed from the observed word through time N, with no X_(N+1), independent query, iid-context premise, or prior on t.
- The R² column for 11 is coordinatewise at least 1/8. Since R(11,01)=1/2, the three-step probability of 01 is at least 1/16 from each state. For M≥6, L=floor((M−1)/3)≥M/6 and all selected source indices 3,6,...,3L lie in 0,...,M−1. The conditional success probability of each selected indicator given the preceding selected history is at least 1/16. Iterating conditional exponential moments and applying Markov's inequality with λ=1 yields the lower-tail bound exp(−L/128); no independence is assumed. Consequently P(J<M/192 | S_N=00)≤exp(−M/768).
- With h=ut/w, D=K−hJ is a sum of reverse-filtration martingale differences, and E[D² | S_N=00]=h(1−h)E[J | S_N=00]≤hM. On J≥M/192, clipping is nonexpansive around t and Cauchy–Schwarz gives expected error at most 192 sqrt(wt)/sqrt(Mu)≤96/sqrt(Mu). The complementary loss is at most 1/2, giving the displayed split bound without treating J as independent of D.
- For x=Mu≥1, exp(−M/768)≤sqrt(384/(eM))<12/sqrt(M)≤12/sqrt(x), and therefore the error is below 102/sqrt(x)≤145/sqrt(1+x). For x<1 or M<6, clipping is sufficient. All stated t endpoints are included. The strict u endpoints 0 and 1/4 are outside the claimed domain. At M=0, J=K=0 and the rule returns 1/2; conditional worst-case loss is exactly 1/4.
- The lower bound in the cited antecedent is for a single parameter-independent rule given u, applied to the same terminal-conditioned full-history experiment. Its two parameters differ by Δ=1/[16 sqrt(1+Mu)]. The conditional KL bound (256/3)(1+Mu)Δ²≤1/3 and Pinsker imply the displayed constant (1−1/sqrt(6))/32. It compares correctly with the construction's uniform upper bound, establishing minimax order, not matching constants or an architecture lower bound. Bernoulli total variation is absolute error in the predicted 1-probability.

## Section 5 realization and costs

- At each i≥3, the two singleton-mask copies provide X_(i−2) and X_(i−1); ReLU(X_i−X_(i−2)−X_(i−1)) is exactly 1{001} and ReLU(X_i−X_(i−1)) is exactly 1{ending in 01} on binary inputs. Early-position values do not enter the terminal mask. The zero-score head over {3,...,N} produces exact (K/M,J/M) in ideal real arithmetic for M≥1. M=0 correctly bypasses the empty aggregation.
- Multiply by M, then the triangular tents ReLU(1−|z−j|) are exactly one at their own integer and zero at every other integer. Since observed counts obey 0≤K≤J≤M, precisely one of the pair gates ReLU(ψ_j(J)+ψ_k(K)−1) is one. Its stored coefficient gives the claimed clipped answer, including the unique (0,0) fallback. The lookup has (M+1)(M+2)/2 pair gates and coefficients, hence O(M²), plus O(M) individual tents. Absolute value and tents have constant-depth ReLU implementations; any additional tokenwise stages or Transformer blocks must be counted under the chosen execution model.
- For the optional precision port, coordinate errors below 1/(4M) imply count-coordinate errors below 1/4 after exact multiplication by M. The stated plateau tents then equal exactly one at each intended count and zero at all other count indices. One pair gate is therefore one. If its coefficient is perturbed by at most η, the output error is at most η, assuming the other operations are exact. This is only a port assumption, not a complete numerical-error proof.
- Lookup output coefficients are in [1/4,3/4], so their magnitudes do not grow like 1/u. The factor M and integer location thresholds have magnitude O(M), requiring O(log M) bits for their integer parts in a fixed-point representation; the gates also use constant-size slopes and offsets. The exact coefficients can nevertheless be arbitrary real functions of exact public u. Exact evaluation/storage for an arbitrary real u is not a finite-bit construction. Approximation to absolute η requires a representation and preparation budget, and public-u encoding and accuracy, division/clipping near decision thresholds, small-u scaling, copy/softmax/multiplication errors, and aggregate storage and execution costs remain to be specified. Naively forming w/u may incur large intermediate magnitude even though the final clipped coefficients are bounded. The candidate explicitly reserves these issues rather than claiming they are solved.
- The terminal 00 gate can be built from the exact retained pair, and an off-event fallback is allowed; its unconditional risk is not controlled by the conditional bound. Structural masks, copy heads, two aggregate value coordinates, positional access, storage of O(M²) coefficients, terminal-only versus all-token execution, and any compilation into standard dense-causal attention are resources, not free consequences of the statistical proof.
