非线性因果行更新的通信边界与可核验闭合条件

本轮固定刚审阅过的共同模型：原始二元符号、合成终端、全前缀含自身因果注意力、两个有效标量头、固定宽度和完整范数预算、正 ε PreLN，以及普通词表 softmax。问题是非线性逐行更新是否必然破坏终端通信折叠。

答案需要分开三种主张。平滑平方已经能破坏指定终端切口的精确加性折叠，但仍可获得误差 Oc(log N/N) 的廉价源平均模拟；另一种带放大的非线性前缀能量，则把已有最坏历史通信硬例带入同一规范化模型。精确可加性也获得了一个必要且充分的混合差分证书，但它不是所有廉价模拟的必要条件。

没有获得新的二阶预测率、一般非线性多读主定理或训练结果。两个具体程序的统计风险均明确核算，不能把小模拟误差误当成低预测风险。

一 固定模型与评价对象

输入 N 个符号 Xi∈{−1,1}，附加 T=N+1 的零符号合成行；参数只依赖公开 N，不编码未知核。每次注意力都包含自己的行，Q/K 为零，有效值维数为一。所有位置运算、非线性、放大、输出准备、清空及恢复步骤全部付费。每层 LN 使用 ε=1、gain=2、shift=0，最后是常规仿射 logits 和 softmax。

共同包络是一个预先固定、足够大的完整状态/矩阵/偏置/原语增益界，覆盖此前有序双读程序与本轮两个程序；不把它等同于未经核验的旧数字 MK。数学语义为精确实数。小振幅编译必须先纳入全部 logit 复制、范数填充和清空微步骤，再定完整参考长度 m 和精度 M。

源平均分析使用固定 c∈(0,1/2) 的平稳内部二元二阶类 Pc。最坏历史模拟则要求每条输入都准确。这两种保证不能互换，某个困难词在源下有正概率也不等于它构成源平均困难分布。

二 保留一阶项后的非线性源平均闭合

两个公开线性统计量

第一次均匀注意力产生 mi=Si/i，其中 Si是前缀符号和，ST=SN。各行应用非线性 f，第二次读取 Af=T−1Σf(mi)。保留全样本均值 M=SN/N 和谐和位置加权统计

R=(1/T)Σi=1Tmi=(1/T)Σj=1NajXj，aj=Σi=jT1/i。

M、R 都是公开加权的本地符号和。Alice 只需量化两个部分和，Bob 加入自己部分；不需要重建每个跨方前缀。定义 Taylor 替代值 Bf=f(M)+f′(M)(R−M)。若 |f″|≤L2，则逐历史

|Af−Bf|≤(L2/2)VN，VN=(1/T)Σi=1T(mi−M)²。

后续输出增益 G 必须再乘入。量化时也须控制 |f′|≤L1，因为替代值对 M、R 的敏感度分别不超过 2L2和 L1。这些常数若随层数增长，不能吸收掉。替代值可能离开实际值范围，解码器误差域必须同时覆盖两者。

源平均余项与精确阶

沿用已经审阅的协方差界 Var(Si)≤Kci，其中 Kc=2/c²−3。对实际行，E(mi−M)²≤2Kc/i+2Kc/N；合成行 mT−M=−M/T。因此

supP∈PcEVN≤[2Kc(HN+1)+T−2]/T，HN=Σi=1N1/i。

固定曲率与输出增益给 Oc(log N/N) 误差，严格小于 N−1/2尺度。此前只用全局均值替换各前缀，会丢掉一阶项并只得到 O(N−1/2)；这里保留 R 后才获得二阶余项。公平 IID 下更有精确公式 EVN=(HN−1+1/(NT²))/T，故这个余项的 log N/N 阶是尖锐的。

平方与同一 PreLN 模型

取 f(z)=z²，A2=2MR−M²+VN。以普通 logits (A2,0) 输出 sigmoid。替代 logits 使用 B2=2MR−M²∈[−3,1]，不能假设其仍在 [0,1]。在这个完整区间上 sigmoid 导数有固定正下界 κσ=exp(3)/(1+exp(3))²，因此

κσVN≤TV(Q2,QTaylor)≤VN/4。

这给出该指定程序与替代计算之间的必要且充分余项判据：输出误差为 o(rN) 当且仅当 EVN=o(rN)。它不是所有架构的最弱闭合定理。

具体网络用已有 dyadic tent-square 电路，以 O(log N) 个有界 ReLU 步骤把平方误差控制到 ν≤T−2，再按已审双读 PreLN 编译，将普通 logits 的物理误差控制到 ζ=min{1/1000,T−2}。无需额外对数近似。两个标量头均有实质作用，单头消融在全正号历史上留下固定输出差。

通信 O(1+log(1/ε)) 位、上限 N，可以获得源平均误差 ε+Oc(log N/N)+ζ+ν/4。例如 ε≈N−1/2时只需 O(log N) 位，额外余项为 little-o。但该网络在固定 θ∈(0,1−2c] 的平稳 lag-two 源上预测风险趋于 θ/2，而不是趋零。因此没有与预测测试下界冲突。

三 指定终端切口的精确加性证书

固定分布式行更新结束后、付费本地解码器之前的完整终端向量 z:{0,1}N→Rv，要求所有数据通道都包含其中，且 ||z(x)||≤V。想要核验的是这一特定切口能否写成 z(x)=z(0)+Σaixi。

对任意不同 i、j 和其余符号背景 w，定义混合差分 Dijz(w)=z11−z10−z01+z00。所有这些差分恒为零，当且仅当上述精确加性表示成立。证明是：零混合差分让每个单符号增量独立于其他符号，再从全零历史逐位求和。

该证书在逻辑上必要且充分，但穷举背景需指数级检查，不是高效通用算法。固定公开 ReLU/裁剪活动区域只在其余映射也全是仿射或公开线性时才足够；数据相关 softmax 与全特征 LN 不能因 ReLU mask 固定就被忽略。

Alice 的部分和半径不必粗暴累计所有系数：它等于 z(xA,0B)−z(0)，故不超过 2V。对完整半径 V 的投影域有增益 G 的解码器，可用有界向量量化得到对数精度通信。若混合差分只满足 ||Dijz||≤γij，逐位展开给余项≤Σi<jγijxixj；增长的总余项与解码器更大定义域必须计费。

平方前缀统计在 j<k 时具有 DjkA2=(8/T)Σi=kTi−2>0。使用有限描述 dyadic 系数的逻辑参考实现（规范化编译前）仍有 D12>0。物理 PreLN 通过统一输出误差与参考比较，并不声称保留内部混合差分恒等式。因此温和非线性也能破坏精确加性切口，却不妨碍上一节的廉价源平均模拟。不能从非零混合差分直接推断通信困难。

四 同一规范化模型中的非线性通信硬例

已有 v10 结果用第一次均匀读取 St/t、付费恢复及裁剪整数平台得到 vt=clip(St,−4,4)²/16，再由第二读得到 EN。最后用 O(log N) 个有界步骤放大 EN减去公开基线，产生 FN∈[0,1]。在旧的输入编码上，它精确区分集合不相交或 Gap-Hamming 的两种承诺。

本轮没有重新声称发现这套硬函数或编码，只把它移入同一正 ε PreLN/普通 softmax 契约。选择有界普通 logits (4FN−2,0)，编码输出由旧的0/1变成 σ(−2)、σ(2)。两者距离1/2均超过3/8；完整准备后的编译误差≤T−2仍留下超过1/3的间隔。

所以在相应 N 的子序列上，确定性交互式协议若逐历史 TV 误差≤1/4，仍须 Ω(N) 位。随机协议若每条历史以至少2/3概率达到该误差，也须 Ω(N) 位；每条历史上对随机性的期望误差≤1/16同样足够推出该下界。随机结论继续调用既有 Chakrabarti–Regev Gap-Hamming 定理，没有新的通信下界技术。

硬例不是低风险估计器。在公平 IID 源下，EN趋于1，FN趋于1，最终预测风险趋于 σ(2)−1/2>1/3。困难词在内部 Markov 源下有正概率，也不能把这一最坏历史下界改写成源平均下界。此前已有的低风险 rare-marker 构造是另一结果，不在此处偷换为标量头规范化版本。

七 失败路线与后续边界

  • 有限普通 logits 不会精确输出0/1，不能照搬旧的误差阈值 ε<1/2；现用真实间隔和1/4阈值。

# Taylor-corrected source-average closure for smooth nonlinear prefix rows

6 November 2026 UTC. Analytical candidate v2; v1 is preserved. Independent review requested after adding the horizon, witness-interiority, ablation and exact IID remainder checks. The new target is a nonlinear row-update simulation certificate, not a new estimator or statistical rate.

## 1. Frozen nonlinear two-read program

Assume N>=6 to match the current two-read normalized construction. N signed binary raw tokens, T=N+1 synthetic zero-symbol row, full-prefix inclusive causal attention, fixed width, paid local operations and source-independent parameters. Let S_i=sum_{j<=i}X_j for i<=N, and S_T=S_N. The first zero-score scalar head produces m_i=S_i/i for all i<=T. A nonlinear row map f is applied, and the second zero-score scalar head returns

    A_f=(1/T)sum_{i=1}^T f(m_i).

For the main concrete example f(z)=z^2. Its ideal terminal output is Q_f=(sigma(A_f),1-sigma(A_f)), sigma(u)=1/(1+exp(-u)); these are ordinary logits (A_f,0), not an uncharged probability projection. Section 6 gives a fixed-width positive-epsilon PreLN realization approximating this ideal with O(log N) depth. The ideal square is an analytical comparator, not an exact multiplication primitive.

The statistical class is stationary interior binary order-two P_c, fixed c in (0,1/2). Error of SIMULATION is averaged under this class. No unknown source parameter is supplied to the network or simulator.

## 2. Two public linear sufficient statistics for the approximation

Set M=S_N/N and

    R=(1/T)sum_{i=1}^T m_i
      =(1/T)sum_{j=1}^N a_j X_j,
    a_j=sum_{i=j}^T 1/i.

Both are public weighted token-local sums, even though A_f is nonlinear in distributed prefix states. Their absolute ranges are at most one. In particular sum_{j<=N}a_j/T=N/T+N/T^2<1. Alice can send quantized partial M and R; Bob adds his own partial sums. Neither party needs to know the individual cross-party m_i.

Define the Taylor surrogate

    B_f=f(M)+f'(M)(R-M).

If |f''(z)|<=L_2 on [-1,1], Taylor's integral remainder gives the PATHWISE bound

    |A_f-B_f| <= (L_2/2) V_N,
    V_N=(1/T)sum_{i=1}^T (m_i-M)^2.                     (1)

If a subsequent public output map has Lipschitz constant G on the required domain containing A_f and B_f, its output error is <=G L_2 V_N/2. Any implementation approximation to f and output must be added separately. The surrogate may be outside f([-1,1]), so its decoder domain may not be silently restricted to the actual range.

For quantized M,R, require fixed |f'|<=L_1 and |f''|<=L_2. On the square [-1,1]^2, partial_M B_f=f''(M)(R-M), partial_R B_f=f'(M), hence |partial_M|<=2L_2 and |partial_R|<=L_1. Project the combined quantized statistics into [-1,1]^2, fixing the exact pair. This yields O((2L_2+L_1)eta) pre-output error for coordinate accuracy eta, uniformly over histories. The precise bit cost is O(log(1/eta)) at fixed derivative/value budgets, or the raw-bit cap N. Curvature/decoder gains that grow with depth remain explicit resources.

## 3. Uniform second-order source-average remainder

The reviewed pair-chain minorization gives, for stationary P_c,

    Var(sum_{j=1}^i X_j)<=K_c i,   K_c=2/c^2-3.

For i<=N, both m_i and M have mean mu and

    E(m_i-M)^2<=2K_c/i+2K_c/N.

At the synthetic row m_T-M=-M/T, so its squared difference is at most T^-2. Therefore

    sup_{P in P_c} E V_N <= [2K_c(H_N+1)+T^-2]/T,        (2)

where H_N=sum_{i=1}^N 1/i. Under fair IID signs the exact identity is E V_N=(H_N-1+1/(N T^2))/T: for i<=N, Cov(m_i,M)=1/N, so E(m_i-M)^2=1/i-1/N; the synthetic contribution is 1/(N T^2) before division by T. Therefore the order log(N)/N of this canonical remainder is sharp, including its induced sigmoid discrepancy by (3). Combining (1)-(2) gives an O_c(G L_2 log(N)/N) source-average closure defect. It is o(N^-1/2) for fixed derivative/decoder budgets. More generally the displayed sufficient comparison condition at any desired scale r_N is G_N L_(2,N) log(N)/N=o(r_N), with K_c and implementation errors retained.

This is a condition, not an assertion about all nonlinear row maps. Intermediate amplification can make L_2 grow. A shared dependence on the row's own symbol f(X_i,m_i) generally creates the cross-party quadratic statistic sum_i X_i m_i in the first-order term; it does not inherit the two-scalar closure for free.

## 4. Quadratic example and a necessary-and-sufficient residual test for this surrogate

For f(z)=z^2 the Taylor identity is exact:

    A_2=2MR-M^2+V_N.

Thus B_2=2MR-M^2 lies in [-3,1], while A_2 is in [0,1]. The sigmoid derivative on [-3,1] lies between

    kappa_sigma=exp(3)/(1+exp(3))^2 >0

and 1/4. Hence on EVERY history,

    kappa_sigma V_N <= TV(Q_2,Q_Taylor) <= V_N/4.        (3)

For this PARTICULAR quadratic-program/Taylor-surrogate pair, the source-average output discrepancy is o(r_N) if and only if E V_N=o(r_N), with sup over the same source class if required. This is the strongest necessity statement made here. It is not the weakest possible closure assumption over all architectures or all simulators.

Quantizing M,R with each error at most eta gives |Bhat_2-B_2|<=6eta and output error <=3eta/2. Choosing eta<=2epsilon/3 gives a deterministic one-way protocol using

    C<=min{N,K[1+log(1/epsilon)]}

with source-average error <=epsilon+[2K_c(H_N+1)+T^-2]/(4T) against the ideal quadratic network. This is not a worst-history guarantee: a long +1 block followed by a long -1 block has V_N bounded below by a positive constant.

## 5. This is not a useful second-order estimator

On the stationary symmetric lag-two source with a fixed theta in (0,1-2c], for example theta=min{1/16,(1-2c)/2}, mu=0 and E A_2<=K_c(H_N+1)/T up to an inessential bounded synthetic-row term. Thus sigma(A_2)->1/2 in L1, while the actual next law is (1+theta X_(N-1))/2. Consequently

    E TV(Q_2,p_actual) -> theta/2.

The same holds for an o(1)-accurate physical implementation. The source-average closure is a positive result for a genuinely nonlinear distributed memory statistic, but it is not a statistical consistency or learning result. No earlier suffix-estimation rate is repackaged as a new theorem.

## 6. Concrete positive-epsilon PreLN realization and full resources

Approximate z^2 on [-1,1] via b=(z+1)/2 and the already-reviewed dyadic tent-square interpolant s_L(b). Since |s_L(b)-b^2|<=4^-L/4, the clipped value

    f_L(z)=clip(4s_L((z+1)/2)-4((z+1)/2)+1,0,1)

has |f_L(z)-z^2|<=nu=4^-L. Take L=ceil(log_2 T), so nu<=T^-2. This is a fixed-width O(log N)-depth bounded affine/ReLU program with fixed per-primitive gain and complete-state norms. Its two useful heads remain scalar and zero-score. The second answer a is in [0,1] and the reference logits are simply ell=(a,0); no logarithm approximation or probability-floor circuit is needed.

Compile this EXACT bounded reference program by the reviewed TWO_READ_PRELN_COMPILATION v4 mechanism. Explicitly append fixed scaled-logit copies, norm-padding at accuracy kappa, and ALL scratch clears BEFORE defining total m and choosing eta_small=2^-M. Choose output tolerance zeta=min{1/1000,T^-2} and xi,kappa,tau_restore as the largest permitted dyadic constant multiples of zeta. # Exact additive terminal cuts: a necessary-and-sufficient certificate with limited scope

6 November 2026 UTC. Analytical candidate v3; v1 and v2 are preserved. Independent review requested after clarifying the affine-path premise and full decoder domains. This is a finite-domain algebraic characterization, not a computationally efficient universal test or a characterization of all cheap communication.

## 1. Freeze the object being certified

Choose a fixed terminal cut AFTER all distributed row updates and BEFORE a charged local terminal decoder. Let z:{0,1}^N -> R^v be the complete data-carrying vector at that cut, with fixed v and ||z(x)||<=V for every raw binary history. All other retained decoder coordinates must be public or included in z. Source-independent parameters are fixed. Downstream decoder gain and all actual neural work are separately charged, as in the public-linear collapse theorem.

The desired property is an EXACT additive representation of THIS cut:

    z(x)=z(0)+sum_i a_i x_i.                             (A)

Changing the cut or adding another summary changes the question. A nonlinear downstream decoder is allowed and need not satisfy (A).

## 2. Exact certificate

For i!=j and every assignment w of the other N-2 bits, define

    D_ij z(w)=z(w;i=1,j=1)-z(w;i=1,j=0)
               -z(w;i=0,j=1)+z(w;i=0,j=0).

Then (A) holds if and only if D_ij z(w)=0 for every i,j,w. In that case a_i=z(e_i)-z(0).

Proof. Necessity follows by canceling each affine term. For sufficiency, D_ij=0 says that the effect of turning bit i from 0 to 1 is unchanged when any other bit j is changed. Moving between any two contexts one bit at a time therefore makes that effect independent of every other coordinate, equal to a_i. Telescoping from the all-zero history to x gives (A).

This is the weakest exact condition for the narrowly specified additive representation, in the logical sense of necessity and sufficiency. Exhaustively checking it uses exponentially many histories; no efficient certification algorithm is claimed. Public input-independent ReLU activation/clipping patterns give a sufficient syntactic route ONLY when all other maps on the certified path are affine or public-linear; input-dependent softmax weights and full-feature LN remain nonlinear despite fixed ReLU masks. Under that full premise, the resulting coefficient/norm behavior and all input channels still need checking. Algebraic cancellation can also make (A) hold even when internal masks vary, so public masks are not necessary.

## 3. Bounded message radius without a hidden sum-of-coefficients cost

Alice sends her partial z_A=sum_{i in A}a_i x_i, Bob adds z(0) and his own partial sum. Even if individual coefficients were not separately bounded, the all-history cut bound implies

    ||z_A||=||z(x_A,0_B)-z(0)||<=2V.

Thus one eta-net index in a radius-2V ball costs O(v log(1+V/eta)) bits. The decoder can project the combined estimate to the radius-V ball, which fixes the true cut. Require the decoder to be G-Lipschitz on the FULL projected radius-V ball, not merely exact reachable cut points. A G-Lipschitz decoder on that domain then gives error <=epsilon with eta=epsilon/max(1,G). This proves the same one-way logarithmic-gain communication bound with the binary N-bit fallback. It does not require nonnegative intermediate matrices or linear internal computation; it requires the terminal identity (A).

## 4. Approximate mixed-difference certificate

Suppose instead ||D_ij z(w)||<=gamma_ij uniformly over w, with gamma_ij=gamma_ji. Using a_i=z(e_i)-z(0), the same telescoping argument gives

    ||z(x)-z(0)-sum_i a_i x_i||
       <= sum_{i<j} gamma_ij x_i x_j.                   (B)

Indeed when bit i is added after earlier active bits, its effect differs from its all-zero-context effect by at most the sum of gamma_ij over those earlier active bits; each active unordered pair is counted once.

Let E0=sum_{i<j}gamma_ij. The affine surrogate cut has norm <=V+E0 on every history. Its Alice partial message radius is at most 2V+E0 by comparison with z(x_A,0_B)-z(0) and (B). If E0 is a declared fixed bounded resource, the same net simulation applies; growing E0 remains in the radius cost. Use the full radius-(V+E0) ball as a proof-side projected decoder domain containing both actual and surrogate cuts, with a uniform gain G on that ENTIRE domain, not just reachable points. Source-average output error before quantization is at most

    G sup_{P in P_c} E_P sum_{i<j}gamma_ij X_i X_j,

using binary 0/1 notation here. This is a sufficient certificate; replacing gamma by empirical sampled differences would not certify all histories. A naive uniform gamma only gives O(N^2 gamma), which may be useless.

## 5. Smooth nonlinearity already breaks exact additive collapse

For the ideal quadratic prefix statistic A_2 in TAYLOR_PREFIX_CLOSURE, use signed raw X_j=2x_j-1. For j<k<=N,

    D_jk A_2 = (8/T)sum_{i=k}^T 1/i^2 >0.               (C)

This follows by expanding each prefix square; its X_j X_k coefficient is (2/T)sum_{i>=k}i^-2 and a +/- mixed difference multiplies it by four. Thus even a bounded smooth square breaks exact additive representation at this final scalar cut.

For the concrete dyadic square interpolant with uniform statistic error <=nu<=T^-2, its D_12 differs from the ideal by at most 4nu. The ideal D_12>=2/T, so it remains positive for T>2. Hence the actual bounded affine/ReLU logical implementation also fails the exact certificate, not merely an ideal multiplication oracle.

Nevertheless the Taylor-corrected source-average simulator is cheap. This is why failure of (A) is NOT a communication lower: a different low-dimensional nonlinear decoding of public linear summaries, or a source-average approximation, can still work. Conversely, the normalized prefix-energy hard example supplies a real worst-history communication lower for a different nonlinear map, rather than inferring hardness just from a nonzero mixed difference.

Vanishing mixed differences characterize degree-one functions on a binary cube; net quantization and telescoping are elementary existing tools. The local use is to state exactly what can be checked when claiming a nonlinear body still has an additive terminal cut, and to separate that claim from universal communication hardness or a minimal architecture condition.
