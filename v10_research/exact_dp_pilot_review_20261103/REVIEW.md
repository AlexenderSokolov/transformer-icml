# Independent review: selected-row gate microbenchmark and four-counter DP

2026-11-03. Reviewed saved artifacts without changing the experiment units. Independent computations are in `review_all.py` and `review_all.log`. This is a local technical audit, not a claim of independent preregistration, statistical significance, learned-model performance, or training feasibility.

## Findings

1. **Four-counter floating-input interface.** `solve_banks` correctly retains integer banks and minimum edit costs, but `maximize` first passes the bank through `model_inputs`, which performs Python floating-point division. An exact utility comparator alone cannot recover the rational features. For example, with `h=(0,0,0,0,0)`, M=3, k=1, a bank with first rail exactly 1/3 is reachable, but `maximize(h,1,lambda x: int(x[0] == Fraction(1,3)))` returns 0. Exact rational or real readouts require lossless counters/rational inputs as well as an exact comparison oracle. The research note correctly states its exact-comparison assumption and diagnostic limitations, but its generic interface should not be interpreted as providing exact rational feature evaluation. The four-counter `SHA256SUMS` omits its imported pilot dependency; the separately saved pre-run pilot hash does match the current dependency.

2. **Timing and numerical qualification.** The follow-up's `entire_case_wall_s` measures proposals plus process launch, DP, parsing and validation, rather than isolated child-process time. C++ `wall_s` does not measure launch. Terminal cross-entropy optimization and proposal comparisons are float64, without an exact-real separation/error proof. Both caveats are correctly and explicitly stated in `TIMING_AND_NUMERIC_CORRECTION.md`, including the valid explanation of the compiler indentation warning.

3. **Preservation.** The actual original words, tables, starts, seed, code, 20 proposal entries, selected DP witness and outputs are saved, but original uniform draws, per-step RNG state, and full DP terminal bank/state trace were not captured contemporaneously. `randomness_replayed.json` and the test archives are reconstructions after outcomes, not original traces. The additive `PRESERVATION_CORRECTION.md` makes this clear and controls any ambiguous reading of `REPORT.md`; matching replayed words does not prove capture of the original draws. Hash files verify content consistency but do not constitute trusted timestamps.

## Same-bank bitwise probe

With the saved pilot module and single-thread settings, `bitwise_probe.py` compared 100,000 seeded random words at each M=6,8,10,16,32,64,128, grouped by endpoint and (J,C,D,E), looking for different pilot feature vectors within a group. No paired-word mismatch was observed. This finite, platform- and batch-specific probe does not prove bitwise equivalence for all words, horizons, BLAS implementations or execution configurations. The original pilot verifier established agreement with the rational reconstruction only to 1e-15; no general bitwise characterization or executable-function argmax certificate is established here.
