Correction: rare-family signal and finite-prefix head expectation

This immutable correction supplements route_b_terminal_identifiability_v1.html, SHA-256 b557994fb9ee0c6cb2ff87ea26980c049d4b94cc65b66c82264c92ac4199a368. Its §4 sentence saying that the older-coordinate head's “population mean contains t-dependent signal” is ambiguous and must not be read as a proof about the expectation of the finite-prefix ratio q₁, either unconditionally or conditional on S_N=00.

The exact identity established there is instead E_t[A/M]=ut+w/2 for the unnormalized successor marginal A=C_00+C_01 under stationary initialization. Recovering A from the actual finite head requires the declared exact J, C and β ports as well as q₁; q₁ by itself has not been shown to identify t. Conditioning on S_N=00 changes finite-prefix expectations and is treated separately in bound (∗), not by substituting an unconditional mean into a conditional expectation.

If one defines a separate deterministic population-ratio functional by inserting stationary occupation proportions and stationary successor expectations into the head formula, then for terminal query 00 its value is

q_pop(t) = [e^β(ut+w/2)+e^(−β)(3w/2−ut)]
           / [e^β(u+w)+2w e^(−β)],
∂q_pop/∂t = u(e^β−e^(−β))/[e^β(u+w)+2w e^(−β)].

For u>0 and β≠0 this functional has nonzero t slope. In general E[numerator/denominator] is not the ratio of expectations, so this equation does not establish the exact E[q₁] for random finite occupations and does not establish a terminal-conditioned mean claim. The numerical bound, under its exact-side-port and head-accuracy assumptions, remains the candidate stated in the original. No RNG, numerical tests, or edits to earlier artifacts were used for this correction.
