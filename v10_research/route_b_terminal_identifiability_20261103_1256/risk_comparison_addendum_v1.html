Rare-query baseline comparison

3 November 2026. This note supplements, without modifying, route_b_terminal_identifiability_v1.html, SHA-256 b557994fb9ee0c6cb2ff87ea26980c049d4b94cc65b66c82264c92ac4199a368. It records the exact comparison; it does not establish that the restricted-port estimator is minimax optimal or suboptimal.

All comparisons below use the same stationary u-family, M=N−2, a common parameter-independent rule and the actual event S_N=00. The older-marginal estimator is allowed public exact u and w=(1−u)/3, exact J and C, and finite nonzero β, but it does not know the test kernel or t. It has the sufficient bound

E_t[|t̃−t| | S_N=00] ≤ min{1, [sqrt(65M)+16]/(Mu)+ρ(β)ε_q/u},
ρ(β)=1/[1−exp(−2|β|)].

For fixed head accuracy and positive u, the displayed sampling term is O(1/(u√M)+1/(Mu)). This is a loose upper bound derived using the variance of the older-coordinate aggregate, which also counts the common 01 state. It is not a necessary sample requirement or lower bound for the restricted port, and does not preclude a sharper algorithm or analysis.

The independently scoped rare-family two-point lower bound, for every same rule that knows u but not t and may use the entire path, is

max_{t∈{t₋,t₊}} E_t[|p−t| | S_N=00]
 ≥ (1−1/√6)/[32√(1+Mu)],
t±=1/2±1/[32√(1+Mu)].

This is Ω((1+Mu)^−1/2) with an absolute constant, but it does not prove that any particular estimator attains that rate. In particular, the much larger displayed bound for the older-marginal estimator is not a lower bound.

As a same-prefix comparator, the direct joint-count add-one rule B_00=(C_00+1)/(J_00+2), with zero-count fallback 1/2, has the previously derived conditional upper bound, for v=Mu≥60 and any η∈(0,1),

E_t[|B_00−t| | S_N=00] ≤ min{1, η+1220/v
   + sqrt(log(2(M+1)/(uη))/v) + 1/(v/2+2)}.

This is a sufficient bound under the declared assumptions, with a square-root 1/(Mu) term and logarithmic/secondary terms; it is not an exact matching minimax theorem. The earlier result and independent check must accompany its use. A joint-feature head must still pay for historical state/successor features, masks, vector values, readout and precision; a streaming counter must pay for state, updates and division. Conversely, the older-head port already receives exact joint state counts and C and is thereby granted substantial information. An equal-budget comparison needs an explicit accounting; the two displayed upper bounds and the full-path lower bound do not establish a strict same-budget architecture separation.

Source references: the family derivation is in family_audit_report_v3.html and its independent scope review; the joint-count conditional bound is in route_a_terminal_report_v3.html and INDEPENDENT_BOUND_REVIEW.txt. This addendum does not review their proofs afresh. No RNG or numerical tests were run.
