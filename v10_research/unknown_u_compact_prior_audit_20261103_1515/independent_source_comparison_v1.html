Source comparison for the unknown-u compact conditional-risk synthesis

3 November 2026. This is a source-to-claim check independent of the compact author's synthesis; its author also wrote the separate unknown-u statistical candidate, so this note is not an independent proof review of that candidate or the combined corollary. Sources were inspected at the theorem, assumptions and selected proof/implementation sections specified below, not exhaustively line by line. No claim of a completed literature search, novelty, formal verification, or human peer review is made.

Finding: D’Angelo, Rajaraman and Izzo each directly overlap the attention/statistical ingredients, but the specified statements inspected do not themselves display the entire combined u-blind actual-terminal 00 conditional minimax rate and the particular compact implementation with its specialized arithmetic ledger. That is a narrow no-direct-statement finding, not a proof that the combination cannot be derived from those or other results, and not a positive novelty determination. Han–Jana–Wu supply especially close earlier terminal-conditioned count-moment and add-one analysis; the nonreversible pair lift blocks direct use of their stated reversible lemma without a bridge.

1. Claim being compared

The combined local corollary unknown_u_compact_synthesis_candidate_v2.html, SHA-256 0976b559badd3d63fe51d7aa5e2399a0f59a1b6ce3fb649d264f412c37749003, composes two distinct ingredients. Its statistical candidate, SHA-256 37a1b01e27f315097c5c910005605b877ba9b6d0de160940c7acc375b6af849b, says the same add-one rule B_(S_N) works without u or t and, on the stationary rare family conditioned on the actual S_N=00, has TV risk O((1+Mu)^−1/2), M=N−2; the matching lower holds even for full-history rules given u. Its compact source, SHA-256 f844ffde90e836e7fd345a6bf4a1076bbe0e562d832d51413477d1a0bc921d8d, approximates the same-history add-one statistic on all binary words with a two-block constant-width full-causal model, plus a specifically defined rational/dyadic softmax backend. These are source descriptions here, not independently re-proved by this comparison.

“Finite/bounded logits” must be interpreted at each finite N, not as a bound uniform in N. The compact source declares positional coordinates as large as N² and raw score magnitude O(cN²+b), where c,b grow logarithmically with N and requested accuracy. Its P=O(log(N/η)) finite-word statement is for its specialized, fully specified evaluator and exact canonicalization, not generic exp-based hardware or arbitrary floating point. It does not prove necessity of any layer, width, head or bit resource.

3. Close statistical antecedents and a specific open bridge

Han, Jana & Wu, arXiv 2106.13947v2 §4.2 and Lemma 16 study stationary next-state prediction with expected KL loss weighted by the actual terminal state; they explicitly use add-one transition counts and moments of residuals and occupations conditional on X_n=i. Their Theorem 3 and Corollary 4 give spectral-gap-dependent add-one statements for irreducible reversible chains. This is direct antecedent for the target and endpoint-conditioned concentration technique, not merely a generic Markov citation.

The four-state binary pair lift here is not reversible: P(01,11)=1/2 but P(11,01)=0. Han et al.'s Remark 2 discusses a pseudo-spectral-gap extension of their Theorem 3(ii), under its large-k regime; the fixed k=4 corollary cannot simply be imported from that statement. Their higher-order results address a different expected-KL objective. Paulin, arXiv 1212.2015v5 provides variance and concentration tools including a nonreversible pseudo-spectral gap; mapping one of these theorems, including the terminal conditioning and smoothing support, is a genuine implication check, not done here.

A useful bridge to investigate is this: if a valid fixed-state, nonreversible, uniform-mixing O(1/M) average-terminal KL add-one upper applies to this pair-chain family, then positivity of the rare terminal mass u, conditional Jensen/Pinsker, and correct renormalization to the two structurally allowed successors could yield a conditional-TV bound of order 1/√(Mu). That outline still needs every theorem assumption, support normalization, small-M case and constants checked; it is not established by citing the reversible result. The four-step π-minorization in the local family may help prove a uniform pseudo-spectral gap, but no implication from a specific published theorem has yet been demonstrated.

4. Internal predecessors and comparison conditions

The local R011 theorem already gives same-history add-one results for broad homogeneous order-two sources with average actual-terminal risk; its loss and quantifiers do not silently become conditional on 00. BCJ001 already supplies a paid complete triple/positional bank with precision, noise and source-edit results in its own cut and certificate model. FOT003 already records exact triple-flow endpoint reconstruction. The new conditional derivation and compact synthesis must be measured against these, the exact one-pass J,C counter, and both trivial/unknown-u baselines. Their cited local acceptance is not external peer review.

To turn a prior implication into a defensible claim, explicitly map (i) the observed path and real terminal query, (ii) conditional versus π-weighted loss and parameter quantifiers, (iii) reversibility or nonreversible mixing certificate, (iv) zero-count smoothing, (v) exact input/positional/mask/readout access, and (vi) finite score/bit/FLOP models. A cited expressive identity or an averaged-risk theorem alone is not the entire synthesis; a source which supplies these bridges could remove any residual distinction. Conversely, absent that map, formulation differences alone do not justify an originality claim.

5. Read scope and limitations

I inspected the stated D’Angelo v1 and Rajaraman v1 theorem/proof passages, the official 39-page ICLR PDF sections and its independently recorded indexing/zero-match correction, Han–Jana–Wu v2 theorem and Lemma 16 statements, and Paulin's v5 source abstract; I compared the hash-bound local compact-v3, unknown-u and synthesis-v2 statements. I did not inspect all external references or every proof line, did not independently validate the new conditional proof, and did not claim that no previous work implies it. No RNG, enumeration, numerical testing, source overwrite or external publication was used.
