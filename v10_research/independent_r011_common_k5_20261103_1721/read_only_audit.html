Read-only audit: unrestricted common K⁵ minimax lower

3 November 2026. Independent AI-agent mathematical inspection, not formal certification. No tests or random operations; existing files were not modified. Scope is the β=0 COMMON lower only, not every claim in R011.

Finding. The reviewed dependency chain supports the stated actual-terminal, complete-history, expected unsquared TV lower in the unrestricted arbitrary-initialization model. The hard process can be periodic and starts at a horizon-aligned phase, generally not its stationary law. It therefore does not contradict a smaller stationary rapidly mixing subclass with a K³ scale. The infimum is over one common full-history predictor at each fixed public K,M; a hard source can depend on that predictor and on M. No same hard source or initialization is proved simultaneously hard for all horizons.

2. Direct inspection of the original witness

For K≥12 let m=⌊K/4⌋≥3 and L=m². Four disjoint size-m sets C,U,V,W are subsets of the original alphabet. An Eulerian cyclic word on the complete directed C graph visits each C-pair once. The constructed homogeneous shift table replaces five clock transitions by C→U→V→W→C and then resumes the clock; all rows, including unused ones, have defined probability vectors. Each lap is exactly L transitions, independent of signs and sampled symbols. The pair-state process contains no extra free clock state.

Write M=qL+d with 0≤d<L. The initial pair law is selected from the six listed phase cases so that the actual terminal pair is always in U×V. This law depends on public K,M and is the same across the hidden-sign family. The initial law is arbitrary as permitted by the model; no requirement that it be stationary is present. The symbol table and hidden sign vector are fixed throughout each trajectory.

3. Full history and testing

For each remainder, the entire M+2-symbol history is in public bijection with q triples (U,V,W) and the actual terminal pair T∈U×V, not merely a discarded-data projection. The special initial phases 1 and 2 count the initial random U/V symbols exactly once; phases 3 and 4 have fixed initial W values, not hidden extra labels. The q training triples are iid only within this constructed family, with P(U=u,V=v,W=w)=m⁻²qσ(w|u,v); T is uniform on U×V and independent of those triples. It is nevertheless the actual endpoint of the original path, not an extra test query. There are exactly q observed departures from vulnerable U×V rows; the terminal departure is unobserved.

Flipping one sign changes two W-coordinate probabilities on one of m² rows. The full-history conditional KL given T=s is 2qαm⁻³ log[(1+α)/(1−α)]≤8qα²/m³≤1/8 for α=min{1/2,(1/8)√[m³/(q+1)]}. Pinsker is used only to bound the TV distance between observation laws in a testing argument. For any source-independent randomized test, neighboring-sign error probabilities sum to at least 3/4. Under one uniform sign prior, the error probability for each bit of the actual row is at least 3/8 after conditioning on T=s. Comparing the predictor's W-coordinate order with the true pair gives an unsquared target-TV loss ≥(α/m) per incorrectly decoded bit. Summing disjoint coordinate pairs and averaging the actual uniform terminal row gives prior-average expected TV ≥α/8. The supremum over sources dominates this average for each fixed common predictor; only then is the algorithm infimum taken.

5. No conflict with stationary fast mixing

The active clock class has deterministic phase advancement modulo L and period L. A stationary chain cannot concentrate on the single horizon-aligned phase as the MM5 witness does; as a set, the uniform two-sided-fast-mixing stationary family studied in the separate A³ audit is a strict restriction. Upper bounds on a smaller class do not upper-bound the full arbitrary-initialization class. The distinct lower bounds can coexist: this clock spends a 1/L fraction of observed departures on the difficult rows while its selected horizon makes the terminal row a difficult one with probability one. A stationary/fast-mixing endpoint is instead averaged according to its own distribution and cannot be substituted for that horizon-aligned transient or periodic sampling pattern.

At β>0, S012 uses a related construction with absorbing symbol 0 and stationary witness δ_(0,0), allowing the active start to be singular with respect to this witness. Its full-history no-hazard event has probability (1−β)M and the target contrast contributes one additional 1−β. This proves a damped lower, not an undamped K⁵ bound for fixed positive β. These details do not assert a positive-mass stationary or fast-mixing hard family.
