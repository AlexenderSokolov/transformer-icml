# GN001 preserved route and obstruction

1. Starting question: full-packing robust RG gives statistical+Q^-J at O(log Q) depth, but does not by itself prove the statistical-order shallow-depth frontier for every Q. IC noiseless partial folding has that frontier but reflection attacks invalidate naive noisy transfer.
2. Reparameterizing the old plateau snake with row count N≈(sqrt(M)/Q)^(1/(J−1)) leaves an N^-J term in scalar recovery. When Q/sqrt(M) stays near one, N stays bounded and that term need not decay at all. This is not just algebraic slack: the old map has distinct scalar inputs with exactly equal curve output on its vertex plateaus; no decoder can distinguish those inputs pointwise. This invalidates that route as a pointwise all-Q inverse proof, although it is not itself a minimax impossibility proof for all IID estimators.
3. Shift the k-fold tent by eta/(2N), subtract eta/2 and divide by 1−eta. Then even-row active parameter is r/(1−eta), odd-row parameter 1−r/(1−eta). The final flat parameter interval is precisely the moving outer connector, and the initial flat disappears. Recursion preserves injectivity and minimum speed.
4. The rational eta shift and 1/(1−eta) scaling are not dyadic. Therefore RG's lattice-exact special evaluator cannot be inherited. FA001/GD literally give dyadic ideal-data descriptions; a new finite-rational conversion lemma is needed. Signed long division gives the identical <2^-P coefficient error, so the same bounded-primitive induction proves the E=1 tube. All parameter inputs, division, precision and decoder rational arithmetic are paid.
5. Mean approximation is already in that common E=1 tube. The robust bank mismatch is c/Q+1/Q+1/Q=(c+2)/Q, not an additional unproved exact-mean term. This yields inverse error O_J((c+2)/(Q N^(J−1))) without plateaus.
6. Necessary and sufficient capacity constants remain different. We claim sufficiency only for the explicit sufficiently large fixed ratio. Q>=sqrt(M) is handled by scalar mean, so small-Q cases are not silently excluded.
