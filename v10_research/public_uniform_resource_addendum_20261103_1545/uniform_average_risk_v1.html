Uniform second-order average risk under explicit access models

3 November 2026. This note extends two elementary access-model arguments to the actual-terminal unconditional binary-TV risk, uniformly over unknown binary order-two sources. It is not a novelty claim, generic Transformer lower bound or formal verification.

A fixed b-bit streaming summary needs b≥log₂[1/(128ε)], and a separate adaptive individual-bit probe model needs k≥3/(131072ε²), for the scopes and ε range below. Standard same-history counting matches these orders in the particular regime N=Θ(ε⁻²), if the stated companion R011 upper bound is supplied. Neither lower bound yields depth, head count, width, generic arithmetic operations or physical runtime.

1. Experiment

Observe X₁,…,X_N from an unknown homogeneous binary order-two process. The target is θ_(S_N)=P(X_{N+1}=1 | S_N), with S_N=(X_{N−1},X_N). Loss is binary total variation |p̂−θ_(S_N)| averaged jointly over the trajectory and source-independent algorithm randomness. A single estimator must work over the whole source class. The stationary iid Bernoulli(p) sources, p∈I=[1/4,3/4], are members; under those sources θ_(S_N)=p and P_p(S_N=00)=(1−p)²≥1/16. All lower bounds below use only this subclass. The terminal pair and public N are given free.

2. Streaming bits: a shared-random-tape covering bound

Suppose the decoder receives only b bits of history-dependent retained state, the terminal pair, N, and an arbitrarily long source-independent random tape shared with the state update. The decoder and update are independent of the unknown p. Rereads, other history-dependent real values, residuals, and side channels are forbidden unless charged to those b bits; a read-only full history would change the model.

Condition on S_N=00 and fix the complete random tape r. Since the tape is independent of the source, conditioning on this terminal event leaves its distribution unchanged, separately for every p. The decoder has at most K=2^b possible output levels A_r, though its chosen state can depend on both p and r. Every path has error at least dist(p,A_r). The interval I has length L=1/2. Partitioning it into Voronoi cells with lengths ℓ_j gives, for every fixed tape,

(1/L) ∫_I dist(p,A_r) dp ≥ (1/(4L)) Σ_j ℓ_j² ≥ L/(4K)=1/(8K).

Levels outside I can be projected into I; cells with zero length do no harm. Integrate also over the source-independent tape. Then use P_p(S_N=00)≥1/16 for each p:

sup_(p∈I) E_p |p̂−p| ≥ (1/16)(1/(8K)) = 1/(128·2^b).

Thus a uniformly valid risk bound ε requires b≥log₂[1/(128ε)] when positive. This applies even when the public tape is correlated with the stored state, because the proof fixes the entire tape before enumerating levels. It is a classical output-resolution/covering argument, and exact reals or uncharged hidden channels are not finite-bit states.

3. Adaptive bit probes

Separately, consider a model granting S_N for free and allowing at most k adaptive inspections of other individual input bits. The query schedule and algorithm randomness are source independent. Repeated probes reveal no new information, and conditional on S_N=00 the other bits in the iid subclass remain iid Bernoulli(p), including under adaptive selection.

If the unconditional worst-case risk is at most ε, then on each iid p its conditional risk given S_N=00 is at most 16ε. Put δ=16ε and assume ε≤1/256, hence δ≤1/16. Choose p_−=1/2−2δ and p_+=1/2+2δ. Let P_−,P_+ be the conditional transcript distributions. The two risks sum to at most 2δ. Since |a−p_−|+|a−p_+|≥4δ for every output a, the overlap inequality gives

2δ ≥ 4δ[1−TV(P_−,P_+)],   so TV(P_−,P_+)≥1/2.

The adaptive KL chain rule charges at most one Bernoulli divergence per fresh bit. Since p_+∈[1/4,3/4],

KL(P_− || P_+) ≤ k kl(p_−||p_+) ≤ (256/3) k δ².
Pinsker: KL(P_− || P_+) ≥ 2 TV² ≥ 1/2.
Therefore k ≥ 3/(512δ²) = 3/(131072ε²).

This uses a worst-case cap k, not an unproved conditional-iid claim about an adaptively chosen sample size. The classical two-point, KL chain-rule and Pinsker technique appears widely; see the adaptive-query arguments in Bhaskara et al., ICML 2023. The measurement unit is an individual input-symbol inspection. A parallel attention operation is not one free bit probe unless the execution model explicitly chooses to charge it that way.

4. Same-history comparator and remaining dependency

One streaming rule keeps the two most recent bits and, for each of the four binary contexts, the number of previous occurrences J_ab and number followed by 1, C_ab. It scans each observed symbol once and outputs (C_(S_N)+1)/(J_(S_N)+2). It takes O(log N) working bits for the fixed alphabet, O(N) symbol inspections, O(N log N) straightforward bit operations for counter updates, plus separately charged division and output precision. Rounding the final probability to O(log(1/ε)) bits adds at most O(ε) error.

An independently reviewed companion result, R011, states that this particular add-one rule has worst-case unconditional actual-terminal expected-TV risk O(1/√(N−1)) over homogeneous order-two sources with arbitrary initial pair distribution at its β=0 endpoint. Taking N=Θ(ε⁻²) and a large enough constant gives risk ≤ε with O(log(1/ε)) working bits and O(ε⁻²) symbol inspections. These orders match the two restricted-model lower bounds above in that regime; no matching bit-operation or Transformer lower bound is established. R011 is an inherited result rather than proved here: its proof and review must accompany any self-contained public package.
