局部概率保持变换：任务风险折叠的条件与边界

v10 研究增量 · 2026 年 11 月 6 日 UTC

结论：可以把单个相位反例推广到一类有限群局部改写证书，但真正必要的新增条件是“输出轨道重心可由后缀计算”。概率保持或输出不变性本身不够。本轮证明了多相位参考函数的折叠，尚未构造新的多相位真实 PreLN 网络。

一 固定任务与证明范围

输入为长度 N 的原始二元历史，来源为未知齐次二阶过程，各转移概率在 [c,1−c]，0<c<1/2。目标是在实际末端二元上下文上的下一符号分布，损失为期望终端 TV。主问题的平稳来源类包含在此；多数改写结论还能覆盖任意初始二元分布。

通信沿用 AA/BB：Alice 持有每四位的前两位，Bob 持有后两位。N 为四的倍数，末端后缀长度 b 是不超过 N/2 的二次幂且 b≥4 时，Alice 发送恰好 b/2 位即可交付后缀。真实架构仍为正 ε、完整特征归一化的实数 PreLN 与普通最终 softmax。新的参考函数不自动满足这个架构；物理误差 ζ 的传递均以已证实现为前提。

二 非循环的充分结构证书

设有限群 H 通过显式双射作用于历史，每个变换保持初始对、全部八个三元计数和末端后缀 Z。参考输出具有分解 Q(x)=q(Z(x),Φ(x))。在群不变的良好事件 G 上，有限相位 Φ 按一个传递群作用变化；各分支 q(z,a) 有明确求值方法。

齐次 Markov 似然在每个轨道上不变，目标也不变。经典凸风险平均因此给出 RP(QH)≤RP(Q)，逐来源成立。在 G 上轨道平均等于后缀函数 M(z)=|A|−1∑aq(z,a)。若分支 TV 直径为 D(z)，则

RP(M)≤RP(Q)+EP[D(Z)1Gᶜ]。

实际网络与 Q 的统一误差 ζ，以及 M 的求值误差 η，分别再加在右侧。此证明没有使用待证深度下界。它也没有免费提供计算效率：分支表、相位数、群描述与求值复杂度必须分别支付。昂贵的轨道平均只是证明工具，协议直接计算 M。

三 多相位局部改写例子

把后缀之前、初始对之后的部分分成 r 个不重叠区域，第 j 个区域含 mj 个八位试验块，8∑mj≤N−b−2。每区的相位 Fj是区域内重新计数的二次奇偶函数。在该区首个匹配位置对换 00110000 与 00011000。两词首尾各两位相同、内部三元计数相同，故整个历史似然不变；对换仅翻转本区相位。

不同区域的对换交换，得到 H=(Z/2)r。这不是重复使用同一个相位。对 r=2、两个不同正振幅，可得到四个不同分支 logit。

令 q(z,a)(1)=σ(d(z)+∑ρj(2aj−1))，ρj≥0，γ=1−2c⁸。逐块条件概率下界给出第 j 区没有匹配的概率至多 γmⱼ，不需要区块独立。逐坐标概率中点比较得到

RP(M)≤RP(Q)+¼∑ρjγmⱼ。

令 q₀(z)=σ(d(z))。均匀独立符号下的一阶项消失，二阶余项给出 TV(M,q₀)≤⅛∑ρj²。因此，在两端物理实现确有统一误差 ζh、ζb时，旧单读基线的风险增量至多 ζh+ζb+¼∑ρjγmⱼ+⅛∑ρj²。这里没有声明新的多相位物理实现已完成。

四 为什么概率保持与不变性仍不足

守恒障碍：若一个固定双射保持所有任意初始分布及开区间内所有独立二阶转移参数的来源律，它必须保持初始对和每一项三元计数。证明对似然比逐行参数取对数导数即可。因此，这些变换生成的轨道不会混合整个固定后缀纤维；轨道重心的后缀可计算性不能由概率保持自动推出。此必要性证明使用任意初始分布，不冒充只要求平稳来源保持时的结论。

真正的任务风险不足例子：对两个平稳 IID 来源 Bernoulli(c) 与 Bernoulli(1−c)，全历史总计数多数预测器 QN在所有共同保来源双射下不变。以两个来源等权作证明先验，TV 的 Bayes 风险为 (1−2c)eN；任意仅看 b 位后缀、允许随机化和任意概率输出的预测器，其平均风险至少 (1−2c)eb。N≥b+2 时 eN<eb，所以不存在在两个来源上都无损支配 QN的后缀预测器。

这个例子并不反驳一般廉价通信：Alice 只需用 O(log N) 位发送其总计数。它也不是整个二阶来源类上一致的预测器。仅证明了“所有不变预测器均能由指定短后缀逐源无损替代”错误。新的实际 PreLN 实现尚未完成，只有逻辑电路和带误差的条件传递。

强化到全类一致性：完整三元计数的经验转移预测器 Q_type 对整个内点二阶来源类、任意初始分布都有 O_c(N^(−1/2)) 的风险，并在保持末端上下文的类型移动下不变。占用数下尾与鞅分子界给出这一经典估计率。若 b≤log N/[4 log(1/c)]，每个仅看后缀的预测器在上述两个 IID 来源上的平均风险至少 (1−2c)N^(−1/4)，因此充分大的 N 下仍无法逐源支配 Q_type。这个强化消除了参考预测器不一致的限制，但依然没有真实固定宽度 PreLN 实现或一般通信下界。

另一个滞后二阶自旋例子中，±h 参数在各方单独观测上的分布相同，但末端目标相差 h，故零通信最坏风险至少 h/2。全数据的滞后二阶相关估计能达到 O(N−1/2)。这仅为零通信障碍，不是多轮通信下界。

五 计算账本与现有架构缺口

  • 直接后缀群重心：b/2 位通信；r 相位例子需 2r 次 sigmoid 与 O(Cd+r2r) 实数运算。不能忽略指数本地工作。
  • 单 sigmoid 替代 q₀：无需枚举相位，但支付 ∑ρj²/8。只对已有同一 d，继承其真实单读基线 W=Oc(1)、D=Oc(log N+b)、每系数 Oc(log N+b) 位、未共享总描述 Oc((log N+b)²)。
  • 上述基线仍有 N+1 行状态；跳过零注意力槽时工作 O(N²+ND)。实数表达定理不是浮点执行证书。
  • 新多相位硬网络的宽度、头数、深度、全状态 PreLN 误差均未证明。不能将参考函数公式误报为模型内表达结果。
  • 增长 r 时区间变短，失败概率按 r·exp(−ac(N−b)/r) 一类尺度退化，不能保留与 r 无关的原指数。

# Finite local rewrite groups and sourcewise task-risk folding

6 November 2026 UTC. Symbolic candidate v2; v1 is preserved. This version clarifies that a zone begins immediately after an even time, rather than calling its first symbol index even. No scientific executable, numerical check, training, physical-network construction, external write or upload accompanies this note. Administrative shell file reads/writes are not scientific execution. This is a sufficient structural criterion and a nontrivial multi-phase example, not a generic evolving-memory resource frontier or novelty claim.

## 1. Frozen contract

The source is an arbitrary homogeneous binary order-two Markov law P_(p,mu), with arbitrary initial-pair law mu and c <= p(y|a,b) <= 1-c for fixed 0<c<1/2. Input is the actual raw N-bit sample. The target on that history is tau_p(x)=p(.|x_(N-1),x_N). Risk is E_P TV(Q(X),tau_p(X)), with TV inside any predictor-randomness expectation. All predictors constructed below are deterministic and independent of p,mu.

Retain the final b-bit suffix Z, b>=2. For the original AA/BB physical comparison keep N divisible by four and b a power of two, 4<=b<=N/2. Alice owns positions 4i-3,4i-2, Bob positions 4i-1,4i. Physical claims concern the same positive-epsilon, full-feature real PreLN model with an ordinary final vocabulary softmax, and require a separately established uniform end-to-end approximation bound. A symbolic new phase decomposition is not a physical realization theorem.

## 2. General finite-group criterion

Let H be a finite group, |H|=h, acting by explicitly specified bijections T_g on all length-N words. Suppose:

1. Every T_g preserves the initial pair, all eight overlapping triple counts, and the final b-bit suffix.
2. A public finite phase set A and public map Phi:{0,1}^N -> A give a decomposition Q_ref(x)=q(Z(x),Phi(x)), where each q(z,a) is a probability vector.
3. There is an H-invariant good event G and an H-action on A such that Phi(T_g x)=g.Phi(x) for x in G, all g. The H-action on A is transitive. (More generally each orbit may be identified by a public suffix-computable label; average over that labeled orbit.)

Define

  Q_H(x) = (1/h) sum_(g in H) Q_ref(T_g x),
  M(z) = (1/|A|) sum_(a in A) q(z,a).

Then for every p,mu separately,

  R_P(Q_H) <= R_P(Q_ref).

Indeed homogeneous likelihood is mu(x_1,x_2) times the product of transition probabilities raised to the triple counts. Thus each T_g preserves P exactly, including when mu has zeros, and it preserves the target. Jensen's inequality followed by change of variables proves the claim. This part needs neither positivity nor G.

On G, orbit-stabilizer counting gives Q_H(x)=M(Z(x)): every phase appears h/|A| times in the group sum. The stabilizer need not be trivial; transitivity is the requirement. Let D(z)=max_(a,a' in A) TV(q(z,a),q(z,a')). Both Q_H(x) and M(z) are convex combinations of these branch vectors at the same z, even off G. Hence

  TV(Q_H(x),M(Z(x))) <= D(Z(x)) 1_(G complement),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_ref) + E_P[D(Z)1_(G complement)].       (1)

If sup_x TV(Q_phys(x),Q_ref(x))<=zeta_h, add zeta_h to the right side with Q_phys replacing Q_ref. If a suffix evaluator returns M within uniform TV eta, add eta as well. The expensive orbit predictor Q_H is only a proof device; the task protocol evaluates M directly.

This is a finite algebraic criterion, not an assumption of cheapness. An arbitrary q(z,a) can be computationally hard and its table can have 2^b|A| entries. A large group or phase set can destroy local efficiency even while communication remains small. Section 5 records every such dependence.

## 3. Verifiable local certificates

A block replacement preserves homogeneous order-two likelihood if old/new words have the same first two and last two symbols and identical internal triple-count vectors. Cross-boundary triples are then identical for every surrounding word. Blocks must lie after the initial pair and before the retained suffix.

A useful construction uses a family of distinct same-length words W={w_a:a in K}, all with those boundary/type identities. Let a finite group K act regularly on their labels. At a prescribed bank of disjoint aligned blocks, select the first block belonging to W; replace w_a by w_(g a), leaving unmatched histories unchanged. This is a genuine K-action, since membership and first-hit selection are unchanged. Actions in disjoint banks commute, giving a product group. A union of several K-orbits of words also works when the action is explicitly defined on each and selection membership is invariant.

Phase equivariance must be verified, not inferred from type preservation. One general finite-state verification method is as follows. A public deterministic phase evaluator has finite state S and symbol transitions U_(t,y), allowing a public position clock. For a proposed block rewrite w -> w', find a state permutation V representing its intended phase action. Check the block transformation identity U_w'=V U_w on every reachable incoming state (or all states, which is stronger), and check that all later transitions commute with V. Check that the final readout obeys readout(Vs)=g.readout(s). This proves whole-history equivariance, including later suffix effects. A finite clock can encode all positions for fixed N; this makes verification finite, not necessarily efficient. Multiple generators must additionally satisfy their group relations on states or directly on the full-word maps. We do not infer commuting rewrites merely because their phase labels commute.

For a bank of m disjoint L-bit trials and a membership library of q distinct words, every next trial belongs to the library with conditional probability at least q c^L, regardless of previous failure and initial law. Repeated conditioning gives

  P(no hit in bank) <= (1-q c^L)^m.                       (2)

No block independence is assumed. A union bound over banks supplies the exceptional probability when G requires all banks to have hits. This is valid for fixed c; large L or a growing number of banks may make it ineffective. Variable trial lengths have a product bound with their corresponding q_i c^(L_i). Empty banks have failure probability one.

## 4. Explicit r-phase example

Take r>=2 and nonnegative integers m_1,...,m_r with 8 sum_j m_j <= N-b-2. Starting just after time 2, allocate consecutive disjoint zones I_j of lengths 8m_j. Each zone starts immediately after an even time; its first symbol index is odd. Unused pre-suffix bits and the suffix are outside the zones. In zone j define the RESET quadratic phase

  F_j(x) = sum_(k in I_j, k odd) x_k sum_(ell in I_j, ell<k) x_ell mod 2.

Resetting at zone boundaries is part of the definition, not a discarded cross term of the original full-history phase. Each F_j is a finite-state stream computation using local parity and its accumulated phase; the public clock chooses its zone. This example changes the reference nuisance to r phases. It does not assert that the original one-phase physical network secretly implements r phases.

Partition each zone into its m_j eight-bit blocks. Use

  w=00110000,    w'=00011000.

Both begin/end with 00. Their internal triples are 001,011,110,100 once each and 000 twice. Both have two ones. The involution T_j interchanges w,w' in the first matching block of zone j and is the identity if none match. These r involutions commute because their selection and edits use disjoint zones. They define H=(Z/2)^r on all histories, with group size 2^r.

Let E_j be the event of a hit in zone j. If the incoming within-zone parity is S, the block contributions to F_j are S for w and S+1 for w', because the selected odd local positions are respectively 3 and 5. The outgoing within-zone parity is unchanged (two ones), so all later phase contributions are unchanged. Thus T_j flips F_j on E_j and leaves every other phase unchanged. This also proves the identity in the presence of preceding blocks in the same zone. On G=intersection_j E_j the phase vector ranges over every element of {0,1}^r under H exactly once, so this is a genuinely 2^r-branch orbit rather than a relabeled two-branch example. G is invariant.

For public rho_j>=0 and public suffix function d define

  q(z,a)(1)=sigmoid(d(z)+sum_j rho_j(2a_j-1)),
  q(z,a)(0)=1-q(z,a)(1),
  M(z)(1)=2^(-r) sum_(a in {0,1}^r)
                       sigmoid(d(z)+sum_j rho_j(2a_j-1)).

No assumption is made that the rho_j are equal. If a fixed bounded-logit/state envelope is desired, impose the additional public bound sum_j rho_j<=R for fixed R; this controls the perturbation magnitude but does not itself prove any PreLN compilation or fixed-width implementation. For example r=2 with distinct positive rho_1,rho_2 gives four distinct branch logits and a group of order four. Set m_1=m_2=floor((N-b-2)/16) for an immediate explicit family. All four phases occur on every good orbit, regardless of the source.

Write gamma=1-2c^8 and let gamma^0=1. Equation (2) gives P(E_j complement)<=gamma^(m_j), hence P(G complement)<=min(1,sum_j gamma^(m_j)). A diameter-only application of (1) gives excess at most min(1,sum_j rho_j/2) times that failure bound.

A sharper bound avoids requiring every phase hit. At any x, Q_H averages exactly the hit coordinates and holds each missing coordinate at its observed value. Symmetrizing one missing coordinate changes the first probability by at most rho_j/4: the two branch logits differ by 2rho_j, sigmoid is 1/4-Lipschitz, and a midpoint is half the branch gap. This estimate remains valid after averaging any other coordinates. Telescoping over missing coordinates yields

  TV(M(Z(x)),Q_H(x)) <= sum_j (rho_j/4)1_(E_j complement),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_ref)+sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_phys)+zeta_h+sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j). (3)

The last line is conditional on a proved physical uniform comparison for this r-phase reference. It is not evidence of such a compilation.

An alternative cheap suffix rule is q_0(1|z)=sigmoid(d(z)). Let independent uniform signs epsilon_j define U=sum_j rho_j epsilon_j. Since E U=0, E U^2=sum_j rho_j^2 and |sigmoid''|<=1/4, Taylor's theorem gives

  TV(M(z),q_0(z)) <= (1/8)sum_j rho_j^2.                 (4)

Therefore the original separately compiled one-head baseline for the SAME d, with error zeta_b, obeys the conditional comparison

  R_P(Q_base) <= R_P(Q_phys)+zeta_h+zeta_b
                +sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j)
                +(1/8)sum_j rho_j^2.                  (5)

Unlike enumerating M, using q_0 does not require 2^r branch evaluations. The price is the explicitly displayed quadratic amplitude term. If total variance sum rho_j^2 is large it need not be small. No sourcewise multiplicative ratio is inferred from these additive bounds.

## 5. Resource ledger and model boundary

Under aligned AA/BB ownership, Alice transmits exactly b/2 suffix bits in public order; Bob reconstructs Z and evaluates the public suffix rule. For general alignment at most b bits suffice. Group size does not multiply communication because phases and original prefix are never transmitted. This is worst-case deterministic one-way bit communication, with no real-valued transmissions or free silence/timing channel.

For a general finite phase table, evaluation costs C_M(b,|A|), not automatically O(b). A direct algorithm computes the suffix once, then enumerates |A| branches with total cost C_Z(b)+sum_a C_q(a)+O(|A|) vector arithmetic; public group/phase description size must be added to any parameter ledger. If only a group-indexed branch description is supplied, the naive sum has h terms and may repeat phases; no free orbit reduction is assumed. The selection-and-rewrite proof maps can be implemented by scanning banks, but Q_H still invokes the original reference up to h times and is not claimed cheap.

For the explicit logistic example, enumerate 2^r sign vectors. Compute d once, compute each signed sum in O(r) arithmetic operations, and evaluate one sigmoid per branch. This gives O(C_d(b)+r2^r) arithmetic work and 2^r sigmoid evaluations, using streaming accumulation and O(r) amplitude storage, in addition to the suffix and evaluator workspace. Gray-code reuse can lower arithmetic overhead but is not needed for this bound. Amplitude/phase descriptions cost at least the supplied description of r amplitudes and zone boundaries. If r grows, exponential local work remains real. The q_0 alternative requires only C_d(b) and one sigmoid, with error (4).

For the imported d, the prior symbolic ledger is O_c(b) exact-real local primitive work plus sigmoid, constant-many O(log b)-bit count registers, and a one-head physical baseline with width O_c(1), depth O_c(log N+b), per-coefficient description O_c(log N+b), untied total description O_c((log N+b)^2). These baseline bounds are inherited only for that same d and its separately correct PreLN compilation. Its physical all-row work still includes N+1 rows and the useful dense head, O(N^2+ND) when zero heads are skipped; constant width is not constant total memory/work.

No multi-phase physical width, depth, compiler precision, useful-head count, parameter-description or finite-arithmetic theorem is established here. Neither an average of 2^r softmax outputs nor deleting nuisance banks is automatically legal inside the frozen ordinary-softmax full-feature PreLN architecture. Equation (5) uses the already separately compiled baseline and assumes a proved new hard-family approximation if such a family is later supplied. Full-feature LN coupling is not ignored.

## 6. Failed routes and precise limits

- A measure-preserving rewrite need not change any nuisance, and a nuisance-flipping rewrite need not preserve Markov likelihood. Both certificates are essential.
- The shorter five-bit signed-kernel paths 11000 and 01100 are not substituted for a local source-measure-preserving permutation: their first two bits differ, so cross-boundary triple counts can change. Signed cancellation and exact permutation averaging are different mechanisms.
- Merely averaging logits does not equal M. The only permission to replace M by q_0 here is the paid Taylor error (4).
- Transitivity cannot be dropped: different phase orbits can have different means depending on unrevealed prefix information. An orbit label must be suffix-computable, or this route does not yield a suffix predictor.
- Independent block occurrences are not assumed; the failure bound is conditional minorization. Zero transition bounds preserve exact orbit domination but remove useful exponential decay.
- Repeating the original phase r times would not create r independent group coordinates. The reset-zone definition and separate phase-flip proof are what establish the multi-phase example.
- If r grows with N with balanced m_j roughly (N-b)/(8r), the union bound scales as r exp(-a_c(N-b)/r), not exp(-a_c(N-b)) with a uniform constant. The sharper excess scales with the amplitudes but still has this zone-length deterioration.
- The companion UNIVERSAL_REWRITE_CONSERVATION.candidate.v1.md proves that preservation of every source with arbitrary initial law and independent kernel rows ranging over an open interior forces any deterministic bijection to preserve the initial pair and all triple counts. Thus universal rewrite groups cannot mix the entire suffix fiber, whose histories can have different total counts. Output algebra is indispensable. This necessity statement is not asserted for the stationary-only source class, and is not a task-risk lower bound.
- No architecture-preserving universal Rao-Blackwellization, minimax improvement, new output-simulation lower, necessity of the criterion, or generic task-risk/depth frontier follows.

# Universal deterministic rewrite conservation

6 November 2026 UTC. Candidate v1. Symbolic statement; independent review pending. No architecture or communication conclusion is implied by this lemma.

Fix N>=3 and binary homogeneous second-order path laws with arbitrary initial-pair distribution μ and four independent transition probabilities p_ab in the open interval (c,1-c), 0<c<1/2. Let τ be a bijection of {0,1}^N that preserves EACH such path measure. Then τ preserves the initial pair and every triple count. Conversely every bijection with these properties preserves every homogeneous second-order path measure, even with zero probabilities.

Proof. In a finite space, preservation by a bijection means P(x)=P(τx). Concentrating μ on x's initial pair proves that τx has the same initial pair: every path with that pair has positive probability, all other initial pairs have probability zero. Write A_ab=C_ab1(x), B_ab=C_ab0(x), and primed versions for τx. Equality of path masses gives

  product_ab p_ab^(A_ab) (1-p_ab)^(B_ab)
  = product_ab p_ab^(A'_ab) (1-p_ab)^(B'_ab).

Fix the other three row parameters. For each row this says p^u(1-p)^v is a positive constant throughout an open interval, with integer u=A-A' and v=B-B'. Logarithmic differentiation yields u/p-v/(1-p)=0 throughout that interval, hence u-(u+v)p=0, so u=v=0. All counts agree. The converse follows directly from the likelihood factorization.

The total number of ones is x_1+x_2+sum_ab C_ab1. Hence every such rewrite preserves that count. In particular no family of universally preserving bijections can mix across different values of this statistic. A chain or group built only from such bijections cannot be uniformly mixing on the whole set of histories sharing a terminal suffix: that suffix class contains differing counts whenever N>b>=2.

This is a limitation on an exact universal orbit-to-suffix reduction for arbitrary functions. It is NOT a lower bound on task risk, nor a proof that a count-dependent predictor lacks some other cheap risk replacement. The assumed arbitrary initial laws are essential to the stated initial-pair proof; this lemma is not asserted from preservation of the stationary-only source class alone. Positive row parameters on an open interval are enough; no boundary transition is used in the necessity proof.

Failure retained: a broad attempt to infer whole suffix-fiber mixing solely from universal probability preservation fails because the full Markov type is conserved. Additional output algebra, rather than source preservation alone, must make an orbit barycenter suffix-computable.

# Universal local invariance does not imply suffix-only risk domination

6 November 2026 UTC. Analytical candidate v2; v1 is preserved. This version adds the scaling and full-class limitation in Section 7. Symbolic proof; no mathematical executable, training, upload, or public mutation. This is a narrow task-risk counterexample, not a communication lower bound or a new statistical rate.

## 1. Precise conclusion

Fix 0<c<1/2 and integers N>=b+2, b>=1. There is a source-independent binary next-symbol predictor Q_N, invariant under every probability-preserving bijection common to all homogeneous binary order-two source laws, for which NO predictor depending only on the final b symbols has risk no greater at both IID Bernoulli(c) and IID Bernoulli(1-c).

The same conclusion holds for the specific local involution in LOCAL_TYPE_INVOLUTION_CERTIFICATE.candidate.v2.md whenever its horizon restrictions are met. Thus even exact invariance under all universal type moves does not imply exact sourcewise domination by a prescribed short suffix predictor. The loss is actual prediction TV; a replacement may output arbitrary distributions and need not reconstruct Q_N.

This does NOT refute cheap general distributed task folding. Indeed Q_N can be evaluated with O(log N) communication by exchanging counts. It also does not refute minimax domination with slack or a constant-factor risk allowance.

## 2. Two sources and a fully invariant predictor

Let P_- be IID Bernoulli(c), P_+ be IID Bernoulli(1-c). Both are stationary homogeneous binary order-two sources in the usual interior class P_c. They have constant next-one probabilities c and 1-c. Put Delta=1-2c and S(x)=sum_i x_i.

Define Q_N's probability of one to be c when S<N/2, 1-c when S>N/2, and 1/2 on a tie. This is one predictor, independent of the actual source.

The complete Markov type determines S:

 S=x_1+x_2+sum_(a,b) C_ab1.

Consequently Q_N is full-type invariant and invariant under the reviewed local rewrite. More strongly, suppose a bijection j preserves every source law in the model. Finite-space measure preservation gives P_-(x)=P_-(j(x)); since c!=1/2, equality of c^S(1-c)^(N-S) forces S(j(x))=S(x). Hence Q_N is invariant under every such bijection. This statement concerns bijections, not arbitrary measure-preserving randomized kernels.

## 3. Bayes TV risk, including arbitrary replacement outputs

Introduce an equal prior on the two sources only as a proof device. Given an observed word of length n, the likelihood ratio is

 L_n=((1-c)/c)^(2S-n).

For posterior probability pi of P_+, the conditional TV loss of a reported probability q is

 pi |q-(1-c)|+(1-pi)|q-c|.

Clipping q to [c,1-c] cannot increase this loss. On this interval it is affine in q; its minimum is Delta min(pi,1-pi), achieved at the more probable endpoint (and every point of the interval when pi=1/2). Thus majority is a Bayes-optimal TV predictor, not merely an output classifier. Randomized predictions cannot do better, by the same pointwise minimization or convexity.

Write

 e_n = Pr_(Bin(n,c))(S>n/2) + (1/2) Pr_(Bin(n,c))(S=n/2).

Symmetry between the two sources gives equal-prior optimal prediction risk Delta e_n. The full-history Q_N attains Delta e_N. Under IID sources the final b symbols are just a length-b IID experiment, so every suffix-b predictor Q_s satisfies

 (R_-(Q_s)+R_+(Q_s))/2 >= Delta e_b.

## 4. Strict improvement from at least two additional observations

Bayes error cannot increase with more observations: the later observer can ignore them. It strictly decreases between n and n+2.

For even n, the event S=n/2 has positive mixture probability and posterior pi=1/2. Two additional observations have nonzero probabilities of 00 and 11 under both sources. These yield posterior values on opposite sides of 1/2. The posterior is a martingale, and min(pi,1-pi) is concave with a strict corner at 1/2. Conditional expectation of this function is therefore strictly smaller after the two observations on that event.

For odd n, use the positive-probability event S=(n+1)/2, whose likelihood-ratio exponent is +1. Two observations 00 yield exponent -1, whereas 11 yield exponent +3. Their posterior values again lie strictly on opposite sides of 1/2, giving strict Jensen improvement. Other old observations cannot increase expected Bayes error. Thus e_(n+2)<e_n.

It follows that N>=b+2 implies e_N<e_b. Set g=Delta(e_b-e_N)>0. If one suffix predictor sourcewise dominated Q_N at both sources, averaging those two inequalities would imply Delta e_b<=Delta e_N, a contradiction. In fact every suffix predictor has at least one source on which its excess risk over Q_N is at least g.

No assertion that g stays bounded away from zero is made. For fixed c and large b, this gap can be very small; it is not a new minimax rate.

## 5. Architecture and approximation boundaries

There is a simple bounded-gain constant-width LOGICAL uniform-head program for Q_N. With the actual synthetic zero-symbol terminal, a zero-score scalar full-prefix attention read returns r=S/(N+1). Subtract N/[2(N+1)]. Repeated fixed-gain doubling with clipping to [-1,1], for ceil(log2(2(N+1))) stages, maps every nonzero grid offset to its sign and preserves a tie as zero. All states and primitive gains remain bounded; depth is O(log N). Use final logits (a t,0), a=log((1-c)/c), with t the resulting sign. Ordinary softmax returns exactly the desired probabilities, including 1/2 at a tie. This is a logical circuit, not an independently audited all-feature PreLN realization.

The reviewed small-amplitude paired-state compiler is a possible physical route. This note does not assert a new complete physical theorem or exact physical invariance. If a separately verified physical construction has uniform TV error zeta, then its risk differs from Q_N by at most zeta at each source and its rewrite modulus is at most 2zeta. If zeta<g, NO suffix-b predictor can sourcewise dominate that physical output either, by the same mixture gap. Exact equality of physical outputs on orbits does not follow from uniform approximation.

The necessary accuracy is tied to g. Selecting zeta<g without charging log(1/zeta), preparation, amplitude, restoration, final normalization, and parameter precision would conceal costs. In particular no O(log N) physical claim is obtained here when b grows with N. No claim about useful-head ablations is supplied.

## 6. Why this is not the missing communication counterexample

Under AA/BB ownership Alice sends her number of ones in ceil(log2(N/2+1)) bits; Bob adds his count and evaluates Q_N. Hence the example is cheap in ordinary interactive communication. It proves that local invariance does not force suffix-only sourcewise domination, but leaves open a general efficient task-folding theorem that may use other cheap statistics.

## 7. Nontrivial scaling and the full-class limitation

The strict gap need not demand exponentially many bits in N. For b>=1 the all-ones sample is an error under Bernoulli(c), so e_b>=c^b. Hoeffding's bound gives e_N<=exp[-2N(1/2-c)^2]. Hence

 g >= Delta {c^b-exp[-2N(1/2-c)^2]}.

Take N divisible by four and b=Theta(log N), with b a power of two if the prior suffix convention requires it. For all sufficiently large N, g>=Delta c^b/2, an inverse-polynomial lower bound in N. The logical circuit has no variable-input logarithm stage; its terminal logit scale a depends only on fixed c. Any physical transfer must still verify full preparation, normalization, restoration, and complete-state error at this tolerance.

Q_N is useful and Bayes-optimal on the two IID witness sources. A theorem claiming sourcewise suffix domination for every invariant predictor is disproved by these two witnesses. A theorem restricted to globally consistent or minimax-rate-optimal predictors, or allowing statistical slack, needs a different argument.

# A globally consistent type-invariant predictor need not admit suffix domination

6 November 2026 UTC. Private analytical candidate v1. Symbolic result; no executable, training, upload, or new-rate claim. This strengthens the consistency scope of INVARIANCE_DOES_NOT_FORCE_SUFFIX_DOMINATION.candidate.v1.md, without giving an actual PreLN construction or an arbitrary-message communication lower.

## 1. Full-type empirical predictor and uniform risk

Fix 0<c<1/2. Let P_c contain homogeneous binary order-two kernels with every transition probability in [c,1-c], with any initial law. For each context v in {00,01,10,11}, define

 D_v=sum_(i=3)^N 1{(X_(i-2),X_(i-1))=v},
 A_v=sum_(i=3)^N 1{(X_(i-2),X_(i-1))=v} X_i.

Use A_v/D_v when D_v>0 and 1/2 otherwise, clipped to [c,1-c], as p_hat_v. Report p_hat_(X_(N-1),X_N) as the next-one probability. This predictor factors through full triple counts and the final pair. It is exactly invariant under the reviewed local type rewrite, and all endpoint-preserving type moves.

Here is an elementary uniform risk bound, included to avoid hiding a statistical assumption. Put L=floor((N-3)/2), and assume N>=6. Among the context pairs counted in D_v are the L disjoint pairs (X_3,X_4),(X_5,X_6),...,(X_(2L+1),X_(2L+2)). Conditional on all symbols preceding any such pair, its probability of equaling v is at least c^2. For their indicators B_j, iterated conditioning gives, for every t>0,

 E exp[-t sum_j B_j] <= (1-c^2+c^2 exp(-t))^L.

The usual one-line exponential Markov optimization (the binomial lower-tail Chernoff bound) therefore gives

 Pr(D_v<Lc^2/2) <= exp(-Lc^2/8).

Let p_v=p(1|v) and Z_v=A_v-p_v D_v. Its summands are martingale differences relative to the raw history filtration, so E Z_v^2<= (N-2)/4 and E|Z_v|<=sqrt(N)/2. On D_v>=Lc^2/2,

 |p_hat_v-p_v| <= 2|Z_v|/(Lc^2).

Clipping cannot worsen the error, and off this event the error is at most one. Hence

 E|p_hat_v-p_v| <= sqrt(N)/(Lc^2)+exp(-Lc^2/8).

The actual final context is random; no independence with the estimator is assumed. Bound its error by the sum of the four context errors to obtain

 sup_(P in P_c) R_P(Q_type)
 <= B_N := 4sqrt(N)/(Lc^2)+4exp(-Lc^2/8)
 = O_c(N^(-1/2)).

The loose constants and classical rate are not claimed as new. Arbitrary initial laws do not affect this proof because the selected pair blocks begin after the initial pair.

## 2. Every sufficiently short suffix loses on at least one IID witness

For equal-prior IID Bernoulli(c) and Bernoulli(1-c), the companion proof shows that every suffix-b predictor has mixture TV risk at least Delta e_b, where Delta=1-2c and e_b is the optimal majority-testing error. In particular e_b>=c^b by the all-ones event under Bernoulli(c).

Choose any integer b>=1 with

 b <= log N/[4 log(1/c)].

Then c^b>=N^(-1/4). Consequently every suffix-b predictor Q_s satisfies

 [R_-(Q_s)+R_+(Q_s)]/2 >= Delta N^(-1/4),

while the globally consistent Q_type satisfies both witness risks <=B_N=O_c(N^(-1/2)). For all sufficiently large N, Delta N^(-1/4)>B_N. No suffix-b predictor can then sourcewise dominate Q_type, even just on these two sources.

Moreover some witness has excess risk at least Delta N^(-1/4)-B_N. Choosing b to be the largest power of two below the displayed bound retains b=Theta_c(log N), and N may remain divisible by four.

This is a real task-risk separation allowing arbitrary randomized replacement outputs. It does not depend on approximating Q_type's original outputs. It also withstands uniform output perturbations zeta<Delta N^(-1/4)-B_N, but such a perturbation only gives approximate invariance, not exact physical invariance.

 max{R_(+h),R_(-h)} >= h/2.

# Universal local invariance does not imply suffix-only risk domination

6 November 2026 UTC. Private analytical candidate v1. Symbolic proof; no mathematical executable, training, upload, or public mutation. This is a narrow task-risk counterexample, not a communication lower bound or a new statistical rate.

## 1. Precise conclusion

Fix 0<c<1/2 and integers N>=b+2, b>=1. There is a source-independent binary next-symbol predictor Q_N, invariant under every probability-preserving bijection common to all homogeneous binary order-two source laws, for which NO predictor depending only on the final b symbols has risk no greater at both IID Bernoulli(c) and IID Bernoulli(1-c).

The same conclusion holds for the specific local involution in LOCAL_TYPE_INVOLUTION_CERTIFICATE.candidate.v2.md whenever its horizon restrictions are met. Thus even exact invariance under all universal type moves does not imply exact sourcewise domination by a prescribed short suffix predictor. The loss is actual prediction TV; a replacement may output arbitrary distributions and need not reconstruct Q_N.

This does NOT refute cheap general distributed task folding. Indeed Q_N can be evaluated with O(log N) communication by exchanging counts. It also does not refute minimax domination with slack or a constant-factor risk allowance.

## 2. Two sources and a fully invariant predictor

Let P_- be IID Bernoulli(c), P_+ be IID Bernoulli(1-c). Both are stationary homogeneous binary order-two sources in the usual interior class P_c. They have constant next-one probabilities c and 1-c. Put Delta=1-2c and S(x)=sum_i x_i.

Define Q_N's probability of one to be c when S<N/2, 1-c when S>N/2, and 1/2 on a tie. This is one predictor, independent of the actual source.

The complete Markov type determines S:

 S=x_1+x_2+sum_(a,b) C_ab1.

Consequently Q_N is full-type invariant and invariant under the reviewed local rewrite. More strongly, suppose a bijection j preserves every source law in the model. Finite-space measure preservation gives P_-(x)=P_-(j(x)); since c!=1/2, equality of c^S(1-c)^(N-S) forces S(j(x))=S(x). Hence Q_N is invariant under every such bijection. This statement concerns bijections, not arbitrary measure-preserving randomized kernels.

## 3. Bayes TV risk, including arbitrary replacement outputs

Introduce an equal prior on the two sources only as a proof device. Given an observed word of length n, the likelihood ratio is

 L_n=((1-c)/c)^(2S-n).

For posterior probability pi of P_+, the conditional TV loss of a reported probability q is

 pi |q-(1-c)|+(1-pi)|q-c|.

Clipping q to [c,1-c] cannot increase this loss. On this interval it is affine in q; its minimum is Delta min(pi,1-pi), achieved at the more probable endpoint (and every point of the interval when pi=1/2). Thus majority is a Bayes-optimal TV predictor, not merely an output classifier. Randomized predictions cannot do better, by the same pointwise minimization or convexity.

Write

 e_n = Pr_(Bin(n,c))(S>n/2) + (1/2) Pr_(Bin(n,c))(S=n/2).

Symmetry between the two sources gives equal-prior optimal prediction risk Delta e_n. The full-history Q_N attains Delta e_N. Under IID sources the final b symbols are just a length-b IID experiment, so every suffix-b predictor Q_s satisfies

 (R_-(Q_s)+R_+(Q_s))/2 >= Delta e_b.

## 4. Strict improvement from at least two additional observations

Bayes error cannot increase with more observations: the later observer can ignore them. It strictly decreases between n and n+2.

For even n, the event S=n/2 has positive mixture probability and posterior pi=1/2. Two additional observations have nonzero probabilities of 00 and 11 under both sources. These yield posterior values on opposite sides of 1/2. The posterior is a martingale, and min(pi,1-pi) is concave with a strict corner at 1/2. Conditional expectation of this function is therefore strictly smaller after the two observations on that event.

For odd n, use the positive-probability event S=(n+1)/2, whose likelihood-ratio exponent is +1. Two observations 00 yield exponent -1, whereas 11 yield exponent +3. Their posterior values again lie strictly on opposite sides of 1/2, giving strict Jensen improvement. Other old observations cannot increase expected Bayes error. Thus e_(n+2)<e_n.

It follows that N>=b+2 implies e_N<e_b. Set g=Delta(e_b-e_N)>0. If one suffix predictor sourcewise dominated Q_N at both sources, averaging those two inequalities would imply Delta e_b<=Delta e_N, a contradiction. In fact every suffix predictor has at least one source on which its excess risk over Q_N is at least g.

No assertion that g stays bounded away from zero is made. For fixed c and large b, this gap can be very small; it is not a new minimax rate.

## 5. Architecture and approximation boundaries

There is a simple bounded-gain constant-width LOGICAL uniform-head program for Q_N. With the actual synthetic zero-symbol terminal, a zero-score scalar full-prefix attention read returns r=S/(N+1). Subtract N/[2(N+1)]. Repeated fixed-gain doubling with clipping to [-1,1], for ceil(log2(2(N+1))) stages, maps every nonzero grid offset to its sign and preserves a tie as zero. All states and primitive gains remain bounded; depth is O(log N). Use final logits (a t,0), a=log((1-c)/c), with t the resulting sign. Ordinary softmax returns exactly the desired probabilities, including 1/2 at a tie. This is a logical circuit, not an independently audited all-feature PreLN realization.

The reviewed small-amplitude paired-state compiler is a possible physical route. This note does not assert a new complete physical theorem or exact physical invariance. If a separately verified physical construction has uniform TV error zeta, then its risk differs from Q_N by at most zeta at each source and its rewrite modulus is at most 2zeta. If zeta<g, NO suffix-b predictor can sourcewise dominate that physical output either, by the same mixture gap. Exact equality of physical outputs on orbits does not follow from uniform approximation.

The necessary accuracy is tied to g. Selecting zeta<g without charging log(1/zeta), preparation, amplitude, restoration, final normalization, and parameter precision would conceal costs. In particular no O(log N) physical claim is obtained here when b grows with N. No claim about useful-head ablations is supplied.

## 6. Why this is not the missing communication counterexample

Under AA/BB ownership Alice sends her number of ones in ceil(log2(N/2+1)) bits; Bob adds his count and evaluates Q_N. Hence the example is cheap in ordinary interactive communication. It proves that local invariance does not force suffix-only sourcewise domination, but leaves open a general efficient task-folding theorem that may use other cheap statistics.

# Finite local rewrite groups and sourcewise task-risk folding

6 November 2026 UTC. New private symbolic candidate v1. No scientific executable, numerical check, training, physical-network construction, external write or upload accompanies this note. Administrative shell file reads/writes are not scientific execution. This is a sufficient structural criterion and a nontrivial multi-phase example, not a generic evolving-memory resource frontier or novelty claim.

## 1. Frozen contract

The source is an arbitrary homogeneous binary order-two Markov law P_(p,mu), with arbitrary initial-pair law mu and c <= p(y|a,b) <= 1-c for fixed 0<c<1/2. Input is the actual raw N-bit sample. The target on that history is tau_p(x)=p(.|x_(N-1),x_N). Risk is E_P TV(Q(X),tau_p(X)), with TV inside any predictor-randomness expectation. All predictors constructed below are deterministic and independent of p,mu.

Retain the final b-bit suffix Z, b>=2. For the original AA/BB physical comparison keep N divisible by four and b a power of two, 4<=b<=N/2. Alice owns positions 4i-3,4i-2, Bob positions 4i-1,4i. Physical claims concern the same positive-epsilon, full-feature real PreLN model with an ordinary final vocabulary softmax, and require a separately established uniform end-to-end approximation bound. A symbolic new phase decomposition is not a physical realization theorem.

## 2. General finite-group criterion

Let H be a finite group, |H|=h, acting by explicitly specified bijections T_g on all length-N words. Suppose:

1. Every T_g preserves the initial pair, all eight overlapping triple counts, and the final b-bit suffix.
2. A public finite phase set A and public map Phi:{0,1}^N -> A give a decomposition Q_ref(x)=q(Z(x),Phi(x)), where each q(z,a) is a probability vector.
3. There is an H-invariant good event G and an H-action on A such that Phi(T_g x)=g.Phi(x) for x in G, all g. The H-action on A is transitive. (More generally each orbit may be identified by a public suffix-computable label; average over that labeled orbit.)

Define

  Q_H(x) = (1/h) sum_(g in H) Q_ref(T_g x),
  M(z) = (1/|A|) sum_(a in A) q(z,a).

Then for every p,mu separately,

  R_P(Q_H) <= R_P(Q_ref).

Indeed homogeneous likelihood is mu(x_1,x_2) times the product of transition probabilities raised to the triple counts. Thus each T_g preserves P exactly, including when mu has zeros, and it preserves the target. Jensen's inequality followed by change of variables proves the claim. This part needs neither positivity nor G.

On G, orbit-stabilizer counting gives Q_H(x)=M(Z(x)): every phase appears h/|A| times in the group sum. The stabilizer need not be trivial; transitivity is the requirement. Let D(z)=max_(a,a' in A) TV(q(z,a),q(z,a')). Both Q_H(x) and M(z) are convex combinations of these branch vectors at the same z, even off G. Hence

  TV(Q_H(x),M(Z(x))) <= D(Z(x)) 1_(G complement),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_ref) + E_P[D(Z)1_(G complement)].       (1)

If sup_x TV(Q_phys(x),Q_ref(x))<=zeta_h, add zeta_h to the right side with Q_phys replacing Q_ref. If a suffix evaluator returns M within uniform TV eta, add eta as well. The expensive orbit predictor Q_H is only a proof device; the task protocol evaluates M directly.

This is a finite algebraic criterion, not an assumption of cheapness. An arbitrary q(z,a) can be computationally hard and its table can have 2^b|A| entries. A large group or phase set can destroy local efficiency even while communication remains small. Section 5 records every such dependence.

## 3. Verifiable local certificates

A block replacement preserves homogeneous order-two likelihood if old/new words have the same first two and last two symbols and identical internal triple-count vectors. Cross-boundary triples are then identical for every surrounding word. Blocks must lie after the initial pair and before the retained suffix.

A useful construction uses a family of distinct same-length words W={w_a:a in K}, all with those boundary/type identities. Let a finite group K act regularly on their labels. At a prescribed bank of disjoint aligned blocks, select the first block belonging to W; replace w_a by w_(g a), leaving unmatched histories unchanged. This is a genuine K-action, since membership and first-hit selection are unchanged. Actions in disjoint banks commute, giving a product group. A union of several K-orbits of words also works when the action is explicitly defined on each and selection membership is invariant.

Phase equivariance must be verified, not inferred from type preservation. One general finite-state verification method is as follows. A public deterministic phase evaluator has finite state S and symbol transitions U_(t,y), allowing a public position clock. For a proposed block rewrite w -> w', find a state permutation V representing its intended phase action. Check the block transformation identity U_w'=V U_w on every reachable incoming state (or all states, which is stronger), and check that all later transitions commute with V. Check that the final readout obeys readout(Vs)=g.readout(s). This proves whole-history equivariance, including later suffix effects. A finite clock can encode all positions for fixed N; this makes verification finite, not necessarily efficient. Multiple generators must additionally satisfy their group relations on states or directly on the full-word maps. We do not infer commuting rewrites merely because their phase labels commute.

For a bank of m disjoint L-bit trials and a membership library of q distinct words, every next trial belongs to the library with conditional probability at least q c^L, regardless of previous failure and initial law. Repeated conditioning gives

  P(no hit in bank) <= (1-q c^L)^m.                       (2)

No block independence is assumed. A union bound over banks supplies the exceptional probability when G requires all banks to have hits. This is valid for fixed c; large L or a growing number of banks may make it ineffective. Variable trial lengths have a product bound with their corresponding q_i c^(L_i). Empty banks have failure probability one.

## 4. Explicit r-phase example

Take r>=2 and nonnegative integers m_1,...,m_r with 8 sum_j m_j <= N-b-2. Starting just after time 2, allocate consecutive disjoint zones I_j of lengths 8m_j. All zone start times are even. Unused pre-suffix bits and the suffix are outside the zones. In zone j define the RESET quadratic phase

  F_j(x) = sum_(k in I_j, k odd) x_k sum_(ell in I_j, ell<k) x_ell mod 2.

Resetting at zone boundaries is part of the definition, not a discarded cross term of the original full-history phase. Each F_j is a finite-state stream computation using local parity and its accumulated phase; the public clock chooses its zone. This example changes the reference nuisance to r phases. It does not assert that the original one-phase physical network secretly implements r phases.

Partition each zone into its m_j eight-bit blocks. Use

  w=00110000,    w'=00011000.

Both begin/end with 00. Their internal triples are 001,011,110,100 once each and 000 twice. Both have two ones. The involution T_j interchanges w,w' in the first matching block of zone j and is the identity if none match. These r involutions commute because their selection and edits use disjoint zones. They define H=(Z/2)^r on all histories, with group size 2^r.

Let E_j be the event of a hit in zone j. If the incoming within-zone parity is S, the block contributions to F_j are S for w and S+1 for w', because the selected odd local positions are respectively 3 and 5. The outgoing within-zone parity is unchanged (two ones), so all later phase contributions are unchanged. Thus T_j flips F_j on E_j and leaves every other phase unchanged. This also proves the identity in the presence of preceding blocks in the same zone. On G=intersection_j E_j the phase vector ranges over every element of {0,1}^r under H exactly once, so this is a genuinely 2^r-branch orbit rather than a relabeled two-branch example. G is invariant.

For public rho_j>=0 and public suffix function d define

  q(z,a)(1)=sigmoid(d(z)+sum_j rho_j(2a_j-1)),
  q(z,a)(0)=1-q(z,a)(1),
  M(z)(1)=2^(-r) sum_(a in {0,1}^r)
                       sigmoid(d(z)+sum_j rho_j(2a_j-1)).

No assumption is made that the rho_j are equal. If a fixed bounded-logit/state envelope is desired, impose the additional public bound sum_j rho_j<=R for fixed R; this controls the perturbation magnitude but does not itself prove any PreLN compilation or fixed-width implementation. For example r=2 with distinct positive rho_1,rho_2 gives four distinct branch logits and a group of order four. Set m_1=m_2=floor((N-b-2)/16) for an immediate explicit family. All four phases occur on every good orbit, regardless of the source.

Write gamma=1-2c^8 and let gamma^0=1. Equation (2) gives P(E_j complement)<=gamma^(m_j), hence P(G complement)<=min(1,sum_j gamma^(m_j)). A diameter-only application of (1) gives excess at most min(1,sum_j rho_j/2) times that failure bound.

A sharper bound avoids requiring every phase hit. At any x, Q_H averages exactly the hit coordinates and holds each missing coordinate at its observed value. Symmetrizing one missing coordinate changes the first probability by at most rho_j/4: the two branch logits differ by 2rho_j, sigmoid is 1/4-Lipschitz, and a midpoint is half the branch gap. This estimate remains valid after averaging any other coordinates. Telescoping over missing coordinates yields

  TV(M(Z(x)),Q_H(x)) <= sum_j (rho_j/4)1_(E_j complement),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_ref)+sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j),
  R_P(M) <= R_P(Q_phys)+zeta_h+sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j). (3)

The last line is conditional on a proved physical uniform comparison for this r-phase reference. It is not evidence of such a compilation.

An alternative cheap suffix rule is q_0(1|z)=sigmoid(d(z)). Let independent uniform signs epsilon_j define U=sum_j rho_j epsilon_j. Since E U=0, E U^2=sum_j rho_j^2 and |sigmoid''|<=1/4, Taylor's theorem gives

  TV(M(z),q_0(z)) <= (1/8)sum_j rho_j^2.                 (4)

Therefore the original separately compiled one-head baseline for the SAME d, with error zeta_b, obeys the conditional comparison

  R_P(Q_base) <= R_P(Q_phys)+zeta_h+zeta_b
                +sum_j (rho_j/4)gamma^(m_j)
                +(1/8)sum_j rho_j^2.                  (5)

Unlike enumerating M, using q_0 does not require 2^r branch evaluations. The price is the explicitly displayed quadratic amplitude term. If total variance sum rho_j^2 is large it need not be small. No sourcewise multiplicative ratio is inferred from these additive bounds.

## 5. Resource ledger and model boundary

Under aligned AA/BB ownership, Alice transmits exactly b/2 suffix bits in public order; Bob reconstructs Z and evaluates the public suffix rule. For general alignment at most b bits suffice. Group size does not multiply communication because phases and original prefix are never transmitted. This is worst-case deterministic one-way bit communication, with no real-valued transmissions or free silence/timing channel.

For a general finite phase table, evaluation costs C_M(b,|A|), not automatically O(b). A direct algorithm computes the suffix once, then enumerates |A| branches with total cost C_Z(b)+sum_a C_q(a)+O(|A|) vector arithmetic; public group/phase description size must be added to any parameter ledger. If only a group-indexed branch description is supplied, the naive sum has h terms and may repeat phases; no free orbit reduction is assumed. The selection-and-rewrite proof maps can be implemented by scanning banks, but Q_H still invokes the original reference up to h times and is not claimed cheap.

For the explicit logistic example, enumerate 2^r sign vectors. Compute d once, compute each signed sum in O(r) arithmetic operations, and evaluate one sigmoid per branch. This gives O(C_d(b)+r2^r) arithmetic work and 2^r sigmoid evaluations, using streaming accumulation and O(r) amplitude storage, in addition to the suffix and evaluator workspace. Gray-code reuse can lower arithmetic overhead but is not needed for this bound. Amplitude/phase descriptions cost at least the supplied description of r amplitudes and zone boundaries. If r grows, exponential local work remains real. The q_0 alternative requires only C_d(b) and one sigmoid, with error (4).

For the imported d, the prior symbolic ledger is O_c(b) exact-real local primitive work plus sigmoid, constant-many O(log b)-bit count registers, and a one-head physical baseline with width O_c(1), depth O_c(log N+b), per-coefficient description O_c(log N+b), untied total description O_c((log N+b)^2). ## 6. Failed routes and precise limits

- A measure-preserving rewrite need not change any nuisance, and a nuisance-flipping rewrite need not preserve Markov likelihood. Both certificates are essential.
- The shorter five-bit signed-kernel paths 11000 and 01100 are not substituted for a local source-measure-preserving permutation: their first two bits differ, so cross-boundary triple counts can change. Signed cancellation and exact permutation averaging are different mechanisms.
- Merely averaging logits does not equal M. The only permission to replace M by q_0 here is the paid Taylor error (4).
- Transitivity cannot be dropped: different phase orbits can have different means depending on unrevealed prefix information. An orbit label must be suffix-computable, or this route does not yield a suffix predictor.
- Independent block occurrences are not assumed; the failure bound is conditional minorization. Zero transition bounds preserve exact orbit domination but remove useful exponential decay.
- Repeating the original phase r times would not create r independent group coordinates. The reset-zone definition and separate phase-flip proof are what establish the multi-phase example.
- If r grows with N with balanced m_j roughly (N-b)/(8r), the union bound scales as r exp(-a_c(N-b)/r), not exp(-a_c(N-b)) with a uniform constant. The sharper excess scales with the amplitudes but still has this zone-length deterioration.
- The companion UNIVERSAL_REWRITE_CONSERVATION.candidate.v1.md proves that preservation of every source with arbitrary initial law and independent kernel rows ranging over an open interior forces any deterministic bijection to preserve the initial pair and all triple counts. Thus universal rewrite groups cannot mix the entire suffix fiber, whose histories can have different total counts. Output algebra is indispensable. This necessity statement is not asserted for the stationary-only source class, and is not a task-risk lower bound.

# Retained limits and a genuine zero-communication task obstruction

6 November 2026 UTC. Private analytical candidate v1. No experiments, mathematical execution, training, or upload.

## A. A useful full-type statistic whose parameter sign is invisible locally

Use spins X_i in {-1,+1} and a stationary homogeneous order-two law

 P_theta(X_i=z | X_(i-2)=u,X_(i-1)=v)=(1+theta zu)/2,

with initial pair independent uniform and |theta|<=1-2c. The two parity subsequences are independent stationary symmetric Markov chains with one-step correlation theta. Under AA/BB raw ownership each party sees every other site in each parity chain. Its marginal transition correlation is theta^2. Consequently Alice's full local marginal, and Bob's full local marginal, are each IDENTICAL at theta=+h and theta=-h.

Bob owns the final pair when N is divisible by four. The next-one probability is (1+theta X_(N-1))/2. Any zero-communication prediction at Bob (with independent shared/private random coins permitted) therefore has

 max{R_(+h),R_(-h)} >= h/2.

Proof: Bob's input and coins have the same law at the two parameters. For each such input the two target probabilities differ by h. Sum the two absolute losses and use the triangle inequality, then integrate under that common law.

Yet useful invariant full-data prediction is elementary. The lag-two products Y_i=X_i X_(i-2), i=3,...,N, are independent signs with mean theta: they are precisely the independent transition innovations of the two parity chains. Let theta_hat=(N-2)^(-1)sum Y_i and report (1+theta_hat X_(N-1))/2. Its TV risk is

 (1/2)E|theta_hat-theta| <= 1/[2 sqrt(N-2)].

This estimator factors through complete triple counts and the final pair, so it is invariant under the specific local rewrite and every endpoint-preserving type move. Thus actual useful task information can survive type projection and be invisible to either party alone.

Scope: this proves only a ZERO-communication obstruction. It does not prove a superlogarithmic interactive lower, a fixed-width PreLN realization, or universal invariance under all source-preserving maps. Deriving a quantitative distributed-testing lower at h of order N^(-1/2) would require an additional argument not supplied here. Identical separate marginals alone never imply a large interactive communication lower.

1. Parity of C_111 is type-invariant and can encode inner product on a selected AA/BB raw-history family. That is whole-output / exact-statistic hardness, not task-risk hardness.
