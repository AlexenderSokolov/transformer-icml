## Verified primary source

James Norris, Yuval Peres and Alex Zhai, *Surprise probabilities in Markov chains*, arXiv:1408.0822v1 (submitted 4 August 2014).

- Metadata/abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.0822v1
- Primary text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.0822
- Read scope: abstract, introductory statements, and complete Lemma 2.1 / Proposition 1.1 proof in section 2 (PDF pages 2–4). Not the complete paper.

Proposition 1.1 establishes the general finite-state first-hit pointmass bound n/t. Lemma 2.1 refines its numerator to a sum of maximal transition probabilities. The proof uses a first-hit decomposition, sums over earlier times, and bounds these maxima. This already establishes the dimension-over-time scale used in CB005. Its reset-row/reciprocal-return derivation is self-contained but must not be presented as discovering that generic scale. The d/(t+1) indexing variant alone is not a novelty claim.

CB005 uses its first-hit lemma inside a target-specific transition bound and a tilted joint occupation/terminal estimate, then in robust prediction. This limited screen does not establish novelty of those combinations or of any adaptation theorem. The frozen mathematical proof is unchanged; attribution should accompany later synthesis.
