1. Leighton Pate Barnes, Yanjun Han, Ayfer Özgür, *Lower Bounds for Learning Distributions under Communication Constraints via Fisher Information*, JMLR 21 (2020), 1–30. Primary PDF https://jmlr.org/papers/volume21/19-737/19-737.pdf . Read abstract and introduction/model, pp.1–4.
