# Source-corruption literature screen (post-stage-seven)

2026-11-02. This is a bounded primary-source screen, with reading scope specified for each item, not an exhaustive novelty audit. No private proof text was sent externally. Do not include it retroactively in the stage-seven scientific freeze.

- Abhik Ghosh, *Robust Parametric Inference for Finite Markov Chains*, arXiv:2004.01249v2, 21 March 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01249v2 . The abstract, introduction, and contamination-definition/robustness discussion in Section2.3 were inspected at https://arxiv.org/html/2004.01249v2 . The model assumes stationary ergodic finite chains and an identifiable parametric family. Its influence-function analysis perturbs the transition matrix by a rowwise mixture with point-mass rows; it is not the fixed-budget post-observation Hamming kernel used here. The work derives asymptotic estimation and robustness properties. Supplementary proofs and the full influence-function derivation were not audited.
- David Zuckerman, *Markov Chain Robustness*, ITCS2026, LIPIcs362,118:1–118:22, published23January2026, https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.118 . Abstract inspected: adversaries modify transition dynamics; the stated objects include limiting frequencies and mixing/hitting/cover times of reversible chains. This is not the stored-word replacement experiment stated by SC001/SC002. No full proof comparison claimed.
- Sujay Bhatt, Guanhua Fang, Ping Li and Gennady Samorodnitsky, *Minimax M-estimation under Adversarial Contamination*, ICML2022,PMLR162:1906–1924, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/bhatt22a.html . Abstract inspected: finite-sample contaminated mean estimation using Catoni's estimator under a bounded (1+epsilon)-moment assumption, with minimax comparison. Full proofs not inspected.

These works establish that robust inference and adversarial contamination are existing research programs. The current SC experiment specifically uses a single corrupted dependent trajectory, true preserved terminal context, unconditional next-law TV risk and a strict symbol-replacement budget. These differences are only a comparison of declared experiments; they do not certify the SC rates or mechanisms as novel, and do not rule out closer literature.
