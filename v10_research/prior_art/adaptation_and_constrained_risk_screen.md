# Adaptation and constrained-risk background: limited primary-source screen

2 November 2026. This is a later literature note, not part of the stage-eight freeze and not a novelty certificate. It records exactly the reading scope, and imports no unchecked theorem into the project's accepted arguments.

## Constrained-risk arguments

John Duchi and Feng Ruan, *A constrained risk inequality for general losses*, AISTATS 2021, PMLR 130:802–810. Primary [proceedings page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/duchi21a.html) and [paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/duchi21a/duchi21a.pdf).

Read the abstract, introduction and Section 2 theorem statements, not the complete proof section. The paper bounds risk at one distribution given a risk constraint at another, using distributional affinity and general losses. Its introduction explicitly credits Brown and Low's 1996 constrained-risk inequality. Thus constrained-risk reasoning and its role in adaptation lower bounds are established methods. The project's integrated prefix/suffix calculations are proved directly; they should not be presented as inventing the general method or as automatic applications of a theorem whose hypotheses were not checked.

## Joint adaptation under IID contamination

Haoyang Liu and Chao Gao, *Density Estimation with Contaminated Data: Minimax Rates and Theory of Adaptation*, [arXiv:1712.07801v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.07801v3), 27 July 2018.

Read primary abstract and version metadata only. The model in the abstract is IID mixture contamination and pointwise density estimation under squared loss. It reports a distinction between adapting to contamination or smoothness separately and adapting to both, with impossibility of the latter under arbitrary contamination. This is relevant conceptual precedent for joint-unknown impossibility; it is not the same as a strict realized-word Hamming budget, Markov dependence, a protected actual-terminal target, or the project's square-root budget-range boundary. Full proof and exhaustive priority comparison remain unperformed.

## Robust testing and unknown contamination

Mengjie Chen, Chao Gao and Zhao Ren, *A General Decision Theory for Huber's epsilon-Contamination Model*, [arXiv:1511.04144v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04144v2), 16 January 2017.

Read primary abstract and version metadata only. It describes robust two-point testing based on a Scheffe estimate, leading to estimators adaptive to contamination proportion, with density and regression applications. This is a potentially useful methodological reference, not a justification for transferring IID mixture guarantees to one dependent, adversarially edited trajectory. No claim about the entire paper's scope is made beyond the inspected abstract.

## Interpretation boundary

The clean homogeneous second-order word likelihood factors into its initial-pair probability times the product of transition probabilities raised to triple counts. This elementary factorization explains why order can be conditionally parameter-independent within a clean Markov type. It does not automatically justify dropping order after adversarial corruption with an unknown budget. The project's histogram-restricted and full-history statements must be compared under their exact observation and adaptation requirements, not collapsed into a general slogan about sufficient statistics.

Search failure is not evidence of originality; more targeted literature review is still needed before any novelty or submission claim.
