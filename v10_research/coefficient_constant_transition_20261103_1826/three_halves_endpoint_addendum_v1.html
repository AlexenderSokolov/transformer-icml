The endpoint c=3/2 retains positive terminal risk

3 November 2026. This immutable candidate supplements the fixed-c open-interval proof, SHA-256 2d8d6b8c668d1726cd16673b161f8c4a779f3889ea9f13dc0ccc1381ae0d27d0, and awaits independent review. It uses the identical source and graph. No experiments, enumeration or source overwrite were used.

Claim

At c=3/2, i.e. ηN=3/[2(N+2)] in the same lag-one coefficient, the actual-terminal expected TV risk tends to 27/160 on the same fixed stationary positive binary source. The changed scalar weight is 4ηN; this is an N-dependent model sequence. This endpoint result does not address c just above 3/2 or a sequence cN approaching the endpoint.

1. History: drop both critical neighborhoods

Use L=N+2, D=L+3/2, r=i−1, ζᵢ=(3/2)r/D. As in the companion, the score center is r−ζᵢ and integer shifts switch at r₁=D/3 and r₂=D. For H=⌈100ln L⌉+10, discard rows i<H, |r−r₁|≤H, and i>L−H. This is O(log L) rows. For every remaining row, the first boundary is more than H away and r₂−r≥H, so the two possible half-integer ties are both excluded. As in the companion proof, if i<D/6 then ζ<1/4 and gap≥i/2≥H/2; otherwise i≥D/6 and the score gap to the runner-up is at least 3iH/D≥H/2. Thus the wrong softmax mass is ≤L e−H/2<1/4 and the snap is exact.

The two remaining good segments have limiting proportions 1/3 and 2/3, and respectively use the same unshifted and one-position-shifted lag-one contexts as the companion proof. The O(log L) omitted keys and two BOS pseudocounts have negligible scaled weight versus the Θ(N) matching count for each anchor. The finite-state stationary ergodic theorem therefore gives the same limiting anchors as the companion at α=1/3:

p₀₀=13/20,   p₀₁=1/2,   p₁₀=1/4,   p₁₁=2/5.

2. Terminal query: no tie

The terminal i=L was excluded from the history proof and must be checked separately. Here ζL=3/2−(15/4)/(L+3/2), so for L≥4 its nearest integer shift is 1. Let j*=r−1=L−2 be the winner. The gap to its shift-2 neighbor is exactly 15/2. The gap to its shift-0 neighbor is 2L−9/2≥L/2. Any key at integer distance m≥2 from j* has gap at least Lm(m−1), since the real center is within 1/2 of j* and the quadratic curvature is ≥L. The total incorrect relative weight is at most

e^(−15/2)+e^(−L/2)+2e^(−2L)/(1−e^(−2L)) < 1/3,  L≥4.

For example, e⁴>32, e²>7 and e⁸>1024 give an upper bound 1/32+1/7+1/256<1/3. Hence wrong attention mass is <1/4 and the terminal snap is exact. For N≥3 the winning key is the data token XN−2, so the actual query is (XN−2,XN). This statement does not assign X₀ when N=2.

3. Risk

As in the companion, the selected scalar differs by o(1) in L1 from pXₙ₋₂,Xₙ, and the final ordinary vocabulary softmax has uniform error at most 168/√(N+2). Stationarity makes the moving finite-window target distribution independent of N. Substituting α=1/3 into the independently checked companion's same-history risk calculation yields

lim_N E TV(Q_N, θ_(X_(N−1),X_N)) = (11−6/3)/[32(2−1/3)] = 27/160.

Nothing here asserts that the moving random target itself converges, or that the result is uniform for a sequence cN→3/2. Prior-art and exact-real scope are those of the companion.
