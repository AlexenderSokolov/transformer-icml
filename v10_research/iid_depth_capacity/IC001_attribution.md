# IC001 attribution and comparison boundary

Multi-coordinate packing of a high-precision scalar into low-precision outputs is established, not claimed new here. Uss et al., *Predicting High-precision Depth on Low-Precision Devices Using 2D Hilbert Curves*, ICML 2025, explicitly use two low-precision curve coordinates and subsequent reconstruction: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/uss25a.html ; author text https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14024v2 . Root inspected Sections 2.1–2.4; the source-bound local record is `../prior_art/folded_capacity_comparison.md`. This child did not independently audit the full paper.

The distinction asserted by this project is its stated mathematical question: IID statistical-order risk versus bounded primitive depth and finite-interface capacity, with a paid finite evaluator. That is a scope distinction, not a certified novelty claim or a criticism of the paper's differently specified noise/empirical results. Tent-map iteration itself is already attributed to Telgarsky (2016) in accepted FG001. IC001 extends the fold schedule to arbitrary partial depths and combines it with accepted IR001; no priority claim is made for the construction or theorem.
