## Primary-source gap

I inspected the actual [Håstad, *Some Optimal Inapproximability Results*, JACM 48 (2001), PDF](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/BLOGPAPERS/max3satl.pdf), page 36 (journal page 834). Theorem 6.5 states hardness of distinguishing satisfiable E3-CNF from (7/8+ε)-satisfiable E3-CNF, with the text explicitly noting perfect completeness; Definitions 2.2–2.4 define satisfiability by the optimum number of clauses and E3 by exact clause length. The equality-defined soundness subclass is contained in the ≤7/8+ε promise used by the candidate. Theorem 2.24, page 9, separately states a universal c<1 gap with each variable appearing exactly five times; the text attributes the exact-five strengthening to Feige. After normalization, occurrences can decrease, so the derived form is bounded by five, not necessarily exactly five. No sharp 7/8 gap under bounded occurrence is inferred. I checked the primary theorem statements and definitions, not a line-by-line independent proof of the PCP theorem or a comprehensive novelty search.
