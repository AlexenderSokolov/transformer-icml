# Source-blind gate attack feasibility: local microbenchmark

2026-11-03. Exploratory local run, not preregistration, no statistical inference or optimization guarantee for neural training. See `TIMING_AND_NUMERIC_CORRECTION.md` for controlling timing and float64 limitations. Independent review remains pending at this writing.

## What was actually compared

The exact-combinatorial selected-row DP attacked the fixed smoothed ACG gate Q, not the trained pilot neural predictor. Its utility was CE(B(h),Q(z)), with B computed once from the clean history. The original pilot's training/heldout legal proposal families were unioned, scored against the identical clean B and Q, and the best of 20 proposal entries selected. Both use only the observed word and fixed predictor, no source or next label; the final pair stays protected, with at most k editable-prefix flips. Data and source parameters entered the source generator; true-source |Q(z)-theta_terminal(1)| was computed only after attacks returned. The two selectors need not maximize actual-source error. C++ DP keeps a minimum-cost representative of every reachable bank, but its CE comparison uses float64, not an exact-real oracle.

The initial fixed grid comprised one independently generated word at each of three fixed source tables for each M=16,32,64,128, and k=1,2,4: 36 cases. Every case finished below caps. Internal DP wall times ranged 0.061–7.38 ms; largest observed layer had 1,433 states and 43,273 accumulated reconstruction nodes. Float64 CE was greater than the proposal union in 27/36 cases and tied to 1e-12 in 9/36. Post-selection true-source absolute error was larger in 22, smaller in 4, equal in 10. The largest observed CE difference was 1.436. These are descriptive case counts, not statistical confidence statements.

After seeing that grid, the fixed follow-up reused the saved histories at the pilot's actual unique budgets: M=64,k=8, and M=128,k=11,16, three source histories each. All 9 cases finished without hitting the 300,000-state, 3,000,000-node or 10-second limits. Internal DP wall ranged 1.19–237.13 ms (median 63.15 ms); maximum observed layer 41,726 states and maximum retained reconstruction nodes 1,833,734. Recorded peak RSS ranged 22,020–32,456 KiB for these subprocesses; this includes executable/runtime overhead and is platform-specific. Float64 CE exceeded the proposal union in all nine cases, with gaps 0.0328–1.4472; their post-selection true-source absolute error was also greater in all nine. A larger surrogate objective does not imply a larger true-source error in general; the initial grid contains counterexamples. These are few fixed examples, not source-population estimates, a no-cap complexity guarantee or training-throughput evidence.

Neither grid benchmarks the fixed-half neural model, backpropagation, training loop, batched implementation, arbitrary learned pooling or larger horizons. The neural fixed-half feature bank has an additional first-half successor-one counter E beyond J,C1,D. The independently derived four-counter exact DP and tests are in the separate `../four_counter_dp_20261103/` unit. Its O(M^5) transition and O(M^4) state upper bounds are not observed runtimes for this experiment.

## Tests and audit record

The independent small brute-force oracle checked all 320 M=4 word/budget combinations and 128 seeded cases at M=5–8, including full legal edit enumeration and original pilot Q equality, endpoints, budgets, proposal inclusion and objective agreement to 1e-12 (`test_cases.jsonl`, `test_results.log`). The Python test is independent in its transition/statistic enumeration but shares the original pilot code when explicitly testing agreement and proposal generation. Its PRNG states and every enumerated oracle candidate are archived in `test_random_states.json` and `test_oracle_candidates.jsonl.gz`.

The original data, source tables, initial pairs and generated histories are in `data.json`. `randomness_replayed.json` reconstructs the original draw exactly from seed and stores every per-word PRNG state, every random uniform and every state before each uniform; the replay validates every stored history. Each `results.jsonl` and `followup_results.jsonl` record retains the clean history, all proposal candidates and their CE values, selection, DP status, witness (when completed), counts, objectives, resource measurements, post-selection true-source scores and budget. No separate list of all combinatorially represented DP paths was generated; the deterministic executable, inputs, code, limits and full witness are saved for replay. Finite comparisons do not establish exact-real optimality.

Scripts `dp.cpp`, `test_dp.py`, `run.py`, `followup.py`, `archive_randomness.py`, `archive_test_oracles.py`, compiled `dp`, and all logs/settings are retained. `PRE_RUN_SHA256.txt` binds the initial code, executable, original pilot module, protocol and test log before generation; `FOLLOWUP_PRE_RUN_SHA256.txt` binds the later follow-up before its execution. These local hashes are not trusted timestamps. The fixed follow-up was chosen after initial outcomes, expressly not an independent confirmatory test. `environment.txt` records compiler and platform. `compiler.log` retains its warning, explained without changing code in the correction note.
