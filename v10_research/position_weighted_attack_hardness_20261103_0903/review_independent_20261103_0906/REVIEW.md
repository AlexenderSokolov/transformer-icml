## Reduction and architecture

For each positive binary SUBSET-SUM input, choose M as the least power of two at least n², k=n, pad a_i with zeros, let C=max a_i and G the least power of two at least A=sum a_i. Then n²≤M<2n², k≤sqrt(M), and writing M positional embeddings and the all-zero length-(M+2) history is polynomial in input length. A positive n≥2 instance with target in [0,A] is a standard NP-hard restriction; trivial/out-of-range cases can be decided or mapped to fixed yes/no instances in polynomial time.

At editable position i, the shared token/position input is (a_i−C,z_i). The single shared unit ReLU((a_i−C)+Cz_i) equals 0 for z_i=0 and a_i for z_i=1, including padded zero items. This is a fixed affine map plus ReLU, not an uncharged multiplication of two input variables. A public causal mask at the protected last query selects the first M positions, and all zero scores yield exact uniform weights 1/M in the ideal hybrid model. A following scale by M recovers S=sum a_i z_i; two ReLUs form |S−B| and yield r=−|S−B|/(2G). The construction assumes this specified pointwise-ReLU/uniform-attention/ReLU composition and exact mask, without layer normalization. It is not a proof for an arbitrary standard software Transformer implementation or the trained tanh pilot.

The clean word is all zeros, with protected final pair 00, J=M and C1=0, so the stipulated clean add-one target is t=1/(M+2). It is computed from h, not the current source parameter or future label. The edit mask affects only the first M bits. Every original subset has at most n edits and is legal; every legal mask with S=B determines an original-item subset with that sum, since flipped padding positions contribute zero. Thus the reduction is source/label-blind even though it uses the public model and observed h. The add-one target is an explicitly chosen history statistic and is not claimed to equal the posterior under the later single iid source.

## CE, dyadic decision and complexity

Since 0≤S,B≤A≤G, r∈[−1/2,0]. Because exp(1/2)<2, sigmoid(r)>1/3; smoothing toward 1/2 preserves q>1/3. With M≥4, t≤1/6, and CE(t,q) is strictly increasing throughout q∈(t,1/2]. Therefore its maximum possible threshold log 2 is reached iff r=0 iff S=B. A proposed mask is polynomial-size; for this syntactically restricted family its membership can be checked by integer addition and equality, without a general transcendental comparison oracle. This establishes restricted-family NP-completeness, not membership of arbitrary real-valued neural-verification problems in NP.

For the separate dyadic-output variant, q_d=3/(2M)−|S−B|/(2MG) lies in [1/M,3/(2M)] and exceeds 1/(M+2). Consequently CE(t,q_d)≥CE(t,3/(2M)) iff q_d≥3/(2M) iff S=B. The CE threshold is symbolic and generally transcendental; the equivalent rational comparison is exact. M and G are powers of two, so the pre-logit and this distinct output path admit finite fixed-point arithmetic with O(log M+log(A+1)) value bits; explicit position tables and integer arithmetic/storage must also be charged. The original sigmoid/log implementation remains exact-real mathematical semantics, not a certified float or libm calculation.

The hardness is weak and bit-complexity dependent: this family is equivalent to binary SUBSET-SUM and admits the standard pseudo-polynomial reachable-sum DP, with O(nA) bit/Boolean update units and O(A) Boolean positions, plus representation, witness and word-size costs. The reduced exact score margin can be exponentially small in encoded weights; no constant-additive or inverse-polynomial approximation hardness follows. Fixed k admits O(M^k) mask enumeration with separate model-evaluation costs. The earlier fixed-half bank DP is not contradicted: here the variable position-weighted sum is outside that bank's retained information, and weights are part of the input. No learned-attention, trained-pilot, SGD, average-case or universal robust-risk claim follows.

For the separately stated iid Bernoulli source with p=1/M², the two-step pair transition distribution is exactly the iid-pair stationary distribution for every initial pair, so the all-state certificate holds with beta=1. Since q_d≥1/M>p, its absolute source error is at most 3/(2M)−1/M² for every attack, and the all-zero word probability is (1−p)^(M+2)≥1−(M+2)/M² under iid initialization. This is a source-specific illustration, not a uniform bound over the general source class.

The author's verifier independently reruns byte-identically and reports 17,287 deterministic instance checks; its original aggregate log does not retain each candidate, so the saved independent archive is a new deterministic verification record, not a claim that all author-run intermediates were preserved.

The corrected [Shi et al. ICLR 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.06622) description appropriately distinguishes Transformer verification's nonconvex optimization from its norm-ball input sets. [Sälzer and Lange](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13179) already establish NP-hard neural reachability for simple ReLU networks. [Karp's classical reduction](https://www2.seas.gwu.edu/~simhaweb/champalg/tsp/papers/KarpOriginal.pdf) includes the binary equality/knapsack problem underlying SUBSET-SUM. These references support caution against claiming generic neural-verification or subset-sum hardness as novel; no exhaustive novelty review or claim about this exact protected-endpoint formulation is established.
