The original direct-affine-head draft omitted mandatory final normalization. The corrected block overwrites the existing balanced logit pair with (lxy,-lxy), retaining padding and constant clean total energy. The final full LayerNorm therefore has a fixed known scale removable by its charged affine projection. The two blocks use ordinary one-hidden-layer ReLU FFNs, each with two units. Positive epsilon and all thirteen features are retained.

B0, the conservative B1, and the final probability Lipschitz constant sigma_f/(4sqrt(epsilon_f)) correctly cover arbitrary full-vector errors without assuming they preserve pairing or norm. An independent rerun of the CURRENT script exactly reproduced all 360 clean and 1440 noisy cases. A separate 756-case check previously verified the paired-overwrite final-normalization repair. The included reproducible test is a float64 finite check, not interval certification or hardware-rounding proof.
