## 1. The coherent answer and threat model

There is no single unconditional efficient-attacker theorem. The boundary depends on (i) what information the attack may use, (ii) the representation of the predictor's sufficient feature bank, (iii) the magnitude and number of its feature coordinates, and (iv) whether the objective is ideal exact arithmetic or a specified floating execution. A legal episode attack edits at most k of positions 0,...,M−1 of a clean binary history H of length M+2, preserving the last pair, and receives H and allowed fixed training/model information, not the current latent source θ or future label Y. A history-only map can depend on the known population law and predictor. For a random attacker its fresh randomness must be conditionally independent of the current latent variables and Y given H and fixed training/model information. The pointwise maximum below is taken *after* conditioning on H, not after observing Y or θ. Exact count-bank optimization is polynomial in M for the fixed banks discussed below, but its exponents and bit/readout costs are substantial. General positional weights or growing feature dimension cross separate worst-case hardness boundaries. Actual float execution adds a genuine implementation gap even for fixed banks.

## 2. What legal supervised adversarial learning estimates

Let q=θ_(H_M,H_(M+1))(1), p(h)=E[q|H=h]=P(Y=1|H=h), and let f take edited words to [0,1]. For iid full episodes from any fixed distribution over homogeneous binary order-two source/initial-law pairs, including latent dependence between them, define L(f)=sup_a E[(f(a(H))−q)^2] over one legal history-only map. The finite word space allows

L(f)=E_H max_(z in A_k(H)) (f(z)−p(H))² + E Var(q|H).

A supervised square-loss objective against Y has the same predictor ranking, with the distinct additive constant E[p(H)(1−p(H))]. No minimax interchange follows. The cited iid Bernoulli(1/5) example gives legal risks 0.01 versus 0.04 for two predictors, but choosing the edit after seeing Y reverses their supervised ranking (117/500 versus 1/5); label-aware training is a different, stronger game. The DP cannot conjure p(h); a permitted population value or estimate must be supplied.

For clean histories, initial pair, terminal pair, and eight transition counts form a sufficient likelihood statistic for (θ, initial law), even with arbitrary latent dependence. Pair-flow identities and the fixed total leave at most S=16(M+1)^4 group keys. For any **fixed data-independent finite** class F of N bounded predictors, n1 independent episodes estimate group conditional labels, and n2 further independent episodes evaluate the robust loss using those estimates. With δ1,δ2 in (0,1), an empirical minimizer with objective gap ξ_ERM satisfies with probability at least 1−δ1−δ2:

L(f_hat) ≤ min_(f in F) L(f) + 4 sqrt(2S/(n1+1))/δ1 + 2 sqrt(log(2N/δ2)/(2n2)) + ξ_ERM.

This is a fixed meta-distribution average, not simultaneous worst-source, unknown-budget, single-trajectory, SGD, or tractable optimization learning. The bound can be highly vacuous; Markov's δ dependence is deliberately loose. For the inherited comparator only, M≥64 and almost every source must have a stationary pair law Π and all-state P_θ²(u,v)≥βΠ(v), β≥1/M; initial law need not be stationary. If the exact protected-endpoint ACG comparator belongs to F, its inherited absolute-error bound min(1,131072/sqrt(M)+6433056k/M) transfers to squared error and yields an absolute-error bound only after an additional square root of the comparator plus learning terms. Finite precision, inclusion in F and output costs remain separate.

Exhaustive evaluation requires B=Σ_(j≤min(k,M)) C(M,j) edits per history and up to N n2 B predictor calls/comparisons, plus O(N n2 B M) naive word construction, prediction, arithmetic bit growth, class indexing and storage. B is polynomial for each fixed k, potentially exponential for growing k. An exact bank DP may replace the inner maximum **only for predictors whose whole utility uses the retained bank/interface and the same p-hat**; the empirical objective and statistical bound then remain unchanged. It does not cover every member of an arbitrary F.

## 3. Exact bank optimization and its costs

For full first/second-half triple histograms and fixed initial/split/current endpoint bits, pair-flow constraints reduce each half to four independent bounded counters. Retaining the smallest prefix Hamming cost per state is exact for an **at-most** k budget: identical states have identical continuations and incremental costs. It returns a legal maximizing word for any fixed exactly evaluable/comparable utility of that bank and recorded endpoints. Worst-case upper bounds are O(M^9) transitions, O(M^8) rolling states, O(M^8) final evaluations, and O(M^8 log(M+1)) rolling bits; all-layer predecessors use O(M^9 log(M+1)) bits, or prefix recomputation O(M^10) transitions and rolling storage. The selected-row J,C,D interface instead takes O(M^4) transitions, O(M^3) rolling states and evaluations, plus a witness cost. It includes the nominal exact fixed ACG/FPA bank, not arbitrary word readouts.

The pilot's fixed half-pooling rails require four counters J,C1,D,E: total selected departures, successor-one count, first-half departures, and first-half successor-one count. With L=floor(M/2), R=M−L, its six ideal rails are ((J−C1)/M,C1/M,(D−E)/L,E/L,(J−D−C1+E)/R,(C1−E)/R); append endpoint bits and 1/M. For M≥2 the corresponding exact-bank DP has O(M^5) transitions, O(M^4) rolling states/evaluations, O(M^4 log(M+1)) rolling bits and O(M^5 log(M+1)) full predecessor bits; traceback by reruns costs O(M^6) transitions. All these counts omit actual readout/comparison, bit operations, input and witness costs. The proof covers adjacent edits and protected/split boundary triples, not an exact-k budget unless cost is included as state. Finite exhaustive tests corroborate but do not prove the general induction.

**Implementation correction:** the supplied `four_counter.py` converts reconstructed rails using Python floating division. At M=3 the exact utility testing equality to 1/3 gives a different result on its float input; an exact comparator cannot recover exact rationals after conversion. Reachability/minimum-cost maps and integer-valued tests are unaffected, but its generic `maximize` is not an exact-rational or exact-real neural oracle. The original verifier's imported `pilot.py` binding was omitted from its original manifest and supplied additively; see bindings and disclosure.

## 4. Actual floats are not the bank model

A fixed-shape, two-row NumPy/OpenBLAS invocation at M=1025 uses A and B all ones except positions {515,518} and {519,522}. Both have (J,C1,D,E)=(1021,1019,512,512), identical first/second-half complete eight-triple histograms and identical initial/split/terminal pairs. Yet their recorded uniform successor-one features differ by one ULP: `0x1.fd00bfd00bfd6p-1` and `0x1.fd00bfd00bfd7p-1`. An intentionally constructed, untrained readout amplifies the difference into q=0.5 versus 0.6816993976515352. The words are mutually reachable with k=4. This shows that count-equivalent words need not be utility-equivalent under this recorded float protocol, **not** that a trained checkpoint's global argmax is wrong, nor that the exact-rational identity fails. The same A also has a different rail in a singleton invocation. A separate M=5 example shows direct float division of counters can disagree with the actual pooling output. The earlier M=257 differing-batch-size search is not a fixed-shape collision. Results are specific to documented environment, layout, and reduction.

Fix candidate order, batch size/layout, slot, dtype, backend, threads, weights, tie and nonfinite rules. Exhaustive legal-mask scoring then gives an exact maximum of that protocol's **computed finite values**, not an ideal-real maximum. A fixed B-row filler context costs O(BM) pooling/storage per candidate, while one N-candidate batch costs O(NM) pooling/storage for N=Σ_(j≤k) C(M,j), plus readout, transcendentals and comparisons. Singleton reevaluation is not automatically the same objective. An augmented-state float DP requires a proved equivalent backend reduction/circuit state; the conditional r-accumulator bound can be exponential and no polynomial exact-float DP is established.

The mathematical numerical bridge assumes a specified rounded addition tree, normal arithmetic and no unmodelled underflow/overflow or lower precision. With u=2^-53, γ_n=nu/(1−nu), nu<1, a rail of n terms has absolute error at most d_n=u+(1+u)γ_(n−1). With saved float weights interpreted as exact reals, S_i=Σ_j |v_j||W_ij| and V=Σ_j |v_j|, the report's full logit radius R includes Σ_i S_i d_i, affine-rounding terms and a coefficient times an independently established tanh error τ_h. For smoothed sigmoid q=e+(1−2e)σ(r), e=(M+2)^−2, CE is 1-Lipschitz **in logit**, not in probability. If output error τ_q<e, target error β, and CE arithmetic error τ_CE are also uniformly bounded, then

ε = R + τ_q/(e−τ_q) + β log[(1−e+τ_q)/(e−τ_q)] + τ_CE

bounds |U_float−U_ideal|. These backend, transcendental, target and CE premises have **not** been verified for actual NumPy/BLAS/libm. Checkpoint numbers computed with τ_h=0 are components, not certificates. If the premise holds uniformly in one fixed scoring context and a bank witness is η-optimal for the ideal score, float regret is at most 2ε+η. It does not prove identical argmax. A sharp factor 2 is shown only for abstract bounded-error scalar objectives, not the specific pilot CE. Cross-context comparisons require separate radii.

For each bank b, suppose rigorous ideal rational intervals L_b≤I_b≤H_b and a separately proved per-bank backend radius d_b cover **all** its candidate words and contexts. For a witness w, max U−U(w)≤max(0,max_b(H_b+d_b)−(L_bank(w)−d_bank(w))); if its actual finite score y is retained in that same context, replace the lower term by y. A strict winning-bank gap excludes other banks but does not identify the best word within that bank. Minimum-cost dominance does not count legal alternatives. The proposed capped-at-two multiplicity DP is not implemented or costed. The implemented exact-rational interval demonstration uses one active unit and three M=2 words, with a hypothetical 1/1000 backend radius; its positive margin is conditional, not a real backend certificate or generic 9×32 implementation. Rational interval refinement need not terminate on exact ties and general bit complexity is not established.

## 5. Complexity boundaries: weights, dimension, and precision

**One position-weighted feature, binary magnitudes.** A polynomial reduction from positive binary SUBSET-SUM sets M to a power of two at least n² and k=n≤sqrt(M), h=0^(M+2), with protected zeros. Position embedding and a shared ReLU form a_i z_i without assuming a free token-position product; a public-mask uniform-attention head averages it. A two-unit ReLU head yields r=−|Σ_i a_i z_i−B|/(2G), with G a power of two ≥Σa_i. Under the specified exact-real smoothed sigmoid/CE and t=1/(M+2), CE≥log 2 iff Σa_i z_i=B. Membership in NP for this restricted family follows by integer-sum checking, not generic exact transcendental comparison. Therefore restricted exact attack decision is NP-complete, with weak NP-hardness in binary positional/model magnitudes. Its separate dyadic probability-output variant replaces the CE decision by an exact rational comparison and requires O(log M+log(A+1)) scalar bits, with the explicit M-position table and arithmetic paid. It is a specified hybrid without layer norm, not the frozen tanh pilot or an arbitrary library Transformer. The reduction's gaps can be exponentially small; it does **not** imply inverse-polynomial or constant-additive hardness. Classical subset-sum costs O(nA) update units and O(A) positions, pseudo-polynomial in A. Fixed k allows direct enumeration.

**Fixed d, bounded aligned grid.** In a left-to-right scan with r-bit local window, Q automaton states and d additive integer features B_j=Σ_t g_j, set L_j=Σ_t min(0,l_tj), H_j=Σ_t max(0,u_tj), R_j=H_j−L_j+1 and S=2^r Q∏_j R_j. These zero-inclusive bounds cover intermediate signed sums. For T=M+2, exact minimum-cost dominance gives O(TS) transitions and O(S) final exact readouts, O(S log(k+2)) rolling cost bits, plus local-rule, d integer additions, indexing, comparisons, input and witness costs. Full predecessor layers cost O(TS log(S+1)) bits; recomputation O(T²S) transitions. If |g_j|≤W_j, R_j≤2TW_j+1. Thus fixed d,r, polynomial Q and **polynomially bounded aligned integer numerators**, plus polynomial local/readout/comparison costs, give polynomial time. O(log M) bits in an *unaligned significand* alone do not suffice: common-denominator alignment and exponent spans can expand numerators. Exactly k edits or simultaneous distinct resource budgets require additional state/frontiers.

For normalized dyadic heads, keep one denominator and one numerator per value channel, d=Σ_h(p_h+1); denominators must be strictly positive. Arbitrary standard softmax exponentials, global query interactions, unbounded automata/windows, or backend-dependent floating reductions are not automatically covered. The sparse bound uses K_t=Σ_(j≤min(k,min(t,M))) C(min(t,M),j) raw budget/protected prefixes **before optional restrictions** and S_t≤min(S,K_t); at most 2Σ_t min(S,K_t) trial transitions, with key/bit/dictionary costs extra. Fixed k is polynomial given polynomial scoring, but this is not uniform polynomiality as k grows.

**Growing d, small coefficients.** For normalized 3CNF with n variables and m surviving clauses, pad M to a power of two ≥max(n²,m²,4), let k=n, P the least power of two ≥m, and use d=m additive clause features A_j=Σ_i s_ji z_i, s_ji∈{−1,0,1}, at most three nonzeros per coordinate. With C_j the number of negative literals, L_j=C_j+A_j counts satisfied literals, V=Σ_j ReLU(1−L_j) counts violated clauses, and q=3/(2M)−V/(2MP). A shared 2m-unit pre-attention ReLU layer realizes s_ji z_i from additive token and position embeddings; one uniform head with m value channels and an m-unit readout supplies the specified exact dyadic model. It uses O(log M)-bit scalars, explicit polynomial-size model data and q∈[1/M,3/(2M)]. Legal q≥3/(2M) iff the formula is satisfiable; the symbolic CE threshold is equivalent because q>t=1/(M+2). Restricted decision is NP-complete. Exact q-gap is Δq=1/(2MP); the CE gap is at least ΔCE=4/[M²(M+2)P]. A polynomial-time algorithm that always outputs a legal witness with strictly smaller additive regret than the relevant gap would decide 3SAT. This is inverse-polynomial witness-approximation hardness, not constant-additive hardness, a bound for fixed d, natural distributional risk, trained models or SGD. Every feature has at most four attainable values, but the product table can have 4^m tuples. The construction proves neither a matching 4^m lower bound nor a blanket strong-NP-completeness classification for every rational-network encoding. Width and feature number grow; the fixed-d theorem does not apply. Likewise, fixed-k enumeration does not conflict because k=n grows.

## 6. Position against earlier v10 and primary literature

BCJ001 already established the accepted twelve-coordinate noisy positional-bank phenomenon, budget-blind decoder and joint fixed-bank precision/noise/edit rate Θ_c(min(1,max(M^−1/2,k/M,δ,1/Q))) on its specified certificate band, with paid positions, aggregation and evaluator. It already implies the half-log normalized-bank precision balance. ACG provides a modular direct J,C,D approximation interface, PBP a changed rounding bank/lower proof, and FPA a more explicit paid two-layer/three-head attention plus Boolean/ReLU realization; these are not the first positional-bank precision principle. FPA's direct normalized bank is not the same experiment as BCJ's rounded twelve rails, and arithmetic, masks, access, width/depth and same-information classical comparator must be charged. AB001 already rules out one full-history predictor blind to both budget and certificate attaining the whole certificate-dependent benchmark simultaneously; its clean alternatives/attacked null must not be replaced by a same-certificate lower comparison. The new game and attack-optimization results answer different questions and do not resolve AB's obstruction.

The literature already contains adversarial-learning generalization (Attias, Kontorovich and Mansour, https://jmlr.org/papers/v23/20-1353.html), in-context adversarial training/pretraining (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05189v1 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14042), Transformer verification (https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.06622), ReLU reachability hardness (https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13179), and classical subset-sum/multidimensional DP. The local review checked only explicitly reported primary-source or metadata/abstract scopes, not every theorem in full; the Bellman 1957 book was identified but its exact passage not inspected, and the multidimensional-knapsack publisher full text was unavailable in that audit. No comprehensive novelty or priority search is established. Useful framing is an explicit protected-history/feature/precision interface and its scoped reductions, not a generic discovery of DP, robust learning, neural verification hardness, or positional precision.

## 8. Major next research directions, with stopping conditions

1. **Backend-valid certificate:** pick a reproducible actual batch protocol, prove the pooling and affine operation graphs and rigorous tanh/sigmoid/log/CE/target errors, validate exceptional behavior, then test whether real bank margins exceed the resulting radius. Output either an auditable actual checkpoint regret/unique-optimum certificate or a quantified reason the bounds are vacuous. Exact interval code must be generalized and costed before claiming practicality.
2. **Structure beyond dimension:** identify position-feature classes and finite-state/rank/treewidth or coefficient conditions allowing tighter exact/approximate algorithms, versus parameterized lower bounds when d grows. Establish whether inverse-polynomial gaps survive stronger restrictions (e.g. one scalar-width head or a fixed architecture) without smuggling exponential precision; do not infer constant-gap hardness from the present reduction.
3. **Statistical efficiency and learning:** seek nonvacuous occupancy-dependent rates, shared sample computation and same-information classical comparisons; prove or falsify feasible optimization of the legal objective. Measure source-conditional and worst-source behavior separately from mixture averages. Preserve all candidate/sample records in future experiments, subject to explicit authorization.
4. **Learning positional features with resources:** establish a training procedure, finite width/depth/precision and all costs, with unknown budget/certificate scope consistent with AB001. Distinguish representing an inherited comparator from learning it or outperforming a classical learner.
5. **Complete scope and literature audit:** primary-source theorem-by-theorem review for the exact legal-edit feature/precision interface and approximation gaps, including classical parameterized and verification results; keep chronology, missing artifacts and publication identities intact. None of these directions follows automatically from a finite numerical replay.
