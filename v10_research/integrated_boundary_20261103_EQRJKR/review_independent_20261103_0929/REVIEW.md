## Evidence-scope recommendation

The current 40-bound-source synthesis does not include the learned-position smoke, Q-supervised calibration, rare-weight ablation or its later confirmation. That omission is compatible with a theory/float-only synthesis, but §7's “Evidence, preservation and publication boundaries” could be read as a complete account of the broader research. Add an explicit scope sentence saying those empirical studies are not covered, or separately cite and summarize them, including the critical ablation preservation failure (128,000 discarded rejection-set words, 139,264 unretained latent tables and unretained transition uniforms), and distinguish the subsequent newly preserved but historically nonblind confirmation with its natural-MAE tradeoff. The smoke does not establish fixed-half incapacity, and the Q-supervised, collision-weighted calibration is not natural next-token learning. This is an omission/scope issue, not evidence that the present scoped mathematical claims are false.
