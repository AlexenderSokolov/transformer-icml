# Independent review: tied positional-code scalar-sign obstruction

2026-11-12. Verdict: PASS for the explicitly restricted local-reconstruction interface. This does not certify a lower bound for arbitrary QK, multiple nonlinear sketches, actual complete PreLN predictors or terminal statistical risk.

1. Exact scalar correction is sound. Multiplying the biased head by its public denominator/i, subtracting the uniform head and the current-token coefficient leaves (1/i) sum_(j<=i-1)(exp(gamma<u_(i-1),u_j>)-1)X_j. The self term is fully removed. The correction is expressly granted; a position-dependent public multiplier is not thereby a compiled shared FFN.
2. The radius-1/2 net bound 5^d follows by disjoint radius-1/4 balls around a maximal separated subset of the unit sphere, all contained in a radius-5/4 ball. Two assigned codes are at distance at most 1, hence their dot product is at least 1/2. Discarding the first K target occurrences in each cell leaves at least K strictly earlier keys for each remaining target t. All these indices j<t occur in the causal prefix read at row t+1; no future key is used. At most K 5^d of the N-1 targets are discarded.
3. At theta=0 the complete raw word is iid fair. For each row, nuisance Z_t contains each earlier independent sign once with deterministic coefficient, and is independent of target X_t. Same-cell coefficients are at least B=exp(gamma/2)-1>0, so K=ceil(2A^2/B^2), A=exp(gamma)-1, ensures variance at least 2A^2. The identity A/B=exp(gamma/2)+1 is correct. Other signed coefficients only add nonnegative variance.
4. Rademacher fourth moment is at most 3sigma^4. Paley-Zygmund on Z^2 at threshold A^2 gives P(|Z|>A)>=1/12; the bound is valid also when sigma^2=2A^2. Conditional on such a nuisance value, the independent target has opposite sign with probability 1/2, producing strict wrong sign and error at least 1/24 without invoking any tie convention. Averaging expectations over rows gives the displayed bound; row errors need not be independent. Fixed gamma and d are essential to the asymptotic conclusion. Gamma=0 is separately trivial.
5. The conditional initial PreLN implementation has width 4d+6 and squared row norm 8: token energy 2, two unit code banks total 4, anchor 2. Full LN divisor sqrt(1+8/W), the stated shared Q/K/V projections and Frobenius norms are correct. Whole key-array norm sqrt(gamma N) is not a bounded learned projection norm. Increasing d grows both LN gain norm and Q/K Frobenius norms. Public neighboring position-code features are supplied; their generation from scalar positions is not proved.
6. The exact predecessor-indicator rank argument is correct: selecting rows 2..N and columns 1..N-1 yields a triangular matrix with nonzero diagonal, regardless of unused upper-triangular entries. A full row-constant shift is rank 1, so the rank can fall by at most 1. This says nothing by itself about approximate retrieval or statistical risk.

Independent diagnostic replay: I ran the frozen check.py and compared parsed JSON with saved results; every field matched. It includes 24 exhaustive fair-sign code/row cases, 19 satisfying the variance premise, 3 exact binomial all-row averages, and 80 direct correction identities. Finite numerical tests do not prove the covering argument; that step was checked analytically. No actual full-network noise or risk test was performed.

Scope: the proof rules out one plausible tied positional-code sign decoder even with favorable exact correction and exact arithmetic. Sign-preserving amplification cannot repair an already wrong sign. It does not rule out untied Q/K, richer decoders, contextual values or a prediction algorithm avoiding predecessor reconstruction. In particular theta=0 admits the perfect constant probability predictor 1/2, so the average bit-reconstruction lower bound cannot be relabeled a terminal-risk lower bound. No novelty or formal-verification claim is made.
