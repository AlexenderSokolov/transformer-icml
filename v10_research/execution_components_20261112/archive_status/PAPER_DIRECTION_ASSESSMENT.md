## What the historical plan actually asks for

The November6 mother-theorem reset explicitly rejects indefinitely expanding local symmetry/compiler variants and does not certify a publishable main innovation. It preserves two routes: explicit execution-robustness with a meaningful new arithmetic/risk obligation, or an estimator-relevant exact-real statistical lower bound stronger than the inherited constant-risk logarithmic-depth result. It expressly warns that coefficient bits are not state bits, uniform attention log precision is prior, and all-row evolving state cannot be compressed by dropping the factor N without proof.

The new scalar-position construction is genuine progress in compiling one public source, but does not meet that mother frontier. More broadcast/component variants alone should not be treated as closing the plan.

## Best alternative to a full matching construction

The most coherent existing paper-shaped unit is a source-average decision-approximation theorem for correlated finite-prefix inputs and genuinely evolving full discrete PreLN, combined with:

1. same-trajectory terminal-risk separation from the unrestricted statistical benchmark;
2. direct tag-aware RMS and squared-risk bounds, permitting heterogeneous public positions without a global key-energy restriction;
3. exact-real full-PreLN output injectivity as a counterexample to interpreting this approximation as information destruction;
4. a precise resource/metric boundary explaining why tiny output ranges, preserved TV/KL information, bounded-gain decisions and finite observation precision are different.

This is more unified than a catalog of local retrieval mechanisms and need not promise a matching constructor. It should be presented as an approximation/decision limitation, not a sharp resource frontier, training theorem, universal information contraction, or new statistical rate.

## Why this is not yet a confident ICML recommendation

The current fixed-spin model has unusually convenient source-independent one-token marginals. A general unknown four-parameter second-order source has source-dependent marginal frequencies, so the common theta-independent template argument cannot just be reused. Extending the theorem to a meaningful broader family with matching low-order marginals, or identifying a genuinely new quantitative testing limitation, would substantively strengthen the contribution.

Hahn-style bounded influence, Xiang-style full-PreLN prompt influence, Xu-Fang layerwise-mixing forgetting, and continuous-time interacting-particle limits are serious predecessors. Removing layerwise hidden-state mixing in a finite-prefix correlated discrete model is a potential contract distinction, not proof of originality. The L2 tag selection factor improvement is routine by itself. Exact-real injectivity is also a familiar phenomenon, even with an explicit PreLN realization. Standard root-N benchmarks and local linear obstructions belong in support, not the headline novelty claim.

Concrete evidence gaps: exact theorem-by-theorem primary-source comparison for the full architecture and source assumptions; independent verification of which assumptions are materially weaker; a nonvacuous experimental regime for the theoretical approximation rather than loose bounds above one; and trained-model or carefully controlled architecture interventions if empirical relevance is claimed. Existing finite diagnostics test implementation/algebra, not learning or frontier sharpness.

## Other alternatives

A realistic arithmetic-precision paper is still plausible only after selecting a single explicit arithmetic/execution contract and charging normalization, exponentiation, summation, storage, coefficient/input rounding and final observation. Adversarial residual errors, scalar-output resolution and deterministic rounding are different models. A matching vanishing-risk/depth/precision statement would be stronger than the existing fixed-risk erasure plus generic stability combination, but is not presently proved.

A standalone uniform-linear or one-source alignment paper currently looks weaker: the former is restricted polynomial functional calculus, the latter is one public source rather than all local contexts or forecasting. They are useful lemmas and sharp guardrails, not automatically top-tier main contributions.

Recommended stop rule: commit to one main claim and source/model contract, identify the closest competing theorem, and demonstrate a quantitative or assumption-level separation before producing more auxiliary modules. If none survives that comparison, retain a technical report rather than representing the present collection as an ICML-ready paper.
