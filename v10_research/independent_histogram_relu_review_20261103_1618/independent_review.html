Independent review: fixed histogram ReLU obstruction

3 November 2026. Independent analytical check, not formal peer review, exhaustive prior-art clearance or a full-Transformer lower-bound certification. No numerical tests, random operations, source edits or external writes were used.

Reviewed artifact: fixed_readout_population_obstruction_v1.html, SHA-256 b4e518e2d69970ea2ca00509f894edff7d191d9684a0f8ae39ce1b97e092f4c8.

Disposition: no mathematical blocker found for the declared fixed finite readout and input interface. The witnessing source and limiting positive gap may depend on the readout. No uniform gap over all finite network sizes is established.

  • Family and coordinates. With w=(1−u)/3 and v=ut, the given transition probabilities yield the four successor-bit frequency rows (u−v,v), (w/2,w/2), (v,w−v), (w/2,w/2), and correct stationary balance. On 1/10<u<1/5, u/4<v<3u/4, all transitions are positive and min π≥1/10. Every bank limiting coordinate (π_rθ_r,π_r) is affine in (u,v). The target θ₀₀=v/u has mixed derivative −1/u², nonzero throughout the wedge.
  • Fixed readout obstruction. Fixing the terminal input to 00 and restricting a finite ReLU network, with optional clipping, to the affine two-parameter image gives finitely many affine pieces. A finite cover of a nonempty open wedge by such polyhedral pieces has a full-dimensional open cell, and v/u is not affine on any open cell, so some source has positive gap Δ. For two finite ReLU logits followed by binary softmax, exact equality with v/u on an open cell requires their piecewise-affine logit difference to equal log[v/(u−v)], whose mixed derivative is −1/(u−v)². Thus the same conclusion holds. A fixed finite network can approximate the ratio arbitrarily closely when its size is allowed to depend on a desired fixed accuracy, so no architecture-size-uniform positive gap follows.
  • Uniform mixing. For the forward chain P²(a,11)≥1/8: for a=00 it is t/2≥1/8; the other rows have probabilities bounded below by the same constant under the parameter ranges. Reverse flow gives R²(a,11)=wP²(11,a)/π_a; the four values are t/2, (1−v/w)/2, 1/4 and 1/4, each ≥1/8. Hence both two-step kernels have a common atom of mass 1/8; geometric contraction gives the displayed bound and reverse mixing sum 16. Neither a false two-step π-minorization nor reversibility is needed.
  • Actual terminal and finite M. Stationary bounded edge-function covariance at lag h is bounded by δ(P^(h−1)); summing δ(P^k)≤(7/8)^⌊k/2⌋ gives 16. Therefore Var(count)≤M/4+2M·16=(129/4)M. For each C-coordinate the +1 normalization contributes at most 1/(M+1), and for each J-coordinate the +2 contributes at most 2/(M+1), so summing eight gives [8√(129M/4)+12]/(M+1). A fixed finite ReLU/clipped or ReLU/sigmoid readout has a finite Lipschitz constant on the bounded domain. Defining G_M by counterfactually plugging terminal 00 into the same bank, the inequality E[1{S_N=00}|G_M−t|]≥uΔ−E|G_M−F(b,00)| is valid without independence. Stationarity fixes P(S_N=00)=u, and L1 convergence yields the conditional limit Δ and unconditional liminf at least uΔ. The finite bound follows by the same inequality.
  • Quantifiers and exclusions. The network and its parameters must be one fixed finite choice for all lengths and sources; the witness is chosen afterward. Extra attention, division, unbounded length/position signals, source- or horizon-dependent weights, and other residual/value channels are outside this model. The length-indexed compact causal construction is not ruled out. The source prior claim requires a density strictly positive on the open wedge; continuity of the error then gives positive integrated loss for each fixed network.

The linked ReLU-piecewise-affine and histogram sources are antecedents, but this review does not establish that the scoped reduction is novel or that the public archive includes all companions.
