Why the stationary A³ rate does not extend to arbitrary starts

3 November 2026. An audit and consequence of inherited source MM5, not an independent claim of novelty. The risk is expected half-L1 TV of next-symbol prediction at the actual same-history terminal pair, over all common, possibly randomized predictors. No tests, enumeration, or source overwrites were performed. This is not a compute, network, or precision lower bound.

1. Exact inherited lower and scope

The MM5 statistical proof, SHA-256 19d2b5e4d5a5c7c5f152f14b287bd42a5f130de4428c82f82e1a5916fe9400f3, states for every A≥2, M≥1:

inf_predictor sup_(θ,μ) E TV(Q(X₁:₍M₊₂₎), θ_(X₍M₊₁₎,X₍M₊₂₎))
    ≥ 2⁻¹⁶ min{1, √[A⁵/(M+1)]}.

Here θ ranges over homogeneous legal order-two tables and μ over arbitrary initial pair laws. The estimator may use A, M and independent randomization. The particular lower-bound table and initial law may vary across M and the worst case, but are fixed during one trajectory. The lower is statistical even for unlimited computation and access to all M+2 symbols. It cannot be obtained by claiming conditional iid observations for an arbitrary source: MM5 constructs a special source and proves the complete-history path-law factorization for that family.

The R011 assembly gives the full-history add-one upper 2¹³ min{1,√[A⁵/(M+1)]} at β_mix=0 and also inherits a common minimax lower. The two display the same A exponent, conditional on their underlying proof chains. This is not the A³ stationary or uniformly fast-mixing setting. In particular, an all-source A³ minimax upper is incompatible with MM5 whenever A grows; a constant hidden in O_A notation can of course depend on A.

2. Why the hard source yields five powers

For A≥12, MM5 sets m=⌊A/4⌋, L=m², and separates four disjoint m-symbol sets C,U,V,W. An Euler cycle on C² supplies L distinct context states as a clock. A homogeneous legal shift table replaces five successive clock edges by a five-step detour, drawing u∈U, v∈V, then a label w∈W from an unknown row quv. The detour rejoins the clock at the correct state without changing its period L. All other rows are explicitly specified, including unused symbols. No separate clock variable is given to the model.

The initial phase depends only on A,M, is identical across all hidden sign choices, and aligns the observed endpoint with a U×V query. With n=⌊M/L⌋, MM5 gives a sign-independent public bijection between the full observed word and n independent training triples (U,V,W) plus its actual terminal pair T; T is uniform on U×V and independent of the n triples for this special constructed family. Its full likelihood retains every observed token, including the initialization and clock. There are m² possible queried rows, m possible labels and only n≈M/m² informative departures. Paired signs with amplitude min{1/2,(1/8)√[m³/(n+1)]} and a full-history testing argument give the cited √(A⁵/M) order. The proof covers all phase residues, including n=0, and handles smaller A via a separate iid lower. For fixed A, the constant is tiny and does not imply that every source or a single horizon-independent initial distribution is hard.

3. The stationary occupation bound genuinely fails

The stationary proof gives E[1/(J+1)]≤d HM+1/(M+1), with d=A² and J the number of earlier occurrences of the actual terminal pair. Its averaging step uses equal expectations at different times under stationarity; it does not apply for arbitrary initialization.

More strongly, MM5 supplies a counterexample to this very inequality. Take A=4m, m≥3; L=m² and M=L⌊m²/2⌋. The prescribed initial phase makes T a uniformly random element of U×V, and the n=⌊m²/2⌋ earlier U×V contexts are independently uniform and independent of T. The sets of clock phases are disjoint, so no other historical pair equals T. Thus J∼Binomial(n,1/m²), and the union bound gives P(J=0)≥1−n/m²≥1/2. Consequently E[1/(J+1)]≥1/2. The stationary right side is A² HM+1/(M+1)=O(log m/m²), which tends to zero. This is a symbolic construction, not numerical testing.

4. Positive transitions do not suffice without a uniform mixing bound

Fix A,M and any table θ in the hard family. Set θεab=(1−ε)θab+ε Unif(A), 0<ε<1, leaving μ unchanged. Every legal transition is positive; the pair chain is irreducible and aperiodic. Indeed its two-step kernel dominates ε² times uniform probability on the A² pair states, with a minorization constant that can be arbitrarily small.

Couple the first M departures of the old and smoothed processes by sharing each old draw unless its independent ε-contamination coin fires. With probability at least 1−Mε the observed histories coincide. On identical histories the same randomized predictor has the same output under a shared seed, and the two target rows are at TV distance at most ε. Since TV loss is bounded by 1, every predictor's risk changes by at most (M+1)ε, uniformly over all signs. Therefore the supremum over strictly positive, irreducible and aperiodic sources has the same MM5 lower after ε↓0. Equivalently, for c=2⁻¹⁶ and g=min{1,√[A⁵/(M+1)]}, choosing ε=cg/[2(M+1)] gives a risk lower cg/2 on the corresponding positive family. This is not a lower under a fixed positive minorization constant independent of A,M: ε here changes with the problem size, and the original source is periodic and has zero edges.

5. Relation to prior work and what remains

Norris, Peres and Zhai, Proposition 1.1 bound the probability that an arbitrary-start, d-state homogeneous chain reaches a previously unseen state at time t by d²/t (the pointwise proposition is stated for t>d; smaller t follows from the trivial probability bound). On the pair chain d=A², this yields A⁴/t. It is an actual-terminal zero-visit estimate, not by itself a transition-row TV risk bound. The inherited universal occupation proof also uses d² factors. Han, Jana and Wu, supplement §4 explicitly separate nonstationary redundancy from the stationarity-dependent step connecting redundancy to final-time risk. Their stationary higher-order KL theorem and resulting TV upper do not establish an arbitrary-start A³ result.

Promising alternatives remain under explicitly stronger restrictions: stationary initialization, uniformly controlled forward/reverse mixing, bounded effective recurrent/transient complexity, or a different risk such as a time-averaged query. A common predictor and identical history must still be used. A separately verified fixed-minorization upper may be transferred to arbitrary starts by a paid burn-in/coupling argument, but is not an unrestricted-source claim. Source MM5 and R011 are local inherited work rather than externally published theorems; an independent analytic audit should inspect the lower proof and its byte provenance before publication or a formal novelty claim.
