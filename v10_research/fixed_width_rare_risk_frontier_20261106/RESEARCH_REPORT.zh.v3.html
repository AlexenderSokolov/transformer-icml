固定宽度稀有二阶预测的历史深度与精度关系

2026 年 11 月 6 日。T1/T2 阶段研究报告。

结论

本轮给出一个可审查的联合充分条件：在固定宽度、真实 PreLN、零 shift 最终 LN 与普通 softmax 下，一次有效注意力可以读取公共选择的 b 个末尾符号，经过已计费解码，达到既有 rare-u 统计风险加上显式概率量化、输出范围及实施误差。

同时得到一个直接针对任务风险的必要条件：单次读出后，若解码器的完整 Lipschitz 增益太小，就不能准确估计真实末端 A=00 的未知转移概率。证明采用联合再生耦合与 Wasserstein 距离，允许精确实数编码保持全部 KL，因此不受上一轮单射反例推翻。

这还不是匹配的最优历史—深度—精度前沿。上界采用顺序解码；下界只有一读增益门槛，不限制任意多次数据相关注意力。旧统计率、一般量化下界及已有编译器不作为创新主张。

一 同一个过程风险与模型

二阶状态 A=00、B=01、C=10、D=11。设 w=(1-u)/3、q=ut/w，平稳分布为 (u,w,w,w)，转移行为

(1−t,t,0,0), (0,0,1/2,1/2),
(q,1−q,0,0), (0,0,1/2,1/2).

使用 0<u≤1/100、1/2≤t≤3/4。全链统一快速混合；A 状态罕见不等于全链慢混合。预测时刻是 X₁:N 的 N+1，真实终端状态 T=(X_{N−1},X_N)。主风险为同历史期望 TV；条件于 T=A 的风险单独报告。

模型使用原始符号、公共归一化位置、合成终端，全特征 ε_LN=1、gain=2、shift=0 的 PreLN 残差块，以及普通最终 LN、仿射词表头和 softmax。宽度固定，完整状态、矩阵、bias 和单阶段增益有固定上界。参数可以依赖公共长度和预算，但不能依赖未知 t 或真实 u。

固定一个事先声明的二进有理裁剪水平 α∈(0,1/4]。范数和复杂度常数可以依 α，但不能在 u 变小时免费改变 α。

二 固定宽度联合风险上界

公共选择二次幂 b、G，满足 8≤b≤N、G≥8，并选择实施容差 0<ε≤1/16。存在一族不读取 u,t 的网络，宽度和 value 维度固定，只用一次有效均匀注意力，且实际概率输出严格属于 [α,1−α]。

其同历史条件风险满足

E[|Q_N(1)−t| | T=A]
  ≤min{1,157/√(1+(b−2)u)}+1/G+ε.

同一个网络的总体风险满足

R_N≤1300u/√(1+(b−2)u)
      +w(α−q)₊+(u+w)/G+ε.

在同一实际输出范围下，任何预测器也有 R_N≥w(α−q)₊。这里 b 是使用的末尾历史量，G 是有意选择的概率量化网格，ε 是真实网络对参考计算的误差。三者不是一个“精度”变量。

令 H=ceil(log₂(N+1))、h=ceil(log₂(b+2))，可达深度为

D=O_α(H+b+log²(b+2)+h logG+log²(1/ε)).

每个系数的描述长度为 O_α(D)，保守总参数描述位数为 O_α(D²)。这些是上界，不意味着任何网络都只能使用 O(D) 个历史符号。

证明链与真正补上的接口

复用已有固定宽度 packed-suffix 程序：一个标量读出携带长度 b 的基数编码，已计费的连续平台分类器逐个解码，内部累积二阶转移计数，并恢复真实终端二元组。每个物理行都对应合法虚拟后缀，保证算术域与全范数合法。

A 行使用 add-one。C 行必须使用无平滑频率和零计数回退；否则会破坏旧总体 rare-u 率。固定宽度 restoring division 显式处理分子零、分子等于分母和分母零，输出网格误差至多 1/G。没有外部除法、取整或免费比较器。

旧固定宽度 PreLN 结果的最终输出是带非零 shift 的概率坐标投影，不能直接当作普通 softmax。本轮先在有界概率区间内，用 Taylor 展开与已计费乘法顺序计算 log，深度 O_α(log²(1/ε))、宽度固定；再把归一化 logit bank 放进固定宽度 norm-padding 程序，使目标全特征方差为 3。已计费幅度恢复后，gain=2、shift=0 的最终 LN 恢复 logits，再接普通仿射 softmax。

为确保实际输出而非仅参考估计器严格处在 [α,1−α]，先留出小幅向内裁剪余量，再把编译误差控制在余量以内。该裁剪偏差明确包含在 ε 中。

统计部分依赖已有真实终端 A 的 157 界及总体混合估计器的 1300 界。平稳性使末尾 b 段具有相同过程律，其终端状态就是原始终端状态；没有把查询独立化。

预算量词与例子

若预先选择 b≈logN、G≈b 且 G≥16、ε=1/G，则 D=O_α(logN)，条件风险上界为 O(1/√(1+u logN))+O(1/logN)。总体上界仍保留范围地板与量化项。

这不意味着对完全未知 u 自动获得最优预算。把目标风险反解成 b≈1/(u r²) 后再选择权重，已经使用了 u；这种选择只能在已知 u 或已声明稀有程度下界的预算问题中使用。一般情况应保留对同一个公共 b 的点态风险式。

取 b 为不超过 N 的最大二次幂，则 b>N/2，可以达到与全历史同阶的统计基线，但解码深度付出了线性 N 项。没有由此证明线性深度必要，也没有排除更快的多次注意力算法。

三 实际执行量化是单独的条件契约

不能把 O(D) 位系数描述直接叫作 O(D) 位可靠执行。本轮另列固定网格 evaluator 的充分条件：标量运算、参数与输入转换绝对误差不超过 Δ=2^(−p)；零 Q/K、exp(0)=1 与因果 mask 精确保留；LN 按非负方差、add-one、经认证的 sqrt/div 计算；最终有界 logits 的 exp/div 同样需误差证书；整数范围覆盖前缀分母 N+1，不能溢出。

为得到严格二元概率输出，有限输出格式明确量化一个概率，再以精确网格减法输出其补数。分别舍入两个概率只保证向量接近，不自动保证和为一。这是有限 evaluator 的输出编码，不是额外插入原实数网络的隐藏层。

设 m 为恢复前计算深度、初始缩放为 η=2^(−M)，固定常数 A_α>1。可审查充分条件是

p≥M+m log₂A_α+log₂(N+2)+log₂(1/ν)+C_α,

其中 ν 是允许增加的最终 TV 误差，需满足 0<ν≤ν₀(α)，ν₀(α) 是用于保持有界误差邻域的固定小常数。于是一个充分的分数位数量级为 O_α(D+logN+log(1/ν))，整数范围另付 O(logN)+O_α(1) 位。总体和条件风险上界各增加 ν；若要保留严格 α 范围，还要求 ν≤μ/2，其中 μ 是证明中预留的向内裁剪余量。

这不是对任何现成 dtype 的保证，未实例化或运行 evaluator。sqrt、exp、division 的实际位操作成本和常数仍需机器级实现证书。该契约是分析充分条件，不是必要精度下界。

四 直接针对风险的一读传输界

设单读之前所有操作都逐位置进行，因此合成终端的 query 是公共的。先考虑完整欧氏范数有界的加性统计

Z=[a_*+Σ_i a_i(X_i)]/(N+1)，‖a_i(x)‖≤R,

其中位置函数可以任意复杂，维度也可以增长；a_* 公共。比较同一 u 下参数 t,s，记 h=|t−s|、m=ceil(N/2)。条件于实际终端 A，两统计分布满足

W₁(Law_t(Z|A),Law_s(Z|A))
  ≤R[32+√(8320muh)]/(N+1).

这是 Wasserstein 距离，不是 KL 或 TV。精确实数单射可以同时保留完整 KL 并满足该距离界。

为什么耦合包含真实末端和相关性

反向条件历史从 A 出发，仍使用同一二阶核。两步核都有共同 D 原子，质量至少 1/4。用共享再生 coin 构造联合链，不能只因两个边际各自混合就断言联合混合。

联合平稳分布的两边际相同，均为 π；参数变化只通过 A 的罕见访问及 C 的小转移影响分歧，平稳分歧概率≤40uh。把反向相邻两个符号视为一个 pair-state 块，每块向量差均值为零。共同再生给可求和协方差，得到总差平方期望≤8320mR²uh。由平稳联合链切换到真实起点 (A,A)，首次再生前最多增加 32R 的期望差。整个论证没有使用独立样本公式。

付费解码如何进入同一总体风险

若完整解码器 F 满足 TV(F(z),F(z′))≤Λ‖z−z′‖，实际网络在 A 条件下对该接口的平均近似误差至多 η_A，则

max{R_t,R_s}≥(u/2)
   [h−ΛR(32+√(8320muh))/(N+1)−2η_A]₊.

若仅知道无条件接口误差≤ε，安全版本是在没有 η_A 的下界后减 ε，不能免费沿用条件误差 ε。稀有条件与总体误差相差的 u 因子始终保留。

一个有界分数的真实单头读出可以写成带权 numerator/denominator 的加性统计。分数范围 S、value 范数 V、后续完整解码增益 L 都明确计入，不能漏掉 output projection、residual、FFN、LN 或最终 head。

取固定参数间距，在 A 条件误差足够小且接口近似误差已计入时，需要

L≥c·N/(1+√(Nu))
  =Ω(min{N,√(N/u)}),

常数依赖声明的 score/value 上界。若 L≤C₀M^D，得到 D≥log_M[N/(1+√(Nu))]−O(1)。这是单读增益门槛，不是匹配风险曲线，也不覆盖读取更新后非终端 K/V 的多次调用。

读出接口若按已声明量化器产生欧氏误差≤δ，可以用 η_A≤Lδ。这个误差项削弱必要条件，不能把它反过来称作精度下界。全栈有限执行仍由上一节的单独契约处理。

同一 rare 源的终端访问门槛更强

该传输计算不能作为新的总体深度主下界。对同一个 rare 源，若 X_{N−1}=1，最后一个符号是公平位；它决定真实终端为 C 或 D，目标概率之差 g=1/2−q≥21/44。一读只改变末端原始符号时，head 输出距离至多 4V exp(S)/(N+1)。因此

R_t≥w[g−4V exp(S)L/(N+1)]₊.

该公式首先对精确接口预测器 Q₀ 成立；实际 Q 若仅在每个源下以无条件 TV 误差 ε 近似 Q₀，右侧必须再减 ε 并取正部。

对精确接口，总体低风险要求 L=Ω(N)，已支配 N/(1+√(Nu)) 这一参数估计门槛。这个推导是旧末端符号影响机制在同一源上的直接适配，不能称新主定理。传输界的独立用途只在终端已经固定为 A、仍需估计未知转移概率的条件任务；它不再借助复制末端符号的困难。

五 已被否决的拼接与标准下界

上一轮总通信位数下界与通用 Lipschitz 网格模拟直接拼接，可能完全无法推出正风险下界。原因是模拟成本本身含 log(1/ε)，代回指数型信息下界后，右侧可能只剩 ε 的一次或更高次幂，减去模拟误差即失去正值。附录保存全尺度的判据，不继续把该拼接包装成主结果。

若完整可见终端信息最多有 M 个状态，标准码本畸变论证给条件风险≥1/(16M)，总体风险≥u/(16M)。只有全部 W 个可见坐标各有不超过2^p个状态且没有额外标签、缓存或再次读取时，才可写 M≤2^(Wp)，其中 p 是每坐标全部编码位，不只是浮点尾数或网格分数位。这个标准误差地板不自动包含深度。完整历史的统计下界、输出范围地板、有限状态畸变下界可以同时列出，却不能因此声称得到匹配 D—precision 前沿。有限状态字母表与本轮 evaluator 的误差保证，只有严格对齐语义后才能比较。

3. Fixed-width dyadic quotient, including C's exact zero fallback

The existing restoring-division comparator from `../shallow_readout_audit_20261104_1037/sources/proof.v3.md` is

H_delta(x)=min(1,Q max(0,x+delta)), delta=1/Q.

It is implemented by one ReLU initialization and h clipped doublings, each gain at most two. On the delta lattice it equals 1{x>=0}. We use this exact-real reference fact, not a discontinuous comparator primitive or an assertion about perturbed inputs.

The following minor extension handles 0<=n<=d<=1, lattice n,d, and d=0. Compute

z=H_delta(-d), d'=d+delta z,
e=H_delta(n-d'), v=ReLU(d'+e-1),
r=n-v, f=e.

On the legal domain, z=1 precisely at d=0, hence d'>=delta. Because n<=d', e=1 precisely at n=d'. The gate v equals e d'. Thus 0<=r<d' and f is the endpoint indicator. If n=d'>0, f=1 and r=0; otherwise f=0 and r=n. If d=0, then n=0, d'=delta, f=r=0.

For j=1,...,g, perform paid steps

a=H_delta(2r-d'), v=ReLU(d'+a-1),
r <- clip_[0,1](2r-v), f <- f+2^(-j) a.

On every reference row a is an exact bit, v=a d', and 0<=r<d'; the clipping is inactive on the valid recurrence. For n<d' standard induction gives

f=floor(G n/d')/G,

while n=d' yields f=1 exactly. The d=0 case yields f=0. No large integer F, ceiling, division, floor or endpoint test occurs as a network primitive. All values and complete fixed-size states stay bounded. The extra initial comparator and gate cost O(h); the g rounds cost O(hg), with maximum coefficient description O(h+g).

Apply this algorithm to

Set t=1/2,s=3/4. For an exact interface eta_A=0, (R) gives a nonzero rare-event risk threshold whenever

for a sufficiently small constant c depending on S,V. In particular, risk o(u) requires

L >= c' N/(1+sqrt(Nu))
  = Omega(min{N,sqrt(N/u)}).                    (8)

D >= log_M[N/(1+sqrt(Nu))]-O(1).                (9)

A coefficient-description length is not used as an activation precision. Width does not appear in (8): the estimate is full-vector and dimension-free. Wide one-read O(log N)-depth constructions have sufficiently large polynomial L and are not excluded.

For a quantized actual head output with Euclidean error at most delta before the same L-Lipschitz G, set eta_A<=L delta. For d coordinates rounded with step zeta and no overflow, delta<=sqrt(d)zeta/2. This is an interface error assumption, not a theorem about finite arithmetic throughout all layers. Its error term weakens (R); it cannot be relabeled a precision lower by itself.

6.1 Parallel heads require an aggregate bound

If H heads read in parallel from the same pre-read local states and each query is public, concatenate the H weighted numerators and H denominators. If head h has full value bound V_h and all head score ranges are at most S, the full local augmented vector has bound

R_H=exp(S)sqrt(sum_(h=1)^H V_h^2+H).

For the concatenated ratio outputs, a valid Lipschitz factor is exp(S)(1+max_h V_h). Thus (T) and (R) apply with R replaced by R_H and Lambda replaced by L exp(S)(1+max_h V_h), where L is the paid continuation bound in the full concatenated Euclidean norm, including the output projection. Both H and the total value dimension are charged. Merely per-head bounds do not make R_H constant when H grows. This extension permits one simultaneous physical attention layer; it does not permit sequential reads with data-dependent queries, intermediate memory mixing, or an uncharged later reread.

1. Frozen risks and source

Use exactly the stationary binary rare family of RARE_ONE_READ_TRANSPORT.candidate.v2.md. Write

2. Task-directed one-read quantization: a correct but empty inequality

The checked rare communication theorem gives, for a source-independent interactive b-bit protocol whose output is normalized,

risk >= a_b := c u 2^(-b)/sqrt(1+m u^2),
c=exp(-1/100)/(20000e),                         (1)

with Bob's entire parity observation and actual terminal pair supplied freely, m<=N. The proof already handles parameter-dependent Bob marginals and conditional terminal KL.

For a one public-query bounded-score read with d>=1 value coordinates, its augmented numerator-denominator partial sum belongs to a radius-R ball of dimension k=d+1, where

R=exp(S)sqrt(V^2+1).

A public Euclidean delta-net transmits Alice's partial sum using

B(delta)=ceil[k log2(1+2R/delta)]

bits. For delta<=exp(-S)/2, the denominator stays positive. If the terminal continuation is globally L-Lipschitz into binary TV, a safe uniform simulator error is

epsilon(delta)<=K delta,
K=2L exp(S)(1+V).                               (2)

The full off-manifold continuation is required here. If its output is not a normalized Bernoulli distribution, clip its next-one coordinate to [0,1]; the clipping preserves the scalar Lipschitz error to the actual valid network output. Equivalently take L as the globally Lipschitz scalar probability continuation. The already-free terminal pair adds no communication bit.

Combining (1)-(2) correctly yields

R(Q) >= c u 2^(-B(delta))/sqrt(1+m u^2)-K delta. (3)

This is a source-average RISK inequality, not an exact-output-information assertion. But it is empty at EVERY admissible delta under the usual envelope L>=1. Indeed k>=1 and

2^(-B(delta))
  <= [delta/(delta+2R)]^k
  <= delta/(2R).

Set a=c u/sqrt(1+m u^2). Then the right side of (3) is at most

delta[a/(2R)-K] <=0,                            (4)

because R>=1, K>=2, and a<c/100<1. Optimizing delta or setting the simulation tolerance proportional to the advertised risk cannot change this sign. Replacing k=d+1 by the more favorable k=1 would still leave the same obstruction.

2.1 Charging error only on the task event does not cure it

If the comparison is made directly under E, the rare entropy argument gives conditional protocol loss >=c 2^(-b)/sqrt(1+m u^2). A conditional output approximation tolerance K delta therefore yields

R_A(Q)>= c 2^(-B(delta))/sqrt(1+m u^2)-K delta.

It is still nonpositive by the same argument, now with a=c/sqrt(1+m u^2)<1. Hence the missing factor is not an accidental failure to multiply simulator error by u. Correct task-directed conditioning alone does not create a width/depth lower.

This is a boundary of THIS combination of the rare total-bit lower and a generic geometric simulation. It does not prove that no better architecture-dependent or task-dependent simulation exists. If a truly smaller source-average message radius or entropy is separately established, it must be analyzed rather than substituted without proof. The new transport note supplies such a direct source-specific comparison, not a claim of improved bit cardinality.

3. Finite visible terminal state: standard precision and sample-size bounds

Suppose the complete source-dependent information available to the final decoder takes at most M states. This includes EVERY visible terminal activation, exponent, branch/index tag, cached read, and any other source-dependent side channel. The decoder may depend on public N,u, network parameters, and independent randomness. It may not reread other rows or raw tokens. This assumption does not follow from width alone or from short coefficient descriptions.

Then

R_A(Q)>=1/(16M),     R(Q)>=u/(16M).             (5)

Proof. Let t be uniform on I=[1/2,3/4], of length a=1/4. Conditioning on E does not change the prior because Pr_t(E)=u. Conditional on all independent decoder coins, at most M possible predicted next-one probabilities exist. Whatever source-dependent state selection occurs, absolute loss is at least distance to that M-point codebook. For any M real centers, the uniform average distance over an interval of length a is at least a/(4M): split into nearest-center cells of lengths l_j, use integral absolute distance >=l_j^2/4, and sum l_j^2>=a^2/M. Average over the independent coins and multiply by u. Public/decoder coins remain independent of t under E. This proves (5) without a false lossless-history decoding claim.

If each of W visible coordinates has at most 2^p states and there are no extra tags or side channels, M<=2^(Wp), so

R(Q)>=u 2^(-Wp)/16.                            (6)

Consequently a full-history rare-rate envelope C u/sqrt(1+Nu) requires

Wp >= (1/2)log2(1+Nu)-log2(16C).

This is ordinary finite-codebook quantization. It is architecture-independent, contains no D, permits an arbitrary paid or unpaid decoder, and must NOT be advertised as a new attention-resource theorem. A finite state at one internal cut need not limit a network that subsequently reads data again.

Independently, the established full-history conditional KL bound is

KL(P_t^(X|E)||P_s^(X|E))<=60(1+Nu)(t-s)^2.

Take h=1/[16sqrt(1+Nu)] around midpoint 5/8. Pinsker bounds conditional TV by sqrt(30)/16. The two-point absolute-loss overlap bound gives

R_A(Q)>= [1-sqrt(30)/16]/[32sqrt(1+Nu)]
       >=1/[64sqrt(1+Nu)],
R(Q)>=u/[64sqrt(1+Nu)].                         (7)

This sample-size obstruction also applies to any randomized predictor by data processing. The conditional full-history KL is explicitly proved in EXACT_PRELN_INFORMATION_OBSTRUCTION.candidate.v2.md, Section 4.1. It is not derived by dividing stationary path KL by u.

4. The old terminal-context mechanism already forces gain Omega(N) here

The published-internal predecessor MATCHING_ORDER.v2.md proves a late-token influence obstruction using a different fixed noisy-XOR source. Its exact source is NOT the rare family. Nevertheless its elementary one-read mechanism transfers directly to this rare family, as follows.

Consider ONE bounded-score read with public terminal query, ||v_i||<=V, score range <=S, and a terminal continuation that is L-Lipschitz into binary TV. Fix any t in [1/2,3/4], even allowing the predictor to know t. Conditional on a prefix X_1:N-1 ending in symbol 1, the last symbol X_N is a fair bit: both applicable preceding pair rows B,D assign probability 1/2 to each next symbol.

If X_N=0 the actual terminal pair is C=10, whose next-one probability is 1-q_t. If X_N=1 the pair is D=11, whose next-one probability is 1/2. Their TV gap is

g_t=1/2-q_t >=1/2-1/44=21/44.

The two histories differ only at raw position N. Every other pre-read row and the public terminal query are identical. The head outputs can be written

o_b=(1-alpha_b)r+alpha_b v_b,

where r is the SAME normalized average of all other rows and ||r||,||v_b||<=V. Bounded score range on each history gives alpha_b<=exp(S)/(N+1). Therefore

||o_0-o_1||<=4V exp(S)/(N+1).                  (8)

The conditional fair-bit triangle inequality and stationary Pr(X_(N-1)=1)=2w give

R_t(Q)>=w[g_t-4V exp(S)L/(N+1)]_+.            (9)

Thus any overall risk below, for example, w g_t/2 requires

L >= (N+1)g_t/[8V exp(S)]=Omega(N).           (10)

The V=0 case simply cannot achieve that risk. A source-average approximation error epsilon to this smooth one-read interface weakens (9) by at most epsilon. Uniform rounding error delta before the same decoder weakens the inside bracket by at most 2L delta.

This is a short transfer of the prior late-token argument, not a new principal lower. It is valid on the exact rare family, without changing source, stationarity, or terminal query. It applies even to a source-aware predictor because the obstruction is acquiring the actual terminal context, not estimating t.

The new transport note's rare-parameter threshold is

L=Omega(N/(1+sqrt(Nu))),

whose required scale N/(1+sqrt(Nu)) is no larger than N. Hence (10) dominates it for the overall low-risk task. When Nu is large, the domination is strict in order. The transport result's remaining content is the fixed-terminal-A conditional estimation task: the two sources have the SAME fixed terminal pair A, so (9)'s last-bit/context distinction is absent. No new main depth frontier should be claimed from the transport calculation.

5. Ordinary final softmax adds an independent existing floor

If the complete final binary logit contrast has magnitude at most B_out, both predicted probabilities are at least beta=1/(1+exp(B_out)). At terminal C, the true next-zero probability is q_t=ut/w. Thus, for every t,

R_t(Q)>=w[beta-q_t]_+=[w beta-ut]_+.           (11)

For fixed B_out this remains positive as u tends to zero. In particular an overall envelope C u/sqrt(1+Nu), uniform over arbitrarily small u, is impossible before asking about depth, width, or precision. The frozen full-feature epsilon-positive PreLN and bounded affine final head must be audited to obtain B_out, rather than assuming it is absent. Conditional-A estimation remains meaningful because t lies in [1/2,3/4].

The ordinary final-softmax contract is not interchangeable with the older probability-register projection contract in STANDARD_PRELN.candidate.v2.md. That compiler explicitly excludes bounded-logit vocabulary softmax. Its upper cannot be silently used to remove (11).

6. An honest combined resource statement and stopping conclusion

To combine the terms without hiding quantization discontinuities, let Q_0=G(o(X)) be the smooth one-head interface, let L bound G into TV, and suppose

sup_t E_t TV(Q,Q_0)<=epsilon,
sup_t E_t[TV(Q,Q_0)|E]<=eta_A.

These are separate admissible bounds; a global epsilon alone only implies eta_A<=epsilon/u. For Q=Q_0 both may be zero. Apply the finite-state and output-range assumptions to the ACTUAL predictor Q. Under these hypotheses the independent lower terms may be combined by taking their maximum:

R(Q) >= max{
  u/[64sqrt(1+Nu)],
  u/(16M),
  [sup_t w[g_t-4V exp(S)L/(N+1)]_+-epsilon]_+,
  sup_t [w beta-ut]_+,
  (u/2)[1/4-Lambda R(32+sqrt(2080 ceil(N/2)u))/(N+1)-2eta_A]_+
}.

The context term must retain epsilon when Q is only an approximate or rounded version of Q_0. It may be set to zero only for an exact interface or a separately proved zero-error comparison. For a uniform rounded head with error delta, epsilon<=L delta is valid, while the direct two-history bound in Section 4 is another valid route.

Here M<=2^(Wp) only under the complete finite-visible-state contract; L<=C_0 M_0^D only under a fixed primitive envelope; Lambda=L exp(S)(1+V) for the bounded-score augmented sketch; beta applies only when the logit contrast bound is part of the contract; and the last term is the new source-specific transport comparison, with h=1/4. The M used for state cardinality and M_0 used for a primitive gain are different quantities.

This is explicit and reviewable, but it is NOT a sharp joint frontier. The positive finite-state term is standard. The task-directed bit/net reduction is empty. The new conditional-parameter transport threshold is dominated on the overall task by the transferred terminal-context threshold, and both are at most logarithmic necessities after substituting paid exponential sensitivity. The bounded-output floor may dominate all vanishing-rate discussions.

A defensible next claim would need a same-source risk lower that remains informative AFTER terminal context is paid, at allowed output range, and for L large enough to pass these thresholds; or a genuinely architecture-specific precision/decoding lower beyond finite codebook size. Neither is supplied here. Exact scalar injection invalidates exact-information shortcuts, and the wide one-read statistical witness prevents converting logarithmic required parameter information into a required number of useful invocations.

5. Lower 的正确分类与不能推出的结论

5.1 Finite terminal alphabet 是标准 distortion/quantization

假设最终输出只能使用一个完整 terminal statistic S，|range S|≤M，外加已声明且不携带 t 信息的 public metadata/独立 coins。所有 residual、历史的 terminal copies、其他 heads、精确 side ports 都必须包含在 S 内或证明对输出无用。有限 d 不足以给有限 M。

给 Θ 在 [1/2,3/4] 上均匀先验，长度 a=1/4。Pr_Θ(E)=u 独立于 Θ，所以条件 E 不改变该先验。Data processing 和 |S|≤M 给 I(Θ;Q|E)≤log M。标准 absolute-distortion entropy inequality 给

E[|Q(1)−Θ| | E] ≥ a/(2eM) = 1/(8eM)。

该 lower 允许任意编码、任何计算深度、随机 encoder/decoder（独立 coins 按条件处理）。这是 Xu–Raginsky Theorem 3 Eq.(39) 的直接参数代入，不是本研究创新。

若每个 complete terminal scalar 有 A_p 种值且 d fixed，M≤A_p^d；若每标量总 storage bits≤p，则 A_p≤2^p，得到 R_A≥c2^(−dp)。固定 bounded-range fractional grid 给 log A_p=O(p)，但 exact-real coefficient bits 或 error-radius p 不给这个结论。若输出允许读取整个 terminal transcript，而非最后 state，则需要重新计 M；若之后还能读取非terminal rows，也不是该 cut。

5.2 与 full-statistical lower 取 max 仍非 architecture frontier

同源旧 conditional full-history lower 与上式合成

R_A ≥ c max{(1+(N−2)u)^−1/2, M^−1}

（常数可取两者较小值）。这只是任意完整历史估计的 sampling lower 加上最终输出的 finite-message lower。

它不含 b_s，也不下界计算 depth 或 useful read 数。Suffix upper 使用 b_s≪N 时损失的信息是所选算法的代价，不能由 full-statistical lower 证明不可避免。把实现中 b_s≈D 倒代进 upper 不能得到 lower。b_s 只有在整个受限类已被证明/定义为只能看到该 suffix 时才进入 statistical lower；那又是更窄且不同模型。

M 的充分成本可能 O(D) bits，而 finite-message necessity 只有 log(1/r)。这没有 matching。一个 Θ(log(1/u)) 信息需求也不强迫多个 reads，一次高精度消息即足以承载。固定有限字母表 theorem 不依赖 evolving-row attention 结构，不能解决母问题中的 repeated useful attention lower。

5.3 强同预算 baseline

自由读取完整 raw history 的普通 streaming counter 可维护 A-row successes/departures 以及最后两个符号，使用 O(log N) integer bits，在 O(N) arithmetic steps 得到 full-data estimator；再用 O(log(1/r)) bits量化 final estimate。该算法是 statistical/storage baseline，不是免费嵌入 candidate Transformer。若比较总体计算成本，不能将其排除；若比较 architecture，必须明确它获得不同访问接口。

7. Hahn 的覆盖与非覆盖

Primary： https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.11.pdf ，Lemma 5、Theorem 6，printed pp.165–167；修正版 https://mhahn.info/files/transformers-proof.pdf ，§S2 Eqs.(34)–(35)。本轮重开核定位。

可以从Hahn式pathwise influence加coupled Hamming distance得到一个较粗思路，但直接triangle summing O(Nu h+1) mismatches只给O(R[u h+1/N])。候选的√(u h/N)来自**中心化的加法差值在joint Markov chain上的方差**，不是单token sensitivity原句的直接重述。该方差加强本身已有上节标准工具。

在fixed u、固定条件精度下，把Λ≤M^D倒代得到D=Ω(log N)与大量既有bounded-sensitivity深度结论同阶；候选应强调source-specific u-dependence和完整visible-interface定量式，而不称新的general logarithmic-depth necessity。

修正的Hahn proof包含attention weights变化项。候选用实际numerator/denominator分解控制bounded-score ratio时已经保留该效应；不能引用未修正的单value差项推导任意attention。

8. Risk reduction 与最终定位

对于任何共同Λ-Lipschitz F，两个conditional target相差h，triangle inequality和transport coupling给

R_A(t)+R_A(s) ≥ h−ΛW1−2η_A。

这是标准two-target risk reduction；乘同一个terminal probability u然后取max给candidate的u/2。它不是KL contraction或exact-data-processing information loss。若η只无条件控制，conditional化须付1/u，candidate的−ε版本保留此点。

固定h=1/4得到ΛR必须至少常数乘N/(1+√(Nu))才能使conditional risk低于足够小固定阈值；o(u)overallrisk是另一充分要求来调用该lower。这个threshold不是sharp error curve，不证明upper匹配，不下界read次数，也不是固定宽下界。它允许value dimension增长，但收费full norm和decoder sensitivity。
