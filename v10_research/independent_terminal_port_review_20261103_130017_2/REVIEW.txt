Precision equality correction to the paragraph above: The candidate's blanket sentence that at equality no tie rule guarantees correct decoding is too strong when a rule may use the exact side ports. At M rho epsilon=1/2, attaining actual reconstructed error 1/2 requires D=M exp(|beta|). For beta>0 this forces every candidate coordinate to match, hence C_match=C is known from exact C; for beta<0 this forces no matches, hence C_match=0 is known. Otherwise D is smaller and the error is strictly below 1/2. Thus rounding with side-information-aware tie handling guarantees decoding even at equality; fixed half-up/half-down tie handling need not. The strict inequality in the candidate is still a valid, clean sufficient condition. This correction supersedes the earlier paragraph's claim that the blanket statement is proper.
