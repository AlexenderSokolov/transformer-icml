## Why not start another variant now?

A dense-causal-only replacement for singleton copying could be technically useful, but without a genuine lower/comparison question it risks another standard realization exercise. A lower for FPA's canonical fault port would repair a minor interface mismatch, not answer a new mother question. Full-beta classification and approximate gate stability are already done. Minimal supplemental alphabet is already settled in the stated scan model. More channel constants do not repair the lack of a training result. None is presently the best use of another theorem cycle.

A genuine center-level question still absent from these sources is **whether a specified trainable attention model learns to retain early-versus-late row evidence across unseen transition tables, rather than merely having a hand-coded implementation**. But broad training claims require a concrete distribution over source tables, initialization, objective, optimization procedure, test-shift/attack evaluation and same-information baselines. Selecting those prematurely can turn an implementation theorem into an unsupported optimization narrative. This reassessment therefore records that question as open, not as an immediately authorized scientific conclusion or a new experimental project needed before synthesis.
