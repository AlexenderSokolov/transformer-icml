## Proof and scope

1. For p=1/M², every pair state shifts left and appends an independent Bernoulli(p) bit. Therefore P²(u,v)=Pi(v) for *every* u,v, Pi=(1−p,p) tensor (1−p,p), including all four states, yielding beta=1. Stationary initialization gives P(H=0^(M+2))=(1−p)^(M+2)≥1−(M+2)/M²>0 for M≥4. This is an occurrence bound, not a computational reduction for the expected-over-history objective.
2. Under arbitrary initial pair law mu, the same transition certificate and next-bit law hold, while P_mu(H=0^(M+2))=mu(00)(1−p)^M. The chosen history has positive probability iff mu(00)>0. It can be null for another allowed initial law, so the stationary high-probability guarantee cannot be carried over without that qualification. The author clarification states this accurately.
3. Reuse the previous construction: normalize bounded-length 3CNF, explicitly encode variable-width positions and ReLU/uniform-head predictor, M a power of two ≥max(n²,m²,4), k=n≤sqrt M, P the least power of two ≥m. The original exact dyadic q=Q−V/(2MP), Q=3/(2M), has 1/M≤q≤Q and V integer counting violated clauses. Any truth assignment is realized by at most n flips; dummy edits have no effect and last two bits remain zero. The previous independently reviewed proof supplies polynomial encoding, three nonzero incidence coefficients per clause, exact additive token/position product via shared ReLUs, and the m-channel uniform head. All rational scalars, including p=1/M², have O(log M) bits; actual encoding, bit operations and all-position costs remain paid.
4. Since q≥1/M>1/(M+2)=t>p, TV(Bernoulli(q),Bernoulli(p))=q−p and surrogate CE(t,q) strictly increases in q. Thus all three pointwise objectives have identical maximizers. A legal source-independent attacker can maximize q from h and the supplied model, without p or a future label as input. p is public if one later reports TV or evaluates the rational threshold; no known-source oracle needs to be consulted for selecting this special model's attack. Any chosen witness on h extends to a legal global history-only map by identity on other words.
5. Threshold Q−p is reached iff V=0 iff the 3CNF is satisfiable; a polynomial-size mask is checked by exact rational/integer computation. Therefore this restricted exact pointwise threshold problem is NP-complete. For any unsatisfying word, threshold−TV=V/(2MP)≥Delta=1/(2MP); any polynomial-time legal-witness algorithm guaranteed strictly less than Delta regret on each such instance would decide 3SAT. This is inverse-polynomial additive-witness hardness assuming P≠NP, not constant-additive hardness, a generic neural-network result, hidden-source oracle game, source-risk or expected-history hardness, training result, or a trained-pilot finding.
6. For every word of this model, TV≤3/(2M)−1/M², but absolute error O(1/M) does not imply relative accuracy against p=1/M²: (q−p)/p≥M−1. The converse distinction also matters: identity is legal and its value is at least 1/M−1/M², whereas the optimum is at most 3/(2M)−1/M². Thus identity is already at least (M−1)/(3M/2−1) of the optimum (≥3/5 for M≥4). The fine additive-gap hardness is not a constant-factor inapproximability result. The known source p itself can of course be predicted exactly by another constant predictor; this construction concerns attacking the specified model, not impossibility of source prediction.
