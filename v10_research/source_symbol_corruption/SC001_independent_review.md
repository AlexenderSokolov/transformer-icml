## Checks performed

1. **Experiment and adversary.** The final two actual positions remain unchanged, so clean and corrupted terminal row indices agree. The proposed kernel is defined for every K-letter input word, uses fixed named symbols and public M,k, and nowhere uses p or theta. Pattern detection followed by uniform deletion of at most k ones is a legitimate offline history-adaptive kernel. It preserves the marker boundary and the two terminal symbols on every input, including off-family inputs via identity fallback. The kernel is common across the entire lower family and can be made known to the predictor without affecting the proof.

2. **Exchangeability.** For fixed original count n and rounded count r, a target weight-r subset is contained in exactly binomial(l-r,n-r) original weight-n subsets. Every nested pair has probability 1/[binomial(l,n)binomial(n,r)]. The ratio is exactly 1/binomial(l,r). Thus output arrangement conditional on r is source-independent, even though the distribution of r depends on p. The factorization includes the protected two bits as a four-valued message U. This handles all original and corrupted order information, not just histogram statistics.

3. **Full-observation versus finite-message reduction.** Given j,r,U, the complete corrupted history is generated by an independent uniform-arrangement channel. Composing an arbitrary full-history estimator with that channel gives a randomized decoder from at most 4(floor((j-2)/(k+1))+1) messages. A fresh independent random variable can implement that finite conditional channel; no actual attack seed needs to be revealed. This is a valid Blackwell/data-processing direction. The proof does not assume the predictor literally receives only a compressed summary.

4. **Source and target.** The absorbing active IID chain embeds in the homogeneous second-order class on every pair. Its stationary law exists, as required by D2(0), although initialization is WW. Exactly M-j waiting emissions precede the active entry. The selected-event probability is alpha(1-alpha)^(M-j), independent of p. Conditional on the event the entire active suffix is IID Bernoulli(p), before observing its final bits. The target at each of its four actual terminal pairs is the same Bernoulli(p) law. This is why finite endpoint accounting is legitimate without substituting an external target.

5. **Bayes argument and minimax order.** The same uniform prior on [1/4,3/4] is used across every suffix length. It stays uniform after E_j, not after U; the latter is correctly included in the message alphabet. The finite-message error lemma is valid for arbitrary stochastic channels and randomized real outputs. The resulting conditional bound 1/[32(1+(j-2)/(k+1))] is valid without concentration and for every j. Summation over disjoint events is unconditional. The single explicit attack proves the corruption lower even for an adversary precommitted independently of the source. Identity corruption gives the clean R011 floor separately; their maximum, not their sum, is justified.

6. **Constants and edge cases.** For M>=2, event mass >=1/(4M) is safe. The sum-to-integral direction is correct. Monotonicity of b ln(1+A/b) and ln(1+t/2)>=(1+ln t)/4 for t>=1 prove the displayed 1/512 constant. k=0 yields the clean floor without dividing by k. k=M is legal even though there are fewer than M editable active coins on the selected events; the proof's weaker budget bound remains valid. l=0, j=2 and incomplete last count cells are covered by the exact combinatorial identity. The statistical-order tolerance corollary follows from the displayed uniform bounds.

7. **Upper.** One symbol replacement changes at most three triples; histogram l1 changes at most six. Selecting the common preserved row cannot increase this difference. The normalization inequality with the CLEAN denominator is valid and gives min(1,6k/(J+K)). I independently checked the terminal-occupation argument: the maximum-start probability is nonincreasing with horizon by the Markov suffix argument, the per-path count of times having fewer than w prior visits is <=dw, and max<=sum over starting states yields the d^2w/(M+1) bound. Layer-cake with w=ceil(A/t-K)<=A/t is legitimate for K>=1. R011's beta=0 scales satisfy L=H=G, giving exactly the imported clean lower and upper constants.

## Nonblocking scope/wording note

The displayed `sup_source sup_kernel` quantifiers allow different maximizing kernels when taking different source suprema; merely calling each map source-independent does not itself force a global adversarial commitment. This does not weaken the theorem or create a gap: the upper holds for every allowed map, and the actual lower uses a single explicit C* for all p. The prose already makes this stronger common-kernel fact explicit. If desired, a separate `sup_C inf_A sup_source` known-kernel risk can be defined, with the same lower proof; it is not needed for the current result.
