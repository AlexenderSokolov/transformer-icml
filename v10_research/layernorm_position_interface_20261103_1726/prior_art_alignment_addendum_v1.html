Hahn's O(1/n) influence theorem is the closest general sensitivity precedent. The separate causal recurrence in the candidate accounts for the terminal query, the two changed residual positions and positive-ε LayerNorm with unbounded token-independent residual position coordinates. Its source-specific probability bridge is an additional consequence, not a claim of a novel general attention lower bound.

Barbero et al., “Transformers need glasses!”, analyze causal-residual positional influence; the cited sensitivity analysis simplifies normalization and treats attention weights as input independent, so it does not by itself establish the content-dependent Q/K recurrence here. Yang et al., “Simulating Hard Attention Using Soft Attention”, discuss known escapes using length-sensitive resources, normalization and unbounded position features; those architectures must not be subsumed into this bounded-score result. Makkuva et al., “Attention with Markov” v2 withdraws its earlier higher-order failure claim, so that older claim must not be cited as a theorem supporting the risk candidate.

The search found no published theorem with exactly all the stochastic and architectural quantifiers stated here. That is an explicitly limited search finding, not a novelty certificate. The source's θ and risk target, fixed-network versus finite-N infimum, positive ε, full causal mask, bounded token-independent positional bias, and all residual/readout assumptions remain essential.
