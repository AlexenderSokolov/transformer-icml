Result and qualification

The positional Q/K identity, gate construction, pseudo-count readout, symbolic truncation analysis, dyadic exact-softmax comparison, and stated conditional-risk transfer are valid. The constant 32-dimensional residual layout has sufficient distinct slots under the given 16-dimensional-per-head, two-head convention. The bit ledger needs an explicit representation invariant: use a common denominator for each first-FFN gate's inputs, and canonicalize exact Boolean gates and small Y values before second-layer Q/K arithmetic. Under that evaluation strategy, the proposed P bound is conservatively adequate. Without such a representation invariant, merely aligning attention weights does not bound intermediate denominators in arbitrary unreduced rational evaluation.

2. One-layer direct gate and value

Let g(z)=ReLU(z)−ReLU(z−1), z=2A₀−5/2 and −5/2≤z≤3/2. At integer i=1, g(z)+g(1−z)=1 and g(z+4)=1, so G=0. At i=2, the first two terms cancel and the remaining pair is 1−1. For i≥3, g(z−4(i−2))=0 and the remaining pair is 1−1, so G=g(z). If each copy is within 1/16 of its intended bit, a true pair has z≥5/4 and a false pair has z≤−1/4; the indicator is exactly one or zero. Each G is a sum of four clips, i.e. eight ReLU hinges of affine inputs, in a single hidden layer. No preceding nonlinear validity-flag layer is needed.

zi=ReLU(2−i) is 1 exactly at i=1. Yi=xi−ReLU(xi−(i−1))+zi/2 is 1/2 at i=1 and xi elsewhere. Its affine x term may be passed as ReLU(x) because x is nonnegative, or by an equivalent output/residual linear path. At most 72 hinges is sufficient, including these terms and reuse.

3. Add-one and numerical bounds

At the terminal query the pseudo-count position has weight 2b+1 and value 1/2; full historical matches have weight 2b and sum C. Their normalized contribution is (C+1)/(J+2). Each of the N−1−J remaining positions has weight 1 and value in [0,1]. The error is at most

(N−1−J)/(2ᵇ(J+2)+N−1−J) ≤ N·2⁻ᵇ/2 ≤ η/8.

In particular, N=3 and J=0 are included. The two-token/zero-match boundary is not replaced by an independent-query assumption.

After row centering, first-head logit coefficients have absolute value at most c(N+1)²; second-head coefficients have absolute value at most b+1. With A=max{c(N+1)²,b+1} and the report's p, each dyadic-versus-symbolic logit error is ≤η/128. The likelihood-ratio TV bound gives ≤η/64. Thus the true dyadic-real-softmax copy error is <1/64+η/64≤1/32<1/16 and gates match the symbolic model. Its final scalar deviation from the symbolic second head is at most η/64, yielding 9η/64 total.

The truncated backend forms centered nonnegative integer exponents k and drops weights with k>T=b+1. Since a row maximum has weight 1, dropped ideal mass is ≤N2−T≤η/8; adding the dyadic-versus-symbolic TV gives the reported rowwise approximation. At valid positions the intended lag has k=0, and dropping other keys cannot increase its wrong-key mass. In the second head, the first position is always a maximum and relative exponents are 0, 1 and b+1, all retained, so the backend output is the ideal second-head output. It therefore has the sharper η/8 pathwise bound. This lookup-and-truncation backend is not exact generic exp of dyadic scores.

4. Bit ledger and implementation invariant

Each centered k=c[(j−a)²−minh≤i(h−a)²] is an integer needing O(log(cN²)) bits; deciding k>T can be done without materializing 2k. The ordinary uncentered score magnitude is O(cN²+b), with the separate +b term needed for small η. Align retained weights at denominator 2T. A row numerator or normalizer then has at most T+⌈log₂N⌉+2 bits. Constantly many arithmetic operations on two copy fractions, with their shared D₁D₂ denominator, and integer positions bounded by N² stay within the very generous stated P=10[T+p+b+c+⌈log₂(N+2)⌉+100].

Required explicit evaluation invariant: align all hinge terms for a given gate to the common D₁D₂ denominator; after saturation, encode exact gate outputs as canonical 0/1 and Y as canonical 0, 1/2 or 1. Otherwise generic unreduced rational operations can repeatedly multiply denominators through successive FFN sums and second-layer Q/K dot products, so the report's small P does not follow just from the first attention's aligned weights. Canonicalization is exact, inexpensive, and consistent with the proved margin. Under this invariant, O(N²P²+(P+pout)²) bit operations and O(NP+pout) working/output bits are safe schoolbook upper bounds for a streaming dense-attention executor. These are not conventional fixed-word floating-point guarantees.

The p+2-term arctanh series for ln 2 has tail ≤(3/4)9−(p+2); nearest p-bit rounding keeps total error below 2−p. Unreduced rational preprocessing uses O(p²) bits and O(p⁵) schoolbook bit operations, plus O(T) for a power-of-two table, as stated. The position table or generation is separately charged.
