Perturbation sensitivity of the fixed-parameter decoder

3 November 2026. Explicit exact-real perturbation models and a weight-instability counterexample. This is not an IEEE implementation guarantee, an architecture-wide lower bound or a new softmax-stability theorem. No random or numerical experiments were performed.

Findings. The specific lag-copy circuit is not uniformly robust to any fixed positive adversarial perturbation of one score coefficient: at sufficiently long positions an incorrect neighbor scores higher and the snap gate fails on an explicit word. Conversely, a deliberately loose all-weights/all-position bound proves robustness when per-coordinate perturbations shrink sufficiently quickly with L. These are different statements: the necessary O(1/L) control is for one coefficient and the copy-gate guarantee, while the global polynomial sufficient rate is highly conservative and not necessary.

1. Exact score-error interface and snap margin

The base is the four-block binary causal graph with L=N+2 positions, two BOS values 1/2, fixed weights, full causal support and paid features (1,i,i²,ln i), followed by ordinary two-class vocabulary softmax. See the exact-real candidate and its rate addendum. The first two heads copy lag h=1 or 2 with score

s_h(i,j)=2i(i−h)j−ij²=i(i−h)²−i(j−i+h)².

On the relevant valid rows the ideal wrong mass is at most 2e^(−i)/(1−e^(−i))≤2/(e⁴−1)<2/15. Its value vector is in [0,1]. Suppose, on exactly the same valid support, the perturbed scores differ by a row vector Δ with oscillation osc(Δ)=max Δ−min Δ≤ω, and supplied values differ by at most ν in absolute value; suppose any separate numerical weighted-output error is ≤τ. The copy-value error is at most

2/15 + tanh(ω/4) + ν + τ.

If this is <1/4, the fixed snap T(a)=2ReLU(a−1/4)−2ReLU(a−3/4) returns the exact binary bit. The available margin under this conservative bound is 7/60. This conclusion assumes the snap/gating parameters themselves are exact; perturbed gates need separate accounting. The oscillation formulation removes any common rowwise score shift and is sharper than a bound on raw score magnitude. It follows from the existing Cohen–Fausti total-variation bound, using conventional half-ℓ1 TV.

2. A necessary coefficient scale for this copy implementation

Consider only a perturbation of the coefficient of −ij², leaving the term 2i(i−h)j unchanged:

s_h,η(i,j)=2i(i−h)j−(1+η)ij²,    η>0.

Write r=i−h, and assume r and r−1 are data positions. At the desired key r and its left neighbor,

s_h,η(i,r)−s_h,η(i,r−1)=i[1−η(2r−1)].

Whenever η(2r−1)>1 the wrong neighbor scores strictly higher, hence the desired attention mass is <1/2. Choose an allowed word with value 1 at the desired data position and value 0 at all other data positions; the two BOS values remain 1/2. The copied scalar is at most 1/2+P(desired)/2<3/4, so the stated snap is not exactly 1. A fixed η>0 triggers this for all sufficiently large i. Thus, to preserve this specific all-word, all-positions copy guarantee through length L against such positive perturbations, η must be O(1/L), with the threshold explicit above.

This is not a lower bound on end-to-end prediction risk, a necessity for other copy circuits or a bit-complexity lower bound. In particular the exact-integer backend stores the integer coefficient exactly, so its coefficient error is zero; the counterexample concerns a nonzero analog/entrywise coefficient error that does not decrease with length.

3. A coarse fully specified all-parameter model

Now allow every scalar entry of every Q/K/V/output projection, FFN matrix, bias and final linear-logit readout to change by at most ε≤1. Allow each supplied positional coordinate i,i²,ln i to have absolute error ≤δ≤1 at every position; token/BOS values and exact causal masks remain unchanged. All operations, including softmax, are otherwise exact real operations. The architecture, dimensions, activations and support are fixed; no LayerNorm or dropout is introduced. Let P and P̃ denote the resulting two-class output distributions for one word.

All ideal scalar parameters have magnitude ≤16. Put R=129·17<2¹². With the perturbed parameters, absolute entries/biases are ≤17. Input coordinates are bounded by 2L² for L≥4. For an activation bounded by B≥1 in ℓ∞, each Q/K/V affine output is ≤RB; attention is a convex value average and its output projection plus residual is ≤2R²B. The two FFN affine maps and residual multiply this by at most 2R², so one block is bounded by 4R⁴B<2⁵⁰B. Four blocks and all their affine intermediates are safely bounded by

B_L=2²¹⁰ L².

This B_L bounds Q,K,V and feed-forward values, not raw attention logits. It intentionally makes no use of sparsity, masks beyond causality, exact snapping or shift cancellation.

Let E be the largest coordinate difference entering a block. Every perturbed affine map differs by at most T=R(E+B_L ε). For head dimension 16 and scaling 1/4, score differences are ≤8B_L T. Exact softmax stability and the value bound [−B_L,B_L] give head-output coordinate error ≤T+8B_L²T≤9B_L²T. Applying the output projection and residual yields error ≤11R²B_L²(E+B_Lε). A ReLU is 1-Lipschitz; the two FFN maps plus residual then give

E_next ≤ K_L(E+B_L ε),     K_L=24R⁴B_L² < 2⁵³B_L².

With E₀≤δ and four blocks, E₄≤K_L⁴(δ+4B_Lε). The final affine map adds at most R(E₄+B_Lε); vocabulary softmax is TV-Lipschitz at most one-half in score sup norm. A deliberately looser explicit bound is

TV(P̃,P) ≤ min{1, 2²¹²⁰ [ L¹⁶δ + L¹⁸ε ]}.

The constants are intentionally enormous and do not predict useful numerical tolerances. They prove only that δ=o(L⁻¹⁶) and ε=o(L⁻¹⁸) suffice for the output perturbation to vanish in this exact-real model. For example δ,ε≤2⁻²¹²⁴ L⁻¹⁹ make it O(1/L), preserving the original O(N⁻¹/²) risk order. The constructed sufficient tolerances are far from shown necessary, and the coefficient counterexample above rules out extrapolating them into a general lower bound.

4. Score scaling, position features and finite arithmetic

  • Directly perturbing i and i² coordinates by at most δ≤1 changes the unperturbed first-copy score, with fixed matrices, by at most a conservative 10L²δ per entry. This raw bound can be much larger than the shift-invariant score oscillation; neither bound asserts that a common perturbation harms softmax.
  • The last Gaussian attention scores 2pj−zj² amplify separately measured errors by at most 2L|Δp|+L²|Δz| before removing a common score shift. If the realised p remains in [0,1], one can instead apply the underlying uniform Gaussian approximation at that realised p and charge |Δp| only once; z, positions and score-computation errors still need accounting.
  • The specialized integer backend uses exact integer coefficients, exactly computed i and i², explicit approximations to ln i and exponentials, row max shifts and length-growing dyadic precision. Its error proof is not a robustness proof for perturbed network weights and is not generic IEEE arithmetic.
  • Ordinary finite precision must separately bound parameter storage, activation/position encoding, matrix products, ReLU thresholds, max subtraction, underflow/overflow, exponentiation, normalization and final logits. Blanchard, Higham and Higham analyze numerical log-sum-exp and softmax; no property of a specific hardware backend is established here.

5. Risk and interpretation

For any source and actual terminal event, adding the pathwise TV error above to the separately established baseline risk is valid by triangle inequality. If the quoted perturbation rates hold uniformly over all words at each N, the bound also holds after the worst-source supremum. A fixed coefficient perturbation can destroy the lag-copy argument, but this note does not establish that its final risk is inconsistent or that all fixed-parameter Transformers are fragile. The full prior-art and source theorem dependencies remain those of the linked candidate.
