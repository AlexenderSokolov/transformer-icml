Disposition: no mathematical blocker found. On each path, (M+1)u_r=C_r+1 and (M+1)v_r=J_r+2 are integers. If all eight coordinates have absolute error η<1/[2(M+1)] and M is known, exact multiplication by M+1 leaves each estimate strictly within 1/2 of its integer. Nearest-integer rounding then recovers it uniquely; strictness avoids tie-breaking ambiguity. Subtracting 1 and 2 respectively recovers C_r and J_r, including zero counts. At M=0 the exact coordinates are (1,2), so the same argument and final output 1/2 apply.

The original direct ratio route's O(M−3/2) bound is sufficient but not necessary for this alternate count-rounding interface: the stronger recovery route permits a suitably small constant multiple of (M+1)−1 absolute boundary error. This is not a lower bound or automatic numerical Transformer guarantee. The paid assumptions remain: known M, exact scaling and integer rounding or their own reliable error analysis, the eight-coordinate bank, context feature construction, masks, selector, final division and output rounding. O(log(M+1)) fractional bits is an asymptotic resource statement; O(log(M+2)) is a convenient all-M formulation including M=0.
