2. The strongest rejection argument: straightforward composition

For J>0, q = (1−λ)r+λ(C/J), where λ=γJ/(m+γJ); algebra also holds without division by J in count form. If, under conditioning on SN=00, r→μ, J/m−u→0, and (C−tJ)/m is small strongly enough relative to the denominator, then q approximates (μ+γut)/(1+γu). Consequently the absolute prediction loss approximates |μ−t|/(1+γu). This is the canonical interpolation bias formula obtained by composition, not a new interpolation law. [L1] makes this quantitative through its explicit D₀, terminal-conditioned occupation and martingale controls. Those controls are nontrivial technical work, and must be evaluated against their own dependencies; the conclusion may still be a useful specialization, but exact expression and constants alone do not establish a research-level advance.

At t=1/2, c(u)=(1−4u)/6. From |risk−c(u)/(1+γu)|≤D₀, the necessary and sufficient inequalities of [L1], (6)–(7), follow by monotonic algebra. If u≤1/8, ε≪c(u), and D₀=o(ε), the scale is γu=Θ(1/ε), hence e^β=Θ(1/(uε)). This is a conditional direct-q requirement, not a network lower bound. When u↑1/4, c(u)→0 and no positive uniform bias lower bound exists; ε near c(u) makes β=0 potentially adequate. For other t, μ−t may vanish even away from u=1/4. A result about one t cannot be generalized to all sources or all estimators: at the singled-out t=1/2, the constant predictor 1/2 has zero true-row risk, so a pointwise direct-q lower bound is especially far from an estimator-class lower bound.

Under the explicit sequence-level condition in [L2], D₀→0 and the risk differs by o(1) from c(um)/(1+γmum) at t=1/2, allowing finite or diverging γu. A fixed source with fixed u>0 and fixed β instead converges to c(u)/(1+γu), not automatically c(u); letting β→∞ removes that bias. The adversarial triangular array um=m−1+a/2 under βm≤(1−a)log m has mu→∞, γu→0, c(u)→1/6, and the stated risk limit 1/6. This follows directly from the finite bound; each path is sampled under one fixed source, but the source varies between experiments. It is not a fixed-source inconsistency theorem. The sufficient β=log(1+m) requires the displayed stronger condition mu/log(2m(m+1)/u)→∞, not merely mu→∞, and does not resolve the log gap or a sharp architecture-independent threshold.

3. Dependence, positivity, and scope stress tests

  • The target is E[|q−t| | SN=00] against the true fixed θ00, not E|q−C/J| and not Bayes log loss. SN, counts and query are correlated. [L1] derives the martingale exponential bound before dividing by P(SN=00)=u and does not assume conditional independence. Any reuse must preserve this conditioning and its u-dependent cost. The reverse-chain occupation bounds and common-atom constants are invoked from the prior rare-family report; this review has not independently re-proved those dependencies.
  • The forward one-step transition family has zeros for forbidden shifts but the stipulated t,u give legal probabilities. At u=0 the event has probability zero and its conditional risk is undefined. At u↑1/4 positivity of c fails. In scarce J=0 cases q remains pooled; add-one B=1/2 is defined but is not equal to q in general. D₀ can exceed 1 and requires v=mu≥60; do not market it as a useful rate without checking the numerical regime.
  • The sample and population interpolation weights are not independent of counts. Substituting E[C/J]=t, replacing E[r | terminal] by μ, or equating expectation of a ratio with ratio of expectations is invalid without the stated bounds. Conditioning also does not preserve the forward martingale argument automatically.
  • The direct head is only a specified uncorrected output. A second pooled head and occupancy port plus a decoder can remove the soft mixture; full residuals, raw histories, counting, smoothing, and bypasses may do more. A q/r/h port collision is a fidelity lower bound against B on restricted observations, not a lower bound for estimating fixed t or an arbitrary transformer. Compare with matched accounting against an explicit counter and empirically estimated transition predictor, not merely a weak q-only baseline.
  • R011’s unconditional risk does not imply the same conditional inequality without a factor 1/u; conversely a conditional rare-row lower contributes only after multiplying by query probability u to a global average. Nonzero query probability and parameter independence across experiments matter.
  • α in [D] is a prior pseudo-count, not a rare-state frequency; u is stationary mass, not a Dirichlet coordinate. Composite z has constrained shift transitions and a nonuniform stationary distribution, so no symmetric independent-row Dirichlet law or uniform-context simplification transfers. Formally identical β formulas are not identical optimality claims.

4. Suggested precise claim and repairs

A defensible limited formulation, pending verification of the common-atom and occupation lemmas, is: “For this stationary binary rare-state family, an explicitly specified two-weight direct output has conditional true-row TV risk within D₀ of the source-level interpolation bias; the same bound yields a triangular-array failure and sufficient source-independent schedule under stated evidence conditions.” Cite [D] immediately for the identical ideal estimator and schedule, and [R], [I], [R011] for conditional-count representation, related softmax count ratio and existing project risk baseline. Describe this as a focused finite-sample specialization and open novelty question, not a first result. Reject a headline claim of a new score law, new q interpolation, general Markov consistency, network-depth lower bound, or score necessity for arbitrary architectures.

Open proof/research obligations: recheck the cited reverse-chain mixing and occupation lemmas, including constants, all index conventions and actual terminal dependence; find sharp finite-sample estimates or matching lower bounds against the strongest same-information model; establish a quantitative gain not already a routine corollary of prior results, with full architecture and computational cost; compare worst-case and conditional rare-row loss on equal budgets; conduct a broader targeted primary-literature search. The current two alternatives, exact-match counting and q/r/h correction, may defeat any claimed general resource barrier.
